From Iris — how we talk about the work (no hex, no measurements, do not turn this into ad copy)

Salt, iron, cold light. That is the whole feeling. Nothing shiny, nothing that shouts.

One ink doing the work, and plenty of air around it. The bottle should earn its place in a hand, not across a shop. If a version looks busy or clever it is wrong.

The wax is the colour of dried blood on the neck. The cap is cold grey. The crate is just the timber and one dark stamp. Keep it that quiet.

Write the supplier sheet like an instruction, not an advertisement. Say plainly what a version can and cannot do. And if something in our own list looks wrong to you, say so before you make it.
