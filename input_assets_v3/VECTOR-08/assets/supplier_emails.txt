Four forwarded supplier messages (pasted by the founders)

--- 1. FROM: Aldous Wax & Seal Co. ---
We pour your neck seals from a single stamping die. We need the mark as ONE solid vector, black on transparent, no tints, no hairlines under 1mm, and nothing enclosed that has to stay open — our die floods any counter smaller than about 2mm. Last batch you sent a file with a fine rope outline and it filled solid on the seal. Send us the plate you actually want stamped.

--- 2. FROM: Kerr Stencil Cutting ---
Stencils are cut on a plotter. One colour, and every shape must be a closed island the blade can lift — no open strokes, no shading, no cross-hatch. If it has more than a couple of thin bridges it falls apart on the crate. We need the simplest one-ink version you have.

--- 3. FROM: Northgate Closures (caps) ---
The cap emboss is tooled from a single-depth relief, so give us one flat shape, high contrast, no gradient and no fine line. Roughly 14mm across on the cap top. If the hull goes to a black blob at that size tell us and we will simplify.

--- 4. FROM: Redgate Print (labels) ---
We still hold your 2025 label file and can re-run it. Reminder it is set two-up on the cutter (front and back on one sheet) so whatever you change has to keep both. We never had the back copy as separate fields, it came to us as one block, so if you are re-flowing it be careful. No bleed on that die.
