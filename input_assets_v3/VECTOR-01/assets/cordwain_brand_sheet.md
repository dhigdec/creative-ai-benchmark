# Cordwain Overland -- brand sheet (how we brief the printer)

## Palette, in words
- BASALT: a near-black warm charcoal. Our workhorse ink.
- TRAIL SAND: a muted tan/oat. Good on dark garments.
- PINE SHADOW: a deep muted green.
- RUST RIDGE: a burnt orange accent, used sparingly.
- BONE: a warm off-white, our light garment colour and a light ink.

## Placement rules (we have always used these)
- Full back: centred, top of print 3 inches below the collar seam.
- Chest: left chest OR centred chest depending on the graphic.
- Badge: centred chest on hoodies.

## Maximum print widths
- Full back: 12 inches wide max.
- Centre chest: 10 inches wide max.
- Left chest: 4 inches wide max.
- Hoodie badge: 3.5 inches wide max.

## What we mean by a tight print
Two or three tight inks with a bit of texture. Honest registration. We would rather run fewer inks well than a muddy six-colour job. Never price the line out of its own customers -- the printer charges per screen.
