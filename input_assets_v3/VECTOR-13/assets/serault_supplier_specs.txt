MAISON SERAULT - PACKAGING ARTWORK REQUIREMENTS
From the new box supplier and the engraver. Treat every figure as a hard limit.

BOX SUPPLIER (foil blocking on dark stock)
  Box stock: Boutique Charcoal (#2A2724), a very dark uncoated board.
  Foil: Warm Brass (#B08D4C).
  Foil spread allowance: 0.15mm. Foil spreads outward into the stock on press, so the reversed/knock-out artwork must be drawn to allow for it or fine counters close.
  Minimum foil stroke on the dark stock: 0.30mm at final size. Anything thinner fills in.
  Clear space around the mark: 6mm on all sides, minimum.
  Box face panel: 90mm x 90mm.

  RIBBON
  Ribbon width: 10mm. The wordmark runs ALONG the ribbon.
  Wordmark used width on the ribbon: 60mm long, so cap height works out to roughly 7mm.
  Same 0.30mm minimum stroke applies on the ribbon.

ENGRAVER (clasp plates)
  Requires a SINGLE CLOSED OUTLINE of each mark, one path, counters present as true holes, not a filled picture.
  Minimum engraved cut width: 0.25mm at final size.
  Clasp plate area for the monogram: 8mm x 8mm.

CARD (the enclosure card, foil on cream)
  Card: 100mm x 70mm on Card Cream (#EFE9DC).
  Monogram used size on the card: about 18mm.
