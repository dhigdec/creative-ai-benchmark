Governor sheave assemblies are manufactured, machined and balanced to EN 81-20 and EN 81-50 and are supplied with a certified overspeed test report and a traceable heat number. The assembly must be installed and commissioned by a competent person against the original equipment manufacturer’s rated tripping speed and re-tested on commissioning, in accordance with the lift safety code in force at the date of despatch.
