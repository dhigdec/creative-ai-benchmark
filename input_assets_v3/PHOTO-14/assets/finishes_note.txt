GIRDERMARK — HOW TO TELL THE FINISHES APART (sales)

Galvanised: the spangled dull one. Dull grey with the crystal pattern in it. Not shiny.

Zinc plate: the bright bluish one. Clean and bright with a slight blue tint. Do not let it go grey and do not let it go brassy.

Clear anodised: the flat grey one. A flat even grey with no blue and no spangle. Do not let it collapse into the powder coat.

Powder coat: the flat black one. Dead flat, even black, no shine.

A buyer specifies a finish off the picture. If plated steel looks brassy or anodised looks the same as powder coat, they stop trusting the numbers next to it. Keep the four clearly different from each other.
