MERIDIAN AND ASH — STICKER PRINTER SPEC
(what our printer sent when we asked what they need)

SHEET
  Gang sheet size: 305 x 457 mm (12 x 18 in), white gloss vinyl, solvent print.
  Safe margin from sheet edge: 10 mm. No artwork and no cut inside this margin.
  Minimum gutter between adjacent cut contours: 6 mm.

CUT CONTOUR — STANDING REQUIREMENT
  Supply the kiss-cut contour as ITS OWN separable object: one closed path per sticker on a dedicated cut layer named 'CutContour'. Do NOT draw the cut line into the picture. Our last supplier baked it into the artwork and the press operator ended up redrawing it by hand — we are not doing that again.
  Contour offset: 2.5 mm outward from the artwork edge.
  Corners: minimum 3 mm radius so the blade can follow it.
  Interior negative space: resolve deliberately (a kiss-cut does not weed interior islands unless you say so).

ARTWORK
  Single stickers reproduce at 55 mm on the long edge on the tent table. Keep fine interior negative space OPEN at 55 mm.

TRANSPARENT PROOFS
  Also supply each sticker as a transparent PNG at press scale for our RIP preview: genuine alpha, no white rectangle behind it.

SIZES / PRICING (reference)
  Singles $3, four-pack $9.
