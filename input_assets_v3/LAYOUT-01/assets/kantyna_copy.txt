KANTYNA NOVA — APPROVED COPY (course titles are set, do not rewrite)

Tagline: One seating. One menu. One evening.
About: A single table set once a night. One tasting, one wine flight, cooked and served by the two of us. The room is dark and quiet by design, so the plate is the only thing lit.
Voice: first person plural, plain, understated. Never salesy.

THE TASTING — fourteen courses, in order (page one of the menu)
1. Oyster, brown butter, sea lettuce — Cold, from the first minute of the evening.
2. Smoked mussel, celeriac, dulse — Smoke, then the sea underneath it.
3. Cured fluke, buttermilk, green apple — Cured that morning, dressed at the pass.
4. Sea urchin, potato custard, brioche — Set warm, a shard of brioche to break it.
5. Roasted beetroot, aged goat, walnut — Roasted whole in the ash, cut to order.
6. Hand-cut tagliatelle, brown crab, chilli — Rolled that afternoon, crab picked by hand.
7. Seared scallop, cauliflower, brown butter — One scallop, seared hard, brown butter to finish.
8. Halibut, leek, mussel cream — Poached soft, the cream built from the shells.
9. Duck, salsify, blackberry — Aged on the bone, blackberry from the reduction.
10. Dry-aged beef, bone marrow, burnt onion — Forty days, marrow and onion beside it.
11. Aged comté, honeycomb — Kept until it is ready, honey from up the coast.
12. Brown butter tart, sea salt — The pastry we make every day.
13. Chocolate, olive oil, malt — Dark, oil poured at the table, malt for the crunch.
14. Petit fours — Whatever we baked that morning. [NOTE: this course has never been photographed]

HOURS (for the reservations page)
Wednesday to Sunday. One seating a night. Doors at 6:30, seated at 7:00. Closed Monday and Tuesday.
The private dining side seats a smaller room on request.

RESERVATION WORDING (approved)
"One seating a night, and the room is small. Book the evening and let it happen — we will hold the table from 6:30. If your plans change, tell us as early as you can; there is no third of us to fill an empty seat."

THE DECK — how the price is built up (final slide, plain, price never a feature)
"The tasting is one hundred and eighty five dollars a guest. The wine pairing is another ninety five. That is the evening: fourteen courses, one flight, cooked and served by the two of us. There is no cover, no service charge added at the table, and nothing else to buy. Eighteen covers a night is the whole business, so the price is what it takes to set this table once and set it well."
