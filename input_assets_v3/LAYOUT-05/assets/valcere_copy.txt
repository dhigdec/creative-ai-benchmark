TAGLINE
One object at a time.

THE HOUSE
Maison Valcere is a four-person high-jewellery house, founded in 2014 in a room above a bookshop in Montreal. Twenty-two references sit in the current book, most cut and set by hand on one bench and shown by appointment. We work to order, for marriages and milestones, for clients who return once a decade rather than once a season.

PROVENANCE (fixed - do not reword)
Every Valcere piece is cut and set by hand on a single bench in Montreal. Stones are chosen one at a time and matched by eye. Metal is worked to the hand it is made for. A piece leaves the bench once, and it is meant to be kept.

APPOINTMENT
The autumn book is shown by appointment above the shop. Allow an hour. We bring out the pieces you ask for and the pieces beside them. Nothing sits on a floor; everything is made to your hand. Prices run nine hundred to six thousand five hundred dollars and are shown discreetly or on request.

FOR STOCKISTS
Maison Valcere is carried by a short list of stockists. Pieces ship on the house's own dark presentation band, so each piece must sit clean with no plate behind it. Metal, assay, stone and carat are stated on the card that travels with each piece; the assay wording is regulated and is not to be altered. Reorder by reference.

A NOTE ON THE MARK
The wordmark is the house. It is purely typographic - no symbol, no icon. Set in the house's high-contrast neoclassical caps with its grave accent and generous letterspacing, in House Black on Bench Ivory, or reversed in Signature White on House Black. It must still read as our wordmark at the size it sits on a boutique tile and reversed on our black, hairline serifs and accent intact.
