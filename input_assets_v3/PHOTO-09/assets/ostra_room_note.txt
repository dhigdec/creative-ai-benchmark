OSTRA — how we talk about the room, and the courses spelled the way the kitchen spells them.

THE ROOM. Twenty-two seats around the pass, one seasonal tasting menu at a hundred and thirty-five dollars a head, four nights a week. Near black, spare, unhurried. One plate at a time. The photographs should feel like walking into the room, not like an advertisement. Quiet, dark, unhurried. Not a bistro and not a hotel restaurant.

FOR THE STUDIO'S DESIGNER. We need FINISHED photographs from you, not raw files. Your build is dark: near black ground, very little on the page, one plate at a time, mobile responsive. The plates have to read as one set scrolled one after another, and they also have to survive the delivery platforms, which put every picture on a WHITE card in a grid next to everybody else.
