Transcriptions of the postcard backs - typed by Margaret, February 2024.
We kept his spelling and his crossings-out. Where we could not read a word we put [?]. Please set these beside each card exactly as typed; do not tidy them.

--- postcard_marvelli_01_back.jpg ---
The Veranda Marvelli Company, third season. That is Aldo second from the left and I am at the back with the cabinet. We played the Bijou at Rutland two weeks. Cold house, good money. - F.

--- postcard_marvelli_02_back.jpg ---
Aldo and the vanishing cabinet. He would not let me touch the catch for a whole year. This was taken by the stage-door man at Keene, I think, Oct. 1910. Send this to your mother, she likes to see the costume.

--- postcard_marvelli_03_back.jpg ---
The whole company outside the Grand at Brattleboro before the matinee. Eleven of us that season. The tall one is the manager, Mr. Pell, who owed everybody. Do not [?] the frame, it is the only one we have.

--- postcard_marvelli_04_back.jpg ---
Myself in the assistant's coat, before I grew out of it. Aldo said I had the hands for it. I did not, but I had the back for the boxes. - your father
