STATIC UNION — AUTUMN SMALL GOODS — MAKER LIMITS (treat as HARD)

These are the three makers' own words. The patch factory turned our first attempt down against the first block. Please meet all of them exactly.

1) EMBROIDERED PATCHES (the patch factory)
   - Ground: dark navy/black twill. Finished size 2.5 in (63 mm) across, merrowed edge.
   - Thread colours: SIX (6) colours MAXIMUM per patch, including the edge. No exceptions, this is a per-head limit on the machine.
   - Minimum stroke / minimum detail: nothing thinner than 1.2 mm at final size will hold in thread. Anything thinner blobs or drops out.
   - Art must be built as bold thread blocks, not fine line art. "If it looks like a printed sticker it is not patch art."

2) ENAMEL PINS (the pin maker)
   - Size 1 in (25 mm). Polished or plated metal, hard or soft enamel.
   - Metal line minimum: raised metal lines and the outline may not fall below 0.30 mm at final size.
   - Enamel bridge minimum: 0.20 mm — every enamel colour well must be fully enclosed by metal so the colour cannot run into the next. Two adjacent colours need a metal bridge between them.
   - Deliver as SEPARATED plates: a metal (line) plate and an enamel (fill) plate. Not one flattened picture.

3) DIE-CUT / KISS-CUT STICKERS (the sticker converter)
   - Size 3 in (76 mm) on white gloss vinyl.
   - Kiss-cut line: a single outward offset of the art silhouette, offset 2.0 mm outward from the art. The cut may not cross the artwork.
   - Minimum enclosed window: 3.0 mm — any weeded window inside the cut must be at least 3.0 mm across or it will not weed.
   - Full detail is fine here; vinyl holds the whole drawing.

GROUND / SIZE SUMMARY: patch 2.5 in on dark twill; pin 1 in on metal; sticker 3 in on white vinyl. Woven cap label: mark reproduced at 45 mm label width, one thread colour on dark ground.
