MARROW AND VANE
Autumn exhibition — desk note from the director

SHOW
Title: GROUND AND FIGURE
Nine artists. Three rooms. Eighteen September through the second of November.
Private view: the evening of the seventeenth.

THE ROOMS, in this order
1. The Front Room
2. The Cabinet
3. The Long Gallery

THE ARTISTS
Marisol Enback, Tomás Grieve, Ilse Vanterpool, Petra Volková, Cyrus Mbeki, Della Hartley, Junot Sarraf, Wilhelmina Roa, Aksel Norr.

THE LABEL
One card per hung work. Title, artist, year, medium, dimensions, the credit where a work carries one, and our accession number small at the foot. No price on the card, no rule, no colour, no mark. Keep it as the printer set it in spring — the rooms are hung the same way and the labels should not look like a new decision. Small, quiet type high on the card, a lot of unprinted stock beneath it. The work carries the room.

CREDITS — set exactly as written, do not paraphrase
Most works carry no credit and take none. Where a work carries one it sits on its own line directly under the dimensions, in the form the registrar has entered it: "Courtesy of ..." or "Promised gift of ...". Two works in this show carry a credit; the rest do not.

THE CHECKLIST
The folded document for the desk and the press list. Same works, in hang order, three room sections, a small reference picture of each. We update it ourselves the week after the opening — two works will sell and one comes off the wall — so it has to stay something we can retype, not a picture of a list.

THE MARK
Our mark is the etched brass plate by the door: MARROW & VANE under a rule. On the checklist we want it as the mark itself, at its own small size, not a photograph of the brass.

PRESS
Press and image requests: press@marrowandvane.example — the registrar's desk, Wednesdays and Fridays.
