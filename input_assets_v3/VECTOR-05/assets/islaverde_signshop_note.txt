ISLA VERDE — SIGN SHOP REQUIREMENTS
(what the new fabricator sent when we asked what they need)

SUBSTRATE AND FINISH
  Directional panels: 3 mm brushed aluminium composite, matt laminate, direct print.
  Door plates: 2 mm anodised aluminium, sub-surface print.
  Door-plate fixings are the EXISTING 2016 drilling — plate trim cannot change.

PANEL CUT SIZES (our standard cut list)
  Directional, corridor: 200 x 300 mm portrait.
  Directional, garden: 250 x 350 mm portrait, weatherised.
  Overhead / junction: 400 x 150 mm landscape.
  Door plate: 150 x 60 mm landscape (matches the 2016 plate).

TYPE — minimum legible cap height
  25 mm at 5 m viewing distance.
  18 mm at 3 m.
  Door plates read at about 0.8 m: 8 mm minimum.
  Do not set below these heights.

CLEAR SPACE
  Keep all artwork and type at least 12 mm inside the cut edge on directional panels; 6 mm on door plates.

COLOUR / FILES
  Two spot colours preferred for the etch feel. Supply vector at true physical size, monogram and symbols as closed paths.

DELIVER
  One editable document with every panel at true cut size, plus the door-plate run as one-per-page press-resolution output, plus a schedule we can work from.
