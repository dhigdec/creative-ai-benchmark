NORDHEIM ATELIER - FW26 PRESS NOTES (approved copy; the two essay passages are the ones press will run)

SEASON NOTE
Twelve looks in undyed Gotland wool, Merino shearling and cotton gabardine. Cut on the straight, fulled at one mill in West Yorkshire, made by hand in Copenhagen. Cloth first.

ESSAY ONE - ON THE CLOTH
We started with the fleece and worked back. Undyed Gotland wool holds a grey no dye can give you, so we left it. The shearling is vegetable-tanned in Tuscany and turned so the skin reads as much as the wool. The gabardine is woven tight enough to hold an edge and cut so it falls straight. Nothing here is finished to hide the making; the seam is the seam and the button is horn. A coat should tell you how it was built when you pick it up.

ESSAY TWO - ON THE RANGE
This is one collection, not twelve pictures. We shot it over two days and lost the light on the second, and the work since has been to make the twelve read as one hand. The argument is restraint: held space, a hard black point, the cloth carrying the page. We would rather show you the shoulder of a coat than a styled room. If the range reads as one thing, the season has done its work.
