From the founder — what the mark is meant to mean (read it, then tell me what you think)

I made the main mark in an image tool at 2 a.m. two years ago. The two arms locking together are supposed to be two schedules meshing without a gap — that is the whole product, things fitting that usually do not. I know it is soft. I still like the glow, it feels calm, halcyon, which is where the name comes from. Tell me honestly what happens to that glow if you rebuild it, I would rather know.

The two sub-product marks I made later, separately, and they have never quite felt like the main one. If they should change to belong, change them.

Our buyers are engineers. They read fuss as sloppiness. Sharp, plain, restrained — no gloss for its own sake. If something in our own list turns out to be a bad idea, tell us before you build it.
