CATEGORY COMPLIANCE SHEET (one page, from our regulatory file)
Anything on this list does NOT run, however good the line reads. This sheet does not flag individual hooks; that judgement is yours.

1. NO COMPARATIVE SUPERIORITY. We may not claim the product works better, absorbs better, or is more effective than any other product, category, brand or competitor. "better than", "more absorbable than", "beats", "outperforms" — all out.
2. NO DISEASE / CONDITION CLAIMS. We may not say the product treats, cures, clears, fixes, prevents or relieves any condition or symptom — this explicitly includes bloating, fatigue, inflammation and deficiency.
3. NO "clinically proven" or "doctor recommended" — we hold no substantiation on file.
4. NO absolute health outcomes: no "boosts immunity", "detoxes", "balances hormones".
5. NO before/after or guaranteed-result language.

PERMITTED: subjective, first-person, habit-and-taste language. Structure/function only, hedged, and never by comparison. "I kept drinking it", "don't have to talk myself into it", "in the morning" are all fine.

A NOTE ON LENGTH (not a compliance matter): a hook has to land in the first read at vertical caption size. If a line has to be shrunk to fit, it has stopped being a hook.
