LOW BEAM — the take (my notes, from memory)

One take, out the passenger window, north of the city, around eleven at night. Maybe forty-five seconds, the whole thing. These are the bits I keep coming back to. The positions are rough — I did them from memory on the couch, so go by the picture, not by my numbers.

~0:03  first lamp comes up, road still empty, everything still. this is the one to open on.
~0:09  wet asphalt catching the amber, the double yellow down the left.
~0:16  the long dark stretch with no lamps, just the low beam on the road. quiet.
~0:22  the warm pool of light by the closed gas station.
~0:29  the overpass with the two red lights up high on the left, the aircraft-warning ones. this is the mood exactly, it has to be in there.
~0:31  the guardrail curving off to the right as the road bends.
~0:40  the tunnel mouth, the lights start coming fast.

Seven beats. Pull the six that feel like one minute of one night. Trust your eye over my timings.
