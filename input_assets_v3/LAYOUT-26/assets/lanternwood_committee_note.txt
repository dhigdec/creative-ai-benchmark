LANTERNWOOD FOLK FEST - committee note to the designer (everything else you need from us)

TICKETS
Sixty-five pounds a day. Two thousand capacity, two stages, fourteen acts across the weekend. Weekend and day tickets both. Under sixteens free with a paying adult.

THE TWO STAGES (please use these exact names on every page and both boards)
- The Barn Stage (the big one, in the old threshing barn)
- The Orchard Stage (the smaller one, out in the orchard by the cider tent)

THE HEADLINERS (this one matters)
Saturday is closed by The Hollow Pines on The Barn Stage.
Sunday is closed by Marla Vance & the Tallgrass on The Barn Stage.
Those two are the reason a lot of people bought a ticket. Whatever running order you settle on, they have to READ as the headliners, closing their own day, not buried in the middle of a list because that is where our spreadsheet happened to put them. Please do not sequence them as though they are just two more acts.

RUNNING ORDER
The line-up sheet is in the order we booked people, which is NOT the order the weekend runs in. The programme has to run in the order the acts actually go on, so someone standing in the field can find who is on next. Two stages running at the same time makes that a judgement rather than a plain sort, so tell us in the note how you decided.

THE LICENCE LINE (must appear in the programme, word for word)
'Lanternwood Folk Fest is run under a Temporary Event Notice granted by Mendip District Council (ref TEN/26/0912). Please drink responsibly and look after the field - it is a working farm and we would like to be asked back.'

THE DEADLINE
The printer needs the press file by Wednesday 23 September. They want three working days after that, so the 23rd is hard, not hopeful. If something cannot be done in time, tell us now, not on the 22nd.

AND
Please tell us anything you find that we should know before it goes to print. We would far rather hear it from you than find it framed on a kitchen wall.
