The Bramble Sisters

The Orchard Stage  ·  Saturday  ·  3:00pm

The Bramble Sisters are not sisters, and only one of them is called Bramble, but the name stuck the way the good ones do. The three of them met singing in a chapel choir and discovered, somewhere between the hymns, that their voices did something strange and lovely when they leaned into a minor chord together. For eight years now they have carried that sound around the country, unaccompanied for whole songs at a time, letting three voices do the work of a full band. Their set moves from old shape-note hymns to their own writing and back again without ever quite telling you where the seam was. It is the kind of thing that can stop a noisy field halfway through a sentence. We have put them on the Orchard Stage in the afternoon light entirely on purpose. Find a bale of hay, sit yourself down, and let them get to work on you.
