REDECKER MACHINE WORKS — WORKS STANDARDS
(for the mark recovery and the November plate run)

THE MARK
Carried on every machine since 1919. One mark, reproduced flat, mechanical, high contrast, generously spaced. Never heritage or distressed, never a thin consumer-tech geometric sans. Weight and spacing do the work.

WORKS GREEN — as the paint shop mixes it
  HEX 33564A | RGB 51 86 74 | CMYK 70 30 45 55 | approx PMS 5535 C
  This is the FIELD colour. The mark reverses out of it clean, including the enclosed interior of the ring.

VARIANTS REQUIRED FROM THE RECOVERED MARK
  1. Master (full colour)
  2. One-colour (Machine Black) for the invoice stamp
  3. Reversed knockout out of Works Green (ring interior must knock out, not fill)
  4. Single closed-outline cut file for the laser house (bay-door sign)
All four off ONE geometry.

NOTE FROM THE FLOOR
Whichever surviving source is used, the current mark is the one WITHOUT the outer place-name arc (the 1974 catalogue added the arc; the machines do not carry it).
