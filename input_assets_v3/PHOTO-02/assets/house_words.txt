HOW WE TALK ABOUT THE ROOM — Corner and Cure

Warm, never fussy. We let the food carry the sentence.

Words we use: burnt butter, the good bread, scrubbed oak, the pass, a glass of something, most nights until late. Warm but never orange. Generous. Somebody cooked it.

Words we do not use: artisanal, curated, elevated, drizzle, deconstructed, journey. Nothing that sounds like a chain and nothing that sounds like a tasting menu.

The room: forty covers and a counter on the west side. Sandwiches and bowls at lunch. Small plates and wine once the pendants come on at four. Regulars who come twice a week and know the staff.

Example line we would write ourselves: "Small plates and a glass of something, most nights until late."

One rule that is not a style note: Danny's dish copy is final. Do not reword it, do not shorten it, do not re-space it. The allergen line matters and it stays exactly as written.
