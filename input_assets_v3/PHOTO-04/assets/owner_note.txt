THE THREE HOUSES, AND WHAT WE DO NOT WANT DONE TO THEM
From the owner. Verranza Coastal Retreats.

We let three houses above the cove at Punta Verranza:
- Casa Verranza, sleeps 6. The oldest, warm and simple, the one my grandfather bought.
- Casa Oliveto, sleeps 8. In the olive grove, a green terrace sail for the heat.
- Casa Scogliera, sleeps 10. On the rocks, the biggest, the best sea view. It sleeps ten, not nine, whatever the sheet says.

We sold the fourth house, Casa Fienile, last year. It is not ours to let any more and must not go to agents.

Seasons, the way we quote agents: we call them Spring, High Summer and Autumn. If the booking sheet has them as S1, S2 and S3, that is Spring, High Summer, Autumn in that order. Please put the words agents know on the cards, not the codes.

The do-not-want list, in my words: nothing cooked. No water a colour that water is not. No stone turned into a computer's idea of stone. No fake evening glow, no orange skin, no crushed blacks. Anders and I have both arrived somewhere the pictures had lied and we will not do that to a guest. A guest choosing between our own three houses should not be able to tell they were shot on different days. That is the whole job.

And the mark: the monogram the village smith cut for my grandfather is the only one there is. Give it back to us as something we can place anywhere, small on a card and reversed out of our deep blue, and still have it be ours.
