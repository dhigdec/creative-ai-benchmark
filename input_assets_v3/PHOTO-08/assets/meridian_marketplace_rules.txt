NATIONAL VEHICLE MARKETPLACE — SELLER FIRST-IMAGE STANDARDS
(copied off their seller help page — this is what keeps bouncing our listings)

EVERY listing's FIRST image (the primary tile) must meet ALL of the following or the listing is rejected:

1. SUBJECT: the vehicle only, fully within the frame, all four corners of the vehicle visible, with clear space ("air") on every side. No part of the vehicle cropped off.

2. BACKGROUND: a plain, single, neutral background. Nothing else in the frame — no other vehicles, no people, no signage, no forecourt clutter. A pure white or a plain neutral studio background is accepted; a clean transparent cut-out composited onto a plain ground is accepted.

3. NO ADDED GRAPHICS OF ANY KIND on the primary image: no dealer logo, no manufacturer badge overlay, no watermark, no border or frame, no starburst, no price sticker, and NO text of any kind burned into the image.

4. DIMENSIONS: primary tile minimum 1600 x 1200 px, exact 4:3 aspect ratio, JPEG or PNG, sRGB, file under 8 MB. Images below 1600 x 1200 are rejected; the platform does NOT upscale for you.

5. The primary image is shown on the platform's own listing grid on a LIGHT GREY card (#ECECEC), so cut-out edges must read cleanly against that grey, not only against white.

COMMON REJECTION REASONS we have hit:
- "Primary image contains added text or graphics" (our old cards had the dealer roundel burned in)
- "Background not plain" (other cars visible in the lot)
- "Image below minimum dimensions"

Images AFTER the first (positions 2+) may carry our own branding and design freely.
