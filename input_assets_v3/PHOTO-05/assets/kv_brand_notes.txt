KESTREL AND VANE - HALCYON QUARTER, NOTES FOR THE DESIGNER

What we sell is the evening. Blue hour. The water should look cold and the rooms should look warm. Brass, not gold. Nothing shiny.

Quiet and expensive. Our buyers are leaving houses they have owned for thirty years, they are not impressed by a starburst or a sunset, and they will count the windows when they visit the site. If a picture shows something the building will not have, that is the one thing we cannot survive.

Phase one is four apartment types: the Loom, the Spindle, the Ropewalk and the Warehouse. That is the set. (The Reel was pulled from phase one; if it is still in the sales export, it is not for sale yet and should not get a card.)

Kestrel Home Lending's wording on the payment cards is theirs. Every line of it, including the rate and the term and the small print, is regulated. Do not edit it, shorten it, re-space it or abbreviate it, and do not let any of it get clipped. If you cannot fit a line, tell us, do not trim it.

Type: a proper text serif with real hierarchy, prices set plainly inside the copy, generous margins, one photograph per canvas. No hex from us, no measurements, that is your craft. Just: quiet and expensive.
