ARGENT & FAITH — HOW WE TALK ABOUT THE WORK (Nora)

Cold light, warm hand. The silver should sit cool and true, the way it looks under the north window, not warmed up and not blued out.

Oxidised recesses stay dark. Do not lift them. The dark in a channel is the drawing.

Nothing bright, nothing bouncing. No hot speculars blown to paper white, no glare. The metal leads and the type recedes.

The negative space in a band or a piercing is the design. A filled centre or a closed piercing is not our work.

Plenty of air. The piece sits small and centred with room around it. Quiet.

If a stone is in it, the stone should read as its own colour, not go grey.
