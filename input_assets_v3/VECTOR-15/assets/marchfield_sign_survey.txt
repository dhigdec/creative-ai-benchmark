THE MARCHFIELD EXCHANGE — SIGN PROGRAMME SITE SURVEY
Prepared by: Halloran Brass & Engraving Co. (fabricator)
Surveyed on site: front elevation and ground-floor lobby.

MACHINE / PROCESS
Rotary and V-groove engraving into solid brass, satin finish, wax-filled lines. We engrave from vector geometry, not from a picture: a filled shape with the pattern painted on it is no use on the machine.
MINIMUM ENGRAVED STROKE WIDTH WE CAN HOLD AT FINAL SIZE: 0.50 mm. Any line finer than 0.50 mm at the size it is actually engraved will not cut cleanly and will either drop out or fill. This is the hard limit for the whole programme.

1. ENTRANCE PLAQUE (replaces the existing plaque)
   Size: 450 x 300 mm, landscape.
   Substrate: 6 mm solid cast brass, satin finish, wax-filled engraving.
   Fixing: 4 x M8 countersunk brass bolts into the limestone, one near each corner. Keep all artwork at least 15 mm clear of each bolt centre.
   Crest engraved at 180 mm across at the head; THE MARCHFIELD EXCHANGE engraved beneath in capitals.

2. DIRECTORY BOARD (lobby, replaces the plastic board)
   Size: 600 x 900 mm, portrait.
   Substrate: engraved brass face on an oak backing panel, satin finish.
   Fixing: concealed cleat brackets, top edge 1600 mm above finished floor. 20 mm clear margin all round; nothing in the outer 20 mm.
   Crest engraved at 120 mm across at the head; the tenant lines beneath in suite order.

3. SUITE PLATES (one per suite)
   Size: 200 x 75 mm, landscape.
   Substrate: 3 mm solid brass, satin finish, wax-filled engraving.
   Fixing: 2 concealed studs plus adhesive, no visible fixings on the face. 12 mm clear margin all round.
   Layout: crest engraved at 32 mm across at the left; suite number and firm name engraved to the right.
   NOTE: at 32 mm the crest's finest interior lines are the tightest test of the 0.50 mm minimum on the whole job. Check them here first before anything is committed.

GENERAL
- All panels are satin brass, so proofs must be judged on brass, not on white.
- We will quote per panel from your schedule. Tell us up front any line you already know falls under 0.50 mm at its final size so we can flag it rather than cut it.
