WHAT WE KNOW, AND WHAT WE DO NOT WANT TOUCHED
Typed by Ruth (Ada's daughter). Two pages, kept since the nineties.

The photographs, oldest first:
- 1912, Brattleboro. Ada as a baby. The oldest one, a studio card, in the black album.
- 1931, Brattleboro. Ada as a young woman, in the album too.
- The wedding of Ada and Walter. The catalogue says 1938 but the back of the print is written in Ada's own hand "June 1936", and Mother was always sure of it. Use 1936.
- 1948, the porch, Ada with me as a baby.
- 1955, Putney, Ada with the three of us (me, Frank and Carol) in front of the old house. NOTE: cousin Dale sent this same photograph round again a few years ago under his own name for it and with the year as 1956. It is the SAME picture. We only need it once.
- 1967, the garden. Gone very orange.
- 1971, at the stove. This is the one with the SCAR on Nan's left hand, from the stove years before. A reprint somebody paid for in the eighties took the scar out and I have never forgiven it. The scar STAYS. Do not remove it, do not tidy it, do not smooth it.
- 1969, Nan with baby Tom.
- 1974, Nan in the garden chair, near the end.

Things that are the photograph, not damage, and must stay: the scar; the grain; the paper texture and deckled edges; the little pencil retouching the old studio did on Nan's cheek in the 1931 card; anything actually in the picture.

Quiet, please. Like a museum, not a memory book. Tell us what you corrected and what you left, and why.
