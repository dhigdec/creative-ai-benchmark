MERIDIAN SUMMIT 2026 - DELEGATE TRACKS (from the producer)
Three tracks. People want to know who they are standing next to, so carry the track on the badge as a quiet typographic mark, never a colour block.
Operators - they run the network: regional transmission and grid operations.
Capital - they fund it: infrastructure funds, investors and lenders.
Policy - they regulate it: utility policy, ratepayer advocacy and commission staff.
