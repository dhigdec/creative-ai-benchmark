How Fennhollow should feel

Sun lit. Warm. Natural. A little restrained. Made by real people, in the valley.

Not a wellness tonic and not a supermarket soda — somewhere honest in between.

Our green is the label green. The true chip lives on the printed swatch card in the studio (the Pantone chip taped inside the folder) — use THAT, not a guess off a screen.

Type should be warm and human and easy to read over a photograph. Never shout.

Everything should look like one summer afternoon.
