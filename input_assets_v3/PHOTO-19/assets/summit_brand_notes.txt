CASCADIA FOUNDERS SUMMIT — BRAND & PORTRAIT NOTES

House ground: a quiet neutral ground behind every sitter. Use our Fog Grey (#D9DDDE) to Warm Paper (#F2EEE7) range for the portrait ground, soft and even, never a coloured or busy background. Everyone should sit on the SAME house ground so the programme grid reads as one event, not eighteen rooms.

Tone: understated, credible, quiet, professional. Serious room, worth the price of admission. Natural skin, no glamour retouching, no visible retouching signature, and nobody made to look like a different person. Not a consumer conference (no glossy warm filter), not a free meetup (no flat hard direct flash).

One scale of head in frame across all eighteen so the grid is even.

The three shapes we actually use:
 1. Portrait shape for the printed programme.
 2. Tight square for badges and avatars.
 3. Wide 16:9 for the lower third of the stage screens (and we need these WITHOUT any background at all so AV can drop them over the session video).

Standing rule: speaker role lines print EXACTLY as the speaker wrote them and as their contract says they print. Not shortened, not abbreviated, not re-spaced. If a role line will not fit, raise it with us before the file goes to the printer.

Palette: Cascadia Slate #23303A, Evergreen #2C4A3B, Fog Grey #D9DDDE, Warm Paper #F2EEE7, Signal Copper #B06B3E, Near Black #1A1F24.
