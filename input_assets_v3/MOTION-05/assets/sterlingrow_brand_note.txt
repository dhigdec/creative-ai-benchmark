STERLING ROW - notes for whoever produces the channel package

WHO WE ARE
Nine full-floor residences in a converted warehouse block. They start just over four million dollars. We sell them one at a time, to buyers who are not in a hurry and who have seen everything. The channel is where most of them meet me first, so every video has to feel made by one hand.

THE RESTRAINT (this is the whole proposition)
Quiet, near-neutral, almost no text on screen. The grade sits near neutral and slightly cool: clean whites, no crushed blacks, no warm saturated push. Motion is slow and largely static. On-screen text is minimal and small. The wordmark reproduces modestly. Anything that looks like a promotion - fast cuts, a saturated warm grade, large benefit captions, a price burned across the frame - reads at this tier as a building that is struggling to sell, and that ends a four-million-dollar conversation. Do not go the other way into pure atmosphere either: a qualified buyer still needs the floor, the square footage and the exposure before she asks to view.

APPROVED LANGUAGE
Tagline: "Nine residences. No hurry."
Voice, spare and composed: "Nine residences. Full floor. Sold one at a time, to buyers in no hurry."
Say: full-floor residence, converted warehouse, exposure, ceiling height, outdoor space.
Never say: luxury lifestyle, act now, limited time, dream home, or anything that sounds like it is selling.

TYPE
One reassuring, near-neutral pairing. No high-contrast display serif (reads as a perfume advert at this tier). No heavy grotesque (reads as a development marketing brochure). Text sits small and calm. Name the family, foundry and the actual weight you use, per role, in the handover.

THE CHANNEL COVER
Every cover still is cut to 2560 x 1440 px. It cannot be a person standing next to a window with a graphic pointing at it. It has to earn a click on composition alone, and it must not embarrass a buyer who forwards it to her advisor.

THE WORDMARK
The only surviving version is the handheld photograph of the cast-brass plaque in the lobby. There is no vector file and no clean scan. Whatever you deliver has to still read as that wordmark, recovered from the plaque rather than redrawn into a font, and it has to read as the words at the small size it actually sits at on the end card - not just at canvas size.

THE TWO DISCLOSURE LINES (contractual - reproduce both, verbatim, on every printed card)
DISCLOSURE 1: "Sterling Row is a converted warehouse development. All square footages are approximate, measured to the exterior face of the building, and must be independently verified prior to contract."
DISCLOSURE 2: "This is not an offer to sell where prior registration or qualification is required. Prices and availability are subject to change without notice. Equal Housing Opportunity."

UNDER CONTRACT
Two residences are already under contract (Residence Two and Residence Six) and their cards must say so.
