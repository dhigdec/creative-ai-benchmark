IRONWAY RUN CLUB — heat transfer shop notes (Providence Print & Press)

Hi folks, here is what we need on our end to cut and press your numbers on the new kit. One colour only, same as always. We cut it out of a single sheet of transfer film, so there are no shades and no outlines, just the one flat shape. The colour you picked off our rack is our stock Marshal Orange film; it is the only orange we keep, so the artwork has to work in that one flat colour sitting on your navy singlet.

BACK PANEL PRINT AREA (usable area, per singlet size):
- S: 9 in wide x 11 in tall
- M: 10 in wide x 12 in tall
- L: 11 in wide x 13 in tall
- XL: 12 in wide x 14 in tall
Keep everything inside that box. We cannot press right up to a seam.

NUMBER HEIGHT: you have always run an 8 inch tall number and it reads well from the far side of the road, so keep it at 8 inches unless you tell us otherwise. Any club letters that sit on the number line should be capped to that same 8 inch height.

MINIMUM STROKE: our cutter cannot hold a line thinner than 3 mm (about 1/8 inch) at final pressed size. Anything thinner than that lifts at the edge in the first wash and peels by June. If any part of the lettering comes in under 3 mm at 8 inch height, put it in your note and we will thicken it, or you will lose it on the press.

We press at 305 F for 15 seconds, so the film has to be one clean single shape with no tiny islands or closed-up holes. Send us one flat cut colour and we are good to go.

— Providence Print & Press
