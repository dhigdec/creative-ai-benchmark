Kilnmore - what we keep losing when someone else designs for us

Clean lines, balanced colour, plenty of negative space, nothing fussy. Calm, full frame, one piece given room. Space is the argument.

The glaze is the point. Oatmeal has to stay oatmeal, ash has to stay ash, cobalt slip has to stay cobalt, bare clay has to stay warm. That is what a buyer chooses and what a stockist orders against, and it is what gets returned when it is wrong. Do not neutralise the four glazes into each other and do not push any of them toward a colour it is not.

One warm, legible type system. A quiet humanist serif for the mark, headings and range names; a clean sans for the specification tables so a stockist reads them without effort. Warmth without decoration. Nothing script, nothing ornamented.

We are considered studio ceramics. Not impulse marketplace: no tiled grid of nine pieces, no price flashes, no countdown, no exclamation colours. Not gallery austerity either: the pieces are made to be used, and a stockist has to be able to read the dimensions to order from them.

Plain and warm, the studio talking about its own work. Made to be used, made in small batches. No sale language, no urgency, no exclamation points.

Tell us if anything in our data looks wrong before it prints.
