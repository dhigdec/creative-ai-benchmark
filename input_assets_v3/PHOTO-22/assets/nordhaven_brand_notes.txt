NORDHAVEN — brand & delivery note (client-supplied)

GROUND
Booking page and lookbook cover both sit on our near-black ground: #14161A. Cut-outs are judged on THIS colour, not on white.

PALETTE
Near-black ink #14161A / Limewash plaster #E7E2D8 / Harbour slate #4A535C / Oak #6B5945 / Brass #A8823E.

THE ONE EXCEPTION
The main room photograph on the booking page stays TRUE COLOUR. A guest paying $600 a night wants to see what the bed and the linen actually look like. Everything else carries the tone. Do not tint the room hero.

PLACEMENTS & DELIVERED SIZES
- Booking page: 3 amenity cut-outs (carafe, robe, brass key fob) composited on #14161A, plus the true-colour room hero.
- Lookbook: a spread (2:1 landscape), delivered as one editable canvas.
- Social: a 4:5 canvas.
- Story: a 9:16 canvas.
(The lookbook spread, the 4:5 social and the 9:16 story all sit in ONE editable file.)

BAND & BOOKER
$600 a night, direct only, design-literate travellers 30-45 who book on aesthetic. Restraint is the product. Rates sit small and quietly. Nothing cheerful or saturated.

SHOOT
Two visits, autumn and winter. They do not match. The winter frames were shot after the blinds were replaced. Bring them onto one base before committing any tone.

ROOM LIST
The room list changes each season, which is why we need the collateral back editable and the cards as a live merge. Three rooms were renamed this season.
