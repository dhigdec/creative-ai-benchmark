SEASON STATEMENT
Ten pieces, cut and sewn in Burlington, meant to be worn hard. We make a small capsule each fall and this is the whole of it. The cloth is chosen to age, the seams are built to last, and the hardware this season we cast ourselves in pewter and silver. One piece carries the crest of a lacrosse club a few streets from the bench, lent to us, not licensed.

Tagline: Cut and sewn to outlast the season.

THE HARDWARE
Cast this season in pewter and silver on the bench: two button styles, a keyring and a collar pin. True metal tone, no cast. It ships on the pieces and, the keyring and pin, on their own.

THE COLLABORATION
The Kestrel Lane crest is the club's, stitched into an old wool pennant on their wall, the only place it still exists. We photographed it with their blessing to put on one jersey, a poster and a tag. It stays their crest, knife-and-all as they stitched it; we do not redraw it into a font.
