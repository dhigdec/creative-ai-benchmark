QUAYSIDE HAND PRESS — notes for the Wharfside twentieth-year run (from Del & Marina, two streets up)

How we print. Everything is pulled by hand on the flatbed. We run FOUR inks plus the colour of the paper. The paper is one of your colours, so leave it working, do not flood it. On the shirt we can only give you TWO inks, no paper showing through as a colour because the cloth is the ground.

Stock.
- Poster: 300gsm uncoated kraft-toned board, natural oatmeal (about #E4D6BC). This is the big one for the shed and shop windows. True printed size 500 x 700 mm, portrait.
- Handbill: same natural oatmeal stock at 170gsm, trimmed to A5, 148 x 210 mm portrait. Same design as the poster, not a new one.
- Shirt: natural undyed cotton, print area about 300 mm wide across the chest.

Inks we are mixing for you: harbour blue, rust red, hill ochre, deep ink. If you need a fifth you have to drop one, we cannot run five plus the paper.

Registration. Hand pulled, so give us corner crop marks on all four corners AND one centre cross target, common to every plate so we can line them up. Expect 1 to 1.5 mm of slop between plates, so trap anywhere two solids butt: overlap the lighter ink under the darker by about 1 pt. Tell us on the spec which plate traps under which.

Line and tone. Minimum positive line 1.5 pt (about 0.5 mm). Minimum reversed (paper knocked out of ink) 2 pt or it fills in. We can only hold a COARSE dot by eye, roughly 20 to 25 lines to the inch, no fine screens and no process rosettes. Pick the coarseness against the size the thing actually prints at, and tell us the number. A screen that looks right on the poster will plug on the handbill if you do not think about it.

Act cards. Same A6-ish card as last year, one act per card, run on the small press. Fourteen this year.

Deadline. Films / positives in our hands by 15 August. Anything after that and the twentieth year prints late.
