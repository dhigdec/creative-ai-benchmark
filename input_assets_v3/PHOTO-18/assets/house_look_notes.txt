FERNWELL & ROWE — HOUSE LOOK (from Rowe)

What our finish is, in plain words:
- Warm, natural, true skin. It should look like the actual day, not a magazine shoot and not a phone filter.
- Never orange, never crunchy, never that filtered look. If skin has gone orange or the blacks have gone crushed, it is wrong.
- Hold the black point open. We do not crush shadows.
- Keep colour honest. Greens of the garden stay green, whites stay white, skin stays warm but real.
- One look that holds across the whole day: the window-lit ceremony, the last hour of sun in the walled garden, and the reception under the venue's own lights should all read as one gallery, not three different edits.

Standing rule: if a frame will not take the house pass, pull it and tell us. We would rather you pull a frame and tell us than force it. Do not ship a forced frame at eighty percent.

For this trial: the Hartley set is sixteen frames the couple shortlisted. The couple have starred six for full-resolution prints, and one of those six is the album cover and the thank-you-card hero. We will confirm the six with you; do not guess.
