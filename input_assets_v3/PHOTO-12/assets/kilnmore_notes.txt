KILNMORE — HOUSE NOTES (for the freelancer)

OUR GROUND
Our store pages sit on our warm grey. We call it House Grey. Hex #CFC7BB. Every store-page tile sits on this, with our mark in the corner. The store page and the marketplace are opposites and both have to exist off the same photograph.

MARKETPLACE INTAKE RULES (exactly as they sent them to us)
- Primary image must show the product only. Nothing else in the frame: no prop, no logo or watermark, no words, no scene, no shadow invented under the piece.
- Plain ground. Near-white or plain neutral.
- Square. 1:1 aspect ratio.
- Minimum 2000 x 2000 px. Maximum 5000 x 5000 px.
- No interpolated / upscaled pixels. Native camera resolution only.

THE THREE TIMES THEY BOUNCED US
1. "Primary image contains additional elements (logo / wrapping tissue visible). Primary must show product only."
2. "Image below minimum dimensions. Minimum is 2000 x 2000 px."
3. "Non-square aspect ratio. Primary must be 1:1."

The glaze is the product. Please keep every glaze true to the piece in the hand. Do not push the celadon greener to make it prettier — a celadon that reads greener than the piece is a return waiting to happen.
