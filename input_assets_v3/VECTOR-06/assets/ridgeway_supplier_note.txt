RIDGEWAY ROVERS FC — KIT SUPPLIER NOTE
(what the supplier told us they can actually do)

WHAT WE CAN RUN
  Heat-press vinyl and flock. ONE spot colour per element on numbering and lettering: no gradients, no inline, no keyline, no second colour on numbers.
  Crest: up to 3 spot colours for the breast (claret / navy / old gold). A single-ink crest is required for the training tops, and a version that holds on the navy away shirt.

CUT AND HEAT-PRESS CONSTRAINTS
  Every glyph must be a SINGLE closed solid shape, generously weighted. Minimum limb/stroke width 4 mm at back-of-shirt size. No thin joins. No open counters (an enclosed counter is cut as an island only if it is 6 mm or wider).
  Back-of-shirt NUMBER height 250 mm; NAME arched above, cap height 65 mm.
  Breast crest height 80 mm; sleeve crest 55 mm.

PLACEMENT HEIGHTS
  Back number centred, top of digits 180 mm below the collar seam.
  Name arched above the number, baseline 30 mm above the digits.
  Breast crest on the left breast, 150 mm below the shoulder seam.

COLOURS (confirm against artwork)
  Claret 6B1F2E | Navy 1E2A44 | Old Gold C09A3E | Shirt White F4F2ED.

BACK-OF-SHIRT SHEET
  One player per page in OUR format (see last year's PDF): NAME, NUMBER, INITIAL, SIZE, small crest.
  Repeated surnames carry a forename INITIAL in front (our rule).
  No bleed on our sheet.

DEADLINE
  Artwork required by the end of the month for the coming season.
