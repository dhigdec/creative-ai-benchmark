NORTHGROVE — WHERE THE PICTURES GO (plain language, from the studio)

OUR PRODUCT PAGES. Ground is our OFF WHITE, #F5F2EC. Product page hero: 1600 x 2000 px, portrait, JPEG, sRGB. Grid thumbnail: 800 x 800 px square. A style can be delivered as a plate ON the off white for the product page.

MARKETPLACE FEED. The marketplace wants every image with NOTHING behind it — a genuine TRANSPARENT PNG, the garment on transparency. Feed file: 2000 x 2000 px, PNG-24 with real alpha, garment centered with even margin, sRGB. They composite it onto their own pages, so the edge has to hold on a coloured ground, not only on white.

LOOKBOOK HEADER. Sits on our CLUB NAVY, #1B2A44, because the supporters' crew is made with our local football club and navy is their colour. Anything reversed on the navy — the mark, a cut-out garment — has to hold its edge and stay legible on that dark ground.
