HARBORLINE REALTY -- a note from Dana on how we want to look (for the designer)

WHO WE ARE
Eight of us, one office on the harbour, since 2009. I run it. We list the whole mid coast book -- a three hundred and twenty thousand dollar corner-lot starter one week and a four and a half million place out on the point the next -- and both matter to us the same. We are grounded, coastal, understated. We describe the house, not ourselves, and we never oversell.

THE TWO OTHER SIGNS IN TOWN
One is a national franchise storefront: loud, red, everything shouting, the same template whether it is a cottage or a compound. The other is a discount flat-fee outfit that makes every listing look like a classified ad. We do not want to be either. When I say a sheet looks cheap I mean this: too much on the page, the price screaming, a photo that fights the type, blacks gone grey, a logo that is nearly ours but not.

THE TWO REGISTERS (this is the whole job)
The point estate at four and a half million sells to people over fifty with cash who read restraint as proof we can be trusted with the sale: one photograph, deep margins, quiet type, the price set small and sure. The corner-lot starter at three-twenty sells to first-timers under thirty-five who need the price, the bed count and the open-house time to reach them from across a room: brighter, denser, price forward. Style the whole book like the estate and the starters look empty and evasive; style it like the starter and the estate looks like volume work in front of a vendor paying a six-figure commission. Make the difference read -- but both still have to be obviously us.

THE MARK
The rope ring around a lighthouse with the H and the R is our mark. It is painted on the office window and that glass is the only place it still exists; the artwork was lost years ago. Whatever goes on the posts has to be that mark, recovered, knuckle for knuckle. Not something close to it, not redrawn into a font.

THE OPEN HOUSE POSTS
Prices move on Thursdays. Whatever you hand us for the open houses I have to be able to open and change a price in myself, in a minute, without paying anyone twice.

THE ROSTER
We should be carrying eleven active this week. The Sea Meadow cottage on Beauchamp went under agreement Thursday -- it should be off the run, but Monday's export may still have it in. If anything else looks off, ask me: two sheets went out last month with January's prices and I am not doing that again.

-- Dana Marlow
