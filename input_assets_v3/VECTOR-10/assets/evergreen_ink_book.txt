INK BOOK — what the decorator keeps on the shelf (anything off this list is a mixing charge)

Standard plastisols we always have:
- Bench Black (dense black)
- Bone Primer (natural off-white, good underbase on darks)
- Cedar Bark (warm mid-brown)
- Sage Field (muted grey-green)
- Aubergine Deep (dark purple-brown) — low stock, tell us early
- Harvest Gold (warm ochre)

Shop notes:
- On black and the black hoodie we usually lay a Bone Primer underbase or nothing prints bright. Underbase counts as a screen and a pass.
- Sage over Cedar Bark is our favourite two-colour combo, it reads hand-painted.
- Aubergine is a pain, it is thin and it sits close to a stained-wood look, do not put it on the olive.
- Halftones: we can hold a coarse dot for a paint-field look. Keep the smallest dot open, fine dots plug on the fleece.
- Every extra screen is real money on a short run. If a design is worth two inks not three, say so.

We like: earthy, few colours, the brush showing. We do not want: neon, foil, or anything that looks like gas-station merch.
