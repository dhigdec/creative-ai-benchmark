ANVIL AND OAK - how the programming works and what the levels mean (from Mara and Deacon)

WHO RUNS IT
Two of us. Mara Voss and Deacon Hale. We ran a barbell club out of a shared space for six years before this and about half our people are coming with us. We coach, we don't sell memberships you don't use.

HOW THE PROGRAMMING WORKS
Everybody is on one system. You warm up the same movements every week - squat, press, pull, carry - and the load moves in a planned way, not by feel. New people run a simple linear progression until the easy jumps stop, then we move them onto four-week blocks that alternate volume and intensity. We write the program; you show up and do the work and add weight. That is the whole pitch. Show up, do the work, add weight. We handle the programming so you can focus on the bar.

WHAT THE LEVELS MEAN (this is the Level column on the roster)
- FOUNDATIONS: learning the lifts, empty bar to first real loads, coached closely. No meet pressure.
- STRENGTH: the main room. Running blocks, chasing lifetime numbers, training three to four days.
- COMPETITION: peaking for a platform, training around meets, attempts and openers planned with a coach.
