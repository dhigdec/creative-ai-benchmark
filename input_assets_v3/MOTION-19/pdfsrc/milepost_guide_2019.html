Left turns across traffic

1. Begin slowing well before the junction. 2. Move to the correct position near the centre line. 3. Signal left across the oncoming lane in good time. 4. Stop at the give-way line if you cannot see a clear gap. 5. Watch the oncoming traffic and judge the gaps, not the cars. 6. Look through the turn to where you want to end up. 7. Check for pedestrians crossing the road you are entering. 8. Wait for a gap you are certain of, not one you are hoping for. 9. When the gap is clear, turn smoothly across the lane. 10. Keep your wheels straight while you wait so a knock will not push you forward. 11. Complete the turn into your own lane and cancel the signal.

When in doubt, wait. A missed gap costs seconds; a wrong one costs more.
