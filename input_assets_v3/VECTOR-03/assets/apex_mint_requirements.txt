APEX RALLY CLUB — MINT REQUIREMENTS
(received from the mint; artwork must meet these before a die is cut)

PRODUCTS
  Hard enamel lapel pins and struck challenge coins. Six designs, two products each.

DIAMETERS
  Pins: 30–32 mm (crest 32 mm).
  Coins: crest / flagship 45 mm; event coins 40 mm.
  Coin thickness: 3.0 mm struck.

HARD ENAMEL (the pin version)
  COLOUR CEILING: maximum 5 enamel colours per design, plus the metal line. Do not exceed.
  Minimum enamel WELL width 0.30 mm; minimum metal LINE 0.15 mm. Anything finer bleeds in the well and is rejected.
  Simplify detail out rather than leaving it and hoping — a pin is not a coin.
  Pin plating: polished gold or black nickel.

STRUCK COIN (the coin version)
  The raised-metal face must be LINE ONLY: closed line work in raised metal against a recessed field. NO filled interiors where struck metal should show through.
  Minimum raised line 0.20 mm at struck diameter.
  Coin plating options: struck gold, antique silver, antique bronze.

CREST COLOURWAYS
  Supply 2–3 crest colourways shown ON THE STRUCK METAL (not swatches on white) for the committee to choose the flagship.

HANDOFF
  One vector file per design per product, closed paths, colours within the ceiling, diameter stated in the file. Put edition size on the capsule insert, never on the die.
