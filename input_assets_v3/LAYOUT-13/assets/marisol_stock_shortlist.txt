MARISOL COVE - coastal stock shortlist (from the library)
From the office. These are the ones I liked in the previews for the autumn/winter campaign. The number is the library's; my note is just what I saw in the little thumbnail.

SL-4471  cove at dusk  -  MY FAVOURITE. Warm gold light on the water, looks exactly like our hour before sunset. This should be the big hero on the page.
SL-2210  cliff path down to a beach  -  could be our own path, soft morning light.
SL-3980  tide pools on wet rock, close up  -  nice texture for a smaller picture.
SL-1755  cypress trees on a headland in fog  -  very quiet, very us.
SL-6302  empty beach with a piece of driftwood  -  good wide calm shot.
SL-5518  waves breaking on dark rock, long exposure  -  soft and slow, pretty.
SL-2984  aerial looking down on a cove and headland  -  gorgeous blues. Looked almost too perfect, nearly painted, but I loved it.
SL-7126  a single gull on a mooring piling  -  sweet, maybe too cute.
SL-0899  sea grass on the bluff at golden hour  -  warm, goes with the dusk one.

One thing: these are only the little previews. I couldn't really judge the true colour from them, so pull the full-size ones before we commit. - office
