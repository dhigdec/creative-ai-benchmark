MARISOL COVE - copy for the page and the brochure (approved, use as written)

Tagline: The quiet side of the coast.

WHAT IS HERE
- Fourteen rooms on the bluff, most looking out over the cove.
- A path from the terrace down to a small private beach.
- The dining room, for breakfast and for dinner.
- Wood stoves in the suites, and firewood laid on the cold evenings.
- No pool and no spa. The point of the place is the water, the light and being left alone.
- Parking, slow mornings, and two rooms that take dogs by arrangement.

THE DINING ROOM
Breakfast is for guests. Dinner is open to anyone by reservation, a short menu that moves with what the boats bring in. Book the dining room when you book the room; the tables go first on the weekends.

CANCELLATION
Cancel seven days ahead and we refund in full. Inside seven days we hold one night. We are a small house, and a late cancellation is a room we cannot fill.

THE SEASON
Autumn and winter are our quiet months and they are the best ones. The crowds are gone, the dining room is easy to get into, and an hour before sunset the light comes off the water and the whole cove goes gold. That is when to walk down. Bring a coat for after dark.
