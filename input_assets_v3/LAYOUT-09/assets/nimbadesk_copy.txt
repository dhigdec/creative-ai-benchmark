TAGLINE

VOICE
Direct, specific, understated. Numbers carry the argument and every claim states its period. We sell to the person who defends the line item to finance, so everything we show is real.

=== SELL SHEET 1 - UNIFIED QUEUE ===
Header: One queue. Clear ownership.
Advantage: Shared inboxes hide who owns what. Nimbadesk puts every request in one queue with a single owner, a status and an assignee, so nothing sits unclaimed.
Core bullets:
- One queue for email, chat and web requests
- Explicit owner and status on every ticket
- Saved views and search across the whole history
- Handoffs that keep the thread intact
CTA: See your queue in a 20-minute walkthrough.

=== SELL SHEET 2 - SLA & ROUTING ===
Header: Rules that route, targets that hold.
Advantage: Priorities and escalations run on rules you set, not on whoever happens to be watching. First-response and resolution targets are measured, not hoped for.
Core bullets:
- IF/THEN routing by priority, tag and queue
- First-response and resolution SLA targets with business hours
- Automatic escalation before a target is breached
- An escalations board that shows what is at risk
CTA: Set your first SLA in an afternoon.

=== SELL SHEET 3 - REPORTING FOR DIRECTORS ===
Header: Reporting a director can actually read.
Advantage: The numbers your finance director asks for, on one page, with the period stated. No dashboards nobody opens.
Core bullets:
- Open, resolved and median-response KPIs at a glance
- Volume and load by team and by agent
- CSAT trend with the comments behind it
- Every figure labelled with the window it covers
CTA: Get the launch reporting pack.
