Conventional Deadlift
Posterior chain - hamstrings, glutes, spinal erectors, lats, grip

Set the bar over mid-foot

Grip just outside the knees

Drop the hips, chest up, flatten the back

Take the slack out of the bar

Brace hard against the belt

Push the floor away, bar close to the shins

Stand tall, lock the hips, do not lean back

Return it the way it came up, hinge first
