GROUND
The brand story page and all stand panels sit on our dark ground: iron black #1A1A1C. The maker's mark reverses out of it. Cut-outs and the mark are judged on THIS colour, not on white.

PALETTE
Iron black #1A1A1C / Furnace orange #C6591F / Cast bronze #7A5A2E / Ash grey #6E6E6B / Foundry sand #C9BCA3.

BRAND STORY (body text)
We have cast iron and bronze in this building since 1911. Every piece is poured, cooled, finished and regulated by hand, and struck with our maker's mark before it cools. We sell permanence: the heat, the hands, and the fact that the piece will still be here in eighty years.
