# Palewell Health -- internal brand notes

There is no brand book. This is it.

## Colour, in words
- A DEEP SLATE, close to a blue-grey charcoal. This is our ground; the header sits on it.
- A WARM CHALK, an off-white with a little warmth in it, for pages and space.
- ONE muted SIGNAL colour, a soft sage green, used sparingly for a link or a small accent. Never more than one accent.
- A soft ink for body text, and a near-white paper white.

## Tone
Plain, warm, unhurried. We speak to a person, not a patient. No hype, no superlatives, no clinical jargon. Example line we would write: "Manage the everyday stuff with someone in your corner, from home."

## What we mean by calm
Real rooms, ordinary daylight, generous air, restrained type. Faces that are present, not beaming. Not a hospital (never cold, sterile, blue). Not a wellness brand (never glossy or cosmetic). Not concierge (never near-black and sparse). Calm, credible, human.
