Rivermeadow Trust
Dear Mr. Quiller,

I wanted to write to you myself. The work we were able to do this year — forty more acres held under easement, the Fenn Hollow trail reopened, the river bend at Weir Brook kept open to the public — rested on the households who carry this trust. Yours is one of them.

Your support this year, $2,500.00, places you in our Easement Circle. I do not take it for granted, and neither does the board.

The land will outlast all of us. That is the point of it, and it is why your generosity matters the way it does.

Yours,
Claire Ashford
Executive Director, Rivermeadow Trust
