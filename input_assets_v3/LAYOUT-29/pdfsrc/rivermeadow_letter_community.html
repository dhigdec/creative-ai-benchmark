Rivermeadow Trust
Land held in common, kept for good

Dear Owen,

The hay came in twice off the lower fields this year, and the herons were back on the river by April. None of that happens on its own. It happens because neighbours like you decided the valley was worth keeping.

Whatever you gave went straight into the land — into the meadow you walk, into the easements that keep the fields as fields. Your giving this year came to $85.00, which places you among our Friends of the Valley.

Thank you for keeping the valley in common hands. We will see you on the trails.

With gratitude,
Claire Ashford
Executive Director, Rivermeadow Trust
