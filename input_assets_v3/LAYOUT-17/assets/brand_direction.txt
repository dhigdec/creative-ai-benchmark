Apexguard Surface Systems - direction for the rollout

Premium, modern, performance automotive. Minimalist and confident. Not flashy, not factory catalog, not tuner.

No mascots. No vintage badges. No heavy gradients. No starbursts, no percentage flashes, no chrome bevels, no exclamation points. We state numbers and let them carry.

A full install runs eighteen hundred to six thousand dollars on a car worth sixty thousand, so it has to look like that. A dealer principal has to believe the ten year warranty printed beside the film, and a two hundred dollar tint-shop flyer kills that. Equally, do not push it into moody enthusiast car-culture; we are a supplier a dealer group puts on its reconditioning line, not an Instagram page.

The master mark and the three tier marks (Guard, Guard Pro, Guard Elite) have to read as one family. A dealer looks at all four and sees one supplier, not three products.

One restrained accent against a deep neutral. Confident spacing. Performance stated as a plain table, not a burst. Photography clean and even, not dramatic.

If anything in our data looks wrong to you, tell us before it prints.
