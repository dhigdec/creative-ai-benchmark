CELLAR & CRU - autumn selection, how I'd talk you through it (Rob)

THE SHAPE OF THE BAY
Fourteen wines. Two of them anchor the bay and everything else sits around them: the Cote Brune Syrah from Domaine des Escaliers, 2019, and the Cascina Voltone Nebbiolo, 2016. Those are the two people photograph the card of and come back for at Christmas. They earn the top of the bay.

THE TWO I WANT PEOPLE TO TRY
The two I will put a card in someone's hand for are the Cave de Trevigne co-op red at fourteen pounds - it drinks miles above its price if you open it an hour before - and the Domaine Ravel Morgon, the one that changes how people think about Beaujolais. If a card sells one bottle of each this month I'm happy.

THE WHITES AND THE ROSE
The Muscadet is the everyday white, sur lie, saline, fourteen pounds, buy it by the case. Alsace Riesling, Chablis, the Mosel Kabinett and the Gruner are the four that do the work on the white shelf. The Sancerre is on the list but I have not got a picture of it - we ran out of evening, so flag it. The Provence rose and the Vermentino round it off.

ON THE SCORES
We print the score where there is one and we leave the space empty where there is not. I do not chase them and I will not inflate them. A score is one reader's note, printed small, under the wine, never over it. The co-op red has no score and I do not want one invented for it.

ON THE NOTES
The long notes on the two anchors and the two I'm pushing are mine, written the way I would say them at the counter. The shorter ones came off the buyer's card. Do not shorten mine to make them fit the card - if they will not fit, tell me and I will cut them myself before it prints.
