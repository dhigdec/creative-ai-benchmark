UMBRA LOFT — ROOM SPECIFICATION (what promoters ask before they will talk)

CAPACITY: 250 standing / 120 seated cabaret.
ADDRESS: 14 Foundry Court (basement), Providence, Rhode Island, under the former Foundry textile mill.
STAGE: 5.2 m wide x 3.0 m deep x 0.4 m high. Low ceiling, 2.4 m clearance over the stage.
LOAD-IN: street-level loading door on Foundry Court to a goods lift, then one flight down. No step-free path to the stage itself.
POWER: 2 x 32A single phase, 6 x 13A rings on stage.
PA: d&b point-source mains, 2 x subs, 4 x wedges + 1 drum fill. FOH 24-channel digital desk.
BACKLINE (house): upright piano (tuned to A440), 4-piece drum kit, bass and 2 guitar amps, 5 x boom stands.
MONITORS: 4 mixes on wedges.
LIGHTING: 12-way rig, warm tungsten wash, 2 x follow spots, coloured gel bar (magenta/amber/cold-blue). No moving heads.
CURFEW: music off by 12:30 AM Fri/Sat, 11:30 PM Sun-Thu. Hard curfew, residential above.
SPLIT TERMS: 70/30 door split to the act above a 250-dollar room guarantee, or a straight guarantee by negotiation. Bar retained by venue.
MERCH: 15 percent commission, act sells.
