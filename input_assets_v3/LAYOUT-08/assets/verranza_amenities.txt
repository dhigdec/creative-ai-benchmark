VERRANZA COASTAL RETREATS - amenities, capacities, included services and the season calendar (for the villa pages and the rate ladder)

INCLUDED WITH EVERY WHOLE-HOUSE STAY (contractual - do not reword):
- Private welcome and check-in, and a concierge on call through the stay
- A mid-stay clean (stays of six nights or more) and a full changeover clean
- Fresh linens, bath and pool towels, and starter toiletries
- A welcome provision of local bread, oil, fruit and a bottle of regional wine
- Pool and garden maintenance throughout the stay
- High-speed wifi across the house and grounds
- Private parking on site
Not included: catering, private chef, transfers and boat hire (all arranged on request at cost).

VILLA SOLANE
Warm-stone villa above the cove, four bedrooms, four bathrooms, sleeps eight. Private pool and a long pergola-shaded dining terrace, an olive garden, and a sea glimpse from the upper rooms. Air conditioning throughout, a full kitchen, an outdoor kitchen and wood-fired oven, and a covered lounge. Step-free ground floor.

VILLA CASCINA
Rustic stone farmhouse on the vineyard hillside, three bedrooms, three bathrooms, sleeps six. Private pool and a walled courtyard terrace under old olive trees, a farmhouse kitchen, a library snug with a wood stove, and vineyard views. Air conditioning in the bedrooms. A quieter, inland-facing house.

MAISON AUBINE
Modern house set into the rock above the cove, five bedrooms, five and a half bathrooms, sleeps ten. Infinity pool and a lower plunge pool, a roof terrace with a fire bowl, floor-to-ceiling sea glass, a chef's kitchen, a gym and a direct path down to the cove. Air conditioning and underfloor heating throughout. The largest and most contemporary of the three.

THE SEASON CALENDAR (the four seasons the rate ladder and the cards use):
- LOW SEASON: 5 November - 31 March
- SHOULDER SEASON: 1 April - 31 May, and 1 October - 4 November
- HIGH SEASON: 1 June - 30 June, and 1 September - 30 September
- PEAK SEASON: 1 July - 31 August
Rates change twice a year (a winter revision effective 5 November and a summer revision effective 1 June). Whole-house only; no per-room bookings.
