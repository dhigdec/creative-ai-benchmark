HOW WE PRESENT — Cedarline and Vale
From the principal.

Cool, restrained, true. We sell architect-designed homes to buyers who read light as build quality and who are looking at three other agents' listings on the same afternoon. Our credibility is that the pictures are the house.

What I mean by cooked: any picture that has been pushed until it stops looking like the room. Skies dropped in, grass turned fluorescent, whites gone blue, blacks crushed to nothing, a window "view" that is obviously not the view. If a buyer can tell it was retouched, we have lost them. I would rather a window be named as not deliverable than faked.

Type and layout: quiet. Generous margins, one photograph doing the work, nothing that announces itself. Not warm and cosy, not developer gold-and-black.

One housekeeping thing for the window run: we went under contract on 51 Alder Court last week. The office manager has not pulled it off the active export yet, so it will still be in whatever you were sent. Leave it out of the window cards, please, it should not be advertised. That leaves the six existing plus Ridgeway.
