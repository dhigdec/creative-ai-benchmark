APEXGUARD — brand notes (client-supplied)

NAMES
Master brand: Apexguard. Sub-brands: Vantage (paint protection film), Umbra (window tint), Forge (installation hardware). Forge is new this season and has no prior label artwork.

HOUSE GROUND
Boxes, labels and stickers print on our house dark ground: #101114. One signal accent only: #E4552B. The recovered mark sits mostly on the dark ground and sometimes very small (thumbnail sticker). Judge it on the dark ground, not on white.

PALETTE
House black #101114 / Gunmetal #3A3D42 / Cool steel #B7BCC2 / Signal accent #E4552B / Off white #F2F1ED.

EXCLUSIONS (hard)
No mascots. No vintage badges. No heavy gradients. Nothing that reads as tuner. Premium, modern, performance-automotive, minimalist and confident. Not flashy, not factory-catalog.

WARRANTY WORDING (legal-approved, do not change)
'Apexguard products are warranted against defects for the term shown, to the original installer, when applied per the published procedure.'

PRODUCT PAGE RULE
Each part on plain white, square (2000 x 2000 px), the part optically centred with clear air at every edge, nothing touching the edge. Wholesale/dealer usage may need transparent variants.

TRADE SHOW STAND
Installer trade-show piece: 2000 mm wide x 1000 mm tall banner (landscape), full bleed. Plus a set of square 1080 x 1080 px posts. Both in ONE editable file we can change between shows.

BAND
Line items $250-$1,200. Independent installers 25-45. The packaging has to justify the price at the counter.
