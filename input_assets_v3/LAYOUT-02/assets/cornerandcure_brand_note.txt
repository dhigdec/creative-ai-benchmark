CORNER & CURE — how we run the front of house (for whoever we hire next)

WHO WE ARE
A corner deli in Brooklyn. My father opened it, I run it now. Sandwiches, bowls, a cold case, a coffee machine. Twelve to sixteen dollars. People who work nearby and come in three times a week. We are warm, loud and unfussy. We are not fancy and we do not want to look it, but we are not cheap either and cheap is what this stuff usually looks like.

HOW WE TALK
Plainspoken, neighbourhood, a little brash, generous. The food does the talking. A line we would write: "Pulled together, piled high, see you at the counter." A line we would never write: anything that sounds like a chain, anything curated or elevated or artisanal.

HOURS
Mon-Fri 7am - 7pm. Sat 8am - 6pm. Sun 8am - 3pm. Coffee from open. Hot sandwiches all day. Cold case all day.

THE LOYALTY THING (goes on the register card)
Stamp card. Buy nine sandwiches or bowls, the tenth is on us. One stamp per visit. Ask at the register. No app, no points, no email.

THE WALL BOARD — sections, in this order
1. TODAY (the daily specials — these change EVERY DAY, so the board has to let us swap one tile without redoing the whole board)
2. SANDWICHES & HEROS
3. BOWLS & SALADS
4. FROM THE CASE
5. COFFEE & COLD DRINKS

A NOTE ON THE SPECIALS
The TODAY section changes daily. Whatever we build has to be something I can open myself and swap one special in and out in a minute, without touching anything else on the board. If a special needs a photo it comes off the counter shots.

A NOTE ON THE NAME
The sign over the slicer is the sign. It is our father's. Do not redraw it into a font. Whatever the window and the tags use, it has to still read as that sign, knife and all.
