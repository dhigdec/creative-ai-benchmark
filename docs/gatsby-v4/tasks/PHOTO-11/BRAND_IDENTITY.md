# Lumora

Lumora is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Los Angeles, California, and this commission covers phase 1: pre-production lip-oil colour concepts.

## Audience
18 to 32 year olds discovering us on social and buying shade first on a phone, cross shopping a 6 dollar drugstore oil and a 38 dollar prestige one.

## Positioning
A 24 dollar lip oil. We want to look more expensive than we are while staying honest, so the treatment is bright, clean and daylight neutral, never moody prestige and never blown out drugstore.

## Palette
- Amber Hour: #C8792E
- Warm Ink: #3A2A22
- Soft Cream: #F7F0E6
- Brushed Gold: #C6A268
- Rosewood: #9E5B57
- Clear Glass: #EDE7DE

## Typography
Display in a clean confident sans with a little character; text in a neutral daylight sans. The feel is fresh and self assured, neither serif prestige nor bold discount.

## Voice
Warm, direct, a bit fun, never overclaiming. We describe the finish and let the shade speak. Example line the brand would write: 'A sheer wash of colour, a proper shine, no stickiness.'

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark and shade lockups exist as working digital files. There is no printed guideline; the carton photograph we have was shot under shop light and should not be trusted as a colour reference.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
