# FOLD & GRAIN Studio

FOLD & GRAIN shoots small brands' products the way those brands actually sell them, in a row, as a range, rather than one hero at a time. For six years we sold days. We are now selling a product: one continuous take per client and, out of that single take, the shot board, the short films and the platform cuts a small brand needs to launch. Our work sits behind pantry goods at eight dollars and sterling silver at a hundred and eighty, and the whole point is that the same method tunes to each. Precision, repeatability and honesty about what a shoot can and cannot yield are the studio's stock in trade.

## Audience
Small and independent product brands (pantry, personal care, jewellery, homeware) launching or refreshing a web store and needing a full range shot to look like one afternoon. Founders who buy a fixed price and a fixed turnaround, not a day rate.

## Positioning
Repositioning from roughly fifteen hundred dollars a day to a fixed twenty two hundred to thirty five hundred dollars per client run. Mid-market, productized, premium to a freelancer but well under an agency.

## Typography
A clean neo-grotesque for headings and the studio wordmark, paired with a monospaced face for technical specs, parameters and captions on the service page. Type is set tight, quiet and utilitarian.

## Voice
Direct, specific, no salesmanship. We name what a shoot can deliver and what it cannot, and we write our recipes so an assistant could run them. Sentences are short and concrete.

## Supplied Identity
FOLD & GRAIN has a consistent working look built up over six years but never a codified system. There is a set wordmark and studio colours in practice, no written guidelines. This engagement, which includes the studio's own service page, is where the identity gets formalized for the first time.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
