# Continental Cup 2026

Continental Cup 2026 is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is touring competition, Western Europe and North America, and this commission covers phase 2: squad press run and partner files.

## Audience
Collectors aged sixteen to forty five who buy officially licensed sets beside other marquee cards, in grocery aisles, at newsstands and in hobby shops. They notice when a card in the pack looks like it came from somewhere else.

## Positioning
A flagship licensed pack retailing around fourteen dollars, positioned above community and league sets and below fine-art prints. It must read as a marquee cup collectible, saturated and cinematic, never as a cheap programme or a gallery piece.

## Palette
- Pitch Black: #0B0E12
- Floodlight Gold: #E7B24C
- Deep Field Green: #123A2A
- Steel Rule: #8A94A0
- Card White: #F4F1EA
- Kit Crimson: #B4232B

## Typography
A heavy, near-metallic display face for the player name and squad number, set large and confident, paired with a clean condensed sans for the position, club line and set year. The type should feel like a stadium scoreboard, not a school sports day.

## Voice
Bold, terse, terrace-proud. Copy is short and declarative. Example line the brand would write: 'Eighteen players. One set. Every card earns its place in the sleeve.'

## Supplied Identity
There is no surviving design file or logo artwork. The tournament mark now exists only on printed things: the sole reference is a handheld photograph of a pitch-side sponsor board carrying the mark in one deep ink on a pale board. It has to be recovered as usable artwork before it can be placed at any size.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
