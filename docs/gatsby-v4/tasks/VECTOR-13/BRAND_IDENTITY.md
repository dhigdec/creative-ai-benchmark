# Maison Serault

Maison Serault is a small fine jewelry house built around its founder's own hand. Pieces are made and sold through the boutique and a few trusted stockists, and everything leaves in packaging people keep. The house is changing box suppliers, and the new supplier needs proper artwork before it will quote.

## Audience
Buyers roughly 30 to 55 purchasing as a gift or for themselves, plus a small number of stockists. They value discretion and craft over logos.

## Positioning
Fine jewelry from 600 to 4,000 dollars. Positioned as an independent house: hairline refinement, high contrast, a small mark on a large quiet field, never mass market and never loud.

## Palette
- Serault Ink: #1B2A2E
- Warm Brass: #B08D4C
- Card Cream: #EFE9DC
- Boutique Charcoal: #2A2724
- Soft Stone: #C9BEA8

## Typography
A high contrast serif with genuine heritage credibility carries the house name. A quiet companion sets care copy and the address block. Type must never compete with the hairline foil mark.

## Voice
Spare, assured, unhurried. A line the house would write: "Each piece is finished by hand and meant to outlive its first wearer."

## Supplied Identity
The working files are gone. The wordmark survives only as one flat export from 2019 whose source files were never sent, a phone photograph of brushed brass letters on the boutique wall, and a daylight photograph of the monogram foil blocked on an old card. The three disagree about the house colour, and there is no vector master of either mark.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
