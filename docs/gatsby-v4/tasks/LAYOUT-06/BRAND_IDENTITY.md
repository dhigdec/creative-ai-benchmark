# Nordheim Atelier

Nordheim Atelier is a designer-led studio in Copenhagen making a tight seasonal collection in wool, shearling and gabardine. FW26 is twelve looks, sold to press and to roughly thirty independent stores. The house builds one editorial look each season and carries it across everything a buyer and an editor see.

## Audience
Fashion press and independent-store buyers aged 30 to 55. Readers who value cloth and construction over gloss, and who judge a collection on whether the range holds together.

## Positioning
Garments run 600 to 2,400 dollars, positioned as couture-adjacent designer ready-to-wear. Prices live only on the linesheet where buyers expect them; the collateral never discounts or shouts, since a sale treatment would strip the perceived value in a single spread.

## Palette
- Ink: #111112
- Paper: #EDEBE6
- Cool Grey: #6E7075
- Halftone Grey: #B9B7B2
- Bone: #DAD5CC

## Typography
One documentary-editorial system: a strong high-contrast display serif or a condensed grotesque for covers and headlines, paired with a plain workhorse sans for captions, the season note and the linesheet. Hard contrast, tight measure, nothing decorative.

## Voice
Spare and factual, closer to an investigative magazine than a catalogue. Names the fabric and the maker and leaves the rest. Example line: "Undyed Gotland wool, cut on the straight, made in Copenhagen."

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark exists in only one place: a single letterpress card from the first season, photographed. The original artwork is gone. The mark is a compound lockup (wordmark, a hairline rule, and ATELIER in small caps) and has to be recovered whole from that photograph. No live logo files or type spec survive.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
