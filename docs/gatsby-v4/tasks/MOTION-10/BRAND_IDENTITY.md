# DAYDRIFT

DAYDRIFT is a slow, unhurried interview show built around one guest and one real conversation that runs the better part of an hour. It is made by two people in a room they know well, funded by a small paying membership and one renewing sponsor. The promise is intimacy: you are sitting in on a talk between people who like each other, not watching a broadcast.

## Audience
Regular listeners aged thirty to fifty, most of them listening at work through the day. About two hundred paying members at eight dollars a month, plus a few thousand free listeners who click through to the channel page.

## Positioning
Membership at eight dollars a month with one modest renewing sponsor. Squarely independent: above hobby, well below a network production. The look must read considered and hand made, never broadcast and never scrappy meme.

## Palette
- Lamplight Amber: #C68A4E
- Deep Room Brown: #3A2E26
- Foam Cream: #EDE4D3
- Slate Ink: #2B2B2E
- Muted Sage: #7C8471

## Typography
A warm humanist serif for the show name and episode titles, paired with a quiet humanist sans for credits, dates and body. Generous spacing, restrained weights, nothing condensed or shouted. Type should feel like a good paperback, not a title card.

## Voice
Plain, warm and a little understated, the way two friends actually talk. We never oversell a guest. Example line the brand would write: "Episode eight. We sat down with her for an hour and did not want it to end."

## Supplied Identity
There are no brand files left. The show name only survives as artwork on the foam flag on the microphone, drawn years ago by a friend who has since moved away and left no source file. Last season's printed episode card exists only as the print file the printer returned. Everything else has to be rebuilt from those.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
