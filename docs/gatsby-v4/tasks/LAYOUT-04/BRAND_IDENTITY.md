# NORTHGROVE

NORTHGROVE is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Burlington, Vermont, and this commission covers phase 2: wholesale lookbook, hang tags and launch collateral.

## Audience
Buyers and wearers twenty eight to forty five who value provenance and construction over logos, and who read fabric and make before they read a brand mark.

## Positioning
Seventy four to one hundred twenty eight dollars per piece, mid premium wholesale and direct. Muted grounds, full frame photography that shows cloth and seam, and copy that leads on material and make. Deliberately clear of both the twenty four dollar fast fashion grid and the price free editorial luxury a buyer cannot work from.

## Palette
- Charcoal: #33352F
- Workshop Olive: #5E6844
- Oat Cotton: #D8CDB6
- Forge Rust: #9A5A38
- Cast Pewter: #8C8A85

## Typography
The wordmark is a charcoal condensed lockup beside a small olive leaf glyph. A condensed grotesque display carries headers, look titles and the poster; a sturdy text face carries the season statement, fabric and make notes, and the linesheet price grid. Heritage and plain, never costume.

## Voice
Understated and maker focused, provenance before promotion. Example line the brand would write: 'Ten pieces, cut and sewn in Burlington, meant to be worn hard.'

## Supplied Identity
The NORTHGROVE wordmark and olive leaf glyph are set. The whole capsule was shot flat, overhead, on a mid gray backdrop across two mornings, with the cast hardware shot separately on the bench. The collaborating club has no artwork left: its crest survives only as a photograph of an old wool pennant on a wall. Last season's hang tag exists as the printer's press file, same size and stock this year.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
