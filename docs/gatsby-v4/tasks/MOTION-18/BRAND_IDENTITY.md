# Velvet Hour

Velvet Hour is a monthly ticketed jazz and folk night held in the ninety seat back room of a Providence bar. Two sets, no support, one camera in the same corner every month. The night sells intimacy and musicianship to people who read a lineup carefully, so everything we make should feel like a good room rather than a club or a concert hall.

## Audience
Lineup literate adults roughly thirty to sixty who pay eighteen to twenty five dollars to sit close and listen. Locals who follow the acts, not a party crowd.

## Positioning
Mid, intimate. An eighteen to twenty five dollar ticket. Never budget club, never black tie concert hall.

## Palette
- Lamp Cream: #EFE3C8
- Back Room Green: #243026
- Warm Ink: #1C1A17
- Ember Amber: #C8873F
- Dust Rose: #B6837C

## Typography
A quiet editorial system: a warm serif for act names and titles, a restrained humanist sans for billing lines, dates and body. Type stays small, generously set and calm. No display gimmicks, no gold foil gestures.

## Voice
Plain, affectionate, low key. Talks about the room and the players, never hypes. Short sentences, no exclamation, lets the act's face and the music do the work.

## Supplied Identity
Our mark, a moth over a lamp inside a hairline arc of small caps type, was made by the designer who set up the night, and the source file went with them when they left. All that survives is the mark painted in one cream ink on the dark green lamp box by the door, which is why it has to be rebuilt from that as real artwork. No brand typeface was ever formally chosen, so the type system is open for you to set.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
