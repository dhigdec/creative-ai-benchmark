# Verranza Coastal Retreats

Verranza Coastal Retreats is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Liguria, Italy, and this commission covers phase 1: villa image system and booking imagery.

## Audience
Guests aged 35 to 60, American and Northern European families and groups of friends booking a whole house for a week, plus a small trade channel of travel agents working from printed rate cards. They pay for a real place and refuse anything that looks staged or cooked.

## Positioning
Upper-mid coastal rental, 520 to 780 euros a night in season. A restraint band, not a gloss band: honest light and true water rather than saturated marketplace glow, and firmly above budget literalism.

## Palette
- Verranza Blue: #24425A
- Lime Wash: #EFE9DC
- Ligurian Stone: #C4B5A0
- Olive Grove: #6E7355
- Cove Water: #4C7A8C
- Ink Charcoal: #2A2925

## Typography
An elegant, low-contrast serif for the house names and headers, set with air, paired with a quiet humanist sans for detail and rates. Restraint over luxury signalling: no heavy display weights, no gold, generous white space so one photograph carries a page.

## Voice
Warm, plain and place-first. We name the stone, the water and the afternoon light, and we never oversell. Example line: 'Three houses above the cove, let by the family who has kept them.'

## Supplied Identity
The collection has never had a logo file. The monogram exists only as the wrought-iron plate the village smith cut when the first house was bought, and all we hold is a photograph of it on the gate. The travel-agent rate cards survive only as the file the printer sent to press last year, with no working source.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
