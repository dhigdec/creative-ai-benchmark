# Voltcast

Voltcast is an independent consumer-tech review channel run by two people out of Portland. We buy or borrow the gear, run it hard, and say what we actually think. Episodes go out long-form to the main feed plus vertical and square shorts. About a third of the revenue is sponsor money, so the grid has to look like one channel and the sponsor read has to be exactly right.

## Audience
Enthusiast buyers and everyday shoppers who want an honest verdict before they spend. They follow us because we are not the manufacturer, and they can tell the difference.

## Positioning
Mid-tier creator economy. A sponsor integration is worth a few thousand dollars and renewal depends on how the segment looks. The channel has to read as a credible independent reviewer, never as broadcast gloss and never as raw phone-shot filler.

## Palette
- Volt Orange: #F26A1B
- Card Charcoal: #181A1D
- Bench Grey: #3A3E44
- Panel White: #F2F2EF
- Muted Steel: #8A9199

## Typography
A sturdy grotesque sans for headings and callouts, paired with a clean readable sans for body and specs. Type stays out of the way of the product being reviewed. Spec numbers read cleanly at small sizes.

## Voice
Direct, dry, and unhyped. We talk like a person who has actually used the thing. Sponsor copy runs as written and the disclosure line is exact. We never oversell.

## Supplied Identity
The channel bug exists only as a flat export on white and we have no editable brand kit. On-screen cards get rebuilt from scratch every episode, which is the problem we want solved. We need the bug prepared as reusable artwork and the card set delivered as a file we can reopen and change each week.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
