# Gable & Grove Home Services

Gable & Grove takes on full exterior renovations and garden builds between roughly 45,000 and 120,000 dollars a project. Everything is sold twice, once on the website and once out of a quote pack on somebody's kitchen table, so the work has to look like exactly what the firm delivers and no more. The proof is the before and after, and the before has to stay honest.

## Audience
Households aged 45 to 65 with old houses and long time horizons, choosing between two or three bids and buying on trust rather than on price.

## Positioning
45,000 to 120,000 dollars a project. At this band the finish reads cool, calm and material: real neutral stone, deep honest blacks, one photograph doing the work, generous white space, and a mark that behaves like a maker's stamp rather than a sales badge. Never a supermarket flyer, never developer marketing.

## Palette
- Grove Green: #1E3A2F
- Stone Neutral: #C9C3B8
- Honest Black: #17140F
- Slate: #4A4E4C
- Bone: #EDE9E1

## Typography
A quiet display serif for the mark and headings paired with a restrained humanist sans for scope, prices and captions. Legible, unhurried, no starbursts and no oversized phone number.

## Voice
Plain and confident, the voice of a builder who does not oversell. Example line the brand would write: "Full exterior renovation, Ridge Road. Limestone pergola, dry stone terrace, planting."

## Supplied Identity
The mark is a problem the firm names openly: the sign writer who drew it is gone, and all that survives is a picture file he emailed once and a photograph of the mark hand painted on the firm's truck door. The project cards survive only as the retired printer's press PDF. Yard signs print in the deep Grove Green.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
