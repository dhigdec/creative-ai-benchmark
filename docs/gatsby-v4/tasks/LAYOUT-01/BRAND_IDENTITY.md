# Kantyna Nova

Kantyna Nova is a single table set once a night: one tasting, one wine flight, cooked and served by the two people who built it. The room is dark and quiet by design, so the plate is the only thing lit. Nothing is rushed, nothing is upsold, and the evening is the point rather than the meal.

## Audience
Guests thirty to fifty five who book the room as their occasion, not their dinner. They value intention over abundance and read a dark, spare room as confidence rather than as coldness.

## Positioning
One hundred and eighty five dollars for the tasting, ninety five more for the wine pairing. Held back and expensive without raising its voice: above a neighbourhood bistro, deliberately short of a destination tasting palace an eighteen seat opening cannot fill. Price is never a feature.

## Palette
- Cellar Black: #14110F
- Warm Bone: #E9E2D4
- Aged Brass: #A6875A
- Deep Claret: #5A2233
- Ash Stone: #7C7468

## Typography
A transitional display serif for course titles and the room's name, quiet and old world, set large but never loud. A humanist text face beneath it for descriptions, hours and prices, prices sitting at body size in the same text face with no rule, box or currency flourish.

## Voice
First person plural, plain and understated, never salesy. Example line the brand would write: 'One seating a night. Sit down and let the evening happen.'

## Supplied Identity
No formal brand kit exists yet. The course titles and the approved copy are set, there is a single handheld photograph of the finished room, and the owners photographed every plate themselves in the room during service. The private dining printer holds the pairing card press file. The identity is being set now for the first time.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
