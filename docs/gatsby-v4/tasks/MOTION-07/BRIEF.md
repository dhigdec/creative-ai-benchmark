# SB3-087-MOT | Norvant - Product explainer motion system

Norvant needs an explainer for its March release that a finance or operations buyer can follow without a sales call. Set up the problem, show the actual product behaviour, and close on the approved next step. The interface must remain readable, including in the short upright adaptation. Please avoid decorative motion that makes the demonstration harder to understand. Use the cleared script and real product states supplied in the pack, not invented capabilities. We also need a cover that describes the same product story.

Deliverables
1. Product explainer: 1 named export. The demonstration supports the stated product claim. The setup is understandable before the demonstration. The ending gives the approved next action.
2. Explainer platform cut: 1 named export. The product function remains understandable.
3. Explainer cover: 1 named export. The product is identified accurately.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.
- Video delivery means a playable MP4, not a storyboard, still montage PDF or editing log. Audio expectations are stated on each film. Speech-led work requires supplied or explicitly authorised synthetic speech; do not invent documentary speech.
- Authored animation can exceed the duration of still source images. Live-action edits require adequate distinct footage; looping a short clip is not a substitute for the missing coverage.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
