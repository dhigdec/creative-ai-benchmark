# Alderwood Scholars Fund

Alderwood Scholars Fund runs five after school learning centres and a scholarship fund for students across greater Providence. This winter marks our twentieth year. The year end appeal carries roughly half our giving, and it works because donors trust that the money reaches the students rather than the overhead. We show our numbers plainly, name where every dollar went, and let the work speak.

## Audience
Mission motivated donors aged forty five to seventy who give because they believe in the work. Several read the overhead ratio before the narrative and reward organisations that look like they spend on students, not on production.

## Positioning
Average gift 45 dollars, major asks 5,000 to 50,000. Community scale and grassroots: careful and credible rather than glossy, every visible dollar of production reading as an in house afternoon rather than an agency retainer.

## Typography
A humanist serif for headlines and the twentieth year milestone, with a plain humanist sans for body, captions and tables. Numbers set in a clear tabular figure so charts and the spend breakdown read honestly. The feel is settled and civic, not corporate.

## Voice
Plain, specific, quietly proud. It credits students and staff, states periods on every figure, and never begs. Example line the brand would write: "Last year, ninety students finished the programme. Here is where your gift went, line by line."

## Supplied Identity
The crest was drawn by a trustee in 2006 and the artwork files are long gone. The only surviving version is the enamel crest on the front wall of the Eastgate centre, which we have as a single handheld photograph. There is no vector, no guideline sheet and no recorded palette or type.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
