# Lumen Quarterly

Lumen Quarterly is a print magazine about art, craft and the people who make things along the coast. Each issue turns on one photo essay and about two thousand words of reporting. Readers pay for it because it is good to hold and worth keeping on a shelf, not for news.

## Audience
Design literate readers aged 30 to 55 who buy the object as much as the article and renew a subscription because the magazine is made with care.

## Positioning
Eighteen dollars a single copy, sixty dollars a year. An independent object priced above a newsstand title and below an art book, sold on typographic craft rather than volume.

## Palette

- Masthead Ink: #1F3A3C
- Charcoal: #26262A
- Rule Grey: #9C968B
- Signal Ochre: #B4762E

## Typography
The whole product is the type. A refined display serif for the masthead and openers, a readable book face for the two thousand word body at a generous measure, a quiet sans for captions, credits and folios. Real hierarchy, white space the eye rests in.

## Voice
Considered, warm and exact, written like an essay rather than a pitch. Example line the brand would write: He has built the same hull the same way for forty years, and he still stops to look at the grain before the first cut.
