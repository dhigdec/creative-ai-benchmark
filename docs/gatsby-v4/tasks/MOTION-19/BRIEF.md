# SB3-099-MOT | Milepost Driving Academy - Scenario clip training library

Milepost needs short training examples for the listed driving scenarios. Show the manoeuvre clearly enough that a nervous learner can follow the necessary stages, with narration and labels that agree with the approved instruction. The upright example must keep the relevant road context visible. Do not turn a disconnected set of road shots into apparent evidence of a completed manoeuvre. Adequate scenario footage and cleared narration are prerequisites, not something the editor should guess.

Deliverables
1. Driving-scenario training clip: 8 named exports. The narration matches the visual scenario. The instruction agrees with the cleared training copy.
2. Vertical training example: 1 named export. The relevant road context remains visible. The complete instruction is intelligible.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.
- Video delivery means a playable MP4, not a storyboard, still montage PDF or editing log. Audio expectations are stated on each film. Speech-led work requires supplied or explicitly authorised synthetic speech; do not invent documentary speech.
- Authored animation can exceed the duration of still source images. Live-action edits require adequate distinct footage; looping a short clip is not a substitute for the missing coverage.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
