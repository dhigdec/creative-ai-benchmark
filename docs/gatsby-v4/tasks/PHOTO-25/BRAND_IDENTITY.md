# Apexguard

Apexguard is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Charlotte, North Carolina, and this commission covers phase 1: rebrand photography and product rollout.

## Audience
Independent installers, 25 to 45, who judge a supplier on whether the packaging justifies the price to a customer standing at the counter.

## Positioning
Line items from two hundred fifty to twelve hundred dollars, a confident premium supplier rather than a bright discount accessory brand.

## Palette
- House black: #101114
- Gunmetal: #3A3D42
- Cool steel: #B7BCC2
- Signal accent: #E4552B
- Off white: #F2F1ED

## Typography
A tight modern grotesque, mostly caps, with wide tracking on the master mark. Fitment and warranty lines are set plainly and stay legible at small size.

## Voice
Confident and terse, no hype. Example line: 'Film, tint and hardware. Fitted right, backed properly.' No mascots, no vintage badges, no heavy gradients.

## Supplied Identity
Our master mark survives only on a die-cut box sticker and on a decal on an installer's shop glass. The studio that drew it has closed and taken the source files, so it has to be recovered as usable artwork.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
