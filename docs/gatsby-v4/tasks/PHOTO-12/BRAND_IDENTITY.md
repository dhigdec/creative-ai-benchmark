# Kilnmore

Kilnmore is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Portland, Maine, and this commission covers phase 1: ecommerce product image system.

## Audience
Buyers aged 30 to 55 who buy craft and buy a specific glaze, reading the piece and its finish before they order.

## Positioning
Considered handmade stoneware at 38 to 95 dollars a piece. Above mass import homeware, below gallery ceramics. The house look is neutral and airy with the glaze true to the piece in the hand, never punchy and saturated, never near black gallery styling.

## Palette
- Warm Grey House Ground: #CFC7BB
- Celadon Drift: #9FB3A2
- Oatmeal: #E3DBCB
- Bone: #EFE9DD
- Iron Slip: #3B342B
- Kiln Rust: #9A5A3C

## Typography
Display in a quiet humanist serif with real craft warmth; text in a plain grotesque set with generous air. The feel is considered and calm, neither rustic cute nor gallery austere.

## Voice
Plain, grounded, specific about material and process. We name the glaze, the form and the firing and stop there. Example line the brand would write: 'Wheel thrown stoneware, matte celadon, oxidised recesses left dark.'

## Supplied Identity
The only clean copy of our mark left is the printed wrapping tissue we wrap orders in. The wordmark and enclosed kiln arch device need recovering from that photograph as placeable artwork; there is no source file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
