# Vanguard Motors

Vanguard holds around fourteen carefully bought cars at a time and sells to people who have already decided roughly what they want and are choosing where to buy it. The card in the windscreen is the first thing a buyer meets, before anyone walks out, so it has to answer their two questions honestly: what is this car, and what does it cost.

## Audience
Private retail buyers aged 35 to 60 who have narrowed the choice online, driven a distance to see one specific car, and are deciding whether this dealer is careful.

## Positioning
Independent prestige used. Cars from 18,000 pounds to 95,000. Photograph forward and quiet: one large honest picture of the actual car, a short spec of what differs between examples, the price plainly set and the finance line legible but secondary. Never the budget lot register, never a manufacturer brochure with no price.

## Typography
A confident low contrast serif or sturdy grotesque for the marque and headline, paired with a clean neutral sans for the spec block, price and finance footer. Careful and quiet, neither discount loud nor brochure sparse.

## Voice
Factual, unhurried and confident, a dealer who lets the car and the spec do the talking. No exclamation, no urgency. Example: One owner, full history, the colour it says on the card and the colour it is on the forecourt.

## Supplied Identity
The shield artwork is long gone. The only clean version we hold is a photograph of the pressed and painted aluminium plate above the office door, weathered by several winters, carrying the shield at about 180mm. The current stock, specs and prices arrive from the stock feed, and the finance wording is fixed by the broker.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
