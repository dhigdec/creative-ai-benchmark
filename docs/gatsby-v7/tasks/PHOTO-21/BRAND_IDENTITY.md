# Marlo Quill (Quill and Kiln)

Marlo Quill makes small-batch stoneware in a shared Portland studio and sells it four times a year, plus a coffee-bar collaboration each autumn. About a third of what she sells comes straight off her own posts. The brand is the person: a real maker in a real room, warm and quiet, and never a polished label, because that is what her audience follows her for.

## Audience
Followers aged 22 to 34, the same age as Marlo, who buy 38 to 90 dollar stoneware and follow the maker rather than a brand.

## Positioning
A mid-tier creator retainer of 600 to 1,500 dollars a month, stoneware at 38 to 90 dollars. Above careless untreated phone photos, below gallery-ceramics gloss; a heavy uniform filter would read as selling out and cost her directly.

- Sprig Green: #6E7A4E · optional accent. A small footer or credit line, as the maker line and collaboration credit on quillandkiln_card_template.pdf, the glaze dot beside a Sprig Green glaze name, and small dividers. Never a ground, panel, headline, caption or body text.

All type on Warm Ground or Oat: the stacked name and its 0.75 pt rule, headlines, caption decks, subheadings, post captions and other captions, piece names, prices and body text, as the piece name, glaze line and price on quillandkiln_card_template.pdf. - Oat: #F3EAD9 · optional. A secondary panel or photo mat beside the Warm Ground main ground, never replacing it on a page, carrying Bench Ink type, and the glaze dot beside an Oat glaze name. Never a type colour.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Warm Ground #E7D9C4 is the main ground on every output, as the ground and photo mat of quillandkiln_card_template.pdf; Oat #F3EAD9 may set secondary panels and photo mats only, never replacing Warm Ground on a page. No other colour is a ground, panel or photo mat. All type sits on a solid Warm Ground or Oat ground or panel, beside or over a photograph, never on bare photographic pixels. On those grounds the stacked name and its rule, headlines, caption decks, subheadings, post captions, piece names, prices and body text are Bench Ink #33291F, as the piece name, glaze line and price on the card. Sprig Green #6E7A4E may set a small footer or credit line, as the card's maker line and credit. A glaze dot, where used, is printed in the named glaze's own palette colour. Kiln Clay #B4855C, Ember #A6552E and Sprig Green serve only as glaze dots, dividers and, for Sprig Green, a footer or credit line, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground or panel.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient, colour overlay or heavy uniform filter, black and white included, is laid over a photograph, since a heavy uniform filter would read as selling out. Per-frame colour correction held to the fired test tiles in marlo_glaze_swatches.jpg is the commissioned grade, not an overlay or filter. Any text panel sits beside or over the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No gold, foil, metallic or gloss effect on any type, panel, rule, button, icon or motif, since the brand sits below gallery-ceramics gloss and is never a polished label, and no colour outside the six palette hexes (#B4855C, #E7D9C4, #6E7A4E, #A6552E, #33291F and #F3EAD9) for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or motifs, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000 included.
- Photography: Photographs keep their true glaze colour, held to the fired test tiles in marlo_glaze_swatches.jpg and never to a palette hex, even where a glaze shares its name with one (Ember, Kiln Clay, Oat, Sprig Green and Warm Ground), and are never recoloured toward the palette. The owner's rule in marlo_captions.txt stands: if a frame could not be brought to the true colour I cut it.

## Typography
Display face: Erode. Text face: Untitled Sans.

- 01 Display / H1: Erode Medium, 34 pt / 38 pt, Medium, tracking 0 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Erode Medium, 20 pt / 24 pt, Medium, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body: Untitled Sans Regular, 10.5 pt / 15 pt, Regular (400), tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Untitled Sans, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Regular, 500 for prices, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: The name is always lowercase and stacked on two lines, quill over and kiln, tracked at 0 units, with a 0.75 pt Bench Ink rule the width of the longer line tucked directly beneath it. That zero tracking and that rule carry into step 01, so the display row is the wordmark's own setting.

Rules:
- Never set the name in capitals.
- Never letterspace the name; the stacked wordmark stays tracked at 0 units.
- The name is always lowercase and always stacked on two lines, quill over and kiln, never on one line.
- The 0.75 pt Bench Ink rule is always the width of the longer line and sits directly beneath the stack.
- The name and every headline set in Erode Medium; captions, piece names and prices set in Untitled Sans.
- Prices set in Untitled Sans 500 with tabular lining figures.

## Voice
First person, casual and direct. Example line: One look, mine, not a filter.

## Supplied Identity
No formal brand files. Her three motifs, a mug, a sprig and a small kiln, exist only as ink on a photographed sketchbook page she keeps re-cropping. The true glaze colours are pinned by a single reference frame of fired test tiles rather than any spec, and there is no logo file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
