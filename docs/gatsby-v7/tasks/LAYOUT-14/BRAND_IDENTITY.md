# Lumen Quarterly

Lumen Quarterly is a print magazine about art, craft and the people who make things along the coast. Each issue turns on one photo essay and about two thousand words of reporting. Readers pay for it because it is good to hold and worth keeping on a shelf, not for news.

## Audience
Design literate readers aged 30 to 55 who buy the object as much as the article and renew a subscription because the magazine is made with care.

## Positioning
Eighteen dollars a single copy, sixty dollars a year. An independent object priced above a newsstand title and below an art book, sold on typographic craft rather than volume.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every ground is Uncoated Paper or a photograph; no palette colour fills a page, panel or card ground. Masthead Ink carries the masthead, headline, byline, pull quotes and each card's plate numeral and title; Charcoal carries body text, the standfirst and captions; Rule Grey carries hairlines and folios, and on the cards the credit line and issue line; an issue caption's credit line is Rule Grey or Charcoal; Signal Ochre carries only the standfirst band and pull-quote marks in the issue and the plate rule on the cards, as on lumen_spring_issue_press.pdf and lumen_platecard_template.pdf. Rule Grey and Signal Ochre together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, the standfirst band aside.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, with no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. One exception, which lumen_spring_issue_press.pdf already prints: a band of Signal Ochre #B4762E at 20% opacity behind the standfirst. In autumn-issue.pdf it sits over lumen_autumn_essay_01.jpg, the essay's opening photograph. Nowhere else, and never on a plate card.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#F3EEE3, #1F3A3C, #26262A, #9C968B and #B4762E) for type, rules, panels or icons; no brightened version of any ink (lumen_style_guide.pdf: "Ink on uncoated; do not brighten."); no tint or transparency other than the standfirst band.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward a palette colour. lumen_style_guide.pdf: "One light per feature" and "Warm, unhurried. No HDR look, no crushed blacks." The blown window in lumen_autumn_essay_02.jpg stays as photographed and is never filled with Uncoated Paper, another palette colour or invented detail.

## Typography
Display face: Epilogue Black. Text face: Arnhem Blond.

- 01 Display / H1: Epilogue, 64 pt / 68 pt, Black, tracking letterspaced to the exact measure of the text column, roughly +180 units at this size, all caps.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Epilogue, 40 pt / 44 pt, Black, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body: Arnhem, 10 pt / 15 pt, Blond, tracking 0, max 62 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Arnhem, 8 pt / 12 pt, Blond, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: The stacked masthead. LUMEN in Epilogue Black caps at 64 pt on 68 pt, letterspaced until it spans the exact measure of the text column, with QUARTERLY beneath in Epilogue Medium caps at a quarter of that cap height, tracked to the identical width so both lines start and end flush. Two spaced lines locked to one width, always flush left, is what a reader knows before reading a word.

Rules:
- Never centre the masthead.
- Plate numerals stay Roman.
- The masthead is always two letterspaced lines locked to one width, LUMEN in Epilogue Black over QUARTERLY in Epilogue Medium at a quarter of the cap height, both starting and ending flush.
- LUMEN is letterspaced until it spans the exact measure of the text column, so the tracking is re-cut whenever the measure changes.
- The essay sets to a 62 character measure.
- Epilogue is display only. The essay, captions and plate lines are always Arnhem Blond.
- Continuity of type, measure and picture treatment is the identity. Every issue must set to the same four steps.

## Voice
Considered, warm and exact, written like an essay rather than a pitch. Example line the brand would write: He has built the same hull the same way for forty years, and he still stops to look at the grain before the first cut.
