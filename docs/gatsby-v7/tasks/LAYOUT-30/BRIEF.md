# SB3-075-LAY | Emberwell Brew House - Live insert and taproom collateral

Two reusable menu templates for fourteen locations, not fourteen manual edits

Our managers have been retyping the same Emberwell insert in a word processor. That led to a customer finding a different flat-white price twenty minutes down the road. We need two controlled templates that take their local details and prices from the data.

Deliverables
1. Current-menu insert: 15 named exports. The availability wording matches the current record: each room's insert lists every line that room offers, at that room's own price, and no line the room does not offer. The price is legible. Each insert is headed with the brand name, Emberwell Brew House, names its room and carries the house mark, the room's street address, opening hours and its current on-now feature where the room can serve it, the fixed allergen statement, and one of the supplied dish and drink photographs, showing a line that room offers.
2. Taproom counter card: 15 named exports. The store details, the room's street address and opening hours, match this record. The offer, the room's drinks at that room's own prices with its current on-now feature where the room can serve it, is understandable at counter distance. Each card is headed with the brand name, Emberwell Brew House, names its room and carries the house mark and the fixed allergen statement.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
