# Emberwell Brew House

Emberwell runs fourteen rooms under one name and one coffee program, each in a secondary New England town where it is the good room rather than the cheap one. Same room, same welcome, but every store pays its own rent, so the estate is one brand at fourteen prices.

## Audience
Morning trade aged 30 to 55 that comes in early and lingers for two hours. Guests who read a menu the way they read a good book and treat loud pricing as a sign they are in the wrong kind of room.

## Positioning
Upper middle independent. Plates 14 to 26 dollars, drinks 4.50 to 7. Not a chain and never wanting to read like one: type led, generous, prices set quietly at the end of a line. No combos, no starbursts, no value language.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Warm Cream is the page ground of every piece, as on both of last season's press files. Steam, where used, is the only panel colour: one solid panel behind the drinks list. Roast and Slate Ink carry the running text: the brand name heading each piece and the dish or drink name heading each menu line in Roast; the allergen statement, address and hours in Slate Ink; descriptions, the tagline and the town line in Roast or Slate Ink. Every price takes the colour of its own line, Roast or Slate Ink. The mark is reproduced flat, as on both press files: Ember kettle, Roast ring lettering, Kettle Brass ring. Ember is otherwise for thin rules, leader dots, section heads and the on-now line; Kettle Brass is only the mark's ring. Ember and Kettle Brass together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, the Steam panel and the mark included. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark. No price in Ember, Kettle Brass or any other accent, and no bold price, since a price is never coloured and never emboldened. No starburst, burst or badge shape of any colour.
- Photography: Each insert's photograph is one of the supplied dish and drink frames; no stock, generated or AI-extended image stands in, since the brief declares no Stock exception. It keeps its own corrected colour and is never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette; the dishes, drinks and walnut counter are not pushed toward Ember, Roast or Warm Cream.

## Typography
Display face: Kanit. Text face: Freight Text Pro.

- 01 Display / H1: Kanit SemiBold, 34 pt / 38 pt, SemiBold, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Kanit SemiBold, 24 pt / 27 pt, SemiBold, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: Freight Text Pro Book, 9.5 pt / 13 pt, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Kanit ExtraLight, 10 pt / 14 pt, ExtraLight, capitals, tracking +0.50 em, set across the full measure.

Signature move: Every piece is signed at the foot with the room's town set in Kanit ExtraLight capitals tracked 0.50 em across the full measure, the one widely spaced line on the page and the only thing that changes from room to room. It is step 04, at 10 pt, deliberately the quietest size and the widest tracking in the scale.

Rules:
- Never colour or embolden a price.
- Prices are set quietly at the end of the line in Freight Text Pro Book at 9.5 pt with tabular figures.
- The town line sits at the foot of every piece in Kanit ExtraLight capitals tracked 0.50 em across the full measure, and is the only line that changes between rooms.
- Kanit SemiBold sets section heads and the counter card headline.
- Freight Text Pro Book sets dish names at 13 pt and descriptions and prices at 9.5 pt on 13 pt.
- Italic is used only for seasonal lines.
- Screen sets 28 px on 34 px.
- No combos, no starbursts, no value language: never read like a chain.
- One consistent welcome across the estate, with honest local pricing a guest never has to question from one town to the next.

## Voice
Warm and plainspoken, a room talking rather than a brand selling. Short lines, no adjectives stacked for effect. Example: The stout is on until the barrel is done, then it is not.
