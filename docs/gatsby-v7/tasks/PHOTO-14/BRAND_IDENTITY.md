# Girdermark Elevator & Electrical Supply

Girdermark Elevator & Electrical Supply is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Cleveland, Ohio, and this commission covers phase 1: components photography and technical sheets.

## Audience
Working buyers aged 35 to 60 who have specified this class of part for twenty years and judge a supplier by whether the photography and the spec table say the same thing. They order by part number and hold us to the values printed beside it.

## Positioning
Mid to upper industrial. Parts run 400 to 9,000 dollars. The material has to read as trustworthy and technical, never as a consumer hardware brand and never as glossy marketing.

## Palette
- Girder Charcoal: #2B2F33 · mandatory for spec values. Spec values, captions and notes, full-width rules and the hairline keyline around a white panel, as on datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. It may also carry the part name or the whole certification and price footer. Never a ground or panel.
- Sheet White: #F6F6F4 · mandatory. The only ground of every SKU plate, as the sheet ground of datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never type, and never laid over a photograph.
- Technical Grey: #B9BEC2 · optional accent. Hairline dividers only, as the one hairline inside the pricing panel on datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Safety Amber: #E8A31D · optional accent. A single heavy rule only, as the one rule topping the pricing panel on datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never type, a ground or a panel, and never a tint on a photograph.
- Ink Black: #141517 · mandatory. The part number on every plate and cutout, as datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf sets its part number; the 2 pt keyline around the Cinzel part name; spec parameter labels, as that sheet sets its labels. It may also carry the part name or the whole certification and price footer. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Sheet White is the only ground of every plate, as on datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. The only panels are optional plain white #FFFFFF panels keyed with a Girder Charcoal hairline, as that sheet keys its photo frame, drawing frame and pricing panel; a pricing panel may carry the single Safety Amber top rule. All type is Ink Black or Girder Charcoal, on Sheet White or on a white panel: the part number and spec parameter labels Ink Black, spec values Girder Charcoal, the certification and price footer one run in a single colour, Ink Black or Girder Charcoal, the part name either. Safety Amber and Technical Grey are for rules and hairlines only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never type, a ground or a panel. On a cutout the part number is Ink Black and nothing else is added.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. On a cutout the only transparency is the alpha around the part and through its openings; no shadow, glow, reflection or haze is added.
- Forbidden: No metallic, gloss, glow or gradient effect on any type, rule, keyline or icon, so nothing reads as glossy marketing; no colour outside the five palette hexes (#2B2F33, #F6F6F4, #B9BEC2, #E8A31D and #141517) for type, rules, keylines or icons; no ground other than Sheet White and no panel other than plain white #FFFFFF panels keyed in Girder Charcoal as on datasheet_2025_PRINT.pdf.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own true colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: no part is tinted toward Safety Amber, Technical Grey or Girder Charcoal. Each finish keeps the look finishes_note.txt gives it: galvanised dull grey with the crystal pattern in it; zinc plate clean and bright with a slight blue tint, never grey or brassy; clear anodised a flat even grey with no blue and no spangle, never collapsing into the powder coat; powder coat dead flat, even black, no shine.

## Typography
Display face: Cinzel. Text face: IBM Plex Mono.

- 01 Display / H1 · part number: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 72 px / 78 px, Medium, tracking 0, tabular lining figures with the slashed zero.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · part name and section head: Cinzel Bold, 34 px / 40 px, Bold, tracking 0.18 em, capitals only inside a 2 pt Ink Black keyline.
- 03 Body · spec line: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 22 px / 34 px, Medium, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · certification and price footer: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 15 px / 22 px, Medium, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: No digit ever appears in the display face: the name is carved in Cinzel Bold capitals inside a 2 pt Ink Black keyline, and every part number, dimension and price is IBM Plex Mono with tabular lining figures and the slashed zero.

Rules:
- Never set a spec table in a serif.
- No digit ever appears in Cinzel: every part number, dimension and price is IBM Plex Mono Medium.
- Cinzel Bold is capitals only, tracked 0.18 em, inside a 2 pt Ink Black keyline.
- IBM Plex Mono carries tabular lining figures and the slashed zero everywhere it is used.
- Part numbers run 72 px on the 1200 by 1200 px plates, part names 34 px, spec lines 22 px on 34 px.
- The number printed beside the part and the number in the spec table must agree; we would rather lose a sale than print a spec we cannot certify.

## Voice
Direct, factual, no adjectives we cannot stand behind. We state the number, the finish and the price and we stop. If something is wrong we say so before it prints.

## Supplied Identity
We have no working brand files and no written guidelines. Our data sheets exist only as a flat press PDF the printer sent us last spring, and nobody here knows how it was built. Keep that sheet exactly as our customers recognise it. The palette and type notes above are our best account of the look, reconstructed for you, not a formal system.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
