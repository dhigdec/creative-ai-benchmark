# Ironline Forge

Ironline Forge casts bespoke iron and bronze for architects and developers who have mostly never stood on a foundry floor. We sell permanence and provenance: the heat, the hands, and the fact that the piece will still be here in eighty years. The brand has to feel like the shop floor, hot, dirty and precise.

## Audience
Specifiers, architects and developers, 40 to 65, buying craft and confidence rather than a catalogue product.

## Positioning
Bespoke castings from five to sixty thousand dollars a piece, priced on provenance and never presented as a promotion.

## Palette
- Iron black: #1A1A1C · mandatory. The ground of the poster and of both feature pages: the dark ground the maker's mark and every cut-out are judged on (ironline_story_copy.txt). Also the type colour on any Foundry sand panel, as the casting name on ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf.
- Furnace orange: #C6591F · mandatory for the pour rule. The 4 pt pour rule above every headline, a keyline beside a photograph and reference codes, as the reference code and keyline on ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf. Never a ground, panel, headline, standfirst or body text.
- Cast bronze: #7A5A2E · optional accent. Small labels set on Foundry sand, as the footer line on ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf, and hairline rules. Never a ground, panel, headline or body text.
- Ash grey: #6E6E6B · optional accent. Spec lines set on Foundry sand and hairline rules, as the alloy and weight lines and the footer rule on ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf. Never a ground, panel, headline or body text.
- Foundry sand: #C9BCA3 · mandatory where type sits on Iron black. Every headline, standfirst, body line, caption, spec line and the specifier contact set on Iron black, and the maker's mark reversed out of Iron black. It may also be a panel, as the ground of ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf, but never the page ground of the feature or the poster.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Iron black is the ground of the poster and of both pages of the feature, and covers more of the designed, non-photographic area than any other colour. On Iron black, all type and the reversed maker's mark are Foundry sand, except reference codes, which may be Furnace orange. A Foundry sand panel carries Iron black type, with spec lines in Ash grey, reference codes in Furnace orange and small labels in Cast bronze, as on ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf. Furnace orange, Cast bronze and Ash grey are for the pour rule, rules, reference codes, spec lines and small labels only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground or a panel.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient, tint or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on the solid Iron black ground or on a solid panel beside or over the photograph. The tinted photograph frame of ironline_spec_card_2025.pdf belongs to that card and is not carried into the feature or the poster.
- Forbidden: No white ground and no white type: cut-outs and the maker's mark are judged on Iron black, not on white (ironline_story_copy.txt). No bevel, emboss, sheen, glow or shadow on type, rules, panels or the maker's mark, since the brand is plain, declarative and unshowy, process and provenance over polish. No promotional device such as a discount, sale flash or price burst, since work is never presented as a promotion. No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, icons or the maker's mark.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour. They share one dark treatment made in the photograph itself, never a palette tint, duotone or monochrome laid over them, and are never recoloured toward the palette. Cast bronze, cast iron and pale foundry sand stay distinguishable from one another, and molten metal keeps its own bright glow rather than being greyed toward Iron black or flattened toward Furnace orange.

## Typography
Display face: Bespoke Serif. Text face: Neue Haas Grotesk Display.

- 01 Display / H1: Bespoke Serif Bold, 44 pt / 47 pt, Bold, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Bespoke Serif Bold, 16 pt / 21 pt, Bold, tracking 0 units.
- 03 Body: Neue Haas Grotesk Display 55 Roman, 10 pt / 14 pt, 55 Roman, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Neue Haas Grotesk Display 65 Medium, 8 pt / 12 pt, 65 Medium caps, tracking +80 units.

Signature move: A 4 pt furnace orange rule, always 40 mm long and always 12 mm above the headline, so every piece opens with the same short pour line before any word; on screen it is 4 px by 160 px. It belongs to step 01 and sits above that row wherever the headline falls, including the 180 pt poster.

Rules:
- Never reset the struck maker's mark in any typeface.
- The pour rule is always 4 pt deep and 40 mm long, and always 12 mm above the headline; on screen 4 px by 160 px.
- Headlines always set in Bespoke Serif Bold.
- Spec lines, reference codes and the specifier contact always set in Neue Haas Grotesk Display 65 Medium caps, tracked 80 units.
- The struck maker's mark is recovered faithfully, never tidied into a fashion monoline, and must hold small and reversed out of the dark.
- Work is priced on provenance and never presented as a promotion.

## Voice
Plain, declarative, unshowy. Example line: 'Iron and bronze, cast in this building since 1911.'

## Supplied Identity
Our maker's mark was cut into a die generations ago and survives only struck into the metal. There is no artwork for it anywhere, and the trade-fair stand and the brand story page are being rebuilt at the same time.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
