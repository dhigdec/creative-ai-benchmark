# Ironway Run Club

Ironway Run Club is a volunteer community running club in Providence with about thirty eight members. Its identity is the numbering the founder drew by hand in 1994, which the members treat as the club itself, and a screen printed club mark that survives on an old singlet. Each spring the kit goes to the heat transfer shop and every member runs the club's own Founders Five race with a bib.

## Audience
Members aged roughly 22 to 68, half of whom pin a bib on in the dark before a wet April race. Volunteers, not customers.

## Positioning
A forty dollar per season volunteer club with no sponsor. Everything printed must be cheap at volume and survive weather: one colour throughout, nothing that adds a plate or a pass, and nothing that looks like a paid entry event.

- Rail Navy: #1F2A44 · mandatory: the one ink on every bib and the one colour of the club mark. Everything printed on a bib, the drawn number, club mark, club name, member name and details, on Bib White, as on ironway_bib_2025.pdf; the single colour of club-mark.svg, the navy the mark is printed in on ironway_singlet_mark.jpg; one of the two allowed fills for a glyph master. It is also the colour of the new singlet in ironway_kit_swatch.jpg.
- Bib White: #F4F2EC · mandatory on every bib, as the ground only. The paper ground of every bib, as on ironway_bib_2025.pdf. Never an ink: nothing is printed in it, and no glyph master or club mark is drawn in it.
- Marshal Orange: #D46A1E · optional: cut film and glyph masters only. The shop's stock Marshal Orange transfer film, the one flat colour the numbers and club letters are cut in and pressed onto the Rail Navy singlet, as ironway_kit_shop_note.txt and the chip on ironway_kit_swatch.jpg show; one of the two allowed fills for a glyph master. Never on a bib or the club mark.
- Iron Grey: #5B5F63 · optional: supporting previews only. Not an ink on any commissioned file: no bib, glyph master or club mark uses it, since each prints in one ink. It may appear only in supporting previews, which the brief welcomes but which cannot replace the named files.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every commissioned file uses exactly one ink. Each bib is Rail Navy on a Bib White ground and nothing else, as on ironway_bib_2025.pdf: the drawn number, club mark, club name, member name and details are all Rail Navy. club-mark.svg is Rail Navy alone on a transparent ground. Each numeral and letter master is one flat fill, Rail Navy or Marshal Orange, on a transparent ground. Marshal Orange is the kit film on the Rail Navy singlet and never prints on a bib. Iron Grey appears only in supporting previews.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, screen, halftone, transparency, gradient, shadow, outline or blend mode is applied to any colour on a bib, the club mark or a glyph, and no photograph, scan or raster image is placed on a bib: the club mark is the delivered vector, never the raster photograph of the singlet mark placed at the head of ironway_bib_2025.pdf.
- Forbidden: No second ink on any file: no Marshal Orange, Iron Grey or other colour on a bib, and no second colour, outline, shade or keyline on a glyph or the club mark, which ironway_kit_shop_note.txt allows as one flat shape only. No colour outside the four palette hexes.
- Photography: No photograph appears in any delivered file. ironway_numbering_sheet.jpg, ironway_singlet_mark.jpg and ironway_kit_swatch.jpg are sources to trace or judge against: the drafting paper, printed grid, jersey, knit texture and fold shadow never carry into a glyph or the club mark. Glyph strokes and counters are judged in Marshal Orange on the navy of ironway_kit_swatch.jpg, never on white.

## Typography
Display face: Antwerp Bold. Text face: Benton Sans.

- 01 Display / H1: Ironway hand drawn numerals, from Del's 1994 ink sheet, 120 pt / 126 pt, Hand drawn, one weight only, tracking 0, spacing taken off the original sheet.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Antwerp Bold, 14 pt / 16 pt, Bold (700), caps, tracking +0.08 em.
- 03 Body: Benton Sans Regular, 10 pt / 13 pt, Regular (400), tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Benton Sans Regular, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular (400), tracking +0.04 em.

Signature move: A near nine to one size jump on every bib, the hand drawn race numeral at 120 pt sitting under a 14 pt Antwerp Bold club name, with italic reserved for the race year and nothing else.

Rules:
- Never set a race number in Antwerp. Race numerals come from Del's 1994 hand drawn ink sheet.
- Italic is reserved for the race year and nothing else.
- One ink throughout. Nothing that adds a plate or a pass, and nothing that looks like a paid entry event.
- Every bib holds the near nine to one jump, a 120 pt numeral under a 14 pt Antwerp Bold club name.
- The founder's hand is the club, so the numerals are kept faithfully rather than redrawn to taste.
- Everything must be cheap at volume on a heat transfer press and survive weather.

## Voice
Friendly, plain, volunteer to volunteer. A line the club would write: "Founders Five is on. Pin your bib, mind the puddles, we will see you at the line."

## Supplied Identity
No digital brand files exist. The numbering survives only as a phone photograph of the founder's original ink sheet on drafting paper, and the club mark only as a photograph of it screen printed on an old singlet. Nobody knows who made the mark and there is no file of either. Last year's bib survives as the printer's returned press PDF.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
