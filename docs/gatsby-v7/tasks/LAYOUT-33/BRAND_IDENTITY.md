# Continental Cup 2026

Continental Cup 2026 is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is touring competition, Western Europe and North America, and this commission covers phase 2: squad press run and partner files.

## Audience
Collectors aged sixteen to forty five who buy officially licensed sets beside other marquee cards, in grocery aisles, at newsstands and in hobby shops. They notice when a card in the pack looks like it came from somewhere else.

## Positioning
A flagship licensed pack retailing around fourteen dollars, positioned above community and league sets and below fine-art prints. It must read as a marquee cup collectible, saturated and cinematic, never as a cheap programme or a gallery piece.

## Palette
- Pitch Black: #0B0E12 · mandatory. The full-bleed main field around and below the portrait and the name band fade, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf, and the Bungee Regular figure of every squad number under its Floodlight Gold inline, as the signature move sets it.
- Floodlight Gold: #E7B24C · mandatory. The Bungee Inline core stripe of every squad number, the player name, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf, and the tournament mark reversed out of the dark field, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Always flat colour.
- Deep Field Green: #123A2A · optional accent. Not used on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Thin rules or a small band without type only. Never type, never the main field, and never laid over the pitch in a photograph.
- Steel Rule: #8A94A0 · optional type colour, set year and fine print only. The set year, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf, and any fine print, on the dark field. Never a ground or panel, the player name or the squad number.
- Card White: #F4F1EA · mandatory. The position, club and set year line on the Pitch Black field, as the position and club line on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf; may also carry the reversed mark or fine print, or form a solid band behind the squad number so its Pitch Black figure stays visible. Never the main field.
- Kit Crimson: #B4232B · optional accent. The thin separator rule between the portrait and the name band, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never type, a ground or a panel, and never painted onto a jersey in a photograph.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: The main field is Pitch Black #0B0E12, full-bleed behind the portrait, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf; no other colour is the page or image ground, except that a solid Card White #F4F1EA band may sit behind the squad number. On the field, the player name is Floodlight Gold #E7B24C, the position, club and set year line is Card White #F4F1EA with the set year allowed in Steel Rule #8A94A0, as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf, and every squad number is a Pitch Black Bungee Regular figure with a Floodlight Gold Bungee Inline on top. So that figure stays visible, not only its gold stripe, the squad number may sit over the photograph or on that solid Card White band. The tournament mark is reversed out in one solid colour, Floodlight Gold as on cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf or Card White. Kit Crimson #B4232B and Deep Field Green #123A2A are accents for thin rules and small bands without type, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. The one exception is the name band of cup26_teamset_2025_PRINTER.pdf: a Pitch Black #0B0E12 fade rising from clear to solid off the lower part of the portrait, behind the name and the position, club and set year line. No other tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied, and no other scrim or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No gradient, bevel, emboss, glow, drop shadow, foil or other metallic effect on any type, rule, panel or the mark, apart from the one Pitch Black name-band fade the opacity rule allows; gold is always flat Floodlight Gold #E7B24C. No colour outside the six palette hexes (#0B0E12, #E7B24C, #123A2A, #8A94A0, #F4F1EA and #B4232B) for type, panels, rules, the mark or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: no jersey is repainted or filled with flat Kit Crimson, no pitch is recoloured toward Deep Field Green, and no part of a portrait is tinted gold. No face or body is generated, redrawn or replaced.

## Typography
Display face: Bungee. Text face: Trade Gothic LT Condensed No. 18.

- 01 Display / H1: Bungee, 96 pt / 104 pt, Regular 400, caps, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Bungee, 40 pt / 44 pt, Regular 400, all caps, tracking -0.01 em.
- 03 Body: Trade Gothic LT Condensed No. 18, 12 pt / 17 pt, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Trade Gothic LT Condensed No. 18, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular, tracking 0.04 em.

Signature move: Every squad number is Bungee Regular in Pitch Black with Bungee Inline locked exactly on top in Floodlight Gold, so each figure carries a gold core stripe down its centre. The two layers share size, tracking and baseline, and a number is never printed without its Inline layer.

Rules:
- Never print a number without its Inline layer.
- Bungee Inline registers exactly on top of Bungee Regular: same size, same tracking, same baseline, no offset.
- The core stripe is always Floodlight Gold over a Pitch Black figure.
- Surnames and squad numbers are always all caps.
- Press sheets run the surname at 40 pt and the number at 96 pt; partner graphics run them at 84 px and 220 px. Never mix the two sets of sizes on one deliverable.
- Position, club and set year are the only items in Trade Gothic LT Condensed No. 18, at 12 pt in print and 28 px on screen.
- The look holds across the whole squad: no player is given a different scale or a different treatment.
- It must read as a marquee cup collectible, saturated and cinematic, never as a cheap programme and never as a gallery piece.

## Voice
Bold, terse, terrace-proud. Copy is short and declarative. Example line the brand would write: 'Eighteen players. One set. Every card earns its place in the sleeve.'

## Supplied Identity
There is no surviving design file or logo artwork. The tournament mark now exists only on printed things: the sole reference is a handheld photograph of a pitch-side sponsor board carrying the mark in one deep ink on a pale board. It has to be recovered as usable artwork before it can be placed at any size.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
