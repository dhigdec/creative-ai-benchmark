# Halden Greens

Halden Greens is a daily greens powder people actually keep drinking, sold by subscription to buyers who have already been let down by a supplement that promised too much. We grow by putting the product in real kitchens and letting real people talk, not by looking like an advert. That distrust of polish is the brand.

## Audience
Health literate adults 25 to 40 who are specifically wary of supplement marketing and trust a friend's recommendation over a glossy campaign.

## Positioning
Fifty nine dollars a month on subscription. Priced as a considered daily habit, above the fifteen dollar tub and below the studio shot premium brands. The money is real, so the look must never be.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Halden Green is the one full ground: the solid closing panel. On it, all type, the subscription line and the wordmark letters included, is Kitchen Cream, and so is the wordmark's 2 px rule, because a Halden Green rule cannot show on Halden Green; that is the only place the rule is not Halden Green. Hooks and captions are Kitchen Cream or Counter Charcoal and nothing else; a box behind one is solid Kitchen Cream carrying Counter Charcoal type, or solid Counter Charcoal carrying Kitchen Cream type, as the current tub sets its dark wordmark on cream. Mint Lid and Clay Accent are optional accents for small marks and dividers only, never type, a box or a panel, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the footage: any box behind a hook or caption is a solid block.
- Forbidden: No gradient, glow, drop shadow, metallic, glossy or 3D effect on any type, box, panel, rule or icon, and no countdowns, no stacked badges, no burst text, because nothing in the type may look polished enough to read as an advert. No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, boxes, panels, rules or icons.
- Photography: Footage keeps its own colour and exposure and is never tinted, filtered or graded toward Halden Green or any palette colour. ## Typography
Display face: Figtree. Text face: Figtree.

- 01 Display / H1 · hook, lowercase: Figtree Regular, 62 px / 76 px, Regular, tracking minus 0.010 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · subscription line, and the wordmark lockup: Figtree SemiBold, wordmark Figtree Regular capitals, 46 px / 56 px, SemiBold · wordmark Regular, tracking 0 · wordmark plus 0.180 em.
- 03 Body · caption: Figtree Regular, 40 px / 58 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print · compliance line: Figtree Regular, 26 px / 34 px, Regular, tracking plus 0.030 em.

Signature move: The capitals ban. HALDEN GREENS is the only text in the whole system ever set in capitals, Figtree Regular tracked plus 0.180 em above a 2 px Halden Green rule exactly as wide as the word GREENS, while every hook, caption and compliance line stays lowercase, with no capital even opening a sentence, so a viewer reads typing rather than advertising.

Rules:
- Never set a hook in capitals.
- Only the wordmark HALDEN GREENS ever sets in capitals; nothing else does, not even the first word of a sentence and not a proper noun in a caption.
- The wordmark tracks plus 0.180 em and sits on a 2 px Halden Green rule exactly the width of the word GREENS, never wider and never narrower.
- One family throughout, Figtree; only the subscription line lifts to SemiBold, and hooks stay Regular.
- Claims live inside the category compliance sheet, and anything the sheet forbids does not run, however good the line.
- The tub prints 11 pt on 15 pt.
- Nothing in the type may look polished enough to read as an advert.

## Voice
Conversational, honest to a fault, a little self aware, never hype. Example: "We are not going to tell you it changed our life. It is greens. You drink them in the morning."
