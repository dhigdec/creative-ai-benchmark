# SB3-015-PHO | Summit & Sable - Recovery-gear launch campaign

Recovery-gear image system with a controlled Ridge Blue colourway

We need Summit & Sable's field and bench photographs to read as one recovery-gear range. There are twenty supplied captures, with webbing, stitching and hardware that need to remain convincing at close range.

Deliverables
1. Recovery-gear launch poster: 1 named export. The actual Summit & Sable equipment is shown. The poster's headline is our tagline, word for word, the poster carries the Summit & Sable mark, and the poster makes no safety claim that summit_range.csv does not support.
2. Recovery equipment product-page module: 9 named exports. The component geometry matches the named equipment, and the module for any item that summit_range.csv lists in Ridge Blue also shows it in Ridge Blue. Each module gives the item's name, certified load, hardware and price, copied from its row in summit_range.csv, with the certified load exactly as written there.
3. Recovery-gear dealer sheet: 1 named export. The range is clearly identified: page 1 carries the Summit & Sable mark, and the sheet names every item in summit_range.csv. Specifications are readable at print size, and for each item the sheet gives its certified load, hardware, finish, warranty, price and colourways from summit_range.csv.
