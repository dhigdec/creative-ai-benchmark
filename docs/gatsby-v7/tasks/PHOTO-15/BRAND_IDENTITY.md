# Summit & Sable

We make recovery straps, soft shackles and overlanding kit for people with a lifted truck and a real budget. Nine SKUs from 35 to 220 dollars. Our buyer is not paying for a name, he is paying because the gear looks like it will hold when he is stuck at dusk on a fire road. The detail is the whole argument: the stitching and the hardware finish are what separate us from the cheap import sitting next to us in search results.

## Audience
Off-road and 4x4 owners aged 25 to 50, buying on rugged credibility and price. They trust flat daylight and visible workmanship and they distrust anything that looks staged or expensive.

## Positioning
Value band, 35 to 220 dollars. The look is bright, hard-wearing and legible, honest daylight on the plain-ground shots and unstyled dust on the field shots. Never premium-expedition cinematic, never discount-banner loud. Either extreme reads as pretending and loses the buyer.

## Palette
- Sable Charcoal: #26282B · mandatory. The storefront ground: the ground of every product-page module, and a ground or panel on the poster and dealer sheet. All type set on Bone White, and the flat ink of the Summit & Sable mark on Bone White.
- Trail Sand: #C9B79A · optional accent. The colourway swatch for a Trail Sand item, hairline rules and small labels on Sable Charcoal. Never a ground, a panel or body text.
- Recovery Orange: #D95E28 · mandatory. The solid band behind every headline, bleeding off both edges, the colourway swatch for a Recovery Orange item, and hairline rules. Never type, and never a ground beyond the headline band.
- Bone White: #F1EEE8 · mandatory. Every headline, knocked out of the Recovery Orange band; all type on Sable Charcoal; the light ground or panel on the poster and dealer sheet; the mark reversed out of Sable Charcoal.
- Ridge Blue: #2F5D8A · optional accent. The colourway swatch or a hairline flagging the new Ridge Blue colourway. Never type, a ground or a panel. In photographs it is the colour of the new webbing, matched to summit_ridge_blue_spool.jpg.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Sable Charcoal or Bone White and nothing else, apart from the solid Recovery Orange band behind every headline and small colourway swatches. Every product-page module sits on Sable Charcoal, the storefront ground. On Sable Charcoal all type is Bone White, apart from small Trail Sand labels; on Bone White all type is Sable Charcoal; headlines are Bone White on the Recovery Orange band. The Summit & Sable mark is one flat colour, Sable Charcoal on Bone White or Bone White on Sable Charcoal. A colourway swatch is the palette colour of the colourway it names. Trail Sand and Ridge Blue are accents for colourway swatches and hairline rules, and Trail Sand also for small labels, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a headline band or text panel sits over or beside the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No metallic, chrome or shine effect and no gradient or glow on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, since the brand puts durability over shine; no discount-banner device such as a price burst or sale flash, since the look is never discount-banner loud; and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette, with the one exception the brief requires: an item that summit_range.csv lists in Ridge Blue is also shown in Ridge Blue, matched to summit_ridge_blue_spool.jpg. The anodised parts in summit_bench_packshots_03.jpg, summit_bench_packshots_04.jpg and summit_bench_packshots_07.jpg are not pushed toward Ridge Blue.

## Typography
Display face: Bodoni Moda. Text face: National 2.

- 01 Display / H1 · headline: Bodoni Moda Bold, 150 pt / 158 pt, Bold, tracking 0.08 em, all capitals.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · rating callout: Bodoni Moda Bold, 85 pt / 92 pt, Bold, tracking 0.08 em, all capitals.
- 03 Body · range table and copy: National 2 Regular, 12 pt / 16 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · certified load, price, colourway: National 2 Regular, 8 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0.04 em.

Signature move: Every headline is knocked out in Bone White from a solid Recovery Orange band bleeding off both edges, set in Bodoni Moda Bold capitals at 0.08 em, so the type never sits on open ground.

Rules:
- Never set a certified load in Bodoni Moda.
- No headline sits on open ground: every headline knocks out in Bone White from a solid Recovery Orange band that bleeds off both edges.
- Bodoni Moda Bold is capitals only, tracked 0.08 em.
- National 2 Regular sets body and the range table, with tabular figures.
- Headlines run 150 pt on the A2 poster and 60 px on the 1440 by 1800 px modules, rating callouts 34 px on the modules.
- Certified working loads are stated as certified and never rounded or dressed up.

## Voice
Blunt and plain. We say what the gear does and what it is rated for. Certified working loads are stated as certified and never rounded or dressed up.
