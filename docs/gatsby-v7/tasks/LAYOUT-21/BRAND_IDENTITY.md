# Harborline Realty

Harborline is a small owner run brokerage that lists the whole mid coast book, a three hundred and twenty thousand dollar starter one week and a waterfront estate the next. It runs on repeat referrals and reputation in town, and prints a fresh listing sheet for every active property each Monday. Dana Marlow built it to feel like the place it sells, not a national franchise.

## Audience
Two buyers in one weekly run: estate buyers over fifty with liquid capital who read restraint as credibility, and first time buyers under thirty five who need price, bed count and open house time to land at a glance.

## Positioning
One brokerage spanning a 320 thousand dollar starter to a 4.5 million dollar point estate; the sheet has to flex between the two without either register looking wrong. Weekly production retainer, roughly 700 to 1,600 dollars a month.

## Palette
- Cream (window mark): #F4ECDD · mandatory. The sheet ground, as on listingsheet_2025_press.pdf, and the only ground or panel colour. It is the cream the mark is painted in on the office window (officesign_photo.jpg); on the Cream sheet it is never a type or mark colour.
- Deep Harbour Navy: #12324A · mandatory. The headline, the description and the price, as on listingsheet_2025_press.pdf. It may also colour the mark. Never a ground or panel.
- Weathered Rope Tan: #B48A54 · optional accent. The mark only, as one of its three permitted colours. Never a rule or hairline, since the type system allows no rule but the price's, and never a ground, panel, label or line of record text.
- Sea Slate: #5C6E74 · mandatory. The address, the beds, baths and square feet strip, the agent line and the reference code, as on listingsheet_2025_press.pdf. It may also colour the mark. Never a ground, panel or rule.
- Buoy Red: #A6392E · mandatory. Only the 2 pt rule directly under the price, cut to the price figure's exact width, as the signature move sets it. Never type, the price itself, a ground, a panel, the mark or any other rule.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Cream is the ground and the only ground or panel colour, as on listingsheet_2025_press.pdf. On Cream, the headline, description and price are Deep Harbour Navy, and the address, beds, baths and square feet strip, agent line and reference code are Sea Slate, as on listingsheet_2025_press.pdf. Buoy Red is only the 2 pt price rule. The mark is one solid colour: Deep Harbour Navy, Sea Slate or Weathered Rope Tan, and Weathered Rope Tan is used nowhere but the mark. Buoy Red and Weathered Rope Tan together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, the mark is never a ghosted or transparent watermark, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark. No rule or hairline of any colour except the Buoy Red price rule. No red type, red price, red panel or red ground: principal_note.txt names a loud, red franchise storefront as what Harborline is not.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own natural colour and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette. Correcting a frame's tone or cast is allowed; pushing a photograph toward Deep Harbour Navy, Sea Slate, Weathered Rope Tan, Cream or Buoy Red is not.

## Typography
Display face: Bradford. Text face: Söhne.

- 01 Display / H1: Bradford Regular, 46 pt / 50 pt, Regular, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Bradford Regular, 24 pt / 27 pt, Regular, tracking -0.005 em.
- 03 Body: Söhne Buch, 10 pt / 14 pt, Buch, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Söhne Buch, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Buch, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: The price rule: a 2 pt Buoy Red line sits directly beneath the step 01 price, cut to that figure's exact width, so it lengthens for a waterfront estate and shortens for a starter. It is the only rule anywhere in the system, and prices are always set in Bradford tabular lining figures.

Rules:
- Never rule anything but the price. The 2 pt Buoy Red rule is the only rule in the system.
- The price rule is cut to the exact width of the price figure, never to the column or the measure.
- Prices are always set in Bradford tabular lining figures.
- Headlines and asking prices take Bradford; the spec strip, body and agent line take Söhne Buch.
- One office look across every listing, whoever shot the photograph.

## Voice
Plain, grounded, mid coast. We describe the house, not ourselves, and never oversell. Example line the brand would write: 'Corner lot, three beds, a short walk to the harbour. Open Sunday, one to three.'

## Supplied Identity
The original logo artwork was lost years ago. The only surviving version of the mark, a rope ring around a lighthouse with the letters H and R, is the one painted in cream on the office window. There are no brand guidelines or type specimen; the retired printer's last press file is the sole reference for the listing sheet.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
