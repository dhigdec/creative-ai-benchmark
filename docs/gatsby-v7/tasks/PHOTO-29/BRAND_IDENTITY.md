# Cape Marren Coast Alliance

Cape Marren Coast Alliance markets a stretch of independent coast to shoulder season visitors. Summer sells itself; the work is October to April, when a harbour seafood room, a cider barn, a point light, a bookshop, a bakery and an oyster landing either make a living or do not. The campaign has to feel like that season honestly: warm low sun, weather, wool, wet stone, a lit window at four in the afternoon, quiet compositions with one or two people. Members have to see themselves in it, because members who cannot see themselves do not renew.

## Audience
Travellers aged 30 to 60, driving from within four hours, mostly couples and pairs of friends rather than families. They spend 200 to 600 dollars per party across a two or three night shoulder season weekend on rooms, meals, cider and a walk.

## Positioning
Mid range and real. Positioned above a peak summer holiday treatment (which would oversell blue water and hard sun) and below a 1,500 dollar luxury coastal weekend (which would go near monochrome and strand the bakery and the cider barn). Editorial rather than promotional, and never promising a season the coast cannot deliver in November.

## Palette
- Warm October Sun: #C8894B · mandatory wherever an Anton headline appears. The single 3 pt rule locked beneath every Anton headline at the headline's exact width, other hairline rules, and trail numbers, as the rule and the trail number on trailcard_2025_print.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, a headline or body text.
- Wet Stone: #5B5F5C · optional accent. Small secondary type on Wool Cream only: labels, captions and contact lines, as the trail label and contact line on trailcard_2025_print.pdf and the COAST ALLIANCE line of alliance_mark.svg. Never a ground, a panel, a headline or body text.
- Wool Cream: #E7DDC9 · mandatory. The main ground, as on trailcard_2025_print.pdf, a solid light panel, and all type set on a Deep Harbour panel.
- Deep Harbour: #2E3A3C · mandatory. Headlines, member names, body text and opening months on Wool Cream, as on trailcard_2025_print.pdf; the alliance mark's drawing and CAPE MARREN lettering; and the only dark solid panel, which then carries Wool Cream type.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds are Wool Cream, and a solid panel is Wool Cream or Deep Harbour, nothing else. On Wool Cream, headlines, member names, body text and opening months are Deep Harbour, and small labels, captions and contact lines are Deep Harbour or Wet Stone, as on trailcard_2025_print.pdf. On Deep Harbour, all type is Wool Cream. Type set directly on a photograph is Wool Cream or Deep Harbour. Warm October Sun is only for the 3 pt rule beneath each Anton headline, other hairline rules and trail numbers; Warm October Sun and Wet Stone together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on a clear area of the photograph or on a solid panel.
- Forbidden: No colour outside #C8894B, #5B5F5C, #E7DDC9 and #2E3A3C for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No summer blue or turquoise anywhere in the design: the brand rejects a peak summer holiday treatment (which would oversell blue water and hard sun).
- Photography: Photographs keep their own colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. A warm, low-light grade is allowed, but after any warm move the line between sea and sky stays visible in cape_marren_member_harbour_seafood.jpg, cape_marren_member_oyster_landing.jpg and cape_marren_member_point_light.jpg; no grey sea is pushed toward blue, no sky is replaced or invented, and no photograph is desaturated or toned closer to monochrome than its source file; the brand rejects a 1,500 dollar luxury coastal weekend (which would go near monochrome and strand the bakery and the cider barn). A frame supplied grey, as those three are, stays honestly grey.

## Typography
Display face: Anton. Text face: Atlas Grotesk.

- 01 Display / H1: Anton Regular, 180 pt / 168 pt, Regular, all caps, tracking 0, Anton is never letterspaced.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Anton Regular, 42 pt / 46 pt, Regular, all caps, tracking 0, Anton is never letterspaced.
- 03 Body: Atlas Grotesk Regular, 10.5 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Atlas Grotesk Regular, 11 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking +15 thousandths.

Signature move: Every headline is Anton caps sitting on a single Warm October Sun rule of 3 pt locked directly beneath it at the headline's exact width, so the eye catches a heavy block of type resting on one orange bar. Steps 01 and 02 both carry the rule.

Rules:
- Never letterspace Anton; tracking on steps 01 and 02 stays at 0.
- Every Anton headline carries a single 3 pt Warm October Sun rule locked directly beneath it at the headline's exact width.
- Opening months are set in Atlas Grotesk Medium tabular figures.
- Keep every member visible; the type system never favours one member business over another.
- Never promise a season the coast cannot deliver in November.
- Step 02 leading was invented because the prose states none; every family in this scale is the brand's own.

## Voice
Grounded and truthful. Warm about the place without overselling it, and specific about what a visitor will actually find when they arrive out of season.

## Supplied Identity
No written brand guidelines exist. The only brand file on hand is the alliance mark left by the previous agency, supplied as a single vector file with nothing else: no color system, no type list and no usage notes. Members submit their own photographs and object shots, and the campaign look is set by this brief rather than by any existing kit.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
