# Meridian Summit

Meridian Council runs a single invitation only summit each year for the small group that plans and pays for the electrical grid. It is deliberately intimate, closed door, and expensive, and everything a delegate touches at the venue is meant to feel like it cost what the seat cost. The summit works across three tracks, Operators who run the network, Capital who fund it, and Policy who regulate it.

## Audience
Eighteen delegates aged forty to sixty five who run regional transmission networks, sit on infrastructure funds or write utility policy, and have been to two hundred conferences already. For half the room the title is the reason they are in it.

## Positioning
Twenty four hundred dollars a seat, eighteen seats, invitation only. The register must read heavy, quiet and photo forward; anything bright or colour blocked reads as a name sticker at this price. Comparable commissions run 1,600 to 3,200 dollars fixed.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are solid Deep Ink Navy and nothing else, except that Warm Stone is the plain ground behind every cut-out portrait in a badge's portrait panel, as the panel fill under the photograph in badge_2026_PRINT.pdf. On Deep Ink Navy, the delegate name and a sign's destination are Paper Off White; the organisation is Warm Stone; track labels and the summit mark are Engraved Brass; the access category and any detail line are Paper Off White or Warm Stone; an arrow is Paper Off White or Engraved Brass. Graphite is an optional accent for hairline rules and dividers only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. No type sits on a light ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. The check-in square badge_2026_PRINT.pdf draws in #3C4148 at about 35% tint over the navy (#27303A) is not carried over.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, icons, arrows or the mark, including the #27303A square and #333D47 border badge_2026_PRINT.pdf draws. Nothing bright and no colour block anywhere in the design, no block or band behind a track label, and no metallic, foil, gradient, reflection or shine effect on Engraved Brass or any other colour.
- Photography: Portraits keep their own colour, balanced to the one neutral white balance the whole set shares, and each is cut out onto the plain Warm Stone ground; they are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward Deep Ink Navy, Engraved Brass or Warm Stone to make them match.

## Typography
Display face: Tiempos Fine. Text face: Founders Grotesk, with Founders Grotesk Mono for every numeral.

- 01 Display / H1: Tiempos Fine Regular, 60 pt / 62 pt, Regular, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Tiempos Fine Regular, 22 pt / 24 pt, Regular, tracking -0.005 em.
- 03 Body: Founders Grotesk Regular, Mono tabular for figures, 10 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Founders Grotesk Regular small caps, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0.12 em.

Signature move: Names are serif, numerals never are. Steps 01 and 02 are Tiempos Fine and carry nothing but names; every seat, room, time and year at step 03 is Founders Grotesk Mono tabular, and nothing in the system is set in between.

Rules:
- Never set a numeral in Tiempos, and never set a name in the sans.
- Every seat, room, time and year is Founders Grotesk Mono tabular.
- The track label is held as spacing and brass, never as a colour block.
- Names and organisations exactly as registered, nothing shortened or abbreviated.
- Nothing bright or colour blocked: at 2,400 dollars a seat the register stays heavy, quiet and photo forward.

## Voice
Spare and precise. We name who is in the room and why, and let the restraint do the talking. Example line the brand would write: 'Two days. Eighteen seats. A closed working session, not a stage.'
