# SB3-007-PHO | Gable & Grove - Home services before-and-after library

Before-and-after renovation photographs that clients can trust

Gable & Grove needs five before-and-after pairs prepared for the website and sales materials. These are evidence of our work. The after pictures should look good, but not because the before pictures have been made deliberately worse.

What we need back
- Completed project case-study panel: 4 named exports. The comparison pairs each job's before with its main after frame, not a detail or a second angle, and preserves meaningful structural differences. The project caption identifies the work actually shown with its job name, location and scope, written exactly as that job's row of projects.csv writes them.
- Estimate-pack cover: 1 named export. The cover identifies Gable & Grove with our mark, the house peak and the trees, together with the GABLE & GROVE wordmark. The selected project is the job whose scope in projects.csv names dry-stone work, shown in its main after frame, not a detail, with its job name, location and scope exactly as that row writes them, demonstrating the service without claiming an invented result.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
