# Gable & Grove Home Services

Gable & Grove takes on full exterior renovations and garden builds between roughly 45,000 and 120,000 dollars a project. Everything is sold twice, once on the website and once out of a quote pack on somebody's kitchen table, so the work has to look like exactly what the firm delivers and no more. The proof is the before and after, and the before has to stay honest.

## Audience
Households aged 45 to 65 with old houses and long time horizons, choosing between two or three bids and buying on trust rather than on price.

## Positioning
45,000 to 120,000 dollars a project. At this band the finish reads cool, calm and material: real neutral stone, deep honest blacks, one photograph doing the work, generous white space, and a mark that behaves like a maker's stamp rather than a sales badge. Never a supermarket flyer, never developer marketing.

## Palette
- Grove Green: #1E3A2F · optional accent. The season line and small accents, as on the retired press card, and the only green allowed on any deliverable. Never a ground behind type.
- Stone Neutral: #C9C3B8 · optional accent. A plate or mat behind a photograph, as under the photograph well of the retired press card, and hairline dividers. Never a type colour or a ground behind type.
- Honest Black: #17140F · mandatory. The job name, scope and all other body type on Bone, the GABLE & GROVE wordmark, and the 1.5 pt rule locked directly beneath it.
- Slate: #4A4E4C · optional accent. The location line, fine print and hairline dividers on Bone, as on the retired press card. Never a ground.
- Bone: #EDE9E1 · mandatory. The ground behind all type on every case-study panel and on the estimate-pack cover: the generous white space the brand asks for, printed as the Bone stock of the retired press card.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Bone #EDE9E1 is the only ground behind type. Type is Honest Black #17140F on Bone, with Slate #4A4E4C allowed for the location line and fine print, and Grove Green #1E3A2F for a season line or a small accent. Grove Green, Slate and Stone Neutral #C9C3B8 together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, and none of them is a ground behind type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no coloured scrim is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No green other than Grove Green #1E3A2F, and nothing brighter; no glow or drop shadow on type, panels or the mark; no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. Befores stay exactly as they came off the phone, so the warm limestone of ridge_road_before.jpg is not cooled to match ridge_road_after.jpg. Afters get no fake blue sky, no cranked-up green grass and no glow: the faint green cast of cornwallbridge_after.jpg is removed, not pushed toward Grove Green, and the pale overcast sky of ridge_road_after.jpg is kept, not replaced.

## Typography
Display face: Austin. Text face: Whitney.

- 01 Display / H1: Austin, 64 px / 68 px, Medium, tracking minus 8 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Austin, 40 px / 46 px, Medium, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: Whitney, 20 px / 30 px, Book, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Whitney, 14 px / 20 px, Book, tracking 30 thousandths.

Signature move: GABLE & GROVE is always centred in Austin Medium caps tracked 160 thousandths, with a 1.5 pt Honest Black rule locked directly beneath at exactly the wordmark width, and every project card is centred on that same vertical axis. Wide caps over a hard rule register first.

Rules:
- Never set the mark in the sans.
- GABLE & GROVE is always Austin Medium caps tracked 160 thousandths, centred, over a 1.5 pt Honest Black rule cut to exactly the wordmark width.
- Every project card is centred on that same vertical axis.
- The price is always Whitney Semibold, never the serif.
- Scope lines stay Whitney Book: 20 px on screen, 10.5 pt on 14 pt in the printed quote pack.
- Headings and the mark are Austin Medium, the Scotch face the sign writer actually painted, and no other face carries a heading.
- The before photograph stays honest: the finish reads as ordered restraint, never as an improved photograph.
- Never a supermarket flyer and never developer marketing: one photograph doing the work, generous white space.

## Voice
Plain and confident, the voice of a builder who does not oversell. Example line the brand would write: "Full exterior renovation, Ridge Road. Limestone pergola, dry stone terrace, planting."

## Supplied Identity
The mark is a problem the firm names openly: the sign writer who drew it is gone, and all that survives is a picture file he emailed once and a photograph of the mark hand painted on the firm's truck door. The project cards survive only as the retired printer's press PDF. Yard signs print in the deep Grove Green.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
