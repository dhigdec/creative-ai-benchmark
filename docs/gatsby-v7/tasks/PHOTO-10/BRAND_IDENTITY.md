# Northgrove

Northgrove is an apparel label based in Burlington, Vermont, making elevated everyday basics. Customers shop by garment, colour and fabric, so the identity needs to make those differences easy to understand and trust.

## Audience
Women and men aged 22 to 38 choosing on a phone, cross shopping against a 9 dollar fast fashion tee and a 140 dollar heritage one, buying a 32 to 60 dollar piece they expect to keep.

## Positioning
Elevated basics at 32 to 60 dollars. We sit above fast fashion and below heritage. The whole proposition is that the cloth is better than the price suggests, so nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.

## Palette
- Charcoal: #2E2E2C · mandatory. All type, rules and the NORTHGROVE wordmark on Off White, Oat or Warm Grey Paper, as the Charcoal text and rules on northgrove_colourcard.pdf.
- Warm Grey Paper: #C9C4BA · optional. The warm grey seamless the six garments were photographed on: it may stay inside a photograph, fill a frame behind one (the swatch window on northgrove_colourcard.pdf) or be a secondary panel under Charcoal type on the Off White ground. Not the ground of a product-page module or carousel page.
- Olive: #6B7248 · mandatory in the mark's leaf on light grounds; otherwise optional accent. The olive leaf of the Northgrove mark, and small labels such as the season line on northgrove_colourcard.pdf. Never a ground, panel or body text.
- Oat: #E4DCCB · optional. A secondary panel or band on the Off White ground that carries Charcoal type. Not the ground of a product-page module or carousel page.
- Club Navy: #1B2A44 · optional, only for supporters' crew content. A ground only behind the supporters' crew or its line “Made with Rowanfield Athletic.”, since northgrove_placements.txt ties navy to the club. Everything on it, type and the whole reversed mark, is Off White.
- Off White: #F5F2EC · mandatory. The ground of every product-page module (northgrove_placements.txt) and of every carousel page, as the stock of northgrove_colourcard.pdf, apart from Club Navy behind supporters' crew content; also all type and the reversed mark on Club Navy.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds: Off White #F5F2EC is the ground of every product-page module and of every carousel page, as the stock of northgrove_colourcard.pdf; Oat #E4DCCB and Warm Grey Paper #C9C4BA may be secondary panels or bands on it; Club Navy #1B2A44 is a ground only behind supporters' crew content. Type: Charcoal #2E2E2C on Off White, Oat and Warm Grey Paper, as on northgrove_colourcard.pdf; Off White on Club Navy. Olive #6B7248 is the accent for the mark's leaf and small labels only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, panel or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: any text panel is a solid block beside or over the photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes (#2E2E2C, #C9C4BA, #6B7248, #E4DCCB, #1B2A44 and #F5F2EC) for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000 included; no gold, foil, metallic or gloss effect, since nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.
- Photography: Photographs keep the colour each garment was photographed in, apart from neutralising the shoot's light, and are never recoloured toward a palette hex or another colourway; no recoloured colourway variant is shown as a product. The tee in northgrove_style_02_heavy_tee.jpg is not pushed to the palette's Olive #6B7248, the crew in northgrove_style_01_loopback_crew.jpg and the henley in northgrove_style_04_henley.jpg are not pushed to the palette's Oat #E4DCCB, and the raglan in northgrove_style_03_raglan.jpg is not pushed to Charcoal #2E2E2C. Palette rules govern the design, not the pixels of a photograph.

## Typography
Display face: GT Super Display. Text face: Freight Sans Pro.

- 01 Display / H1: GT Super Display, 96 px / 102 px, Bold, tracking minus 20 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: GT Super Display, 56 px / 62 px, Bold, tracking minus 10 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Freight Sans Pro, 20 px / 32 px, Book, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Freight Sans Pro, 14 px / 20 px, Book, tracking 40 thousandths.

Signature move: NORTHGROVE is always all caps GT Super Display Bold with tracking closed to minus 20 thousandths, so the letters almost touch and read as one solid charcoal block, with the olive leaf locked at cap height one leaf width to its left. That airless word registers first.

Rules:
- Never letterspace the wordmark open.
- NORTHGROVE is always all caps GT Super Display Bold with tracking closed to minus 20 thousandths, the letters almost touching as one solid charcoal block.
- The olive leaf is locked at cap height, one leaf width to the left of the wordmark.
- The price is always Freight Sans Pro Medium at 24 px, never the display face.
- Cloth notes and colourway names stay Freight Sans Pro Book at 20 px on 32 px.
- Hold the jump of near five to one between the 96 px carousel headline and the 20 px body.
- Nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.
- Name the cloth and the cut and leave the adjectives out.

## Voice
Plain, specific, a little dry. We name the cloth and the cut and leave the adjectives out. Example line the brand would write: 'Loopback cotton, dyed in small lots, made to soften with wear.'

## Supplied Identity
The olive-leaf glyph and charcoal wordmark originally survived only in a picture from an old lookbook. Transparent and reversed SVG versions are now supplied alongside that reference for this commission. Keep the supplied mark and use the current type system for the surrounding text.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
