# Voltcast

Voltcast is an independent consumer-tech review channel run by two people out of Portland. We buy or borrow the gear, run it hard, and say what we actually think. Episodes go out long-form to the main feed plus vertical and square shorts. About a third of the revenue is sponsor money, so the grid has to look like one channel and the sponsor read has to be exactly right.

## Audience
Enthusiast buyers and everyday shoppers who want an honest verdict before they spend. They follow us because we are not the manufacturer, and they can tell the difference.

## Positioning
Mid-tier creator economy. A sponsor integration is worth a few thousand dollars and renewal depends on how the segment looks. The channel has to read as a credible independent reviewer, never as broadcast gloss and never as raw phone-shot filler.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Card Charcoal, with Bench Grey for secondary panels and Panel White for any light panel; Muted Steel is never a ground or panel. Volt Orange is never a ground or panel on either film; on the cover it may be one solid ground behind the photograph, and is otherwise never a ground or panel there either. The verdict is Volt Orange on a solid Card Charcoal ground on both films. On Card Charcoal or Bench Grey, all other type is Panel White, with Muted Steel for secondary labels and units, as on voltcast_e42_select_09.jpg. On Panel White, and on the cover's optional Volt Orange ground, type is Card Charcoal. Volt Orange is the only accent: apart from the verdict word and the cover's optional ground, it appears only as thin accent rules and small highlights, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, on every held frame. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, any type or the channel bug, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the footage or the cover photograph. A straight cut or a plain fade between two shots is a transition, not a tint.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules or icons; the one exception is the channel bug, placed in the ink of voltcast_bug_transparent.svg or voltcast_bug_reversed.svg as supplied. No second accent colour, no glow, drop shadow, bevel or other broadcast-gloss effect on any type, panel or the bug, and no Meridian VPN logo on any output in this pack.
- Photography: The supplied footage (voltcast_e42_master.mp4, voltcast_e42_bench_02.mp4, voltcast_e42_panel_03.mp4, voltcast_e42_load_04.mp4 and voltcast_e42_carry_05.mp4) and the cover photograph keep their own colour and are never tinted, duotoned or graded toward the palette; colours inside the picture, such as a unit's orange accent stripe, are not design colours.

## Typography
Display face: PP Radio Grotesk. Text face: Inter.

- 01 Display / H1: PP Radio Grotesk, 96 px / 101 px, Bold, tracking -15 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: PP Radio Grotesk, 40 px / 46 px, Medium, tracking +80 units.
- 03 Body: Inter, 30 px / 42 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Inter, 24 px / 34 px, Regular, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: The verdict is one word only, all caps in PP Radio Grotesk Bold, tracked -15 units and optically scaled until it touches both card margins, Volt Orange on Card Charcoal, at four times the body size.

Rules:
- Never let the verdict run to two words.
- The verdict is always all caps, tracked -15 units, and optically scaled until it touches both card margins, Volt Orange on Card Charcoal: 96 px on the 1080 square, 120 px on the vertical.
- Disclosure never sets below 24 px.
- Spec figures set in Inter Medium tabular at 34 px.
- The episode label sets in PP Radio Grotesk Medium at 40 px tracked 80; no other step is tracked out.
- Sponsor copy runs exactly as written and the disclosure line is exact; neither is rewritten to fit a line.
- Print sheet sets at 9 pt.

## Voice
Direct, dry, and unhyped. We talk like a person who has actually used the thing. Sponsor copy runs as written and the disclosure line is exact. We never oversell.

## Supplied Identity
The channel bug exists only as a flat export on white and we have no editable brand kit. On-screen cards get rebuilt from scratch every episode, which is the problem we want solved. We need the bug prepared as reusable artwork and the card set delivered as a file we can reopen and change each week.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
