# Evergreen Provisions

Evergreen Provisions runs two dispensary shops in Bend, built around six house strain families. Two years ago the owners paid a local sign painter to hand-paint a crest panel for each family onto the shop walls. Customers photograph them constantly and ask where they can buy them, and the first apparel capsule exists to answer that question without losing the hand that made the panels.

## Audience
Craft-minded dispensary customers aged 25 to 45 who buy at the counter on the strength of the wall panels they already photograph. People who pay for the painted origin, not for a logo.

## Positioning
Tees at 32 dollars, hoodies at 70 dollars, short-run and counter-sold. Elevated streetwear priced above gas-station merch and below a designer drop. The painted texture is the product, so the value sits in the brush surviving, not in gloss or foil.

- Sage Field: #7C8A5F · optional accent: a crest ink and presentation ink swatch only. A crest ink in the artwork, and an ink swatch in the presentation. Never a ground, panel, rule or type colour.

- Bench Black: #1A1714 · mandatory where type sits on Bone Primer. Plate numbers, spec copy and body text on Bone Primer, as the wordmark and spec lines of evergreen_spec_card_2024.pdf; it may also set capsule titles, labels and captions, and it is a crest ink. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Crest artwork has no ground: its background stays transparent, and every ink is a shelf ink from evergreen_ink_book.txt, at its palette hex where it has one; Harvest Gold, the ink book's warm ochre, has no palette hex and is used only in crest artwork and ink swatches. In the presentation, grounds and panels are Bone Primer only, as on evergreen_spec_card_2024.pdf. On Bone Primer, plate numbers, spec copy and body text are Bench Black, as the card's wordmark and spec lines; capsule titles, labels, captions and rules are Bench Black or Cedar Bark, as the card's subtitle, labels, rules and footer. Sage Field, Aubergine Deep and Harvest Gold appear in the presentation only inside crest artwork or as ink swatches, never as a ground, panel, rule or type, and the swatches together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, in the crest artwork and in the presentation. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, filter or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and dry-brush breakup is drawn as open gaps in solid shapes, never as transparency. No scrim, gradient or tinted overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph; a crest placed on a garment photograph in a mockup sits solid, at full opacity, with no blend mode.
- Forbidden: No neon, fluorescent, foil, gloss or metallic effect on any ink, type, panel, rule or icon, as evergreen_ink_book.txt and the brand's price positioning rule out. No colour outside the five palette hexes (#3B2B3A, #6B4A2E, #7C8A5F, #E7E0D0 and #1A1714) for type, grounds, panels, rules or icons, so the white proof mount of evergreen_spec_card_2024.pdf is not carried forward. No ink off evergreen_ink_book.txt in crest artwork.
- Photography: Garment and panel photographs keep their own true colour once corrected and are never recoloured toward the palette.

## Typography
Display face: Knockout HTF31 Junior Middleweight. Text face: Founders Grotesk.

- 01 Display / H1: Knockout HTF31 Junior Middleweight, 40 pt / 42 pt, Junior Middleweight, tracking 0 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Knockout HTF31 Junior Middleweight, 20 pt / 22 pt, Junior Middleweight, tracking 30 units.
- 03 Body: Founders Grotesk Regular, 9 pt / 12.5 pt, Regular, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Founders Grotesk Medium, 7.5 pt / 10 pt, Medium, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: Every capsule title is Knockout HTF31 Junior Middleweight caps at 20 pt tracked 30 units carrying its plate number directly above in the same face at 40 pt on the same left edge, so each spread opens with a large figure sitting over a small tight title. Steps 01 and 02 are that lockup, read top to bottom.

Rules:
- Never set a painted crest word in Knockout or any other font. The crests are painted artwork and stay painted.
- The plate number and the capsule title are one lockup: number at 40 pt directly above title at 20 pt, both flush to the same left edge.
- Capsule titles are always caps tracked 30 units. The plate figure is never tracked.
- Ink tables and spec copy always use tabular lining figures so ink counts and run quantities align.
- Screen titles set at 28 px. No other screen title size exists.
- Hold the palette to the inks the decorator already stocks, so type prints in the same pass as the panel art.
- No luxury posturing and no carnival hype in the copy that fills the scale. Warm, plain, shop floor honest.

## Voice
Warm, plain-spoken, shop-floor honest. No luxury posturing and no carnival hype. Example line: "These are hand painted panels and that is the entire reason anyone wants them. Keep the brush in it, and tell us plainly what came back and what did not."

## Supplied Identity
The six crest panels were hand-painted by a local sign painter who has since moved to Montana and left no sketches or files. The only surviving artwork is a set of bench photographs of the finished panels, shot over two visits under mixed light. There is no vector art and no separation art, which is exactly why the screen printer cannot quote a run.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
