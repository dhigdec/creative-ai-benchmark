# Ridgeway Rovers FC

Ridgeway Rovers are a village football club that has worn the same crest since 1974 and drawn their own shirt numbering since the nineties. This season brings the first new shirt since 2019. The club wants its own crest and its own hand drawn numbering back as proper artwork, so any supplier can print it and it still looks like Ridgeway.

## Audience
Players and parents at twenty eight to forty five dollars a shirt, often buying a child's fourth kit in five years, who want something that looks like a proper club.

## Positioning
Grassroots, twenty eight to forty five dollars a shirt printed by one local supplier. Numbering has to reduce to a single robust cuttable colour for heat press and flock, honest rather than pro tier.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels on the kit sheet are Shirt White, with Navy allowed for a header band, as on ridgeway_shirtback_2024.pdf; garments keep their own kit colour. On Shirt White, headings, body and fine print are Navy, Claret sets at most a short caption, and every 30 pt heading carries the 2 pt Claret rule. On Navy or Claret, type is Shirt White, or Old Gold for a short label. On the page design, outside the crests, glyphs and garments, Old Gold is for short labels and thin rules and Pitch Green for thin rules only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area outside the crests and glyphs, never a ground or body text. The crests, the glyphs and the shirt-back name and number use only Claret, Navy, Old Gold and Shirt White: the master crest Navy, Old Gold and Shirt White as stitched in ridgeway_crest_shirt.jpg, with the claret cloth left unprinted; the reversed crest no more than three of them; the single-ink crest one; and each glyph, name or number one.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour in the crests, the glyphs or the kit sheet, and no scrim, gradient or translucent band of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a caption sits beside or below the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No gradient, inline, keyline, outline or second colour on any shirt number, shirt-back name or glyph, as ridgeway_supplier_note.txt rules. Pitch Green never appears on a crest, a glyph, a shirt-back name or number, or a garment. No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules or icons on the kit sheet, and none outside the four kit colours on the crests and glyphs.
- Photography: Photographs appear only as garment mock-ups on the kit sheet. Any shirt photograph shown there reads as its kit colour, the home shirt Claret #6B1F2E and the away shirt Navy #1E2A44, and is not otherwise recoloured.

## Typography
Display face: Champion Gothic Heavyweight. Text face: Freight Sans Pro.

- 01 Display / H1: Champion Gothic Heavyweight, 30 pt / 32 pt, Heavyweight, tracking 0 units, width set by optical fit.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Champion Gothic Heavyweight, 18 pt / 20 pt, Heavyweight, tracking +10 units.
- 03 Body: Freight Sans Pro Book, 9.5 pt / 13 pt, Book, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Freight Sans Pro Medium, 7.5 pt / 10 pt, Medium, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: Every sheet is headed by one line of Champion Gothic Heavyweight caps at 30 pt on 32 pt, optically fitted to fill the column edge to edge, with a 2 pt Claret rule locked directly beneath it. Step 01 carries that behaviour and nothing else in the scale is allowed to imitate it.

Rules:
- Never set a shirt number or the crest ring in any typeface. Both are drawn artwork.
- Every heading is caps and is optically fitted to fill its column edge to edge. Width comes from the optical fit, never from added tracking.
- A 2 pt Claret rule locks directly beneath every heading, on every sheet.
- Headings set at 30 pt in print and 44 px on screen. No other heading size exists.
- Only step 02 may use the display face without the Claret rule beneath it.
- Numbering artwork must still reduce to a single robust cuttable colour for heat press and flock, so type set beside it stays one colour too.

## Voice
Straightforward, warm, club room plain. Example line: Same crest we have worn since 1974, cleaned up so any printer can do it justice.

## Supplied Identity
No artwork survives. The former kit secretary took the crest files when he moved away, leaving only a parent's phone photo of the crest stitched on last season's home shirt. The numbering exists only as two marker on graph paper sheets, an alphabet and a digit set, drawn by a founding member in the nineties and photographed on a kitchen table. Last season's supplier back of shirt sheet is on hand as the format.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
