# SB3-037-VEC | Wharfside Folk Festival - Restored festival identity

Bring our painted festival wordmark into a usable anniversary kit

Wharfside Folk Festival is twenty years old. The shed-painted wordmark is part of that history, and we want to retain its character across the handbill, cloth and supporting anniversary materials.

What we need back
- Recovered festival mark: 1 named export. The source lettering is preserved.
- Festival poster: 1 named export. The billing order follows the approved lineup in wharfside_acts.csv, night by night in the file's order: each night's last act is billed as that night's headline act, and its other acts follow as the supporting bill in the order the file lists them. The event date is readable.
- Festival handbill: 1 named export. The venue details name the harbour town the festival is held in and are readable at print size. The ticket action gives the ticket prices and is visible.
- Festival shirt artwork: 1 named export. The ink limits follow the supplier note. The mark remains recognisable on the garment.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
