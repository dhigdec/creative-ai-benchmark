# Aurelian Skin

Aurelian Skin is a small clinical skincare line built on eight actives-led products at thirty four to sixty eight dollars. In March we open our own online store and enter a specialty chain's twelve doors, so everything a customer sees changes at once. The line is meant to read like a laboratory with good taste: cool, quiet, evidence first, generous with white space and free of decoration. Not a spa, not a discount aisle. Our customer reads the ingredient list before the copy, and the brand is built to reward that: honest claims, clear INCI, restraint in place of ritual.

## Audience
Women roughly twenty six to forty two, ingredient literate, who research formulations and trust plainness over promise. They shop web stores and specialty beauty floors and judge a product by its panel.

## Positioning
Prestige-mass at thirty four to sixty eight dollars per SKU. Above the mass aisle, below luxury conserve pricing. The middle is the position, and the look must not drift warm and cheap or dark and precious.

## Palette
- Clinical White: #F4F6F7 · mandatory. The ground of every designed surface: the product-page modules, the key visual's type area and every carousel page, and the white space the brand is generous with.
- Graphite: #232729 · mandatory. Type (product names, claims, body, INCI and prices) and the 0.5 pt hairline under every product name. Never a ground or a panel.
- Cool Mineral Grey: #9BA5AA · optional accent. Keylines, such as the keyline around an INCI panel, and small labels such as the INCI heading. Never a ground, never body text.
- Signal Blue: #3E6076 · optional accent. Thin rules and dividers only. Never the hairline under a product name, which is Graphite, never a ground, never body text.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every flat, non-photographic ground and panel is Clinical White. All type is Graphite, set on Clinical White or on a photograph's own pale sweep, apart from a small label such as the INCI heading, which may be Cool Mineral Grey. Cool Mineral Grey and Signal Blue are accents for keylines, rules and small labels, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground and never body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, drop shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph; type sits on Clinical White or on the photograph's own pale sweep.
- Forbidden: No gold, bronze, silver or other metallic or gloss effect, no warm tint and no dark or near-black ground in the designed areas, since the look must not drift warm and cheap or dark and precious, and no colour outside the four palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own true, corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The amber glass of aurelian_launch_shoot_01.jpg and aurelian_launch_shoot_04.jpg and the golden retinol drop and black ground of aurelian_actives_macro_04.jpg stay as shot; the no-gold and no-dark-ground rules govern designed elements only. The cool cast of aurelian_launch_shoot_02.jpg and aurelian_launch_shoot_05.jpg is corrected to neutral, never kept or pushed toward Signal Blue.

## Typography
Display face: Signifier Light. Text face: Söhne (Buch 400 and Mono 400).

- 01 Display / H1: Signifier Light, 44 px / 48 px, Light, tracking +340 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Signifier Light, 24 px / 28 px, Light, tracking -6 units.
- 03 Body: Söhne Buch, 15 px / 22 px, Buch 400, tracking 0 units.
- 04 Fine print: Söhne Mono, 11 px / 16 px, Mono 400, tracking +12 units.

Signature move: Product names are set in Signifier Light capitals tracked to 340 units and ruled beneath with a 0.5 pt Graphite hairline measured to the exact width of the name, nothing wider.

Rules:
- Never rebuild the wordmark in a sans.
- Product names are always Signifier Light capitals tracked to 340 units, never another face and never another tracking value.
- The Graphite hairline under a product name is 0.5 pt and runs to the exact measure of that name, never wider.
- Percentages, INCI and prices sit in Söhne Mono 400 with tabular lining figures, never in the serif and never in Söhne Buch.
- Body copy is Söhne Buch 400 only.
- The product benefit stays visually subordinate to the product name.
- State what a product does and claim nothing stronger than the cleared claim.

## Voice
Measured, factual, unhurried. States what a product does and does not claim. Reads like a well-run lab that respects the reader, never breathless and never coy.
