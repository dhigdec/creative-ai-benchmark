# SB3-001-PHO | Aurelian Skin - Ecommerce skincare image system

Make eight skincare products look like one launch, not eight shoots

Our launch range needs to look as considered online as it does on the shelf. At the moment the eight bottles look as though they belong to different brands.

What we need back
- Skincare product-page module: 8 named exports. The photograph shows the product named on this module. The bottle label remains legible at the delivered size. Each module carries that product's name, cleared claim, price and full INCI, and the benefit is visually subordinate to the product name.
- Skincare range launch key visual: 1 named export. The composition names Aurelian Skin and carries its brand mark, recovered as artwork from the photographed carton. The launch line is our brand tagline, reproduced word for word; any product claim shown is that product's cleared claim, and nothing stronger. The product range, all eight launch products shown together, is the primary visual focus.
- Retailer launch carousel: 5 named exports. The opening page names the range, Aurelian Skin, and carries its brand mark recovered from the photographed carton. The closing page ends on one plain shopping action that sends the reader to buy the range, in our measured voice, with no urgency, discount or price-promotion wording. The product sequence has a clear narrative.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. A product is named by its retail name alone: an internal warehouse note that travels with a name in our files stays off every customer-facing image. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
