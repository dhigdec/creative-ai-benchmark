# Halden and Roe

Halden and Roe is a maker led stoneware house where every piece in the Marlow range is thrown and glazed by hand in the back room. It sells to buyers who order from a printed book rather than a website, one piece to a page, the way it always has. The glazes are the whole product, so the book has to let a reactive oatmeal, a gloss celadon and a matte slate tell themselves apart.

## Audience
Independent homeware shop owners and interior stylists aged thirty to fifty five, who buy around forty lines a season across a dozen makers and can tell a thrown piece from a cast one across a room.

## Positioning
Trade 38 to 410 dollars a piece, retailing 100 to 900. The book must read airy and slow, the glaze given room to be the subject and the trade price stated plainly and once; any tight grid or shouted price collapses the margin the house runs on. Comparable work runs 2,000 to 4,000 dollars fixed.

## Palette
- Oatmeal Cream: #E6DCC6 · optional ground. The page ground of the trade sheets and of the cover and contents, as the alternative to the book's own paper #EFE7D6 printed on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf. Never type, a rule or the price.
- Gloss Celadon: #8FA499 · optional accent. Hairline rules and dividers only. Never type, a ground or a panel, and never a colour target for the Gloss Celadon glaze in a photograph.
- Matte Slate: #4A4E4C · optional secondary type colour. The glaze name and any caption or description line, as the caption under the piece name on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf. Never a ground or panel, and never a colour target for the Matte Slate glaze in a photograph.
- Kraft Tan: #B79C74 · optional accent; the colour of any hairline rule between spec rows. Hairline rules between the spec rows, as on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf, and dividers. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Studio Ink: #24211C · mandatory. The piece name, the spec block and MRL code, and the trade price, as on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf, and all other type, the contents included, unless a rule allows Matte Slate. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds are warm paper and nothing else: the book's own paper #EFE7D6, as on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf, or Oatmeal Cream #E6DCC6; never pure white, behind the footer stamp included, and no panel of another colour. All type is Studio Ink #24211C, as the piece name, spec block and price on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf, except that the glaze name and any caption or description may be Matte Slate #4A4E4C, as that page's caption. Kraft Tan #B79C74 and Gloss Celadon #8FA499 are for hairline rules and dividers only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area; the spec-row rules on catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf are Kraft Tan.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#E6DCC6, #8FA499, #4A4E4C, #B79C74 and #24211C) for type, rules, panels, icons or a drawn (not photographed) footer mark, the warm paper ground #EFE7D6 of catalog_page_2026_PRINT.pdf aside; no pure white ground; no box, star, burst or colour panel behind the trade price, which the type system states plainly and once and never shouts.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The glazes are not matched to the palette colours that share their names: a Gloss Celadon glaze stays green-grey, not pushed toward #8FA499 or blue, and a Matte Slate glaze stays matte, not flattened to #4A4E4C (glaze_note.txt: celadon "must not be pushed blue"; slate "must stay matte"). No part of a piece is generated or redrawn.

## Typography
Display face: Ogg Text. Text face: Lyon Text, with Atlas Grotesk on the spec block.

- 01 Display / H1: Ogg Text Regular, 22 pt / 26 pt, Regular, tracking -0.005 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Ogg Text Italic, 14 pt / 17 pt, Italic, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: Lyon Text Regular, 10 pt / 16 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Atlas Grotesk Regular, 8 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: A single italic: the glaze name at step 02 in Ogg Text Italic, set directly under the roman product name, is the only italic anywhere in the book, so the glaze is the first word the eye finds on the page.

Rules:
- Never allow a second italic on a page. The glaze name is the only italic in the book.
- The glaze name sits directly under the roman product name.
- The trade price is stated plainly and once, at 11 pt, and never shouted.
- One piece to a page, airy, with space around it; no tight grid.
- Product and glaze names take Ogg Text; description and price take Lyon Text; the spec block and MRL code take Atlas Grotesk.
- The glaze is the product and must read true on the page.

## Voice
Plain and material. We describe the glaze and the making, not the marketing. Example line the brand would write: 'Oatmeal breaks pale over the rim and pools brown in the throwing rings. Thrown and glazed in Brattleboro.'

## Supplied Identity
The printer laid out the trade book page three seasons ago and it is the only reference we have; nobody here can open or drive that file. The maker's stamp exists only impressed into the base of the pots, with no drawing of it anywhere, so a single photograph of a bowl foot is the only source for the footer mark.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
