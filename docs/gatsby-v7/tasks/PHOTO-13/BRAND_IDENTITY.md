# Argent & Faith

Argent & Faith is a two bench silver studio. Nora cuts and pierces, her partner finishes and sets, and we put out ten considered pieces a season: open bands, a pierced medallion, a signet, a reliquary locket. We sell direct and through eleven independent shops. The negative space in a band or a piercing is the design, so how a piece is made and photographed matters as much as what it costs.

## Audience
Buyers aged 28 to 55, Northeast urban and coastal, buying one considered piece for themselves or as a milestone gift and reading the metal and dimensions before purchase.

## Positioning
Fine but accessible silver at 180 to 900 dollars a piece. Above high street silver, below designer gallery jewellery. At this tier the tile is mostly air, the piece sits small and centred, the type is quiet and provenance reads as editorial copy.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Flat grounds and panels are Warm Ivory Ground or Paper White and nothing else: every product detail tile is grounded in Warm Ivory Ground, and Paper White serves as a page or card ground on the poster and look sheet, as on provenance_card_2026_PRINT.pdf. All type and the maker's mark are Ink or Oxidised Recess: the wordmark, campaign line, piece names and the mark in Ink, the provenance line in Oxidised Recess, fine print in either. Sterling and North Window Grey are for hairline rules and dividers only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, panel or type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, no shadow or glow is added to type, rules, panels or the maker's mark, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. A piece may keep a soft, natural contact shadow on the ground beneath it, as in its source photograph.
- Forbidden: No metallic, foil, gloss, glow or glare effect on any type, rule or the maker's mark, since the metal leads and the type recedes, and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark, including the off-palette greys #3A3A3D and #6B6A66 that provenance_card_2026_PRINT.pdf uses for its specification lines and piece number.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: the silver keeps its polished reflections and is never flattened toward Sterling #C7C9CB or North Window Grey #B9BBB8, oxidised and engraved recesses stay dark, and the garnets of bench_capsule_10.jpg keep their red. The silver reads cool and true under one common light, neither warmed up nor blued out.

## Typography
Display face: Italiana. Text face: Suisse Int'l.

- 01 Display / H1 · wordmark and campaign line: Italiana Regular, 96 pt / 106 pt, Regular, tracking 0.24 em, all capitals.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · piece name: Italiana Regular, 34 pt / 38 pt, Regular, tracking 0.18 em, all capitals.
- 03 Body · provenance: Suisse Int'l Regular, 11 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · piece number, dimensions, price: Suisse Int'l Regular, 8 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0.04 em.

Signature move: The ampersand is always set at three times the cap height of ARGENT & FAITH and dropped so its bowl crosses their baseline, inside a wordmark tracked to 0.24 em.

Rules:
- Never set a price in Italiana.
- The wordmark is Italiana Regular, all capitals, tracked to 0.24 em so the word reads as a thin line of air across the page.
- The ampersand is always three times the cap height of ARGENT & FAITH, dropped so its bowl crosses their baseline.
- Suisse Int'l Regular sets all provenance and specs, with tabular figures.
- Display lines run 96 pt on the A2 poster and 44 px on the 1440 by 1800 px modules.
- The tile stays mostly air: provenance reads as quiet editorial copy and never grows to compete with the piece.

## Voice
Spare, precise, provenance led. We write in Nora's own plain words about how a piece was made. Example line the brand would write: 'Hand sawn and pierced, sterling silver, oxidised and hand finished at the bench.'

## Supplied Identity
Our maker's mark exists only as an engraving in the steel bench block Nora's teacher cut for her, and nowhere else. There is no file and no one to ask, so the mark needs recovering from a photograph as artwork that stays true to the original rather than a tidier version of it.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
