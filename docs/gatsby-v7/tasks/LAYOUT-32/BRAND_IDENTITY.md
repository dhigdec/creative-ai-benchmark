# Vanguard Motors

Vanguard holds around fourteen carefully bought cars at a time and sells to people who have already decided roughly what they want and are choosing where to buy it. The card in the windscreen is the first thing a buyer meets, before anyone walks out, so it has to answer their two questions honestly: what is this car, and what does it cost.

## Audience
Private retail buyers aged 35 to 60 who have narrowed the choice online, driven a distance to see one specific car, and are deciding whether this dealer is careful.

## Positioning
Independent prestige used. Cars from 18,000 pounds to 95,000. Photograph forward and quiet: one large honest picture of the actual car, a short spec of what differs between examples, the price plainly set and the finance line legible but secondary. Never the budget lot register, never a manufacturer brochure with no price.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Midnight fills every card and is the only ground or panel colour, as on windowcard_2025_PRINT.pdf. On Midnight, the price is Signal White and the shield is Chrome Silver, as there; the line carrying the mileage, transmission and fuel, and the finance footer with its monthly figure, are Brushed Pewter, as there; every other line of type, the stacked VANGUARD MOTORS name, the year, the model line, the spec block, the stock number and the registration, is Signal White or Chrome Silver. Vanguard Steel and Marque Red are for hairline rules, keylines and small marks only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the car photograph: the type sits on solid Midnight beneath it.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No chrome, brushed-metal, foil, gradient or shine effect: Chrome Silver and Brushed Pewter are flat colours. No colour on the price but Signal White, and no price burst, starburst, flash or sale badge, the budget lot register the type system rules out.
- Photography: Photographs are not recoloured toward the palette: they are never tinted or graded toward Midnight or any other palette colour, and a silver car is not recoloured toward Chrome Silver nor a white one toward Signal White. A highlight clipped to white stays as shot: no palette colour, texture or panel detail is painted into it.

## Typography
Display face: Righteous. Text face: Suisse Int'l.

- 01 Display / H1: Righteous, 42 pt / 46 pt, Regular 400, caps, tracking 0.18 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Righteous, 34 pt / 38 pt, Regular 400, tracking -0.01 em.
- 03 Body: Suisse Int'l, 11 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Suisse Int'l, 7.5 pt / 10 pt, Regular, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: The name always stacks on two lines, VANGUARD over MOTORS at exactly one third the size, with the tracking fitted on each line, 0.18 em on VANGUARD and 0.42 em on MOTORS, so both lines measure the same width. The two lines are never set as one.

Rules:
- Never set the two lines as one: VANGUARD always stacks over MOTORS.
- MOTORS is always exactly one third the size of VANGUARD.
- Tracking is fitted per line so both lines measure the same width: 0.18 em on VANGUARD, 0.42 em on MOTORS.
- On screen the marque sets at 64 px.
- The price is plainly set in Suisse Int'l Medium 48 pt tabular lining, never styled up beyond Medium and never coloured.
- The finance line is legible but always secondary to the price, and the broker's wording is set verbatim.
- Never the budget lot register and never a manufacturer brochure with no price: the price is always present on the card.
- One large honest photograph of the actual car leads the card; the type sits under it, quiet and plain.

## Voice
Factual, unhurried and confident, a dealer who lets the car and the spec do the talking. No exclamation, no urgency. Example: One owner, full history, the colour it says on the card and the colour it is on the forecourt.
