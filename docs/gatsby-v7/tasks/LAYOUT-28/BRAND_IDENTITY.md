# SEVE Botanicals

SEVE Botanicals makes clean, plant led beauty under one house across two very different shelves. Seve Atelier is the apothecary line, sold over a counter, where restraint and an honest ingredient line are the proof of quality. Seve Everyday is the drugstore line on a peg, where a shade has to win in four seconds. Same mark, same values, two registers.

## Audience
Two buyers under one house: a thirty to fifty five year old counter customer paying for restraint and reading the ingredient list as proof, and a sixteen to thirty five year old drugstore shopper scanning a peg for a shade at arm's length.

## Positioning
Two load bearing tiers. Seve Atelier runs 48 to 85 dollars: quiet, generous negative space, one considered accent, the mark small and confident, the swatch a single honest chip. Seve Everyday runs 6 to 14 dollars: claim forward and shade forward, the shade name and swatch winning at arm's length, the mark holding while small and busy. The same treatment cannot serve both.

- Soft Sage: #A9B79E · optional accent. Hairline rules only, such as the rule under the wordmark and the divider above the net contents on serum_label_PRESS.pdf. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Warm Gold: #C2A15A · mandatory for the wordmark. The wordmark, flat on Deep Botanical as on serum_label_PRESS.pdf (the carton wordmark has no dark version), and the hairline borders of the packshot well and swatch chip. Never a ground, panel, shade name or body text.
- Bone Card: #F6F1E7 · mandatory. The text panel that carries the shade name, ingredient line, net contents, batch line and claim, as on serum_label_PRESS.pdf; and any type other than the wordmark set on Deep Botanical.
- Charcoal Ink: #23211E · mandatory where the ingredient line sits on Bone Card. Every line of type on Bone Card other than the shade name, the ingredient line, net contents, batch line and claim included, as serum_label_PRESS.pdf sets its ingredient paragraph and net weight. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Deep Botanical or Bone Card and nothing else: Deep Botanical is the label ground and Bone Card the text panel, as on serum_label_PRESS.pdf. On Deep Botanical, the wordmark is Warm Gold and any other type is Bone Card. On Bone Card, the shade name is Deep Botanical and every other line of type, the ingredient line, net contents, batch line and claim included, is Charcoal Ink. Warm Gold is the wordmark and the hairline borders of the packshot well and swatch chip; Soft Sage is hairline rules only. Together the two cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, and neither is ever a ground, panel, shade name or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the bottle photograph or the swatch chip.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for any type, ground, panel, rule, border or icon, including the grey #4B4A44 batch line and the #1B2B23 well fill left on serum_label_PRESS.pdf. Warm Gold is a flat ink: no foil, metallic, gradient or shine effect imitates the gold foil of carton_foil.jpg on any type, rule or border.
- Photography: The bottle photograph and the swatch chips keep their own colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The polished gold collar in sample_packshot_06.jpg is photography, not design, so the no-foil rule does not reach it; each swatch chip carries its own drawdown card's shade, never tinted toward a palette colour.

## Typography
Display face: Rozha One. Text face: Inter.

- 01 Display / H1: Rozha One Regular, 11 pt over 7 pt / 10 pt, Regular, tracking +0.42 em on the upper line, 0 on the lower.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Rozha One Regular, 15 pt Atelier, 22 pt Everyday / 17 pt Atelier, 24 pt Everyday, Regular, tracking 0 Atelier, +0.01 em Everyday.
- 03 Body: Inter Semibold, 9 pt / 13 pt, Semibold, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Inter Regular, 5 pt / 6.5 pt, Regular, tracking +0.02 em.

Signature move: The wordmark always stacks on two lines, SEVE above BOTANICALS, the upper line tracked to 0.42 em so both lines lock to one identical width and the mark reads as a small square block of high contrast capitals. That is step 01 and it holds at 11 pt over 7 pt on the quiet Atelier carton and on the busy Everyday peg card alike.

Rules:
- Never set the wordmark on one line.
- The upper wordmark line is tracked to 0.42 em so SEVE and BOTANICALS lock to one identical width.
- Rozha One Regular sets the wordmark and the shade names; Inter sets ingredient lines and claims.
- Compliance copy stays exactly as written, including the 5 pt ingredient line.
- Atelier shade names set at 15 pt and Everyday shade names at 22 pt: the same treatment cannot serve both tiers.
- Ecommerce sets Rozha One at 40 px on 44 px.
- The swatch is the real shade, drawn from the real product.
- Atelier keeps generous negative space, one considered accent and the mark small and confident; Everyday stays claim forward and shade forward with the mark holding while small and busy.

## Voice
Calm, botanical and precise, never gushing. Compliance copy stays exactly as written. Example line the brand would write: "One house, two shelves, every shade drawn from the real product."

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark was drawn years ago by a designer the house has lost touch with and survives only foil blocked on an old carton, with no dark version at all. The only layout owned is last season's serum label as the flat press PDF the label printer returned. Photography is minimal: one physical sample bottle shot on a phone on kitchen marble, plus nineteen shade drawdowns done on card by the window the same afternoon.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
