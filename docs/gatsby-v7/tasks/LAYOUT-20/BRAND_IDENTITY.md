# In Memory of Walter Fernwood

This is a family memorial keepsake for Walter Fernwood, who died at ninety four. It is a book, not a photo album: plates with captions, a contents page, and the granddaughter's eulogy laid out in the middle, alongside a framed page and a small card for mourners to take home. The visual voice is set by two things: the family's wish that nothing be brightened or invented, and the funeral home pieces made for Walter's late wife in 2019, which the new work should sit beside on the shelf.

## Audience
The family and around sixty mourners aged 45 to 75, most of them reading printed pages rather than screens, many of whom remember the people in the photographs.

## Positioning
Heritage and memorial commission, 300 to 600 dollars. Neither a luxury commemorative volume with gilt and foil, nor a discount photo book with grids and script headings. What the money buys is restraint.

## Palette
- Archive Cream: #EFE9DC · mandatory. The ground of every piece: each keepsake card, the framed page, every page of the memory book and the share card, as on keepsake_card_2019_press.pdf and framed_page_2019_press.pdf. The only ground and panel colour. Never type.
- Ink Brown: #3A322A · mandatory. Names and dates: the guest name on each keepsake card, Walter Fernwood's name and years line and any date, as on keepsake_card_2019_press.pdf and framed_page_2019_press.pdf; may also carry headings, the eulogy, contents and captions. Never a ground or panel.
- Slate Charcoal: #4C4A46 · mandatory where a relationship line or verse appears. The relationship line on each keepsake card and any verse on the framed page, as on keepsake_card_2019_press.pdf and framed_page_2019_press.pdf; may also carry the eulogy, contents and captions. Never a ground or panel.
- Mount Grey: #9A948A · mandatory as the keyline round the photograph on every keepsake card and on the framed page. The fine keyline round the photograph at the head of each keepsake card and round the framed page's portrait, as on keepsake_card_2019_press.pdf and framed_page_2019_press.pdf; optional round plates in the memory book. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Muted Sage: #7E8574 · mandatory as the point that joins Walter's two years. Only the small point between Walter Fernwood's two years in his name line, per the signature move. Never letters or figures, a ground, a panel, a keyline or a rule.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Archive Cream is the only ground and panel colour, and photographs sit on it inside their margins. All type sits on Archive Cream, never over a photograph, and is Ink Brown or Slate Charcoal, apart from the Muted Sage point between the years: guest names, Walter Fernwood's name and years line and any date in Ink Brown; relationship lines and verse in Slate Charcoal, as on keepsake_card_2019_press.pdf and framed_page_2019_press.pdf; the eulogy, contents, section heads, captions and folios take either. Mount Grey is for photograph keylines and Muted Sage for the point between the years, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No gold, gilt, foil or other metallic effect on any type, keyline, rule, panel or icon (price positioning: "Neither a luxury commemorative volume with gilt and foil"), and no colour outside the five palette hexes (#EFE9DC, #3A322A, #4C4A46, #9A948A and #7E8574) for type, keylines, rules, panels or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour: a print's own cast may be corrected toward neutral, but no photograph is brightened, or tinted, toned or recoloured toward a palette colour.

## Typography
Display face: Familjen Grotesk Light. Text face: Adobe Caslon Pro.

- 01 Display / H1: Familjen Grotesk Light, 64 px / 70 px, Light, tracking 300 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Familjen Grotesk Light, 16 pt / 19 pt, Light, tracking 120 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Adobe Caslon Pro Regular, 12 pt / 18 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Familjen Grotesk Light, 9 pt / 13 pt, Light, tracking 30 thousandths.

Signature move: Walter's name and his two years always sit alone at the foot of the page, centred on one line in Familjen Grotesk Light caps letterspaced 300 thousandths of an em, the years joined by a small Muted Sage point rather than a rule. The line is never promoted to a heading, at any size, including the 64 px share card setting.

Rules:
- Never a script face.
- The name and years line is always at the foot of the page, centred on one line, never a heading.
- The two years are joined by a small Muted Sage point, never by a rule and never by a dash.
- The name line is always caps letterspaced 300 thousandths of an em, at every size it appears.
- Nothing is brightened and nothing is invented; damage in the scans is shown honestly, handled by crop and placement rather than painted over.
- The pieces must sit beside the 2019 funeral home keepsake card and framed page on the shelf.
- No cheerfulness and no exclamation points.

## Voice
Plain, gentle, unsentimental. Lets the photographs and the family's own words carry. Example line the book would write: The company toured for eleven seasons, and this is his father's hand on the back of the card. No cheerfulness, no exclamation points.

## Supplied Identity
There is no brand file. The look is inherited from the funeral home's two 2019 press files (a keepsake card and a framed page) that the family wants matched so the pieces sit together on the shelf, and from the family's own scans, eulogy and typed transcriptions. The funeral home's designer has retired and left only flat press files. Damage in the scans is to be shown honestly, handled by crop and placement rather than painted over.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
