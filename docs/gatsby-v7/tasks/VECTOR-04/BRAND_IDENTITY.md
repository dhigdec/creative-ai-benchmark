# Meridian and Ash

Meridian and Ash roast single origin coffee in a converted machine shop and sell it across their own counter, at two farmers markets and to about forty wholesale accounts. The merchandise table is half the fun: stickers people put on laptops and water bottles because they actually like the mark. The whole point is a range that feels made by hand, not bought by the case.

## Audience
Cafe regulars twenty to thirty five who buy a sticker at the till without deciding first, and wholesale buyers who hand them out with orders.

## Positioning
Impulse retail, three dollars a single and nine dollars a four pack. Priced to be handed out by the fistful, so the range has to read as abundant rather than precious.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Each recovered design keeps the ink it was painted or printed in: Roast Brown for the wordmark in meridian_window_mark.jpg and the moth on meridian_bag_moth.jpg, Ash Green for the roundel in meridian_window_mark.jpg and the pour-over on meridian_bag_pourover.jpg, Warm Cream for the leaf and hairline ring in meridian_window_mark.jpg, Brick Red for the ridge line on meridian_bag_ridgeline.jpg. Each companion takes its main ink from Roast Brown, Ash Green and Brick Red. Kraft and Warm Cream are secondary: backing shapes, detail reversed out of an ink, or fine lines such as a hairline ring, never the main ink. Ink Black is fine detail only, no more than 5% of a sticker's inked area. Type on a light ground is Roast Brown, or Ash Green for a secondary line; type on Roast Brown or Ash Green is Warm Cream, as on meridian_shelfcard_2025.pdf. Sticker backgrounds stay transparent.
- Opacity: Every palette colour on the stickers, proofs and gang sheet is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode. A proof is transparent only outside the artwork and in its open, uninked negative space; every inked area is fully opaque, and partial transparency occurs only as anti-aliasing along edges, with no white or coloured rectangle behind it.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for any artwork, type, backing shape, label or mark. The cut paths on the CutContour layer are production lines, not artwork, and are exempt. No colour is sampled from a photograph.
- Photography: No photograph appears in any deliverable. meridian_window_mark.jpg, meridian_bag_moth.jpg, meridian_bag_pourover.jpg and meridian_bag_ridgeline.jpg are tracing references only: each ink is matched to its palette hex rather than sampled.

## Typography
Display face: Cirka Bold. Text face: Brandon Text Regular 400 (Brandon Text Bold 700 for gang sheet capitals).

- 01 Display / H1 · arched wordmark: Cirka Bold, capitals only, 18 pt / 20 pt, Bold, tracking plus 0.030 em on the arc.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · counter card and four pack headers: Cirka Bold, capitals only, 11 pt / 13 pt, Bold, tracking plus 0.020 em.
- 03 Body · sticker and supporting copy: Brandon Text Regular, 7 pt / 10.5 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · slug labels: Brandon Text Bold, capitals only, 6 pt / 8.5 pt, Bold 700, tracking plus 0.040 em.

Signature move: A single oversized ampersand set at 1.6 times the cap height of MERIDIAN and ASH, hanging below the arc of the wordmark into the artwork, with the two words arched on a fixed 40 degree curve. At arm's length the ampersand is seen first.

Rules:
- Never set the ampersand at cap height. It is always 1.6 times the cap height of MERIDIAN and ASH.
- The ampersand always hangs below the arc, into the artwork, never sitting on the baseline.
- MERIDIAN and ASH always arch on a fixed 40 degree curve, at every size.
- Cirka Bold is display only; Brandon Text Regular 400 sets sticker copy and Brandon Text Bold 700 the gang sheet capitals.
- Keep the range reading abundant rather than precious: it is priced to be handed out by the fistful.
- Hand made, not franchise: the artwork is drawn once and reused, never redrawn slightly differently each batch.

## Voice
Plain, warm, a little wry, never precious. Example line: Pull one off the sheet, stick it on your laptop, come back for the other three.

## Supplied Identity
No usable source files exist. The window mark was hand painted on the shop glass in 2018 by a sign painter who never handed over a file. The three current illustrations survive only printed on the coffee bags, and the printer still holds last season's counter card artwork.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
