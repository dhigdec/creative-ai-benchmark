# Fennhollow Botanical Soda

Fennhollow Botanical Soda makes three botanical sodas in the Hudson Valley: elderflower and lime, rhubarb and bitters, and spruce tip. Three dollars fifty a can, sold across upstate natural grocers and by subscription, produced through a co-packer whose cleared nutrition claim cannot be reworded. Two founders run everything and neither is a photographer. The whole brand lives on summer: sun lit, warm, natural, a little restrained. The spruce tip flavour steps to the front in August, so the summer campaign has to be built to be revisited as the season shifts.

## Audience
Twenty-five to forty, shopping the natural grocer aisle and reading the ingredient panel. Paying a premium for the promise that this is closer to a real thing than a mass soda.

## Positioning
Craft better-for-you at three fifty a can. Warmer and more human than a twelve-dollar apothecary tonic, and far more restrained and natural than a hyper-saturated ninety-nine-cent soda.

- Deep Ink: #2C2A24 · mandatory where body text sits on Warm Cream Ground. Body text, subheads, fine print and the sourcing line on Warm Cream Ground, as fennhollow_shelftalker_press.pdf sets its store name and sourcing line and fennhollow_brand_note.pdf its body. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Warm Cream Ground or Label Green and nothing else; Warm Cream Ground is the main ground, as on fennhollow_shelftalker_press.pdf and fennhollow_brand_note.pdf. On Warm Cream Ground, flavour names and their half point rules are Label Green, and body text, subheads and fine print are Deep Ink; the co-packer's claim line may be Deep Ink or Label Green, as on fennhollow_shelftalker_press.pdf. A stacked flavour name never sits on a Label Green panel, where its rule would vanish. On a Label Green panel all type is Warm Cream Ground, as the can labels in fennhollow_packshot_01.jpg and fennhollow_packshot_02.jpg set pale type on the green. Type set directly over a photograph, which fennhollow_brand_note.pdf expects, is Deep Ink or Label Green over light areas and Warm Cream Ground over dark ones. Afternoon Amber is for a small price tag and Rhubarb Rose for small labels and thin rules; together they cover no more than 10% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never a ground, a panel or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or to type, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits directly on the photograph or on a solid block. The one tint is a thin Label Green rule above the fine print at 45% opacity, as fennhollow_shelftalker_press.pdf sets its foot rule, and nowhere else.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#3F6B3A, #F4ECD8, #E5AF54, #C56E68 and #2C2A24) for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons, with three exceptions carried from fennhollow_shelftalker_press.pdf: a price figure reversed out of an Afternoon Amber tag may be white, a town line under a store name may be the warm grey #57534A, and the one 45% Label Green rule above the fine print allowed under opacity reads as its tint. No neon or hyper-saturated colour and no glow anywhere in the design, which the brand places against a hyper-saturated ninety-nine-cent soda.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette; the can's aluminium stays silver. The one exception is the printed can label: our green is the label green, and it must still read as our Label Green through every correction.

## Typography
Display face: Staatliches. Text face: Whitney.

- 01 Display / H1: Staatliches Regular, 120 pt / 108 pt, Regular, all caps, tracking -5 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Staatliches Regular, 96 px / 104 px, Regular, all caps, tracking -5 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Whitney Book, 30 px / 42 px, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Whitney Book, 18 pt / 24 pt, Book, tracking +15 thousandths.

Signature move: Every flavour name stacks onto two centred lines of Staatliches caps split by a half point rule in Label Green, so the stacked block registers before the words are read. Steps 01 and 02 both carry it.

Rules:
- Never set a flavour name on a single line.
- A flavour name always stacks onto two centred lines split by a half point rule in Label Green.
- The co-packer's cleared claim line stays exactly as written and is never reworded.
- Whitney Semibold is the subhead weight; body stays Whitney Book.
- Do not fabricate a sellable product image for a missing flavour or packshot; a gap stays a declared gap.
- Step 02 leading and step 04 tracking were supplied because the prose states none; every family in this scale is the brand's own.

## Voice
Plain, warm, a little rural. Names the botanicals and the valley, not a lifestyle. The co-packer's cleared claim line stays exactly as written.

## Supplied Identity
No written brand guidelines have ever existed and the founders asked for the first one to be created. Their brand note points to a printed swatch card that was never sent with the package, so the house green survives only as the printed label on the two studio can shots the brand owns. There is no lifestyle photography of any kind.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
