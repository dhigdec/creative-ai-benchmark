# SB3-027-PHO | Fennhollow - Botanical soda summer campaign

Build a believable soda image set around two imperfect packshots

Fennhollow has two packshots that have already been pushed too far in retouching, and no lifestyle shoot. We need a product retoucher with a good eye for licensing compatible photography and for knowing when to stop correcting a damaged source.

Deliverables
1. Botanical soda summer key visual: 1 named export. The can artwork matches the approved product. The key visual pictures one can, its headline is that can's flavour name, and the headline and can have a clear hierarchy.
2. Botanical ingredient carousel: 5 named exports. The featured ingredients match approved copy. The closing page contains the approved shopping action: the price a can and where it is sold, as our approved copy's price line gives them.
3. Soda retail display poster: 1 named export. Our name, Fennhollow Botanical Soda, is set in full and visible at viewing size. The poster pictures one can, its display line is that can's flavour name, and the pack remains recognisable.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
