# Meridian Motors

Meridian Motors sells certified pre-owned vehicles between 22,000 and 38,000 dollars to families replacing an ageing car. The listings are the whole business, and the visual argument is simple: this is an orderly operation with nothing to hide. Every car should look as if it were photographed in one clean, evenly lit showroom, with honest paint and legible plain information.

## Audience
Buyers aged 30 to 55 replacing a ten year old family car, deciding on trust before they decide on the vehicle.

## Positioning
22,000 to 38,000 dollars certified pre-owned. The treatment is an even, neutral, well lit showroom white with the full vehicle in frame and honest colour. Never a dark dramatic studio look, which reads as evasive at this price, and never hot saturated pictures with starbursts and a price slash, which read as a buy here pay here lot.

## Palette
- Meridian Navy: #1F2A3A · mandatory. Prices, the year, make and model heading, labels and the dealership name, set on Showroom White, as meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf sets its price, labels and masthead. Also the only colour allowed as a band, panel or seal ground, carrying reversed Showroom White type or meridian_roundel_reversed.svg.
- Showroom White: #F6F7F8 · mandatory. The ground behind all type on every card and on the campaign, as on meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf, and the colour of type reversed out of Meridian Navy.
- Graphite: #2E3237 · mandatory where body text sits on Showroom White. Mileage and listing detail on Showroom White, as meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf sets its body values, and fine print unless it is set as the one small muted Steel Grey line. Never a ground or panel.
- Compass Teal: #3C6E71 · mandatory wherever a price appears. The 1 pt rule the price seats on, running the full card width, and other hairline rules, as under the masthead of meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf, and the compass needles of a certification seal, as on the seal of meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf. Never a ground or a type colour.
- Steel Grey: #9AA1A8 · optional accent. Thin keylines, box rules and the photo-well border, the one small muted dealer line, and small seal lettering on Meridian Navy, as on meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf. Never a ground, and never the colour of a price, heading or body text.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Showroom White #F6F7F8 is the ground behind all type on every card and on the campaign. Meridian Navy #1F2A3A is the only other ground, for a band, panel or seal carrying reversed type, and never sits behind a vehicle. Prices, mileage and listing detail always sit on Showroom White, never on a Meridian Navy band. On Showroom White, prices, headings, labels and the dealership name are Meridian Navy, mileage and listing detail are Graphite, and fine print is Graphite or, as the one small muted line, Steel Grey. On Meridian Navy, type is Showroom White, except small seal lettering, which may be Steel Grey as on the certification seal of meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf. Compass Teal and Steel Grey are for rules, keylines, seal detail (that seal's Compass Teal compass needles and Steel Grey lettering) and one small muted line only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a band, panel or seal sits beside or over the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No starburst, no price slash and no hot saturated sale colour on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, which read as a buy here pay here lot; no dark dramatic studio ground behind a vehicle; and no colour outside the 5 palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; the supplied roundel artwork (meridian_roundel_transparent.svg, meridian_roundel_reversed.svg, meridian_roundel_transparent.png and meridian_roundel_reversed.png) keeps its own colours.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The three bays' casts (warm apron, yellow-orange wash bay, dim cool-green back row) are corrected to one even, neutral showroom white, but each car keeps its true paint: the Ardmoor Ridge stays deep red and does not swing orange, the black Brookmere Sable keeps its shadow-side detail, and the pale rooflines of the white Norsted Alta and the silver Calder Kestrel are not clipped into the background.

## Typography
Display face: Publico Banner. Text face: Source Sans 3.

- 01 Display / H1: Publico Banner, 68 px / 72 px, Bold, tracking minus 10 thousandths, tabular lining figures.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Publico Banner, 44 px / 50 px, Bold, tracking minus 5 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Source Sans 3, 26 px / 38 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Source Sans 3, 20 px / 30 px, Regular, tracking 20 thousandths.

Signature move: The price is always Publico Banner Bold tabular lining figures, with the dollar sign shrunk to sixty percent of numeral height and raised to the figure tops, seated on a 1 pt Compass Teal rule running the full card width. That small lifted dollar against big newspaper figures registers first.

Rules:
- Never set a price above 68 px.
- The price is always Publico Banner Bold tabular lining figures, with the dollar sign at sixty percent of numeral height and raised to the figure tops.
- The price always seats on a 1 pt Compass Teal rule running the full card width.
- The compass roundel wordmark sets at 22 px tracked 40 thousandths and belongs on the firm's own collateral, never on a marketplace primary.
- Disclosure stays Source Sans 3 Regular, 20 px on screen and 9 pt on 13 pt in print, and is never dropped.
- No starbursts and no price slash: those read as a buy here pay here lot.
- No dark dramatic studio treatment behind the card: at this price it reads as evasive.
- Every card carries the real vehicle, plainly, in an even neutral showroom white with honest colour.

## Voice
Straightforward and unadorned, the voice of a desk that states the facts. Example line the brand would write: "2017 Calder Kestrel, 68,400 miles. Certified. 24,900 dollars."
