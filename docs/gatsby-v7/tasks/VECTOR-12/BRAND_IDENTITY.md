# Static Union

Static Union is a small Portland label that puts out two considered drops a year and sells them direct and through roughly forty specialist stockists. The marks came out of the founder's own hand and the early tee runs, and they have been carried on garments for two seasons. Autumn is the first move into small goods, so the same marks now have to survive being made rather than printed.

## Audience
Buyers roughly 17 to 30 who follow small labels, shop drops rather than seasons, and buy direct or from specialist streetwear stockists. They notice when a pack does not match.

## Positioning
Accessible independent streetwear. Stickers at 4 dollars, pins at 14, patches at 18, wholesale to about forty stockists. Priced to be cheap enough to collect and good enough to keep, never luxury.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: In production-guide.pdf, page grounds are Bone and all type and rules are Union Black, as on su_hangtag_2025.pdf (one Union Black ink on Bone stock); a solid Union Black panel may stand behind an artwork whose production ground is dark, and any type on it is Bone. Bolt Red, Rail Gold and Slate appear in the guide only inside reproduced artworks and in swatches that name an artwork's colour, never as type, a ground or a rule. In the artworks each colour follows its item's production colours: the Bolt Icon Sticker's bolt is Bolt Red; the Arc Badge Enamel Pin's metal line plate is Rail Gold and its enamel is Union Black and Bone. On a dark ground a mark is carried by any palette colour except Union Black: Bone, as the pale thread woven on su_woven_label_photo.jpg; Rail Gold and Bolt Red, as in su_smallgoods.csv (Arc Badge Patch: Union Black twill / Rail Gold; Bolt Icon Enamel Pin: Black nickel / Bolt Red); Slate, as the lettering of su_mark_union_stripe.png on its Union Black banner. Union Black never carries a mark alone on a dark ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, in every artwork and on every page of the guide. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, bevel, shine or blend mode is applied to any colour; metal, thread and vinyl are shown as flat colour, never imitated with a gradient or texture; and no scrim or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over an artwork.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for any artwork fill or stroke, or for type, grounds, panels, rules, swatches or icons in the guide; the one exception is the sticker's kiss-cut line, a non-printing production stroke. No colour is sampled from a photograph. Union Black never carries a mark alone on a dark ground.
- Photography: The four artworks carry no photograph. A supplied photograph shown in production-guide.pdf is a reference only: it keeps its own colour and never stands in for the adapted artwork on its page (su_hangtag_2025.pdf frames su_woven_label_photo.jpg on its face in the same way). No artwork colour is sampled from fabric, bench light or the cutting mat; colours are matched to palette hexes. Each artwork keeps its own production colours and is not recoloured toward the guide's Bone and Union Black.

## Typography
Display face: Caslon Doric Condensed Black. Text face: Basis Grotesque.

- 01 Display / H1: Caslon Doric Condensed Black, 42 pt / 44 pt, Black (900), caps, tracking -12 units, about -0.012 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Caslon Doric Condensed Black, 30 pt / 33 pt, Black (900), caps, tracking -12 units, about -0.012 em.
- 03 Body: Basis Grotesque Regular, 9.5 pt / 13 pt, Regular (400), tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Basis Grotesque Mono Regular, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular (400), tabular figures, tracking +0.04 em.

Signature move: A Bone bar three quarters of a cap height thick runs straight through the middle of the wordmark, cutting every letter, so the name reads as a struck solid block before it reads as words.

Rules:
- Never artificially condense the display. Caslon Doric Condensed Black is already the condensed cut.
- All display sets in caps, tracked -12 units, so the counters nearly close.
- The Bone knock through bar is three quarters of a cap height thick and cuts every letter of the wordmark, at every size.
- Part codes always set in Basis Grotesque Mono Regular with tabular figures.
- The same lockup has to survive being made, not just printed, so the bar proportion holds down to the 14 pt patch.

## Voice
Direct, plain, a little dry, talking to its own buyers rather than advertising. A line the brand would write: "Autumn small goods. Twelve pieces, made to last, gone when they are gone."

## Supplied Identity
There is no proper brand kit. The four marks that matter survive only as whatever files people happened to send back over three years: two came from a printer, one exists only as a photograph of a woven cap label because the file is gone, and one came out of an old tee artwork folder. They look fine on screen and have been used for two years, but there are no editable masters.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
