# SB3-042-VEC | Static Union - Manufacturing artwork system

One streetwear graphic family adapted properly for four manufacturing methods

Static Union needs production-minded artwork adaptation for small goods. The same detailed drawing will not work unchanged as embroidery, enamel, a sticker and a woven label. We are looking for someone who understands those differences and can make deliberate reductions. Lettering inside the supplied marks keeps that mark's own letterforms, adapted only as the maker limits require. Wherever the STATIC UNION wordmark appears, the Union Stripe woven-label artwork included, the type system's knock-through bar still cuts every letter, at every size.

Scope
1. Eagle Bolt embroidery artwork: 1 named export. The artwork respects the embroidery vendor tolerance and draws the patch's merrowed edge as its border. The smallest reproduced detail remains open at the finished size.
2. Arc Badge enamel-pin artwork: 1 named export. The artwork respects the enamel-pin vendor tolerance. The smallest reproduced detail remains open at the finished size.
3. Bolt Icon sticker artwork: 1 named export. The artwork respects the sticker vendor tolerance and carries its kiss-cut line as a separate path; the cut line sets the sticker's finished size, measured on its longest side. The smallest reproduced detail remains open at the finished size.
4. Union Stripe woven-label artwork: 1 named export. The artwork respects the woven-label vendor tolerance. The smallest reproduced detail remains open at the finished size.
5. Supplier production guide: 1 named export. Each of the four artworks has its own page, which shows the artwork and states its intended medium. The guide carries the Static Union wordmark, set to the brand type system. Each artwork's page states that artwork's finished physical size with its unit.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.
