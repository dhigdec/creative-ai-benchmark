# SentinelMesh

SentinelMesh reduces human cyber risk for regulated enterprises. The platform watches for the risky moment, runs an adaptive simulation, and coaches the person in real time, closing the gap between security policy and daily behavior. We sell to CISOs and IT directors on annual contracts and we win on trust, precision, and proof that the product does what we say.

## Audience
CISOs, IT directors, and the security engineers reading over their shoulder at mid-market and enterprise organizations. Technical, skeptical, and short on patience for hype.

## Positioning
Premium enterprise. Annual contracts from 40,000 to 90,000 dollars. Everything we ship has to read as a considered purchase a security leader can defend internally, never as a self-serve consumer app.

## Palette
- Sentinel Navy: #0B1B2B · mandatory. The main ground, the brand's dark navy ground: always the ground the shield mark sits on, the ground of the closing card, and the ground of any panel carrying a claim card or lower third over footage.
- Deep Slate: #16283A · optional. A secondary solid ground or panel, such as a lower-third panel, carrying Paper White or Cloud Grey type. Never type.
- Signal Cyan: #2FB6C4 · mandatory as the cursor rule. The 4 px cursor rule beneath each claim card, and beneath the closing card when no claim card is in frame, once per frame. The brand's one accent: never type, a ground, a panel or an icon.
- Cloud Grey: #D9DEE3 · optional. Interface labels, fine print and, if wanted, the product name, as in the pale grey of the wordmark on the trade show banner. Never a ground, a claim or the closing card.
- Paper White: #F5F7F8 · mandatory. The claim cards, the closing card and any lower third; the product name and fine print may also use it; and the colour the shield mark is reversed out in. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Sentinel Navy or Deep Slate and nothing else; Sentinel Navy is the main ground and always the ground behind the shield mark. The claim cards, the closing card and any lower third are Paper White; the product name is Paper White or Cloud Grey, as in the pale grey of the wordmark on the trade show banner; interface labels and fine print are Paper White or Cloud Grey. Signal Cyan is the one accent: the 4 px cursor rule alone, once per frame, never type, a ground, a panel or an icon. The shield mark is reversed out light, in Paper White, on Sentinel Navy.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, type, rule or the mark, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the footage; any panel laid over a shot is solid Sentinel Navy or Deep Slate. Cuts and short dissolves between shots are transitions, not tints; colour and opacity are judged on a settled frame.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; the supplied footage, and pure white #FFFFFF on the mark only, are the only exceptions. No second cyan rule in a frame, and no cyan type, ground, panel or icon. No glow, drop shadow, gradient, bevel, outline or metallic effect on any type, rule, panel or the mark: precision over spectacle.
- Photography: The screen captures and the office footage keep their own colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: no navy or cyan grade over the office clips. The dark navy chrome of the interface stays dark navy wherever it appears, in sentinelmesh_demo_raw_screen_01.mp4, sentinelmesh_demo_raw_screen_02.mp4, sentinelmesh_demo_raw_screen_03.mp4, and in sentinelmesh_still_02_lobby_wall.jpg or sentinelmesh_still_05_overshoulder.jpg if either is used: never lifted toward grey.

## Typography
Display face: PP Formula Condensed. Text face: Whitney.

- 01 Display / H1: PP Formula Condensed, 84 px / 92 px, Medium, tracking -6 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: PP Formula Condensed, 56 px / 62 px, Medium, tracking -4 units.
- 03 Body: Whitney, 34 px / 46 px, Book, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Whitney, 28 px / 38 px, Book, tabular figures, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: Every claim card carries a 4 px Signal Cyan cursor rule beneath it that overruns the final letter by exactly one character width, like a terminal cursor that has stopped moving.

Rules:
- Never let the cyan rule appear twice in one frame.
- The cursor rule is 4 px, Signal Cyan, and overruns the final letter by exactly one character width, no more and no less.
- Every number sets with Whitney tabular figures.
- The closing card drops to 64 px on the square cut and 28 pt in print; claim cards hold at 56 px.
- Print body sets in Whitney Book at 9.5 pt on 14 pt leading.
- One accent and one idea per line; no fear language, no exclamation, no buzzwords at any step.

## Voice
Plain, exact, and calm. We state what the product does and let it read as cleared rather than sold. We avoid fear language, exclamation, and buzzwords. One accent, one idea per line.
