# Lanternwood Folk Fest

Lanternwood Folk Fest is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is outside Burlington, Vermont, and this commission covers phase 2: keepsake programme and venue wayfinding.

## Audience
A roots and Americana crowd broadly thirty to sixty, many of them returning regulars who keep the programme, willing to pay for something that feels made rather than mass produced.

## Positioning
Sixty five pounds a day. A considered keepsake register: photographs given real room, editorial type with a proper hierarchy, generous margins, one act to a page breathing rather than a dense fair handout, and never a near empty boutique arts brochure.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every ground and panel in the programme is solid Weathered Cream, as on both printed pages; photographs sit on it and no other colour forms a ground or panel. On Weathered Cream, biographies and running text are Barn Charcoal; act names and section heads are Lantern Amber or Laurel Deep (section heads may also be Field Green); stage, day and set time lines are Field Green or Barn Charcoal; rules are Lantern Amber. The crest keeps the amber lantern and green laurel ring painted on the gate banner, printed in Lantern Amber and Field Green on both printed pages. In the programme, Lantern Amber and Field Green are for type, rules and the crest only, never a ground or panel, together no more than 5% of a page's designed, non-photographic area. No supplied file shows a sign, so a wayfinding sign may take any one palette colour as its one solid ground, with type and arrow that contrast with it: on Weathered Cream, Lantern Amber, Laurel Deep, Field Green or Barn Charcoal; on Lantern Amber, Barn Charcoal or Laurel Deep; on Laurel Deep, Field Green or Barn Charcoal, Weathered Cream or Lantern Amber.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, as every fill on both printed pages is. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on the solid Weathered Cream ground beside or below the photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, arrows or icons, and no gradient, glow or metallic effect on any of them. Photographs and the crest reproduced from lanternwood_banner_photo.jpg are judged by their own checks.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: no sepia, duotone, Weathered Cream wash or Lantern Amber tint is laid over a press photo to make it look like a keepsake, and the warm late-afternoon light of lanternwood_press_photo_04.jpg is not pushed further toward Lantern Amber.

## Typography
Display face: Yeseva One. Text face: Source Serif 4.

- 01 Display / H1: Yeseva One Regular, 42 pt / 46 pt, Regular, tracking 0, never letterspaced.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Yeseva One Regular, 18 pt / 21 pt, Regular, tracking 0, never letterspaced.
- 03 Body: Source Serif 4 Regular, 10.5 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Yeseva One Regular, 14 pt / 17 pt, Regular, tracking 0, never letterspaced.

Signature move: Set times are set in Yeseva One rather than a sans, at exactly one third the act name size, so every stage board and every programme page shows a clock wearing the same flared serifs as the act above it. At the 42 pt act name of step 01 that fixes the set time of step 04 at 14 pt.

Rules:
- Never letterspace Yeseva One. Tracking stays at 0 on steps 01, 02 and 04, including negative tracking at display sizes.
- Set times are always exactly one third the act name size and are always Yeseva One, never a sans.
- Yeseva One Regular sets every act name, section head and wayfinding destination.
- Wayfinding sets the sign destination in Yeseva One at 120 pt with the direction line at 48 pt.
- Screen listings set in Yeseva One at 64 px on 70 px.
- Source Serif 4 Regular is reserved for biographies at 10.5 pt on 15 pt.
- Give photographs real room and keep margins generous: one act to a page breathing, never a dense fair handout and never a near empty boutique arts brochure.

## Voice
Friendly, plain and a little handmade, the voice of a committee that runs the thing itself. Example line the brand would write: "Grab a pint, find your stage, and we will see you down the front."
