# Ferncroft Nursery

Ferncroft Nursery grows heirloom and specialty stock for gardeners who read the botanical name first. Everything is sold off the bench with a tag that reads like a plant person wrote it. Spring opening is the last Saturday of April, and every plant on the rack needs its tag by then.

## Audience
Gardeners aged thirty five to sixty five who buy for provenance, read the Latin name, and ask where the stock was grown. They distrust a rack where one tag looks like a different shop.

## Positioning
An heirloom specialty grower at eighteen to ninety five dollars a plant, above the six dollar big-box rack and below a fine-press botanical print. The tag is provenance-led and botanical-forward: the Latin carries, the price sits small, the photograph is a supporting note.

## Palette
- Slate Grey: #4A4E48 · mandatory. Type: the common name, the pot size and the price, as on ferncroft_hangtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf and ferncroft_benchtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf; it may also carry the nursery mark. Never a ground, band or panel.
- Fern Green: #3E5A34 · mandatory. The botanical Latin and the care icon row, as on ferncroft_hangtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf and ferncroft_benchtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf; it may also carry the nursery mark. Never a ground, band or panel, and never any other line of tag copy.
- Bench Cream: #EFE9DC · mandatory. The tag ground and the only ground colour, as on ferncroft_hangtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf and ferncroft_benchtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never a type colour and never the nursery mark.
- Terracotta: #B0623D · optional accent. Hairline rules, one small ornament or the nursery mark only; neither 2025 printer PDF uses it. Never tag copy, a panel or a ground.
- Bark Brown: #6B4E36 · mandatory. The care line, as on ferncroft_hangtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf and ferncroft_benchtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf; it may also carry the nursery mark. Never a ground, band or panel, and never any other line of tag copy.
- Sage: #9AA988 · optional accent. A panel or mount framing the photograph or its picture area, a hairline rule, or the nursery mark. Never tag copy, never the tag ground, never laid over the photograph.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Bench Cream is the tag ground and the only ground colour. Tag copy takes three colours, as on ferncroft_hangtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf and ferncroft_benchtag_2025_PRINTER.pdf: the common name, pot size and price in Slate Grey, the botanical Latin in Fern Green, the care line in Bark Brown. The care icons are Fern Green. The nursery mark (fern frond and FERNCROFT NURSERY lettering) is one solid palette colour other than Bench Cream; the type-colour limits govern tag copy, not the mark. Sage may frame the photograph or its picture area as a panel or mount, run as a hairline rule, or carry the mark. Terracotta is for hairline rules, one small ornament or the mark, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. Neither accent is ever tag copy or the ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, icons or the nursery mark. No tag copy in Sage, Terracotta or Bench Cream, and no nursery mark in Bench Cream. No ground in Slate Grey, Fern Green, Bark Brown, Sage or Terracotta, and no band, block or panel in Slate Grey, Fern Green, Bark Brown or Terracotta.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted, washed or duotoned toward the palette. Foliage keeps the green of its own frame: the plants on dark wet bench timber in ferncroft_variety_03.jpg, ferncroft_variety_08.jpg, ferncroft_variety_10.jpg and ferncroft_variety_15.jpg are not warmed until their leaves turn yellow.

## Typography
Display face: Trirong. Text face: National 2.

- 01 Display / H1: Trirong, 28 pt / 31 pt, Light 300, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Trirong, 15 pt / 17 pt, Light Italic 300, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: National 2, 9 pt / 13 pt, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: National 2, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: The botanical Latin sits on the next baseline directly under the common name, in Trirong Light Italic 300 at exactly 54 percent of the common name's size with no space between them, and italic is used for nothing else on the tag.

Rules:
- Italic is never used for anything but the botanical Latin.
- The Latin always sits on the next baseline under the common name, at exactly 54 percent of its size, with no space between them.
- Hangtags at 148 by 210 mm run 28 pt and 15 pt; department tags at 55 by 85 mm run 14 pt and 9 pt.
- Care, pot size and price are the only items in National 2 Regular: 9 pt on 13 pt for care, 8 pt for pot size and price, 16 px on screen.
- The Latin carries the tag: the price sits small and the photograph is a supporting note.
- The whole rack reads as one careful hand: no department sets its own type, and no tag looks like it came from a different shop.
- Restraint signals quality here: above the six dollar big-box rack, below a fine-press botanical print.

## Voice
Warm, knowledgeable, unhurried, the voice of a grower. Example line the brand would write: 'Slow to establish, then generous for decades. Give it morning sun and patience.'
