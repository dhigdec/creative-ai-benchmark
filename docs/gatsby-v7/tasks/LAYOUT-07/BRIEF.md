Listing photography and six agent cards for 14 Marlowe Crescent

Harbor Crest Realty needs the house to look consistent across the listing and the open-house cards. The shooter has returned the rooms as captured; wall colour and window handling now vary from room to room.

Please prepare
- Agent open-house card: 7 named exports. The agent portrait matches the record. The property details match the fact sheet: each card gives the list price, the address, the bedroom and bathroom counts and the open house day and time as marlowe_facts.txt states them, quotes any feature line it carries word for word, and shows one photograph of the house chosen from marlowe_capture_01.jpg to marlowe_capture_18.jpg. The contact line is readable.

The rooms are shown truthfully: every room on the fact sheet's room list is listed with its dimensions as written, each room photograph is labelled with the room it shows, and the three feature lines in marlowe_facts.txt appear word for word. The enquiry information is unambiguous: the list price and address, the open house day and time, and each of the six agents in agent_roster.csv with their name, direct number and email as recorded.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. Every agent card and the presentation carry the Harbor Crest Realty wordmark as the sign and business card in harbor_crest_basis.png show it. Wherever the price appears it follows the type scale: 54 pt, with the address headline at 22 pt. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings.
