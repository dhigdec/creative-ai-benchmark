# Harbor Crest Realty

Harbor Crest Realty is a nine-person residential brokerage in Rosewood, listing suburban family homes for owners and guiding first move-up buyers through them. The firm runs a steady volume of listings a year and wants one repeatable kit that makes every listing look like it came from the same firm.

## Audience
Move-up buyers aged 30 to 45 making their first step up from a starter home, scrolling listings on a phone. Also the sellers who list with the firm and the six agents who work open houses.

## Positioning
Homes centre on the mid-market, with 14 Marlowe Crescent at 625,000 dollars and a 2.5 percent listing side. Positioned as sophisticated but approachable, not luxury and not discount: warm, legible, honest, with real facts and real room sizes shown plainly.

## Palette
- Harbor Navy: #1F3A5F · mandatory. The price figures, the address headline and agent-name headings (as the name on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf), and the wordmark as on the sign and business card in harbor_crest_basis.png. May also set room facts and body text. Never a ground or panel.
- Crest Slate: #4A5A6A · mandatory where a contact or licence line is set. The contact line (which may carry the agent name, as in type step 04), the licence line and the office and detail lines, as on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf. May also set room facts and body text. Never a ground or panel.
- Warm Sand: #E8DFCF · one of the two ground colours; optional. A ground or band: the ground of the sign and business card in harbor_crest_basis.png, and the footer band behind the contact lines on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf. Never type or a rule.
- Paper White: #FBFAF7 · one of the two ground colours; optional. The card and page ground, as on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf. Never type or a rule.
- Signal Copper: #C07A3E · mandatory wherever the price appears. Rules only. The 2 pt rule that runs the full measure directly under the price is mandatory there, and the 2 pt full-measure rule appears only under the price. A thinner copper rule, such as the 0.75 pt rule under the name on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf, is optional. Never type, a ground, a panel or an icon.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Paper White or Warm Sand only, as on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf (Paper White ground, Warm Sand footer band) and on the sign and business card in harbor_crest_basis.png (Warm Sand). Type sits on those grounds; type over a photograph sits on a solid Paper White or Warm Sand panel, never directly on photographic pixels. The price, the address headline, agent-name headings and the wordmark are Harbor Navy; the contact line, the licence line and the office and detail lines are Crest Slate, as on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf; room facts and other body text are Crest Slate or Harbor Navy. Paper White and Warm Sand are never type colours. Signal Copper appears only as rules and stays a thin accent: the 2 pt full-measure rule appears only under the price, a thinner rule, such as the 0.75 pt rule on the printer's card, is optional, and copper is never a ground, panel, type colour or icon.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type over a photograph sits on a solid Paper White or Warm Sand panel, never directly on the photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. Signal Copper prints flat #C07A3E as on harbor_crest_open_house_card.pdf; the sheen on the photographed business card in harbor_crest_basis.png is not reproduced, and no foil, metallic, gradient or shine effect is used, since nothing in the kit reads as luxury; no sale burst, starburst or price flash, since nothing reads as discount.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted, washed or duotoned toward the palette; agent portraits keep their own colour.

## Typography
Display face: Sanchez Bold. Text face: Source Sans 3 Regular.

- 01 Display / H1: Sanchez Bold, 54 pt / 58 pt, Bold 700, tracking -5 units, tabular lining figures.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Sanchez Bold, 22 pt / 25 pt, Bold 700, tracking -3 units.
- 03 Body: Source Sans 3 Regular, 9.5 pt / 13.5 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Source Sans 3 Regular, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Regular 400, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: Every card carries the price as one oversized Harbor Navy line of Sanchez Bold tabular lining figures at 54 pt, three times the size of the address, resting directly on a 2 pt Signal Copper rule that spans the full measure.

Rules:
- Never set an address in capitals.
- The price is always Sanchez Bold tabular lining figures in Harbor Navy at three times the address size.
- The 2 pt Signal Copper rule runs the full measure under the price and under nothing else.
- Room facts and room sizes are shown plainly and truthfully, never rounded up for effect.
- Nothing in the kit reads as luxury and nothing reads as discount.
- The kit matches the sign and business card already in the field rather than replacing them.

## Voice
Plain, welcoming, benefit-led without hype. Speaks like a neighbour who knows the street. Example line: "Four bedrooms, a garden that backs onto the ravine, and an open house this Sunday."

## Supplied Identity
The firm is not rebranding. A current sign and business card are already in the field and are supplied as the basis, so the new kit must match what is out there rather than replace it. The open house card artwork is held by the printer as a press PDF; there is no central brand file beyond the sign and card reference.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
