# Apexguard

Apexguard is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Charlotte, North Carolina, and this commission covers phase 1: rebrand photography and product rollout.

## Audience
Independent installers, 25 to 45, who judge a supplier on whether the packaging justifies the price to a customer standing at the counter.

## Positioning
Line items from two hundred fifty to twelve hundred dollars, a confident premium supplier rather than a bright discount accessory brand.

## Palette
- House black: #101114 · mandatory. The house dark ground: the page ground of the dealer poster and the sub-brand application sheet, and any panel on a product-page module, as the card ground of apexguard_label_2025.pdf and the house ground in apexguard_brand_notes.txt. Also the type colour on the plain white ground of a product-page module.
- Gunmetal: #3A3D42 · optional. Secondary panels, keylines and photo frames on House black, as the keyline round the part photograph on apexguard_label_2025.pdf. On House black it may carry only a small, quiet label, as that label's SKU line; on plain white it may carry secondary labels. Never the page ground, a fitment line or a product-module SKU.
- Cool steel: #B7BCC2 · optional. Secondary type on House black or Gunmetal: the product family line and field labels such as Roll width and Warranty, as on apexguard_label_2025.pdf. Never a ground or panel, and never type on a white ground.
- Signal accent: #E4552B · mandatory wherever the master mark appears; otherwise an optional accent. The one accent: the 6 pt bar locked beneath the master mark, the small bar inside the mark's chevron, a short bar beneath a sub-brand name, and at most one small caption, as the 6 pt bar beneath the master mark (type system), and the bar under VANTAGE and the FITMENT caption on apexguard_label_2025.pdf. Never a ground, panel or body text.
- Off white: #F2F1ED · mandatory where type sits on House black or Gunmetal. Primary type and the reversed master mark on House black and Gunmetal: sub-brand names, fitment lines and record values, as on apexguard_label_2025.pdf. It may also be the plain white ground behind a part on a product-page module.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds: House black #101114 is the page ground of the dealer poster and the sub-brand application sheet and the colour of any panel on a product-page module, as on apexguard_label_2025.pdf; Gunmetal #3A3D42 may be a secondary panel or keyline. On each product-page module the part sits on plain white, as the product page rule in apexguard_brand_notes.txt asks: Off white #F2F1ED or pure white #FFFFFF, flat. Type: on House black or Gunmetal, primary type and the reversed mark are Off white and secondary labels Cool steel #B7BCC2, with Gunmetal for a small quiet label, as on apexguard_label_2025.pdf; on plain white, type is House black, with Gunmetal for secondary labels. Signal accent #E4552B is the one accent, for the bar beneath the mark, the small bar in its chevron, a short bar beneath a sub-brand name, as on apexguard_label_2025.pdf, and at most one small caption, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, panel or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#101114, #3A3D42, #B7BCC2, #E4552B and #F2F1ED) for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark, except pure white #FFFFFF as the plain white ground behind a part on a product-page module; no second accent colour; no gradient, glow, chrome or metallic effect on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, since the brand allows no heavy gradients and nothing flashy; no mascot, vintage badge or tuner styling.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and surface texture and are never recoloured toward the palette: the black-anodized, gunmetal and stainless parts in the bench frames keep their own finish, not pushed to House black or Gunmetal.

## Typography
Display face: Clash Display. Text face: Aktiv Grotesk.

- 01 Display / H1: Clash Display Bold, all caps, 140 pt / 147 pt, Bold, tracking 120 units minimum, fitted to the panel measure.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Clash Display Bold, all caps, 60 pt / 66 pt, Bold, tracking +40 units.
- 03 Body: Aktiv Grotesk Regular, 16 pt / 24 pt, Regular, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Aktiv Grotesk Regular, 10 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: The caps mark is always tracked out to span the exact full measure of its panel, flush at both edges and never below 120 units, with a 6 pt signal accent bar locked directly beneath at one fifth that measure. Step 01 is that mark, so its tracking is fitted to the panel rather than fixed, and the accent bar travels with it.

Rules:
- Never track or condense a fitment line.
- The mark is always tracked to span the exact full measure of its panel, flush at both edges, never below 120 units.
- A 6 pt signal accent bar locks directly beneath the mark at one fifth that measure.
- Display and the master mark always all caps in Clash Display Bold.
- Fitment, warranty and body always sentence case and untracked in Aktiv Grotesk Regular; SKUs always in Roboto Mono Medium.
- One accent only, and fitment information stays legible on the shelf.
- No mascots, no vintage badges, no heavy gradients.

## Voice
Confident and terse, no hype. Example line: 'Film, tint and hardware. Fitted right, backed properly.' No mascots, no vintage badges, no heavy gradients.

## Supplied Identity
Our master mark survives only on a die-cut box sticker and on a decal on an installer's shop glass. The studio that drew it has closed and taken the source files, so it has to be recovered as usable artwork.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
