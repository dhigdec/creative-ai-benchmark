# Marisol Cove

Marisol Cove is a small clifftop hotel built around a path down to a private beach and a dining room people plan a trip for. There is no pool and no spa, and the point of the place is the light, the water and being left alone. The autumn and winter shoulder season is the first the owners are pushing properly.

## Audience
Couples and small groups aged 30 to 55, experience led rather than amenity led, who book for the setting and the food and return in the quiet months.

## Positioning
Rooms run 340 to 620 dollars a night. Boutique premium, above a coastal inn and well below a resort or private estate. The rate is carried by the setting, not by facilities.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Bluff Sand and nothing else, as on marisol_rate_card_press.pdf. On Bluff Sand, the wordmark, headlines, room names, body text, rates and captions are Warm Ink, and a rate is never given a colour of its own. Cypress sets small labels only (a sleeps line, an INCLUDES label, the contact line). The wave rule beneath the wordmark takes the colour of its letters, as marisol_sign_photo.jpg paints both in one ink. Cove Grey Blue is for the hairline rules beneath headlines and the tagline and for a decorative wave rule such as the one above the footer of marisol_rate_card_press.pdf; Dusk Clay is for thin keylines. Type set directly on a photograph is Warm Ink or Bluff Sand. Cypress, Cove Grey Blue and Dusk Clay together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the photograph as a solid Bluff Sand block.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; no colour and no bold on a rate, which stays Warm Ink in the body's Book weight; no burst badge or savings callout, as the plain marisol_rate_card_press.pdf card has none.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: no Bluff Sand, Cove Grey Blue or Dusk Clay wash over a frame, and the cove's water and sky are not pushed toward Cove Grey Blue. A colour that belongs to the room, such as a painted wall, stays that colour when a cast is corrected.

## Typography
Display face: Urbanist Light. Text face: Freight Sans Pro Book, Medium for room names.

- 01 Display / H1: Urbanist, 36 pt / 40 pt, Light, tracking +240 units, small caps.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Freight Sans Pro, 16 pt / 22 pt, Medium, tracking +60 units.
- 03 Body: Freight Sans Pro, 10 pt / 16 pt, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Freight Sans Pro, 8 pt / 12 pt, Book, tracking +20 units.

Signature move: The tracking. The wordmark and every headline are Urbanist Light small caps at 240 units, wide enough that each letter reads as a separate mark, with one 0.4 pt hairline rule cut to the exact width of the tracked line sitting beneath it. At brochure size that is 36 pt on 40 pt, on the key visual 88 px on 96 px with the 34 px tagline below, and the long thin band of spaced capitals over its width matched hairline is what a guest recognises first.

Rules:
- Never track the body.
- The wordmark and every headline are small caps tracked to 240 units, and nothing else is ever tracked that wide.
- The hairline rule is always 0.4 pt and always trimmed to the exact width of the tracked line above it, never to the column width.
- Urbanist Light is the only display weight. Freight Sans Pro Medium is reserved for room names, Book sets body, rates and captions.
- Rates are set in the same Book weight as the body. Never colour or embolden a rate.
- Every guest facing piece reads as the same specific cove, honest about a fourteen room hotel rather than dressed up as something grander.

## Voice
Calm, plain and specific. It describes what is actually there and lets the reader picture it. Example line the brand would write: An hour before sunset the light comes off the water and the whole cove goes gold. That is when to walk down.
