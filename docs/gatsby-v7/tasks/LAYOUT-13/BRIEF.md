# SB3-058-LAY | Marisol Cove - Shoulder-season hospitality campaign

Shoulder-season hospitality campaign built from carefully matched photography

Marisol Cove needs a shoulder-season campaign that reflects the light we actually have. Most of the coastal pictures will be licensed; the usable room photographs are ours. Our insurer does not allow generated or AI-made pictures in any piece of this campaign. The work is to make them belong together, not to promise a different coast or a different property.

Please prepare
- Shoulder-season key visual: 1 named export. The pictured property is correctly identified, and the Marisol Cove wordmark sits directly above the tagline. The tagline is exactly “The quiet side of the coast.” No date, discount or unlisted amenity is added.
- Hospitality booking story: 1 named export. The booking action, a line inviting guests to book by the phone number, email or web address printed on marisol_rate_card_press.pdf, stays inside the safe area.
- Seasonal offer card: 10 named exports. The words “Shoulder Rate” appear beside the supplied Shoulder Rate value and “Winter Rate” beside the Winter Rate value, with the room name, sleeps, minimum stay and Includes line from marisol_rates_autumn.csv. The property identity, the Marisol Cove wordmark, is visible.
- Seasonal hospitality brochure: 1 named export. The offer is understandable without the original brief: it gives each room's name, sleeps, Shoulder Rate, Winter Rate and minimum stay from marisol_rates_autumn.csv, and the cancellation terms from marisol_copy.md. The property imagery is truthful.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
