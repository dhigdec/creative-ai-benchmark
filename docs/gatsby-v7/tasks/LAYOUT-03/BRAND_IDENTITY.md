# Lumora Laboratories

Lumora Laboratories is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Los Angeles, California, and this commission covers phase 2: skincare counter launch for the parent range.

## Audience
Women twenty eight to forty five, ingredient literate, who read the INCI list before the claim and treat whitespace and evidence as signs of seriousness.

## Positioning
Forty six to eighty dollars per SKU. Premium but not luxurious: near monochrome grounds, evidence led hierarchy, and a price that sits quietly beside the specification. Deliberately clear of both the fourteen dollar mass aisle and the two hundred and fifty dollar luxury counter, either of which would misread the clinical claim.

## Palette
- Lab Ink: #1C2530 · mandatory. Product names, claims, prices and the shopping action, as the name, claim and price are set on lumora_counter_card.pdf; may also set the active concentration display; the fill or outline of a shopping button. Never a ground or panel, except the fill of a shopping button.
- Clean White: #F7F8F8 · mandatory. The ground of every output, as on lumora_counter_card.pdf. Never a type colour, except the label of a Lab Ink shopping button.
- Slate Grey: #5B6670 · mandatory where an INCI line or a size appears. INCI lines, sizes and the small brand line, as on lumora_counter_card.pdf, which also sets the active line in Slate Grey, so it may set the active concentration display. Never a ground, a panel, a product name or a claim.
- Mineral Mist: #DCE2E4 · optional. Only the specification panel behind the active and INCI lines, as on lumora_counter_card.pdf, solid or at 55% on Clean White, which renders #E8ECED. Never type, never the page ground.
- Clinical Blue: #3E6E8E · mandatory wherever an active concentration appears. The 1 px rule the width of the column under the active concentration, and hairline rules, as the rule on lumora_counter_card.pdf. Never type, a ground, a panel or a button.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Clean White is the ground of every output. Type sits on Clean White or on the Mineral Mist specification panel, as on lumora_counter_card.pdf, or directly on a photograph with no scrim under it; the only Clean White type is the label of a Lab Ink shopping button. Product names, claims, prices and the shopping action are Lab Ink; INCI lines, sizes and the brand line are Slate Grey, as on lumora_counter_card.pdf; the active concentration display is Lab Ink or Slate Grey. A shopping button is Lab Ink with a Clean White label, or a Lab Ink outline with a Lab Ink label. Clinical Blue appears only as rules, the 1 px rule under each concentration included, and Mineral Mist only as the specification panel; neither is ever type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, with one exception taken from lumora_counter_card.pdf: the specification panel behind the active and INCI lines may be Mineral Mist at 55% opacity on Clean White, which renders #E8ECED. No other tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes, or #E8ECED for the Mineral Mist panel at 55%, for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons, and no gold, silver, metallic or foil colour and no gradient effect in type, panels, rules, buttons or icons: the brand is premium but not luxurious and stands deliberately clear of the luxury counter. Photographs are governed by the photography rule. lumora_wordmark_approved.png keeps its own ink.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own product colour and are graded to one neutral white balance, never recoloured toward the palette: the amber glass in lumora_range_frame_01.jpg and lumora_range_frame_02.jpg stays amber, and no sweep is left or tinted toward Clinical Blue or Mineral Mist to match the design.

## Typography
Display face: Spectral. Text face: IBM Plex Sans, with IBM Plex Mono for every figure.

- 01 Display / H1, the active concentration: Spectral, 112 px / 118 px, Medium 500, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2, the product name: Spectral, 56 px / 62 px, Medium 500, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body: IBM Plex Sans, 32 px / 46 px, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: IBM Plex Sans, 16 px / 24 px, Regular 400, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: The active concentration is always the largest element on the surface, set in Spectral Medium at exactly twice the product name, hung flush left so the percent sign falls into the margin, above a 1 px Clinical Blue rule the width of the column.

Rules:
- Never let a product name outsize its concentration.
- The concentration is always the largest element on the surface and always exactly twice the product name.
- The concentration hangs flush left so the percent sign falls into the margin.
- A 1 px Clinical Blue rule the width of the column always runs under the concentration.
- Spectral Medium 500 sets the wordmark and product names only.
- IBM Plex Sans Regular carries claims and INCI. IBM Plex Mono Regular carries every figure.
- Cleared INCI lines and claims are fixed and cannot be edited.
- The counter card name holds at 28 pt.

## Voice
Precise and evidence first, no hyperbole. Example line the brand would write: 'Formulated around actives, at concentrations we publish.'

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark is approved but exists only as a flat web scale PNG of the letterforms in one deep ink on white, exported by the founders' first designer, with no vector source on hand. The six products were photographed in house on a pale sweep across two sessions. The regulatory cleared INCI lines and claims are fixed and cannot be edited, and the specialty chain has supplied its approved counter card press file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
