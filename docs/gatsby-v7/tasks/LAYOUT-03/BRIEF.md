# SB3-048-LAY | Lumora Laboratories - Retail launch and paid social system

Six-product retail launch: one image standard across store and paid social

Lumora Laboratories is preparing six products for specialty retail and a rebuilt store page. The photographs currently do not read as one line: grade all six to one neutral white balance. We need the image foundations corrected and carried into editable launch material.

Scope
1. Retail product-page module: 6 named exports. The pack matches the named SKU. The approved benefit is readable: each module gives its product's name, active and concentration, cleared claim, size and price exactly as in lumora_skus.csv.
2. Retail launch carousel: 6 named exports. The sequence differentiates the products: each of the six pages features one product and pictures its photographed pack, in the order numbered in lumora_copy_approved.txt, with its name and its active and concentration as approved. The brand appears on the opening page as the approved wordmark, lumora_wordmark_approved.png.
3. Paid social creative: 3 named exports. The approved product claim is unchanged: the hero product ad features Lumora C-15 Brightening Serum, product 1 in lumora_copy_approved.txt, shown with its photographed pack, its name, active and concentration, claim and price; the range comparison sets out all six products, each pictured by its own photographed pack with its name, active and concentration and price; the benefit story tells the longest cleared claim, that of Lumora Retinal Renewal Night Serum, in full, with the product shown with its photographed pack, its name and its active and concentration. Every claim shown is its cleared claim, word for word. The shopping action is clear: one plain instruction to shop or buy, with no offer, discount or urgency added.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Where a product's approved active line gives no percentage, set that line, unchanged, as its concentration display. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
