# Isla Verde

Isla Verde is a family owned resort on the Gulf coast where guests come for quiet. Forty two rooms, refinished corridors and a garden that people walk slowly. The signage is being rebuilt this winter so the property reads as calmly as it feels, with the house monogram carried consistently from the door plates to the directional panels.

## Audience
Independent leisure travellers thirty to fifty five paying for calm, who notice when a place is shouting and value being able to find their way without being told loudly.

## Positioning
Upscale independent, rooms three hundred and eighty to six hundred and fifty a night. Signage should read at that tier: generous clear space, delicate marks, confidence rather than contrast for its own sake.

## Palette
- Deep Palm: #2C3B34 · mandatory on every door plate (the ground). The solid ground of every door plate, as on islaverde_doorplate_2016.pdf. It may also be the single colour of monogram.svg and of each pictogram when drawn to sit on a light ground.
- Warm Stone: #D8CFC0 · optional accent. The room numeral or fine print on a Deep Palm plate, as islaverde_doorplate_2016.pdf sets its number. Never a ground, a panel, the English name or the Spanish line.
- Slate Grey: #5A5F60 · mandatory on every door plate. The 0.5 pt vertical hairline at one third of the plate width, and nothing else.
- Mist Grey: #A7ABA8 · mandatory on every door plate. The Spanish line, set beneath the English name, and nothing else.
- Sand Cream: #EFE9DD · mandatory on every door plate (English name and monogram). The English name and the monogram reversed out of Deep Palm on every door plate, as on islaverde_doorplate_2016.pdf. It may also be the single colour of monogram.svg and of each pictogram when drawn to sit on Deep Palm.
- Ink Charcoal: #2A2A28 · not used on these deliverables. Type on a light Sand Cream ground only; not used on the door plates, monogram.svg or the pictograms.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every door plate is a solid Deep Palm ground, as on islaverde_doorplate_2016.pdf. On it the English name and the monogram are Sand Cream, as on islaverde_doorplate_2016.pdf; the Spanish line is Mist Grey and the 0.5 pt hairline Slate Grey, as the type rules set; the room numeral and any fine print are Sand Cream or Warm Stone. Each pictogram and monogram.svg are drawn in one solid colour, Deep Palm or Sand Cream: the mark is one solid Sand Cream reversed out of Deep Palm on islaverde_doorplate_2016.pdf and one light colour on a dark green panel on islaverde_existing_sign_01.jpg, so a transparent mark takes Sand Cream to sit on Deep Palm and Deep Palm to sit on a light ground. Warm Stone never forms a ground or panel, and Ink Charcoal appears on none of these deliverables.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any colour, and no mark carries tonal shading, highlights or texture: the monogram and the pictograms are flat, single-colour artwork. No photograph appears on any deliverable, so nothing is laid over one.
- Forbidden: No gold, champagne, silver or other metallic or foil effect on any mark, type, rule or plate, and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, rules, grounds, marks or pictograms.
- Photography: No photograph or bitmap is placed on any deliverable. The supplied photographs, islaverde_brochure_cover.jpg, islaverde_location_01.jpg to islaverde_location_10.jpg and islaverde_existing_sign_01.jpg to islaverde_existing_sign_04.jpg, are references only; no colour is sampled from them.

## Typography
Display face: Lausanne 300. Text face: Tiempos Text Regular 400 (Tiempos Text Medium 500 for room numerals).

- 01 Display / H1 · house monogram: Lausanne 300, 26 pt / 29 pt, Light 300, tracking plus 0.020 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · English panel line: Tiempos Text Regular, 20 pt / 24 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: Tiempos Text Regular, 13 pt / 19 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · house notes: Tiempos Text Regular, 9 pt / 13 pt, Regular 400, tracking plus 0.030 em.

Signature move: Every plate is split by a 0.5 pt Slate Grey hairline at exactly one third of its width, with the monogram alone in Lausanne 300 to the left of it and all wording in Tiempos Text to the right. That thin vertical is what a guest registers first.

Rules:
- Never let type cross the rule.
- The hairline is always 0.5 pt Slate Grey at exactly one third of the plate width, on every plate and panel.
- The monogram stands alone to the left of the rule; all wording sits to the right of it.
- The Spanish line always sets in Mist Grey, beneath the English line, at 13 pt.
- Room numerals are Tiempos Text Medium 500 at 30 pt tracked 0.040 em.
- Lausanne 300 is the monogram face only; Tiempos Text sets every word on the plate.
- Signage keeps generous clear space and delicate marks: confidence rather than contrast for its own sake, never shouting.
- The monogram is carried unchanged from the door plates to the directional panels; nobody invents a new one.

## Voice
Understated, warm, precise. It gives directions without raising its voice. Example line: The spa is along the garden path, second door on the left.

## Supplied Identity
No monogram source file survives. The designer moved abroad and nothing was handed back. The only decent record of the mark is a foil stamp on the cover of a brochure printed in 2019. The 2016 sign shop that cut the current door plates has since closed, though its press artwork for the plates is still on file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
