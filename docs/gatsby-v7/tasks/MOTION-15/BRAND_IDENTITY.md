# Anvil and Oak Strength

Anvil and Oak Strength is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Providence, Rhode Island, and this commission covers phase 2: coached movement video library.

## Audience
Subscribers aged 30 to 52 with a home or garage gym, already lifting and paying for instruction rather than motivation. They can tell a coach from a cheerleader and buy the difference.

## Positioning
49 dollars a month, coached strength, extended a quarter at a time. Positioned as a working coach's studio: clearly above a 9 dollar mass fitness app and deliberately clear of a clinical or rehabilitation register.

## Palette
- Anvil Iron: #2B2B2E · mandatory. Movement names and cue text on a Chalk or Whitewash Wall ground, the “STRENGTH” line and the certification seal on a light ground, all as on anvil_oak_exercise_card_2025.pdf; and the solid dark panel or ground that carries Chalk or Whitewash Wall type beside or over footage.
- Oak: #6B4A2E · mandatory for the wordmark on a light ground. The wordmark “ANVIL & OAK” on Chalk or Whitewash Wall, and cue numerals if cues are numbered, both as on anvil_oak_exercise_card_2025.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, a movement name or cue text.
- Whitewash Wall: #EDE8DF · optional. A second light value: a light ground or panel carrying Anvil Iron type, or type, the wordmark and the seal reversed out of Anvil Iron. Chalk stays the main light ground and still appears. Never a rule or an accent.
- Chalk: #F4F2ED · mandatory. The main light ground, as the card ground on anvil_oak_exercise_card_2025.pdf, for title frames, cue panels and the closing wordmark frame; and type, the wordmark and the seal reversed out of Anvil Iron.
- Ember Rust: #A6552F · mandatory wherever a movement name is set. The 3 px rule under the lower row of every movement name, cut to that row's exact width (the signature move). Never type, a ground, a panel or an icon.
- Steel Grey: #8A9099 · optional accent. Secondary lines, such as a muscle-group line as on anvil_oak_exercise_card_2025.pdf, and fine print, on Chalk or Whitewash Wall. Never a movement name, a cue, a ground or a panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Chalk, Whitewash Wall or Anvil Iron and nothing else; Chalk is the main light ground, as on anvil_oak_exercise_card_2025.pdf. On Chalk or Whitewash Wall, movement names, cues, the “STRENGTH” line and the seal are Anvil Iron, the wordmark “ANVIL & OAK” is Oak, and secondary lines and fine print are Anvil Iron or Steel Grey, as on the card. On Anvil Iron, all type, the wordmark and the seal are Chalk or Whitewash Wall. Ember Rust is the 3 px rule under each movement name and nothing else. All designed type, the wordmark and the seal sit on a solid ground or panel, never straight on footage, as the card keeps its type off the photograph. Ember Rust and Steel Grey together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-footage area of any frame.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, type, rule, the wordmark or the seal, and no scrim, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the footage: a name or cue over a shot sits on a solid Chalk, Whitewash Wall or Anvil Iron panel. Cuts and short dissolves between shots are transitions, not tints; colour and opacity are judged on a settled frame.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, icons, the wordmark or the seal, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black included. No glow, drop shadow, gradient, bevel, outline or metallic effect on any type, rule, panel, the wordmark or the seal. No Ember Rust type, ground, panel or icon.
- Photography: The supplied takes keep their own colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: the whitewashed board wall in the footage stays as it was shot, not tinted to Whitewash Wall or Chalk, no take is warmed toward Oak or Ember Rust, and any correction only neutralises a colour cast.

## Typography
Display face: Trade Gothic. Text face: Benton Sans.

- 01 Display / H1: Trade Gothic, 96 px / 60 px, Bold Condensed No. 20, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Trade Gothic, 72 px / 45 px, Bold Condensed No. 20, tracking -0.01 em.
- 03 Body: Benton Sans, 48 px / 67 px, Medium 500, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Benton Sans, 28 px / 38 px, Medium 500, tracking +0.04 em.

Signature move: A forced two line break. Every movement name at steps 01 and 02 stacks on two rows whatever its length, flush left, with leading at 0.86 of the cap height so the rows almost touch, and a 3 px Ember Rust rule pinned under the lower row at exactly that row's width.

Rules:
- Never set a movement name on one line.
- Movement names are capitals, flush left, on two rows, leading at 0.86 of the cap height.
- The 3 px Ember Rust rule sits under the lower row only, cut to that row's exact width.
- Numerals are tabular lining.
- Cue copy speaks like a coach giving a cue, never a cheerleader.
- Clearly above a mass fitness app and deliberately clear of a clinical register.

## Voice
Direct, instructional and calm. Speaks like a coach giving a cue, not a brand selling one. Example line: 'Brace, hinge, drive through the floor. That is the whole lift.'

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark and the in house certification seal survive only as stitching on a canvas gym bag patch, held now as one handheld photograph. Both have to be recovered from that photograph as artwork before they can go on the cards and the library sheet. The seal is a compound ring lockup, type set around a central anvil glyph inside a ruled ring. There is no vector file, no colour spec and no prior guideline.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
