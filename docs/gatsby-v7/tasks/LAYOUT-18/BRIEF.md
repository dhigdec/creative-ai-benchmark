# SB3-063-LAY | Kilnmore - Wholesale relaunch campaign

Kilnmore wholesale relaunch: preserve the glazes across the full campaign

The relaunch needs to look like one Kilnmore range. We have fourteen captures from two very different mornings, covering thirteen pieces, and four glazes that customers must still be able to distinguish after correction. Correct every capture to the even daylight of the overcast morning, and keep oatmeal, ash, cobalt slip and bare clay each its own colour.

2. Wholesale paid-social creative: 2 named exports. The trade enquiry action is visible.
3. Storefront range banner: 1 named export. Kilnmore is identified. The range remains recognisable.
4. Product specification tile: 13 named exports. The item geometry matches this record. The piece is cut out cleanly onto a plain Bench Linen ground, and the tile carries this record's piece code, name, glaze, height and diameter as recorded, each with its unit, any recorded capacity, care note and wholesale price.
5. Wholesale range one-pager: 1 named export. The product hierarchy is clear: each of the thirteen pieces is listed with its name, its height and diameter as recorded with their unit, and its wholesale price.
6. Mark and type reference: 1 named export. The recovered mark is shown, and the typography choices are identified accurately: each face with its weight, its size and the role it plays.
