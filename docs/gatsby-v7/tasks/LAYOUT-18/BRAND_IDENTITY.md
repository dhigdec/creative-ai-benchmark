# Kilnmore

Kilnmore is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Portland, Maine, and this commission covers phase 2: wholesale line sheets and storefront launch.

## Audience
Direct buyers aged 28 to 50 who own two or three considered kitchen objects and are choosing between us and a comparable studio line, and independent home goods stockists whose buyers judge a range on whether the line sheet is legible and complete enough to order from.

## Positioning
Mugs 34 dollars, bowls 42 dollars, serving platter 96 dollars, four piece dinner set 180 dollars. Wholesale at roughly half of retail. Considered studio ceramics, not impulse marketplace and not gallery austerity.

## Palette
- Bench Linen: #EAE4D8 · mandatory. The ground of every page, tile and canvas and the only ground or panel colour, as on linesheet_2025_PRINT.pdf; the ground the wordmark always sits on. Never a type colour.
- Kiln Ink: #2B2723 · mandatory. The wordmark and its 0.5 pt rule, and all primary type: names, headings, body copy, specification values and retail prices, as on linesheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. Hairline rules may carry it at 16% or 35% (see opacity). Never a ground or panel.
- Oatmeal: #D8C8A9 · optional accent. Only as a small flat swatch naming the Oatmeal glaze, as the glaze swatch on linesheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never a ground, panel, rule or type colour.
- Ash Grey: #8C8B84 · optional secondary type colour. Specification labels, piece codes, small labels, wholesale prices and the footer line, as on linesheet_2025_PRINT.pdf, a hairline rule at 55% (see opacity), and a small swatch naming the Ash glaze. Never a ground, panel, heading, name or body copy.
- Cobalt Slip: #3A4E6B · optional accent. Only as a small flat swatch naming the Cobalt Slip glaze. Never a ground, panel, rule or type colour.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Bench Linen is the only ground and panel colour, as on linesheet_2025_PRINT.pdf. On it, the wordmark and its 0.5 pt rule, names, headings, body copy, specification values and retail prices are Kiln Ink; specification labels, piece codes, small labels, wholesale prices and the footer line may be Ash Grey, as on the same sheet. Oatmeal and Cobalt Slip appear only as a small flat swatch naming that glaze; Ash Grey appears only as the secondary type colour listed above, a 55% hairline rule, or a swatch naming the Ash glaze; glaze swatches together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. Type set on a photograph sits on its pale linen backdrop in Kiln Ink.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, with one exception taken from linesheet_2025_PRINT.pdf: hairline rules other than the rule under the wordmark, which stays solid Kiln Ink as that sheet prints it, may be Kiln Ink at 16% or 35% or Ash Grey at 55% on Bench Linen, as that sheet prints them, which render #CBC6BB, #A7A299 and #B6B3AA. No other tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or to type, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes, or #CBC6BB, #A7A299 and #B6B3AA for the tinted hairline rules the opacity rule allows, for type, panels, rules, buttons, swatches or icons, bare clay included. No bright exclamation colour, price flash, badge or countdown (brand_notes.txt). No metallic, foil or gradient effect. Oatmeal and Cobalt Slip never set type, and no glaze colour is a ground or panel.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted, washed or duotoned toward the palette: no glaze is recoloured to the Oatmeal #D8C8A9, Ash Grey #8C8B84 or Cobalt Slip #3A4E6B hexes. Every capture is corrected to the even daylight of the overcast morning (range_capture_01.jpg, range_capture_02.jpg, range_capture_03.jpg, range_capture_04.jpg, range_capture_05.jpg, range_capture_06.jpg and range_capture_07.jpg), and oatmeal stays oatmeal, ash stays ash, cobalt slip stays cobalt and bare clay stays warm, as brand_notes.txt asks.

## Typography
Display face: Panchang Medium. Text face: Tiempos Text, with Suisse Int'l Regular for specs.

- 01 Display / H1: Panchang Medium, 72 px / 79 px, Medium, tracking -5 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Panchang Medium, 18 pt / 21 pt, Medium, tracking 40 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Tiempos Text Regular, 11 pt / 16 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Suisse Int'l Regular, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular, tracking 15 thousandths.

Signature move: The wordmark is never enlarged: it is always 11 pt Panchang Medium caps letterspaced 220 thousandths of an em in Kiln Ink on Bench Linen, at the head of the page over a 0.5 pt rule, a small wide word with air inside it. It sits outside the scale and no step above it borrows its letterspacing.

Rules:
- Never set a wholesale price in italic.
- The wordmark is never enlarged: 11 pt Panchang Medium caps letterspaced 220 thousandths of an em, at the head of the page over a 0.5 pt rule, on every piece at every format.
- The wordmark is always Kiln Ink on Bench Linen.
- Specs and prices use tabular figures so the line sheet columns align.
- The line sheet must stay legible and complete enough for a stockist to order from it.
- No sale language, no urgency, no exclamation points.

## Voice
Plain and warm, the studio talking about its own work. Example line the brand would write: Thrown and glazed in small batches, made to be used every day. No sale language, no urgency, no exclamation points.

## Supplied Identity
The mark is a brush drawing inked years ago onto an unglazed clay tile, and the only version that exists is a photograph of that tile on the bench. There is no working file. The mark has to be recovered from the photograph as artwork the studio can use at any size before the relaunch can carry it. The written guideline survives, plus a phone snap of the shop counter kept as a warmth reference.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
