# Halcyon Quarter

Halcyon Quarter is a fifty two apartment scheme by Kestrel and Vane on the old rope works site at Beckett Wharf. Phase one sells to professional downsizers leaving a family home nearby. What the scheme sells is the evening: warm rooms against a cool blue hour on the water, quiet and expensive rather than shiny, carried across the developer, the brokerage and the lender as one look.

## Audience
Professional downsizers aged 50 to 65 leaving a house they have owned for thirty years within twenty miles. Discerning, unhurried, distrustful of shiny marketing, and likely to count windows when they visit the site.

## Positioning
Premium off-plan residential, apartments from 780 thousand to 1.45 million dollars. Positioned as quiet and expensive, above bright price-led property marketing and apart from investor rental stock.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds behind type are Bone Paper or Blue Hour and nothing else, as solid blocks beside or over the photograph, the way the lender's 2025 payment card sets type on Bone Paper beside its photograph. Headings and body copy are Blue Hour on Bone Paper, or Bone Paper on Blue Hour; Canal Steel may carry captions and fine print on Bone Paper. Brass, Lamp Warm and Rope Works Brick are accents for rules, dividers and small labels: together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground and never body copy.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph. The blue hour is graded into the photograph itself, and any text panel sits over or beside it as a solid block.
- Forbidden: Brass, not gold, and nothing shiny, in the type, panels, rules, buttons, icons and monogram: no gold and no metallic, foil, gloss or sheen effect on any of them, Brass included. Flat Brass #A67C3D and Lamp Warm #C99A5B are palette colours, not gold. Photographs may hold warm lamplight and warm brick. No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the monogram.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured or tinted toward a palette hex. The evening is made by grading: the water cold and the rooms warm, a blue hour, never a sunset. The interior frames are graded to the same evening as the exterior frames, by grading, not by a palette overlay.

## Typography
Display face: Domaine Display Narrow Regular. Text face: Graphik Regular.

- 01 Display / H1: Domaine Display Narrow Regular, 120 pt / 126 pt, Regular, tracking -8 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Domaine Display Narrow Regular, 30 pt / 34 pt, Regular, tracking -4 units.
- 03 Body: Graphik Regular, 12 pt / 17 pt, Regular, tracking 0 units.
- 04 Fine print: Graphik Regular, 9 pt / 13 pt, Regular, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: The name is always optically sized to fill the full text measure exactly, 380 pt across the 1200 mm hoarding, 120 pt on the A2 poster and 68 px on the portal panel, so it is as wide as the piece allows every time, inside generous margins.

Rules:
- Never set a price in Graphik Bold and never in a starburst.
- The price stays inside the sentence, in Graphik Regular tabular lining figures at 28 pt.
- The development name is always optically fitted to the full text measure, never set short of it and never outside the generous margins.
- Captions, specification and prices are Graphik Regular only.
- Brass, not gold. Nothing shiny.
- Show nothing the building does not have.

## Voice
Calm, assured and understated. Brass not gold, the water cold and the rooms warm, nothing shiny. We state the address, the light and the offer without overselling. Example line: 'Fifty two apartments on the water at Beckett Wharf, releasing this autumn.'

## Supplied Identity
The Kestrel and Vane monogram exists only as sign paint, brushed by the sign writer onto the site hoarding board, with a single photograph of it and no artwork file. The lender's payment cards survive only as the flat file their printer ran last spring, with no working source held by anyone, and every figure has since changed.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
