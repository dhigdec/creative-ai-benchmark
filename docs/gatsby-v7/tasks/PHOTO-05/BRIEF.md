# SB3-005-PHO | Halcyon Quarter - Mixed-use launch photography

Evening-led property campaign from an overcast shoot

Halcyon Quarter is a former ropeworks development. The audience is downsizers looking carefully at the place, not people buying a dramatic sky. Our capture day was overcast; the campaign needs a credible evening direction across very different placements.

Final handoff

 Halcyon Quarter launch hoarding: 1 named export. The development name is legible at the specified scale. The pictured building is the complete street elevation, faithful to the supplied photograph of that whole elevation. The enquiry action is visible and directs buyers to Harbor Crest Realty, who sell the apartments.

 Mixed-use launch poster: 1 named export. The residential and commercial offer is distinguishable: the residential offer is pictured by the show apartment photograph and names each apartment type released in phase one, with its price; the commercial offer sits under its own heading and says only that commercial space forms part of Halcyon Quarter, with no size, price, use, tenant or date. The developer identity is visible: the Kestrel and Vane monogram together with the name Kestrel and Vane.

 Property portal campaign panel: 1 named export. The panel pictures the canal frontage of the building, with the water in the foreground, and the approved location appears correctly. The composition does not imply completed construction where the source is a proposal.
