# Vitale Labs

Vitale Labs is a small Denver supplement line sold direct to consumers on subscription. Its presenter, Dr. Nadia Reuter, fronts a short explainer series, The Balance Brief, that turns one cleared claim per episode into a calm, credible watch. The brand's whole positioning is restraint: it speaks to buyers who have been burned by overclaiming, and it rewards being believed over being loud.

## Audience
Wellness curious buyers aged 30 to 50, cautious about health claims and burned by overclaiming before. They reward evidence and plain speech, and they close the tab on hype.

## Positioning
Bottles around 40 dollars a month on subscription. Positioned between a discount gummy stack and a 200 dollar longevity clinic: credible and warm, never clinical and never hyped.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds, cards and panels are solid Off White, Warm Neutral or Lab Ink and nothing else. The cleared sentence stands alone on Off White, never on Warm Neutral, Lab Ink or footage. On Off White or Warm Neutral, type is Lab Ink, as the episode number on the Soft Amber dot of balance_card_2025.pdf, except the Clay initial of the cleared sentence and a Clay short link. On Lab Ink or its permitted scrim, type is Off White or Warm Neutral, as the title, claim, presenter and detail lines on balance_card_2025.pdf, with a small Sage label or a Clay short link allowed, as its footline and link. Sage, Clay and Soft Amber are accents (the Sage rule, the Clay initial, a Clay short link, small Sage labels, a small Soft Amber episode-number dot): together no more than 5% of the frame in any frame, and never a ground, card, panel or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, with one exception taken from balance_card_2025.pdf: in the lower part of the frame, behind type laid over footage (the opening lower third, the presenter credit or a closing line), a Lab Ink #23282B scrim may rise from clear at its top edge to no more than 93% opacity at the frame's bottom edge, as the card's scrim does behind its lower copy. No other tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or type, and no other scrim, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage. The cleared sentence sits on solid Off White, never on the scrim. A whole card or shot may cut or fade in and out during a transition; colour and opacity are judged on a held frame.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes (#23282B, #E7E0D4, #7E8F6E, #B5765A, #F6F3EC and #D9A24E) for type, grounds, cards, panels, rules or devices, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000 included; no metallic, glow, bevel or drop-shadow effect on any type, rule or device; no scrim in any colour but Lab Ink.
- Photography: Footage and stills keep their own colour and are never tinted, duotoned or washed toward Sage, Clay, Soft Amber or any palette colour; the white lab coat stays a clean neutral white. Where balance_still_07.jpg, balance_still_08.jpg or balance_still_09.jpg is used, the faint green-tinged LED cast on the lab coat is not pushed further green, toward Sage, by a global warm correction.

## Typography
Display face: Garamond Premier Pro Display. Text face: Whitney.

- 01 Display / H1: Garamond Premier Pro Display, 64 px / 76 px, Regular 400, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Garamond Premier Pro Display, 58 px / 68 px, Regular 400, tracking -8 units.
- 03 Body: Whitney, 30 px / 44 px, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Whitney, 24 px / 30 px, Medium, tracking +40 units.

Signature move: Every episode opens on the cleared sentence alone on Off White, set 64 px on 76 px in Garamond Premier Pro Display Regular 400, with one oversized Clay initial dropped three lines deep and a 2 px Sage rule closing the sentence. The vertical cutdown repeats the move at 58 px on 68 px.

Rules:
- Never set the cleared sentence in caps or italic.
- Garamond Premier Pro is always set at the Display optical size, Regular 400, so the hairlines stay fine at scale.
- The cleared sentence always stands alone on Off White with one oversized Clay initial dropped three lines deep and a 2 px Sage rule closing it.
- Never run a claim the regulatory reviewer has not cleared, and never reword a cleared claim line.
- Whitney Book sets body, Whitney Medium sets labels and credits. Do not swap them.
- Type and colour direction are inherited from the printer's episode card, which sets the sentence at 28 pt on 34 pt.

## Voice
Measured, warm and plain. States what is known and stops there. Example line: 'One change, explained clearly. No promises we cannot keep.'

## Supplied Identity
The brand runs on the artwork its printer used last season. The episode card exists as the press PDF the printer worked from, and the per episode details live in a supplied sheet. Type and colour direction are inherited from that card rather than from a formal guideline, and the regulatory reviewer's cleared claim lines are fixed and cannot be reworded.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
