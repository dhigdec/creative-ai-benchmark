# Kantyna Nova

Kantyna Nova is a single table set once a night: one tasting, one wine flight, cooked and served by the two people who built it. The room is dark and quiet by design, so the plate is the only thing lit. Nothing is rushed, nothing is upsold, and the evening is the point rather than the meal.

## Audience
Guests thirty to fifty five who book the room as their occasion, not their dinner. They value intention over abundance and read a dark, spare room as confidence rather than as coldness.

## Positioning
One hundred and eighty five dollars for the tasting, ninety five more for the wine pairing. Held back and expensive without raising its voice: above a neighbourhood bistro, deliberately short of a destination tasting palace an eighteen seat opening cannot fill. Price is never a feature.

## Palette
- Cellar Black: #14110F · mandatory. The ground of every designed surface and the picture surround, as on kantyna_pairing_card_press.pdf. The only colour used as a ground or panel.
- Warm Bone: #E9E2D4 · mandatory. Type on Cellar Black: display lines, headings, names, course titles and course lines, descriptions and body text, and the producer and price of each wine on the menu, as the guest name and course on kantyna_pairing_card_press.pdf. Never a ground or panel.
- Aged Brass: #A6875A · mandatory. The 0.5 pt hairline rule under every opening small caps line, and the wine set in italic: the whole wine designation on each card, as on kantyna_pairing_card_press.pdf, and the wine after each producer on the menu. Never a ground, panel or body text.
- Deep Claret: #5A2233 · optional accent. Thin dividers and small marks only. Never a ground, panel, type colour or the hairline under the opening line.
- Ash Stone: #7C7468 · mandatory where an allergen line is set. The allergen line, as on kantyna_pairing_card_press.pdf, and optionally quiet fine print such as hours and house notes. Never a ground, panel, heading or display line.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Cellar Black is the only ground and panel colour and surrounds every picture, as on kantyna_pairing_card_press.pdf. On Cellar Black, display lines, headings, names, course lines, descriptions and body text are Warm Bone; the whole wine designation on each card is Aged Brass italic, as on kantyna_pairing_card_press.pdf; the allergen line is Ash Stone, which may also carry quiet fine print such as hours. The hairline under each opening small caps line is Aged Brass. Deep Claret is an optional accent for thin dividers and small marks only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. A price takes the colour of the line it ends. On page 2 of the menu, where a wine line mixes colours, the producer is Warm Bone roman, the wine after it is Aged Brass italic, and the price is Warm Bone.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on solid Cellar Black or directly on the photograph's own dark surround.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. Aged Brass is the flat #A6875A, never a metallic, foil, gradient or shine effect. No price is picked out in a colour of its own, and no colour but Cellar Black is used as a ground or panel.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own colour and are never tinted, washed or duotoned toward the palette. The blown highlights in kantyna_plate_04.jpg, kantyna_plate_07.jpg, kantyna_plate_08.jpg and kantyna_plate_10.jpg are not painted in, textured or tinted toward Warm Bone.

## Typography
Display face: EB Garamond. Text face: Neue Frutiger.

- 01 Display / H1: EB Garamond, true small caps, 40 pt / 44 pt, Regular 400, tracking +120 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: EB Garamond, true small caps, 26 pt / 30 pt, Regular 400, tracking +120 units.
- 03 Body: Neue Frutiger, 14 pt / 21 pt, Book 450, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Neue Frutiger, 9.5 pt / 14 pt, Book 450, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: Every surface opens with a single line of EB Garamond true small caps tracked 120 units, sitting six points above a 0.5 pt Aged Brass hairline rule that runs the full measure. Italic is reserved for the wine alone.

Rules:
- Never set a price in lining figures. Prices are EB Garamond old style figures at 9.5 pt.
- Never set a price in its own column. It runs inline at the end of the description.
- Price is never a feature, so it is never enlarged, coloured or emboldened.
- Italic is reserved for the wine alone. Nothing else in the system is italic.
- Display is EB Garamond in true small caps only, always tracked 120 units.
- The opening small caps line always sits six points above a 0.5 pt Aged Brass rule that runs the full measure.
- Neue Frutiger Book 450 carries descriptions, hours and every line that is not display.
- Menu hierarchy holds at course title 16 pt over a 9.5 pt description on 14 pt leading. The place card holds at guest name 26 pt over dish 13 pt.

## Voice
First person plural, plain and understated, never salesy. Example line the brand would write: 'One seating a night. Sit down and let the evening happen.'
