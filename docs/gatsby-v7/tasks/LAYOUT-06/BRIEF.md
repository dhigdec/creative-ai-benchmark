# SB3-051-LAY | Nordheim Atelier - Showroom line-card system

Showroom line cards for twelve looks, with a controlled editorial treatment

Nordheim Atelier is preparing for buyer appointments. Twelve looks were photographed over two days, including two outside as the light changed. We need one greyscale editorial treatment across all twelve looks, but wool, shearling and gabardine must still read as different materials.

Deliverables
1. Showroom line card: 13 named exports, every card carrying the Nordheim Atelier wordmark lockup recovered whole from nordheim_letterpress_card.jpg and used as printed there, with its one hairline rule between NORDHEIM and ATELIER; where the type system describes the lockup differently, as with its two hairlines, the printed card governs. The pictured style matches this record, and the card names it by its look number and by its look name as nordheim_fw26.csv writes it. The photographed garment uses the fabric named in the Fabric Composition field. Material information, the look's Fabric Composition word for word and its Colourway, is readable. The trade price is easy to locate: it is the look's Wholesale figure, labelled as wholesale and set beside the labelled Retail figure, each figure printed as nordheim_fw26.csv gives it, with no thousands separator.
