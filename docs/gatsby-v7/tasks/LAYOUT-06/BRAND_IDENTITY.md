# Nordheim Atelier

Nordheim Atelier is a designer-led studio in Copenhagen making a tight seasonal collection in wool, shearling and gabardine. FW26 is twelve looks, sold to press and to roughly thirty independent stores. The house builds one editorial look each season and carries it across everything a buyer and an editor see.

## Audience
Fashion press and independent-store buyers aged 30 to 55. Readers who value cloth and construction over gloss, and who judge a collection on whether the range holds together.

## Positioning
Garments run 600 to 2,400 dollars, positioned as couture-adjacent designer ready-to-wear. Prices live only on the linesheet where buyers expect them; the collateral never discounts or shouts, since a sale treatment would strip the perceived value in a single spread.

## Palette
- Ink: #111112 · mandatory. All record type on Paper: the look name, fabric composition, colourway value and prices, as on nordheim_linecard_2025_PRINT.pdf; and the wordmark lockup, which nordheim_letterpress_card.jpg prints in one deep ink. Never a ground or panel.
- Paper: #EDEBE6 · mandatory. The ground of every card, as on nordheim_linecard_2025_PRINT.pdf. The only ground or panel colour.
- Cool Grey: #6E7075 · mandatory where a caption or field label is set. Captions and field labels such as Colourway, Wholesale and Retail, as on nordheim_linecard_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never the look name, fabric composition, colourway value or prices, and never a ground or panel.
- Halftone Grey: #B9B7B2 · optional accent. Hairline dividers and small marks only. Never a ground, panel or type colour.
- Bone: #DAD5CC · optional accent. Hairline dividers between the fabric, colourway and price blocks, never directly above or below the look name. Never a ground, panel or type colour.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Paper is the ground of every card and the only ground or panel colour, as on nordheim_linecard_2025_PRINT.pdf. On Paper, the wordmark lockup, the look name, fabric composition, colourway value and prices are Ink; captions and field labels such as Wholesale and Retail are Cool Grey. Bone and Halftone Grey are for hairline dividers and small marks only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, and no divider runs directly above or below the look name.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules or icons, and no sale, discount or shouted treatment: no price is set above 11 pt, emboldened, boxed, burst or picked out in a colour of its own.
- Photography: Every photograph takes the one neutral greyscale treatment, with no colour cast, and is never tinted or duotoned toward Paper, Bone or any other palette colour. The figure and garment come from the supplied frame, with nothing painted in, generated or extended.

## Typography
Display face: Bitter ExtraBold. Text face: Soehne Buch 400.

- 01 Display / H1: Bitter ExtraBold, 34 pt / 37 pt, ExtraBold 800, tracking +40 units, all caps.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Bitter ExtraBold, 20 pt / 23 pt, ExtraBold 800, tracking -4 units.
- 03 Body: Soehne Buch 400, 8.5 pt / 12.5 pt, Book 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Soehne Buch 400, 7.5 pt / 11 pt, Book 400, tracking +12 units.

Signature move: NORDHEIM stays in caps tracked plus 40 units, pinned between two 0.4 pt hairlines set 3 mm apart with ATELIER floating in the band between them in tracked sans caps, so the double rule registers before the letterforms do.

Rules:
- Never collapse the two 0.4 pt hairlines into a single rule.
- The wordmark is always the whole lockup: caps wordmark, both hairlines 3 mm apart, and ATELIER in tracked sans caps between them.
- NORDHEIM is never set below plus 40 units of tracking.
- The double rule frames the wordmark only, never a season title or a look name.
- Prices are Bitter Medium oldstyle figures at 11 pt and live only on the linesheet, where buyers expect them.
- No sale, discount or shouted treatment anywhere in the collateral.
- Captions stay 7.5 pt Soehne Buch in Cool Grey, never in the slab.

## Voice
Spare and factual, closer to an investigative magazine than a catalogue. Names the fabric and the maker and leaves the rest. Example line: "Undyed Gotland wool, cut on the straight, made in Copenhagen."

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark exists in only one place: a single letterpress card from the first season, photographed. The original artwork is gone. The mark is a compound lockup (wordmark, a hairline rule, and ATELIER in small caps) and has to be recovered whole from that photograph. No live logo files or type spec survive.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
