# Corner & Cure

Corner & Cure is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Brooklyn, New York, and this commission covers phase 1: food image system and seasonal launch assets.

## Audience
Neighbourhood regulars aged 25 to 45 who come in twice a week, know the counter staff, and read the screens over the pass from across the room. Local, food-literate, loyal, and quick to distrust anything that reads as styled to death.

## Positioning
Mid-market all-day cafe. Mains 16 to 24 dollars, wine by the glass 11 to 15. Sits comfortably above fast-casual counters and well below the tasting-menu rooms downtown.

## Palette
- Burnt Butter: #B8823A · optional accent. The hairline rule between the allergen line and the provenance line, as on last spring's card. Never type, never a ground.
- Scrubbed Oak: #C9B79C · optional accent. Hairline rules and dividers only. Never type, never a ground.
- House Cream: #F4ECDD · mandatory. The ground of every card, behind all type, as on last spring's card (dishcard_2025_print.pdf). The only ground colour.
- Deep Olive: #4A5233 · optional, body type only. The alternative to Bread Crust for the description, allergen line, provenance line, price and restaurant name. Never the dish name, never a ground or panel.
- Pass Marble: #E7E3DB · optional accent. The fill of the photo frame where no photograph shows, as on last spring's card. Never type, never a ground behind type.
- Bread Crust: #7A4A24 · mandatory. The dish name on every card, as on last spring's card. May also carry the description, allergen line, provenance line, price and restaurant name. Never a ground.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: The card ground is House Cream #F4ECDD and nothing else. Type sits on House Cream in Bread Crust #7A4A24 or Deep Olive #4A5233, never over the hero photograph, as on last spring's card (dishcard_2025_print.pdf), which sets all its type below the photograph. The dish name is always Bread Crust, and the body copy (description, allergen line, provenance line, price, restaurant name) takes one of the two, the same on all ten cards. Burnt Butter, Scrubbed Oak and Pass Marble are accents for rules, dividers and the photo frame fill only; together they cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never type or a ground behind type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; no scrim, gradient, panel or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the hero photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules or icons. That includes the body ink #33302B on last spring's card (dishcard_2025_print.pdf), which is not a palette colour and is not carried over.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The burnt-butter sauce in service_shot_01.jpg keeps its hue with no orange cast added; its tone and contrast may change to separate it from the bowl. No frame is given an orange cast: the owner's words are "warm but never orange" (house_words.txt).

## Typography
Display face: Ogg Roman. Text face: National 2.

- 01 Display / H1: Ogg Roman, 34 pt / 37 pt, Roman, tracking 0 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Ogg Roman, 16 pt / 19 pt, Roman, tracking 0 units.
- 03 Body: National 2, 10.5 pt / 14 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0 units.
- 04 Fine print: National 2, 9 pt / 12 pt, Medium 500, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: Dish names in Ogg Roman run more than three times the size of the description, 34 pt over 10.5 pt on 14 pt, with the tall ascenders left uncrowded, and italic is kept for the grower credit alone.

Rules:
- Never set an allergen line in Ogg.
- Italic belongs to the grower credit alone.
- Dish names take no capitals, no rule and no tracking.
- The dish name always runs at more than three times the size of its description, with the ascenders given full air.
- Descriptions, the allergen line and prices are National 2, Regular or Medium 500, with lining figures.
- Approved copy is never reworded or cut, because the allergen line matters.

## Voice
Plain, warm, never fussy. We talk about burnt butter, the good bread and scrubbed oak, and we let the food carry the sentence. Example line: 'Small plates and a glass of something, most nights until late.'

## Supplied Identity
No brand kit survives. The printed table dish cards exist only as the flat file our printer sent to press last spring, and she has since retired, so there is no working layout and no editable source. The photographed dishes and the till copy Danny writes are the only current materials, and both are considered final.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
