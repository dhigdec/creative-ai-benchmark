# SB3-002-PHO | Corner & Cure - Spring dish launch photography

Spring dish photography and a reusable printed card set for Corner & Cure

The spring dishes are ready. The photographs and printed cards are not. We run Corner & Cure, a neighbourhood cafe, and need someone comfortable moving between food retouching and small-format print production.

The commission

 Spring dish counter card: 11 named exports. The hero photograph shows the named dish. Each card carries its dish's name, description, allergen line, provenance line (our grower credit) and price, and the description is readable at printed size. The plate retains natural food texture. The restaurant name, Corner & Cure, is visible on every card.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Dish names keep the capitals Danny writes in the till copy; our 'no capitals' rule means never all capitals or small capitals. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
