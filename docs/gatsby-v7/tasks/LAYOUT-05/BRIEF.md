# SB3-050-LAY | Maison Valcere - Brand story deck and campaign document

An appointment-season story deck with a consistent jewellery image library

Maison Valcere needs its house-story and campaign documents ready for the autumn appointment season. The bench photography spans two sittings, and it does not yet sit together as a collection.

The commission

 House-story presentation: 1 named export. The sequence communicates the house's approved story from valcere_copy.txt: the tagline, the house, the provenance paragraph and the appointment, and it shows pieces from valcere_book.csv in their bench photographs. The deck shows the house packaging, the swing tag or the spec card, and it matches its supplied artwork, valcere_swing_tag.jpg or valcere_spec_card_PRINT.pdf.

 House campaign poster: 1 named export. The campaign identifies Maison Valcere with its wordmark, the house name stacked MAISON over VALCÈRE. The product remains recognisable: one piece from valcere_book.csv, shown in a bench photograph of that piece.

 House campaign feed ad: 1 named export. The approved message, the tagline “One object at a time.” from valcere_copy.txt, is readable, and the ad carries the stacked house name and one piece from valcere_book.csv in a bench photograph of that piece. The brand treatment reflects the stated price position: any price is shown discreetly, no larger than the body text, or not at all.

 House campaign story: 1 named export. The stacked house name and the action line, the approved appointment line “The autumn book is shown by appointment above the shop.”, remain inside the safe area.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. Wherever the wordmark appears, it is the house mark recovered from the swing tag photograph valcere_swing_tag.jpg, restacked MAISON over VALCÈRE; Cormorant Garamond sets the other display type. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
