# Maison Valcere

Maison Valcere is a four-person high-jewellery house working to order for marriages and milestones. Twenty two references sit in the current book, most cut and set in-house and shown by appointment above the shop. The house sells quiet, considered pieces to clients who return once a decade, not once a season.

## Audience
Clients aged 35 to 60 buying for a marriage or a milestone, plus a short list of premium stockists. Considered, appointment-led buyers who research before they visit and expect the metal, stone and carat answered without asking.

## Positioning
Pieces run 900 to 6,500 dollars, positioned as appointment fine jewellery well above a mall counter and just below the named international maisons. Price is shown discreetly or not at all; the room and the book carry the value.

## Palette
- House Black: #0B0B0D · mandatory. The house name, headings and body text on Bench Ivory, as the piece name and spec lines on valcere_spec_card_PRINT.pdf and the wordmark in valcere_copy.txt; the only dark ground, which the reversed wordmark sits on.
- Bench Ivory: #F4F1EA · mandatory for any light ground or panel; the only light ground permitted. The light ground for pages and panels, as on valcere_spec_card_PRINT.pdf, and the ground the wordmark sits on in House Black. Never a type colour.
- Signature White: #FFFFFF · mandatory where type is reversed on House Black. Type reversed out of House Black, the wordmark included, as valcere_copy.txt sets it. Never a ground or panel.
- Fine Grey: #8A8A8F · optional accent. Reference codes, small labels and fine print on Bench Ivory, as the reference line on valcere_spec_card_PRINT.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, the house name or body text.
- Warm Gold: #C6A15B · optional accent. Hairline rules only, as the single rule on valcere_spec_card_PRINT.pdf. Never type, a ground, a panel or a fill.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Bench Ivory or House Black and nothing else. On Bench Ivory, the house name, headings and body text are House Black, and reference codes, labels and fine print may be Fine Grey, as on valcere_spec_card_PRINT.pdf. On House Black, all type is Signature White, as the reversed wordmark in valcere_copy.txt. The wordmark is House Black on Bench Ivory or Signature White on House Black only. Warm Gold is for hairline rules only. Fine Grey and Warm Gold together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, as on the house's own spec card. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on a solid ground or on a solid panel beside or over the photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No Warm Gold, gold, metallic, foil or glow effect on any type, and no icon, symbol or ornament: the identity is purely typographic (valcere_copy.txt).
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: the bench sweep is not tinted Bench Ivory, yellow gold is not pushed toward Warm Gold, and the sapphire, emerald, ruby and aquamarine keep their own hue.

## Typography
Display face: Cormorant Garamond. Text face: Apercu.

- 01 Display / H1, the stacked house name: Cormorant Garamond, caps, 44 pt / 48 pt, Light 300, tracking +160 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Cormorant Garamond, caps, 22 pt / 26 pt, Light 300, tracking +120 units.
- 03 Body: Apercu, 11 pt / 16 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Apercu, 8.5 pt / 13 pt, Regular, tracking +20 units.

Signature move: The house name always stacks on two lines, MAISON over VALCÈRE, in Cormorant Garamond Light caps tracked 160 units, with the second line letterspaced to the exact width of the first so the pair locks into one hairline block.

Rules:
- Never set the house name on one line. MAISON always stacks over VALCÈRE.
- The lower line is letterspaced to the exact width of the upper so the two lock into one hairline block.
- The grave accent on VALCÈRE stays intact at every size.
- Never set display heavier than Light 300.
- Display caps always track 160 units.
- Display holds at 44 pt in the deck, 96 pt on the A2 poster and 64 px on the feed ad.
- Apercu Regular sets specifications and carries every figure, carat, reference and millimetre.
- Price is shown discreetly or not at all.
- The 22 pt subheading is the one size not already stated in the spec, set at exactly half the deck display.

## Voice
Plain, precise, never salesy. States materials and provenance and stops. Example line: "Eighteen karat white gold, set with a single old-cut stone, made to your hand."

## Supplied Identity
The original wordmark artwork left with the designer who set it a decade ago. The only surviving version is a photograph of an old printed swing tag, so the mark has to be recovered from that before it can be used again. No usable logo files, colour references or type spec survive; these guidelines are being reconstructed this season.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
