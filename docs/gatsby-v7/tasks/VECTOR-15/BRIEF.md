# SB3-045-VEC | Marchfield Exchange - Crest and engraving geometry system

Restore the Exchange crest and rebuild the suite-plate workflow

Marchfield Exchange is a listed 1911 building. The crest needs recovering from brass and letterhead references; the suite plates need updating from the 2019 production PDF. We are preserving an identity and an installation, not commissioning a redesign.

Deliverables
1. Marchfield shield-and-wreath crest: 2 named exports. The shield, the keystone device the wreath encloses, matches the authoritative source crest. The wreath matches the authoritative source crest. The crest's original lettering, MCMXI on its ribbon, is preserved as drawn on the source crest.
2. Marchfield engraving artwork: 1 named export. The original crest identity survives the production simplification. Small counters remain open at 32 mm. The thinnest engraved stroke is at least 0.50 mm at a 32 mm crest width.
3. Suite plate: 15 named exports. The suite number matches this schedule record, and the firm name beneath it is exactly as the schedule gives it, with no word added. The approved shield-and-wreath crest is used, taken from the engraving artwork, with the building name and MCMXI beneath it as the brand type system sets for a suite plate. The artwork leaves a 12 mm clear margin.
4. Entrance plaque artwork: 1 named export. The crest is 180 mm across. Artwork stays at least 15 mm from each bolt centre, and the four bolt centres, one near each corner, are shown as guide marks that are not engraved. The building name is correct.
5. Lobby directory artwork: 1 named export. The crest is 120 mm across. Artwork leaves a 20 mm clear margin. Tenants appear in reconciled suite order, and the board lists tenant firms only.
6. Brass reproduction proof: 1 named export. The proof shows the crest on satin brass. The proof states the crest's reproduction size for the entrance plaque, the directory board and the suite plate, and each stated size is accurate. The proof names each engraved detail, crest lines and lettering alike, that falls under the 0.50 mm stroke limit at the size it is engraved on the entrance plaque, the directory board and the suite plate.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.
