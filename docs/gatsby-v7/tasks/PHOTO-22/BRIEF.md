# SB3-022-PHO | Nordhaven - Harbour hotel booking campaign

Give a small harbour hotel a distinct look without losing room accuracy

Nordhaven occupies a harbour warehouse with fourteen rooms. We want a distinctive image treatment for the brand, while keeping one room hero true to colour so guests can see what they are booking.

Please prepare
- Harbour hotel booking hero: 1 named export. The scene is the supplied Nordhaven property: the guest-room frame from the shoot that shows the whole bed and its linen. The booking action is clear: our name, Nordhaven, and one line asking the guest to book direct with us; any rate shown with it is the pictured room's own nightly rate from nordhaven_rooms.csv, not our general $600 a night band.
- Hotel amenity panel: 1 named export. The named amenities are the three in our brand note, the carafe, the robe and the brass key fob, each cut out from its own supplied photograph and set on our near-black ground. The information is easy to scan.
- Hotel room campaign: 14 named exports. The room photograph matches this room category, and the card carries that room's name, floor and bed label, view line and nightly rate from nordhaven_rooms.csv. The advertised rate preserves its supplied conditions: it keeps any per-night wording its row in nordhaven_rooms.csv carries and adds no from, discount or offer wording.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
