# Nordhaven

Nordhaven is a fourteen-room hotel inside a converted brick and timber warehouse on the working harbour. We sell the feeling of the building: cold light, plaster, oak and brass. Guests choose us because the place looks like nowhere else, and everything we make has to carry that.

## Audience
Design-literate urban travellers, 30 to 45, who choose a property on aesthetic and book direct once they trust the point of view.

## Positioning
Six hundred dollars a night, direct only, positioned above the travel-platform grid as a design destination rather than a room count.

## Palette
- Near-black ink: #14161A · mandatory. The ground of the booking hero and the amenity panel, as the brand note fixes it, and the field behind every room name; also the ink for any type set on a Limewash plaster panel, as on nordhaven_room_card_2025.pdf.
- Limewash plaster: #E7E2D8 · mandatory. Every room name and all other type set on Near-black ink; also the one other permitted ground or panel, as on nordhaven_room_card_2025.pdf, where the type on it is Near-black ink.
- Harbour slate: #4A535C · optional accent. Hairline rules, dividers and icons only. Never a ground, a panel or any type.
- Oak: #6B5945 · optional accent. Hairline rules, dividers and icons only. Never a ground, a panel or any type.
- Brass: #A8823E · optional accent. Hairline rules, dividers and icons only. Never a ground, a panel or any type.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Near-black ink #14161A is the ground of the booking hero and the amenity panel and the field behind every room name. Limewash plaster #E7E2D8 is the only other ground or panel, as on nordhaven_room_card_2025.pdf. Every room name is Limewash plaster on Near-black ink; other type on Near-black ink is Limewash plaster, and type on Limewash plaster is Near-black ink. Harbour slate, Oak and Brass are accents for hairline rules, dividers and icons only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel or any type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on a solid Near-black ink or Limewash plaster ground or panel, beside the photograph or over it as a solid block, never directly on photographic pixels.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; no drop shadow or gradient on any of them, since every palette colour is used solid; nothing cheerful or saturated, as the brand note asks.
- Photography: Apart from the main room photograph on the booking hero, which stays true colour with no tint, tone or overlay, every photograph on every output, amenity cut-outs included, carries the same one committed tone, restrained and cold as the brand is, never cheerful or saturated. The two visits are brought onto one base before that tone is committed, and the tone may change colour and contrast but never what a subject or material is.

## Typography
Display face: Zodiak. Text face: Söhne.

- 01 Display / H1: Zodiak Light, 96 px / 101 px, Light, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Zodiak Light, 56 px / 62 px, Light, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body: Söhne Regular, 24 px / 36 px, Regular, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Söhne Regular, 18 px / 26 px, Regular, tracking +20 units.

Signature move: A one word room name in limewash on near black at 96 px with its floor and bed label locked directly beneath in Söhne Medium caps at 18 px, tracked 220 units, a fifth the size, so the drop between them is the first thing a viewer notices. Step 01 carries the name, and the 18 px tracked caps of step 04 carry the label.

Rules:
- Never set a rate larger than its room name.
- The room name is one word, limewash on near black.
- The floor and bed label locks directly beneath the room name at a fifth its size, in Söhne Medium caps tracked 220 units.
- Room names and display lines set in Zodiak Light; running text, amenities and rates set in Söhne.

## Voice
Spare, understated, first person, no exclamation. Example line: 'Fourteen rooms on the water. We open direct booking this autumn.'

## Supplied Identity
We work from a short brand note that fixes the near-black ground, the placements and the delivered sizes. There is no full guideline system yet, and the room list changes each season.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
