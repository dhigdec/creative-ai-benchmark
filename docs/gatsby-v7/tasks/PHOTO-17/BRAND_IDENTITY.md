# Continental Cup 2026 Collector Card Programme

Continental Cup 2026 Collector Card Programme is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is touring competition, Western Europe and North America, and this commission covers phase 1: collector-card portrait finishing.

## Audience
Collectors aged 16 to 45 who buy officially licensed cup sets, know a licensed release from a fan-made one at a glance, and expect deep, saturated, high-contrast presentation worthy of a marquee tournament.

## Positioning
Premium licensed collector tier. Pack twelve dollars, sealed box ninety-five, featured cards trading roughly 30 to 120 dollars secondary. The treatment is deep and saturated with real floodlit blacks, one dominant player per card, type set large and confident. Never a flat, bright community-league sheet, and never a neon gaming poster.

## Palette
- Floodlit Black (dominant ground): #0B0E14 · mandatory. The dominant ground: solid through the upper 58% of the card, around and behind the player photograph, then fading into Pitch Deep Navy by the bottom edge, as on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never type, rules or icons.
- Pitch Deep Navy (retailer display and card field): #101B33 · mandatory. The card field behind the player data in the lower third, reached by the fade from Floodlit Black, as on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never type, rules or icons.
- Stadium Silver (metallic accent and rules): #C7CCD1 · mandatory. The 0.75 pt rule the surname locks onto, the surname, the position and honours line, the licensing line, and the silver parts of the crest; the name, position and honours line are Stadium Silver on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. May also draw a hairline frame inside the trim. Never a ground or panel.
- Cup Gold (marquee accent): #C9A233 · mandatory in the crest, otherwise an optional accent. The gold parts of the crest, its outer ring at least, as the gold thread on cup26_sleeve_patch.jpg and the #C9A233 ring on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf; optionally the set year and the divider rule between photograph and data, both Cup Gold on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, the surname or the data line.
- Signal Red (highlight and squad number): #B32433 · mandatory for the squad number. The squad number on every card (type system step 02), as on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Otherwise only a small highlight; never a ground, a panel, the surname or the data line.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds are Floodlit Black #0B0E14 and Pitch Deep Navy #101B33 only: Floodlit Black is the dominant ground and Pitch Deep Navy the card field behind the player data, as on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Type on these grounds is Stadium Silver #C7CCD1, except the squad number, which is always Signal Red #B32433, and the set year, which may be Cup Gold #C9A233. Cup Gold and Signal Red are accents: apart from the squad number and the crest, together they cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, and neither is ever a ground, a panel, the surname or the data line.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. One gradient is allowed, as on cup26_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf: the card ground runs solid Floodlit Black #0B0E14 through its upper 58% and fades to Pitch Deep Navy #101B33 toward the bottom edge. No other tint, transparency, gradient, glow, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a player photograph.
- Forbidden: No neon or fluorescent colour and no glow on any type, rule, panel or icon (never a neon gaming poster); no white or bright ground (never the flat, bright treatment of a community league sheet); and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, icons or the crest, apart from the permitted fade between Floodlit Black #0B0E14 and Pitch Deep Navy #101B33 on the ground.
- Photography: Player photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: once a cast is removed, every kit still reads its own hue, so the dark navy goalkeeper kit in cup26_portrait_petrov.jpg is not pushed toward Pitch Deep Navy #101B33 and the deep-red kit in cup26_portrait_solano.jpg is not pushed toward Signal Red #B32433.

## Typography
Display face: Young Serif (Regular). Text face: Benton Sans (Regular and Bold).

- 01 Display / H1, player surname: Young Serif Regular, 42 pt / 44 pt, Regular, tracking +20 units, caps.
- 02 Subheading / H2, squad number: Benton Sans Bold, 34 pt / 36 pt, Bold, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body, position and data line: Benton Sans Regular, 7.5 pt / 11 pt, Regular, tracking 0 units, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print, licensing line: Benton Sans Regular, 5 pt / 6.5 pt, Regular, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: The silver bar. Every player surname is set in Young Serif caps, tracked 20 units, locked onto a 0.75 pt Stadium Silver rule that runs the full trim width, at a cap height exactly four times the data line, like an honours board engraving.

Rules:
- Never condense Young Serif.
- The surname always sits in caps, tracked 20 units.
- The surname always locks onto a 0.75 pt Stadium Silver rule running the full trim width.
- Surname cap height stays exactly four times the data line, which is 30 pt of cap against the 7.5 pt line.
- Print the licensing copy exactly as supplied, with nothing cut to fit.
- One dominant player per card, and one surname lockup per card.
- Type is set large and confident: never the flat, bright treatment of a community league sheet, and never a neon gaming poster.

## Voice
Bold, official and celebratory without slang. Speaks with the authority of a sanctioned tournament: it names the players, states the honours and lets the floodlit imagery carry the drama.

## Supplied Identity
The brand files are largely lost. The studio that designed last year's card has closed, and all that survives is the flat file the printer ran, whose layout the retailers already recognise and want kept. The competition crest was never held as artwork at all: the only reference is a daylight photograph of the embroidered patch on an official's sleeve. The recovered mark and the rebuilt card layout therefore have to be reconstructed from those remnants and carried forward as the programme's working assets.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
