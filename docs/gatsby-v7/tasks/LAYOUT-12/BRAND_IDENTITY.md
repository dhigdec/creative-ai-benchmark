# Umbra Loft

Umbra Loft is a two hundred and fifty capacity basement room under an old mill in Providence, programming jazz and after dark nights for a crowd that finds us through friends and phones at eleven at night. This autumn we are running nine nights as one series rather than nine separate shows. It has to look like one thing, and it has to look like the room it actually is: dark, close and made by hand.

## Audience
A scene literate, discovery driven crowd aged twenty one to thirty five, reached almost entirely on a phone late at night, who read polish as inauthentic and reward work that plainly comes from the room.

## Positioning
Tickets 18 to 32 dollars, capacity 250. Independent basement scale: deliberately made and high contrast, credible at a headline ticket without ever reading as corporate or sponsored programming.

## Palette
- Pitch: #0E0E10 · mandatory. The main ground of every poster, post and deck page, and always the solid ground under an act block (type rule: Bone White on Pitch), as on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf. Never type.
- Ash: #2A2A2E · optional. A secondary solid panel only, such as the frame behind a photograph on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf, or behind a table or chart in the deck. Never type and never the ground under an act block.
- Sodium: #E4863B · mandatory for every date line. The date line directly above each act block (type rule and step 02), as on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf; it may also set the advance price and hairline rules, as that card does. Never a ground or a panel.
- Cold Signal: #3F7C8C · optional accent. Hairline rules, dividers and chart marks, or one of the two colours of a photograph's treatment. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Bone White: #E9E4D8 · mandatory. Every act name (type rule: Bone White on Pitch), and every other line of type apart from the Sodium date line and advance price: titles, headings, support, door, set, body, billing, table and fine-print lines, as on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf. Never a ground or a panel.
- Blood: #8E2B24 · optional accent. Hairline rules, dividers and chart marks, or one of the two colours of a photograph's treatment. Never type, a ground or a panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Pitch or Ash and nothing else; Pitch is the main ground and always the ground under an act block. Every act name is Bone White on Pitch with its date line directly above it in Sodium, as the type rules require. Every other line of type, titles, headings, support, door, set, body, billing, table and fine-print lines included, is Bone White; the advance price may be Sodium, as on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf. Pitch and Ash are never type. Cold Signal and Blood are accents for hairline rules, dividers and chart marks only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never type, a ground or a panel. Any palette colour may be one of the two colours of a photograph's treatment.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, for type, grounds, panels, rules, chart marks and recovered act wordmarks: no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph. The translucent type and rule of last season's umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf (printed at 86%, 78%, 70% and 55% opacity) are not carried over. The one place two palette colours mix is inside a photograph, in its two-colour treatment.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, chart marks, icons or recovered act wordmarks; a recovered act wordmark takes one solid palette colour. No sponsor logo or corporate partner mark, since the type rules forbid anything that reads as corporate or sponsored programming.
- Photography: Every photograph carries the series two-colour and grain treatment: rendered in two palette colours with visible grain, never in full colour, and the nine night release posts share one pair so six different rigs read as one series. Photographs are used as shot, with nothing generated, extended, added or painted in.

## Typography
Display face: Sora ExtraBold. Text face: Archivo Narrow Regular, SemiBold for the door line.

- 01 Display / H1: Sora, 180 pt / 148 pt, ExtraBold, tracking -10 units, all caps.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Sora, 46 pt / 50 pt, ExtraBold, tracking +20 units, all caps.
- 03 Body: Archivo Narrow, 11 pt / 15 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Archivo Narrow, 9 pt / 13 pt, SemiBold, tracking +30 units, all caps.

Signature move: The two line stack. Every act name is Sora ExtraBold caps broken onto exactly two lines whatever its length, with leading closed to 0.82 of the size, 148 pt at the A2 size of 180 pt and 123 px at the 150 px post size, so the lines almost collide into one solid block of squared caps with no air inside it, Bone White on Pitch with the date above in Sodium.

Rules:
- Never set an act name on one line.
- Every act name is caps, broken onto exactly two lines whatever its length, with leading closed to 0.82 of the size.
- The act block is Bone White on Pitch, with the date directly above it in Sodium.
- Archivo Narrow Regular sets billing and tables. SemiBold is reserved for the door line and sets nothing else.
- Sora ExtraBold is display only. It never sets billing, tables or body.
- Everything comes from this room, on these nights. It must never read as corporate or sponsored programming.

## Voice
Terse, atmospheric, unvarnished. It names the act, the night and the door and gets out of the way. Example line the brand would write: "Doors 8. Downstairs. Two sets, one room, cash on the night."

## Supplied Identity
We build the look from our own archive, six photographs shot on six nights under six touring rigs, no two matching. Three headline acts sent their wordmarks only as photographs of tour shirts and a tote, which their managers say is all that survives. There is no defined palette, type or treatment yet, and last season's printed card exists only as the press PDF our printer returned.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
