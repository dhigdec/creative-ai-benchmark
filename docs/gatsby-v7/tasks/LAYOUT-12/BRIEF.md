# SB3-057-LAY | Umbra Loft - After Dark event campaign

After Dark: a coherent poster and social campaign from six different nights

Umbra Loft's autumn programme needs to feel like a series, even though our six usable archive photographs were shot under six different lighting rigs. We want a strong two-colour and grain treatment that actually lives in the images, not a collection of unrelated pictures behind matching text.

Deliverables
1. After Dark poster direction: 3 named exports. The billing order follows the supplied list, and each poster direction bills every night of the autumn series with its date; every act name is typeset in the series type, and a recovered wordmark, where an act has one, appears with it rather than in place of it. The event information is readable.
2. Promoter pitch deck: 1 named export. The three directions are meaningfully distinct. The sequence explains the series identity, and the deck charts last season's average draw by month from umbra_room_specs.txt.
3. Night release post: 9 named exports. The date matches this event, and the post carries that night's act, support act, door time, set time and advance price, with one of the six archive photographs in the two-colour and grain treatment; the act name is typeset in the series type, and a recovered wordmark, where the act has one, appears with it rather than in place of it. The advance price is readable.
