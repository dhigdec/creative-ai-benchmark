# NIGHTFORM

NIGHTFORM is a self released electronic project built on mood: late, atmospheric, cinematic tracks that sound like driving at night. The next single, Low Beam, is bought on feeling and on the sense that one person made the whole thing, so every visual has to carry the same nocturnal weight as the music.

## Audience
Streaming listeners roughly nineteen to thirty four who follow the mood. Physical buyers pay twelve to twenty eight dollars for vinyl and cassette through independent shops.

## Positioning
Independent, no label, priced as a self released record. The look has to be dark, grainy and slightly rough, never bright, clean and commercial, which would read as a car advert or a stock reel. Total blackness is the other failure: the road still has to be legible on a phone in daylight.

## Palette
- Sodium Amber: #C77A2E · optional accent. A thin rule, such as the rule beneath the wordmark on nightform_card_2024.pdf. Never a ground, a panel or a line of type.
- Asphalt Black: #0C0E12 · mandatory where type sits on a Tunnel White board. All type on a Tunnel White board, as on nightform_card_2024.pdf, and a solid dark ground or panel behind Tunnel White type. Never a type colour over footage.
- Tunnel White: #E8E4DC · mandatory. The colour of every line of type on the films and the cover, set over night footage or a solid Asphalt Black, Wet Slate or Low Beam Grey panel: the title that pairs with the wordmark as the second of the two white lines, the release line, captions, credits and fine print. A ground only on printed board, as the board of nightform_card_2024.pdf. The wordmark itself keeps the white of nightform_wordmark.png.
- Wet Slate: #1E2530 · optional. A solid dark ground or panel behind Tunnel White type, in place of Asphalt Black. Not a type colour on a dark ground, where it would not read.
- Low Beam Grey: #4A4F58 · optional. Rules, dividers or a solid panel behind Tunnel White type. Not a type colour on a dark ground, where it would not read.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: On the films and the cover, grounds are night footage or a solid Asphalt Black, Wet Slate or Low Beam Grey panel, and every line of type is Tunnel White, while the wordmark keeps the white of nightform_wordmark.png. A Tunnel White ground belongs only to printed board, where type is Asphalt Black with a thin Sodium Amber rule beneath the wordmark, as on nightform_card_2024.pdf. Sodium Amber is a thin rule or divider only, never a ground, a panel or type; Sodium Amber and Low Beam Grey rules and dividers together take no more than 5% of the designed, non-footage area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; no scrim, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over footage or a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules or icons (the recovered wordmark keeps its own artwork); no texture or distress on any type, since nothing is ornamented or distressed; no bright, clean, commercial treatment of type or ground.
- Photography: Footage keeps its own colour and is not recoloured toward the palette hexes: sodium lamps stay amber and the road stays its own colour. It is never lifted into a bright, clean commercial look; a darker, grainier grade toward the brand's night look is allowed as long as the road still reads on a phone in daylight. The near-black low-key ground of nightform_low_beam_take.mov is not flattened to Asphalt Black.

## Typography
Display face: Folio. Text face: Söhne.

- 01 Display / H1: Folio, 96 px / 104 px, Medium, tracking 0.24 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Folio, 72 px / 80 px, Medium, tracking 0.24 em.
- 03 Body: Söhne, 30 px / 42 px, Buch 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Söhne, 22 px / 30 px, Buch 400, tracking +0.04 em.

Signature move: Width matching. The title at step 01 is Folio Medium capitals letterspaced 0.24 em and optically fitted to the exact width of the recovered wordmark above it, so every cover reads as two white lines of identical measure, one tight and one airy. Step 02 holds the same 0.24 em and the same fit at teaser size.

Rules:
- Never break a title onto two lines.
- The recovered wordmark stays untouched artwork and is never retyped.
- The title is always optically fitted to the exact width of the wordmark above it, at 0.24 em letterspacing.
- Numerals are tabular lining.
- Never bright, clean and commercial; never so black the road stops reading on a phone in daylight.
- Anything text bearing stays editable up to the drop date.

## Voice
Spare and evocative, closer to a liner note than a sales line. Example line the brand would write: "Low Beam. Recorded from the passenger seat, north of the city, around eleven at night."

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark survives only as a flat exported picture: separated white capitals on a solid black square, from a source file no one still has. It has to be recovered as usable artwork. The previous release's sleeve card exists only as the printer's press PDF, and the whole visual identity for this single comes out of one forty five second night take.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
