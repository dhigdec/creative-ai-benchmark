# SB3-009-PHO | OSTRA - Restaurant plate photography system

OSTRA tasting-menu images: dark, precise and still appetising

OSTRA is a 22-seat tasting counter. We have thirteen plate photographs from two services, and we need them to feel like the same room and the same menu. Dark should not mean muddy; glaze and food texture still need to read.

Deliverables
1. Seasonal menu announcement: 1 named export. OSTRA is identified by its wordmark, with the house mark from the brass plate in ostra_house_mark_plate.jpg reproduced above it. The featured dish is this season's opening course, Course 1 in ostra_room_note.txt, shown in its own photograph and titled exactly as the kitchen spells it. The reservation action is visible.
2. Booking-page dish tile: 13 named exports. The caption is the pictured dish's course title as the kitchen spells it in ostra_room_note.txt, variant substitutions included, and a listed course keeps its title however it is plated or dressed that service; a plate that is none of those courses is captioned plainly with what is on the plate, never with a course title. The food is recognisable at mobile listing size.
3. Reservation story: 1 named export. The booking action sits within the defined safe area. The restaurant name is readable at mobile size, set as its wordmark with the house mark from ostra_house_mark_plate.jpg reproduced above it.
