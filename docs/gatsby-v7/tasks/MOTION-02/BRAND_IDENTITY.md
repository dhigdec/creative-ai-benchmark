# Ironwood Kitchen

Ironwood Kitchen builds honest, high protein bowls for the office lunch run, in the fridge case by mid morning and gone by early afternoon. We are a working kitchen, not a tasting room. The promise is simple: food you would actually eat at your desk, twelve dollars, nothing invented and nothing dressed up.

## Audience
Office workers 24 to 45 on a weekday lunch run with roughly eleven minutes to spend, buying on macros, speed and trust rather than on spectacle.

## Positioning
Twelve dollars a bowl, the everyday middle of grab and go. Above a supermarket meal deal, well below a chef counter. We are not a tasting menu and never pretend to be one.

## Palette
- Bench White: #F6F5F1 · mandatory. The main ground and panel colour, as on ironwood_linesheet_2027.pdf; the ground under every bowl name and price line; and the type colour on Field Green.
- Ink Charcoal: #23262A · mandatory. Type only: the bowl name (as on ironwood_linesheet_2027.pdf), any closing line, the allergen line and the macro figures, all on Bench White. Never a ground or panel.
- Field Green: #3F7D4E · optional. A solid ground for a closing line set in Bench White (type system step 02), and the allergen line colour on Bench White, as on ironwood_linesheet_2027.pdf. Never a bowl name, price or macro figure.
- Salsa Clay: #E2593B · mandatory for the price figure. The price figure “$12” and nothing else, the only use ironwood_linesheet_2027.pdf makes of it, set level with the bowl name as the type system asks. Never a ground, panel, bowl name or body text.
- Steel Grey: #9AA0A6 · optional, fine print only. Fine print on Bench White only, such as counter, route or time details (type system step 04), as the slug on ironwood_linesheet_2027.pdf. Never the allergen and macro line, a name, a price, a ground or a panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Bench White or Field Green and nothing else. On Bench White, the bowl name, any closing line and body are Ink Charcoal, the price figure is Salsa Clay, the allergen line is Ink Charcoal or Field Green (as ironwood_linesheet_2027.pdf sets it; Field Green on Bench White counts as full contrast for the allergen line only), the macro figures are Ink Charcoal, and fine print may be Steel Grey. On Field Green, all type is Bench White, the ironwood_wordmark.png wordmark excepted. The bowl name and price line and the allergen and macro line always sit on Bench White. Salsa Clay covers the price figure alone and Steel Grey fine print alone. All on-screen type sits on a solid Bench White or Field Green ground or panel, never straight on footage or a still.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or type, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage or a still: type over a shot sits on a solid Bench White or Field Green panel. Cuts and short dissolves between shots are transitions, not tints; colour and opacity are judged on a settled frame.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; the supplied footage, the bowl stills and the ironwood_wordmark.png letterforms are the only exceptions. No Steel Grey on the allergen and macro line. No badge, roundel or sticker behind the price. No outline, glow, drop shadow, gradient, bevel or metallic effect on any type or panel.
- Photography: The footage and the bowl stills keep their own colour and are never recoloured toward the palette; any correction keeps each dish's food colours true to life.

## Typography
Display face: Schibsted Grotesk Bold. Text face: Schibsted Grotesk Regular.

- 01 Display / H1 · bowl name and price, one baseline: Schibsted Grotesk Bold, 88 px / 96 px, Bold, tracking minus 0.020 em, which is the minus two percent in the brief.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · closing line: Schibsted Grotesk Medium, 72 px / 80 px, Medium, tracking minus 0.015 em.
- 03 Body · allergen and macro line: Schibsted Grotesk Regular, 28 px / 38 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print · counter, routes, times: Schibsted Grotesk Regular, 20 px / 28 px, Regular, tracking plus 0.040 em.

Signature move: The level price. Bowl name and price sit on one shared baseline at exactly the same 88 px, one em apart, name in Ink Charcoal and figure in Salsa Clay, tracked minus 0.020 em, so the twelve dollars is drawn as large as the food it buys and a viewer reads the price first.

Rules:
- Never print the price smaller than the name.
- Name and price share one baseline at an identical size, exactly one em apart, never stacked and never in a badge.
- The name sets in Ink Charcoal and the figure in Salsa Clay, never the reverse.
- The whole package is one family, Schibsted Grotesk; the levels are carried by weight alone, Bold, Medium, Regular.
- The allergen and macro line always runs at full contrast, never greyed back.
- In print, case labels set names 22 pt and body 9 pt on 12 pt.
- One thought per line, and nothing dressed up.

## Voice
Direct, warm, counter honest, one thought per line. Example: "Twelve bucks, in the case by half ten, gone by two. That is the whole promise."

## Supplied Identity
No brand kit survives. The wordmark exists only as a flat picture on a white ground, exported by a designer who has since left, with no vector file and no source anywhere. Everything else is a photograph or a spreadsheet.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
