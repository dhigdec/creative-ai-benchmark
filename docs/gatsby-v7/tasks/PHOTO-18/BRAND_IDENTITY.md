# Fernwell & Rowe

Fernwell & Rowe photograph twenty-two weddings a season across Cotswolds country-house venues. Two people run everything, from the first walk-through to the finished gallery, and they promise couples a record of the actual day rather than a styled magazine shoot. These prints sit on parents' walls for thirty years, so the work stays warm, natural and true to skin.

## Audience
Couples aged 27 to 38 booking a Cotswolds country-house venue eighteen months out and paying for full-day coverage; their parents will look at the prints for decades.

## Positioning
Full-day coverage 3,200 to 5,500 pounds, a mid-to-upper independent band. Above the filtered budget packages, below glossy editorial studios. The finish should feel like a real day, not a preset and not a phone filter.

## Palette
- Fernwell Ink: #2E2A24 · mandatory for running text on Soft Cream. Running text on Soft Cream, such as the household line and message on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never a ground or panel.
- Walled Garden Green: #4A5A3E · optional accent. A small date or credit line, as the date line on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never a ground, and never a grade applied to garden greens.
- Golden Hour: #C9A15A · optional accent. Hairline rules only, as the short rule above the greeting on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never type, a ground, or a warm tint over a photograph.
- Warm Vellum: #EFE7D8 · optional accent. A small pale panel behind the seal, as on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never the main ground and never type.
- Seal Wax Red: #7A2E28 · mandatory for the wordmark ampersand. The Fraunces Italic ampersand of the wordmark, as the type system requires, and the seal mark; on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf it also sets the couple's names. Never a ground, panel or tint.
- Soft Cream: #F7F1E6 · mandatory ground of any designed piece; never added to a review image or keeper. The ground and text panel of a designed piece, as on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never laid over or framed around a photograph export.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: On a designed piece, as on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf: Soft Cream is the ground and text panel; running text on it is Fernwell Ink; Seal Wax Red is the wordmark ampersand, the couple's names and the seal; Walled Garden Green, Warm Vellum and Golden Hour are small accents (a date line, the seal's pale panel, a hairline rule), together no more than 5% of the whole piece and never a ground. The 22 review images and keepers in this pilot are photographs only: no palette colour is added to them.
- Opacity: Type, panels, rules and buttons use every palette colour solid, at 100% opacity, with no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; only the studio seal keeps the translucent wax modelling, and its pale panel the faint inset edge, that they have on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. No scrim, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: the warm house look comes from correcting each photograph's own colour, never from a colour layer.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules or buttons on a designed piece. The studio seal keeps its own wax colours, as in fernwell_seal.jpg and on fernwell_card_2025_PRINTER.pdf. On the review images and keepers, no type, wordmark, seal, watermark, border, panel or colour of any kind is added.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: whites are not cast Golden Hour #C9A15A or Soft Cream #F7F1E6, and garden greens are not pushed toward Walled Garden Green #4A5A3E.

## Typography
Display face: Fraunces (Semibold, optical size 144, wonk on, plus Fraunces Italic for the ampersand). Text face: Gill Sans Nova Light.

- 01 Display / H1, studio name and wordmark: Fraunces Semibold, 48 px / 52 px, Semibold, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2, gallery heading: Fraunces Semibold, 34 px / 38 px, Semibold, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body, caption and running text: Gill Sans Nova Light, 18 px / 26 px, Light, tracking 0 units, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print, dates and credit lines: Gill Sans Nova Light, 14 px / 20 px, Light, tracking +20 units.

Signature move: The hanging ampersand. The wordmark always stacks on two lines, Fernwell above Rowe, with a Fraunces Italic ampersand in Seal Wax Red at 2.4 times the cap height hung outside the left margin, the first mark on a gallery or an envelope.

Rules:
- Never set a caption in bold.
- The wordmark always stacks on two lines, Fernwell above Rowe, and is never set on one line.
- The ampersand is always Fraunces Italic in Seal Wax Red, at 2.4 times the cap height, hung outside the left margin.
- Fraunces always runs at optical size 144 with its wonk left on.
- Dates and captions stay in Gill Sans Nova Light, never in Fraunces.
- The studio name sets 48 px on screen and 30 pt in print, nowhere else on the scale.

## Voice
Warm, plain and first person plural, the two of them talking to a couple. Example line: We would rather pull a frame and tell you than force it.

## Supplied Identity
The studio has no working brand files. The thank-you card survives only as the press PDF the printer ran, the designer who made it two years ago is no longer reachable, and the wax seal the studio presses on every envelope has never existed as a file, only as a photograph of one pressed seal.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
