# STATIC FOX

STATIC FOX is a loud, fast comedy gaming channel built on co op chaos and the creator's on camera reactions. Around four hundred thousand subscribers watch for the disasters, the bad calls and the running jokes. The videos land once people click, so the whole brand fight happens on the thumbnail, in the feed, at a glance.

## Audience
Mostly fifteen to twenty five, watching in a crowded feed on phones. They want energy and payoff, not taste. Income comes from advertising and channel memberships.

## Positioning
Mid tier creator funded by ad revenue and memberships. The work has to be loud, saturated and heavy on the face and the moment, because that is what wins the click at this level. Restrained and tasteful reads as the wrong channel; pure noise stops reading as one channel at all. Loud and coherent is the line.

## Palette
- Fox Orange: #FF5A1F · mandatory. The channel's lead colour, the orange of Scrap's fur in staticfox_scrap_render.png and of the fox-head emblem in staticfox_patch.jpg. In every design it appears in the type, panels, shapes or background, not only inside Scrap: a title fill, Inter type on Jet Black, a solid panel or ground, or a shape.
- Static Purple: #7A2FF2 · optional. Solid grounds, panels and shapes behind the title and Scrap, echoing Scrap's purple headset. Carries Signal White or Acid Signal type, and is itself an Inter type colour on Signal White. Never a title fill or outline.
- Acid Signal: #B6FF2E · optional accent. Title-word fills, Inter lines on Jet Black or Static Purple, and small highlight shapes. Never a ground or panel; leaving title-word fills aside, no more than 10% of the designed, non-footage area.
- Jet Black: #101014 · mandatory wherever a title is set. The hard outline on every title word, as the type system requires; also dark grounds and panels, and Inter type on Fox Orange or Signal White grounds.
- Signal White: #FFFFFF · optional. Title-word fills, Inter type on Jet Black or Static Purple, and light grounds or panels.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are solid Jet Black, Static Purple, Fox Orange or Signal White, and so are shapes, apart from small Acid Signal highlight shapes. Title words (Univers) are filled Signal White, Acid Signal or Fox Orange, the palette colours that reach 4.5:1 against the hard Jet Black outline every title word carries. Inter lines are Signal White, Acid Signal or Fox Orange on Jet Black; Signal White or Acid Signal on Static Purple; Jet Black or Static Purple on Signal White; Jet Black on Fox Orange, the pairs that reach 4.5:1. Fox Orange appears in the type, panels, shapes or background of every design, not only inside Scrap. Acid Signal is an accent: title fills, Inter lines and small highlight shapes only, never a ground or panel; leaving title-word fills aside, it covers no more than 10% of the designed, non-footage area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity: no tint, partial transparency, blur, gradient or blend mode on type, outlines, panels or shapes, and no scrim or colour wash of any colour, black and white included, over a capture frame. A hard, unblurred offset shadow or second stroke in a solid palette colour is allowed. In the films, elements may animate position, scale or opacity, with motion blur, while they move; every held frame, and the final end card, is solid and unblurred. A cut or plain fade between shots is a transition, not a tint.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, outlines, panels, shapes, icons or backgrounds.
- Photography: Capture frames keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured or washed toward the palette; no capture is pushed toward Static Purple #7A2FF2 or any other palette hex. Scrap keeps the orange fur and purple headset drawn in staticfox_scrap_render.png.

## Typography
Display face: Univers. Text face: Inter.

- 01 Display / H1: Univers, 190 px / 200 px, 59 Ultra Condensed, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Univers, 150 px / 165 px, 83 Extra Black Extended, tracking -0.015 em.
- 03 Body: Inter, 54 px / 62 px, SemiBold 600, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Inter, 40 px / 52 px, SemiBold 600, tracking +0.03 em.

Signature move: A width flip inside one family: step 01 runs the setup capitals in Univers 59 Ultra Condensed at 190 px and step 02 jumps the final payoff word to Univers 83 Extra Black Extended at 150 px on the same stack, so a narrow column of capitals fattens hard on the last word. Every word carries the black outline.

Rules:
- Never set the payoff word condensed.
- Titles are capitals only.
- Every word in the title carries a hard black outline.
- On long copy the title stack drops to 96 px rather than breaking the flip.
- Loud and coherent is the line: saturated and heavy, but still one channel.

## Voice
Fast, funny, self deprecating and a bit chaotic, always in on the joke. Example line the brand would write: "We had one job. We did not do the job. New one up now."

## Supplied Identity
The mascot, Scrap the fox in a headset, was drawn two years ago and survives only as a flat shaded render on a dark panel, with no clean cutout. The merch fox head emblem's original file is long gone and exists only as a photo of the embroidered patch on the creator's hoodie. There is no brand kit; titles and catchphrases live in a text file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
