# FOLD & GRAIN Studio

FOLD & GRAIN shoots small brands' products the way those brands actually sell them, in a row, as a range, rather than one hero at a time. For six years we sold days. We are now selling a product: one continuous take per client and, out of that single take, the shot board, the short films and the platform cuts a small brand needs to launch. Our work sits behind pantry goods at eight dollars and sterling silver at a hundred and eighty, and the whole point is that the same method tunes to each. Precision, repeatability and honesty about what a shoot can and cannot yield are the studio's stock in trade.

## Audience
Small and independent product brands (pantry, personal care, jewellery, homeware) launching or refreshing a web store and needing a full range shot to look like one afternoon. Founders who buy a fixed price and a fixed turnaround, not a day rate.

## Positioning
Repositioning from roughly fifteen hundred dollars a day to a fixed twenty two hundred to thirty five hundred dollars per client run. Mid-market, productized, premium to a freelancer but well under an agency.

## Palette
- Paper White: #F5F1E8 · mandatory. The ground of every Cobbleway Provisions board page and of any card or panel carrying Cobbleway Provisions material, as the pale warm sweep the Cobbleway pass was shot on; and the colour of type set on Ink Black or on the dark Lunara Silver footage, apart from Slate Grey mono specs and Lunara prices.
- Ink Black: #1A1A17 · mandatory. Type on Paper White or on the pale Cobbleway Provisions footage: headings, body, captions, prices and the FOLD & GRAIN wordmark, apart from Slate Grey mono specs and Kraft Tan small labels; and the ground of every Lunara Silver board page and of any card or panel carrying Lunara Silver material, as the dark slab the Lunara pass was shot on.
- Kraft Tan: #C7A879 · optional accent, Cobbleway Provisions material only. Hairline rules, dividers and small labels on Cobbleway Provisions deliverables and the Cobbleway example of the service page, echoing the kraft cartons. Never a ground, card, panel, heading, body text or price, and never in Lunara Silver material.
- Slate Grey: #6D7378 · optional accent. Sohne Mono Buch specs and parameters on either ground, Lunara Silver prices, which the approved copy asks to be understated, and hairline rules and dividers. Never a ground, card, panel, heading, body text or the wordmark.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds, cards and panels are Paper White or Ink Black and nothing else. Cobbleway Provisions material sits on Paper White, as the pale warm sweep it was shot on; Lunara Silver material sits on Ink Black, as its dark slab. On Paper White or the pale Cobbleway footage, type is Ink Black; on Ink Black or the dark Lunara footage, type is Paper White. Slate Grey may carry mono specs and parameters on either ground and Lunara prices; Kraft Tan may carry hairline rules, dividers and small labels in Cobbleway material only. The two accents together take no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never a ground, card or panel.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or type, and no scrim, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage or a photograph: type sits directly on the picture or on a solid palette card or panel. A fade in or out of a whole shot or card at a film transition is allowed.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the four palette hexes (#F5F1E8, #1A1A17, #C7A879 and #6D7378) for type, cards, panels, rules, icons or the FOLD & GRAIN wordmark, and no Kraft Tan anywhere in Lunara Silver material.
- Photography: Footage and frame grabs are never tinted, duotoned or washed toward a palette colour, and the palette rules above do not apply to their pixels. The Cobbleway Provisions sweep is not pushed toward Kraft Tan or orange; the Lunara Silver slab keeps its own dark stone texture, is not flattened to Ink Black, and the silver is not warmed.

## Typography
Display face: Engravers LT Std Bold Face. Text face: Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman, with Sohne Mono Buch for specs and prices.

- 01 Display / H1: Engravers LT Std Bold Face, 72 px / 78 px, Bold Face, caps only, tracking +60 units, and 180 units on the stacked wordmark.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Engravers LT Std Bold Face, 34 px / 40 px, Bold Face, caps only, tracking +90 units.
- 03 Body: Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman, 18 px / 28 px, 55 Roman, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Sohne Mono Buch, 14 px / 20 px, Buch, tracking +15 units, tabular figures.

Signature move: The wordmark always stacks on two lines, FOLD above GRAIN, with the ampersand set small and centred between them and the whole thing tracked 180 units in Engravers LT Std Bold Face, so it reads as a struck stamp rather than a line of type. The open tracking carries down the scale, 60 units at 72 px and 90 units at 34 px.

Rules:
- Never set the wordmark in the mono.
- Engravers is caps only.
- The wordmark always stacks on two lines, FOLD above GRAIN, ampersand small and centred between them, tracked 180 units so it reads as a struck stamp rather than a line of type.
- Sohne Mono Buch carries specs, parameters and prices, always in tabular figures.
- Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman sets body and nothing else takes its place.
- Printed shot board sets title 28 pt, captions and prices 12 pt, mono specs 8 pt on 12 pt.
- A treatment right for one price band is wrong for another. State what a shoot can and cannot deliver.

## Voice
Direct, specific, no salesmanship. We name what a shoot can deliver and what it cannot, and we write our recipes so an assistant could run them. Sentences are short and concrete.

## Supplied Identity
FOLD & GRAIN has a consistent working look built up over six years but never a codified system. There is a set wordmark and studio colours in practice, no written guidelines. This engagement, which includes the studio's own service page, is where the identity gets formalized for the first time.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
