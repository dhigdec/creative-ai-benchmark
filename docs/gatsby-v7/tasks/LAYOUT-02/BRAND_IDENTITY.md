# Corner & Cure

Corner & Cure is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Brooklyn, New York, and this commission covers phase 2: counter refit, cold-case labels and signage.

## Audience
Walk up trade, mostly twenty to forty five, three visits a week, deciding in about eight seconds at the counter or six on a phone. They want appetite and honesty, not polish.

## Positioning
Twelve to sixteen dollars for a sandwich or a bowl. Impulse priced but not cheap: the price is the offer and sits large and proud, while the whole shop works to look like a place that is worth it rather than a bargain or a franchise.

## Palette
- Deli Red: #B4382C · mandatory. The solid panel or device the price is reversed out of in Butcher Cream, as the price roundel on cornerandcure_label_press.pdf, set directly beneath the item name. Never body text, the allergen line or a full ground.
- Counter Mustard: #D9A536 · optional accent. Thin rules, dividers and small non-text accents only; no supplied brand file uses it. Never a ground, a panel or type.
- Butcher Cream: #F2E7CE · mandatory. The label ground, as on cornerandcure_label_press.pdf, any text panel on a tile, and all type reversed out of Deli Red or Pickle Green, the price included, as the price and tag pills on cornerandcure_label_press.pdf.
- Slate Char: #2A2622 · mandatory. The item name, size and allergen or dietary wording set on Butcher Cream, and thin keylines and photo borders, as the item name, keyline and photo border on cornerandcure_label_press.pdf. Never a ground or panel.
- Pickle Green: #4E6B3A · optional accent. Dietary-tag pills only, carrying Butcher Cream type, as the tag strip on cornerandcure_label_press.pdf. Never a ground, the item name or the price.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every label has a Butcher Cream ground, as on cornerandcure_label_press.pdf. On a tile, Butcher Cream is the only colour that may carry Slate Char type. Apart from Butcher Cream grounds and text panels, the only panels are the Deli Red device the price is reversed out of and Pickle Green dietary-tag pills. On Butcher Cream, all type is Slate Char; on Deli Red and Pickle Green, all type is Butcher Cream. Counter Mustard is for thin rules and small non-text accents only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel or type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or any type, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#B4382C, #D9A536, #F2E7CE, #2A2622 and #4E6B3A) for type, panels, rules, keylines, buttons or icons. The painted sign from cornerandcure_board_sign.jpg is exempt, as the photography rule says.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted or recoloured toward the palette. The pale cream butcher paper under the counter shots stays paper, not repainted Butcher Cream #F2E7CE. The painted sign from cornerandcure_board_sign.jpg is used as painted: its lettering, knife device and board may keep the colour they were photographed with, and the palette's type-colour and opacity rules do not apply to it.

## Typography
Display face: Vollkorn. Text face: Benton Sans.

- 01 Display / H1, the price: Vollkorn, caps, 36 pt / 38 pt, Black 900, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2, the item name: Vollkorn, caps, 14 pt / 16 pt, Black 900, tracking +20 units.
- 03 Body: Benton Sans, 8 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Benton Sans, 6.5 pt / 9 pt, Regular, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: The price carries no dollar sign and sets in Vollkorn Black directly beneath the item name at two and a half times its size, so the number is always the loudest thing on the surface: 14 pt name over 36 pt price on a 90 mm label, 84 px over 210 px on a 1200 px tile.

Rules:
- Never set an allergen line in Vollkorn. Allergens are Benton Sans Regular at 6.5 pt on 9 pt leading.
- No dollar sign anywhere in the system.
- The price always sits directly beneath the item name at two and a half times its size, never beside it and never smaller.
- Item names and prices set in Vollkorn Black 900 caps.
- The ampersand is the only italic in the system.
- Hours and loyalty rules hold at 8 pt in Benton Sans Regular.
- The 1200 px tile holds the same pair at 84 px name over 210 px price.

## Voice
Plainspoken, neighbourhood, a little brash and generous. Example line the brand would write: 'Pulled together, piled high, see you at the counter.'

## Supplied Identity
There is no digital version of the sign: the name survives only as a board hand painted by a sign painter twenty years ago and screwed to the wall over the slicer. Everything else was shot on the owner's phone over the counter. A two page written note holds the voice, hours, loyalty rules and section names, and the label printer holds a press file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
