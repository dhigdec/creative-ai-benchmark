# Marrow and Vane

Marrow and Vane is a Philadelphia gallery that mounts selling shows for collectors and curators. The house look is austere and generously spaced: the label and the checklist stay quiet so the work carries the room. Their autumn exhibition hangs forty one works by nine artists across three rooms.

## Audience
Collectors and institutional curators, plus a press list. Readers who scan a medium line for provenance and expect the gallery to signal seriousness through restraint rather than through decoration.

## Positioning
Works from six thousand to eighty five thousand dollars. At this level the label is small, quiet type high on a large card with unprinted stock beneath, no rules, no colour, no logo on the card. It must read as a selling show, not a community group show and not a museum survey.

## Palette
- Gallery White: #F6F4EF · mandatory on every wall label and installer page; optional checklist ground. The card ground of every wall label and installer page, solid and edge to edge as on mv_spring_labels_press.pdf, and optionally a checklist page ground. Never type.
- Warm Grey Ground: #D8D3C8 · optional ground, checklist only. A solid checklist ground, such as the panel the gallery mark sits on. Never on a wall label or installer page, which keep the Gallery White card, and never type.
- Ink Black: #1A1A18 · mandatory. Every line of type on the labels, installer pages and the checklist: artist, title, medium, year, dimensions, credit, section headings and the press line, as mv_spring_labels_press.pdf sets its story, except accession numbers, which the type system sets in Graphite. Never a ground or panel.
- Brass: #9C7A3C · optional accent, checklist mark only. At most the flat, solid ink of the MARROW & VANE mark on the checklist. Never type, a rule, a ground or a panel, and never on a wall label or installer page.
- Graphite: #55565A · mandatory for accession numbers. The accession number on every wall label, installer page and checklist entry, as the type system rule sets it, and nothing else. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Wall labels and installer pages: the ground is Gallery White and nothing else; every line of type is Ink Black except the accession number, which is Graphite; there is no rule, panel, mark or coloured type (the director: no rule, no colour, no mark), so the only other colour on the card is the artwork photograph. Checklist: grounds are Gallery White or Warm Grey Ground; type is Ink Black and accession numbers Graphite; Brass appears at most as the flat ink of the gallery mark, no more than 5% of any page's designed, non-photographic area, and is never type, a rule, a ground or a panel.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over an artwork photograph or over the gallery mark.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#F6F4EF, #D8D3C8, #1A1A18, #9C7A3C and #55565A) for any type, ground, panel, rule or mark. No rule, coloured type or panel, logo or mark on a wall label or installer page. No metallic, foil, brushed or gradient effect imitating the brass plate of mv_brass_plate.jpg on the mark or on any type, and the mark is never a photograph or cut-out of the plate.
- Photography: Artwork photographs keep the work's own corrected colour and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward a palette hex: the gold leaf in 2013-041.jpg and 2013-101.jpg is not matched to Brass, and no photograph is washed toward Gallery White or Warm Grey Ground. The director's 'no colour' governs the designed card, not the reproduction of the work.

## Typography
Display face: Rowdies. Text face: Lyon Text.

- 01 Display / H1: Rowdies, 9.5 pt / 11 pt, Light 300, caps, tracking fitted per name to a 78 mm measure.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Lyon Text, 9.5 pt / 13 pt, Regular Italic, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: Lyon Text, 8 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Lyon Text, 7 pt / 10 pt, Regular, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: The artist's name is the only thing in Rowdies Light caps, tracked per name so the line always measures exactly 78 mm on the label and 120 mm on the A4 checklist, so every label ends its top line flush at the same point.

Rules:
- No bold anywhere, ever.
- Rowdies is used for the artist's name and for nothing else.
- Tracking on the artist line is fitted per name so it measures exactly 78 mm on the 105 by 148 mm label and exactly 120 mm on the A4 checklist.
- Every label ends its top line flush at the same point.
- No rules, no colour and no logo on the card.
- The type sits small and high on a large card, with unprinted stock beneath it.
- The A4 checklist sets body at 9 pt on 13 pt, and screen sets 15 px.
- Accession numbers set at 7 pt in Graphite.
- It must read as a selling show, not a community group show and not a museum survey: the label stays quiet so the work carries the room.

## Voice
Spare, precise, factual. The gallery states rather than sells. Example line the brand would write: 'Nine artists. Three rooms. Eighteen September through the second of November.'

## Supplied Identity
There is no layout file for the labels; only the printer's spring press PDF survives as the look to keep. The gallery mark exists as an etched brass plate beside the door and a single photograph of it, and has to be recovered as real artwork before it can sit on the checklist.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
