# Milepost Driving Academy

Milepost is an accredited driving academy in Vermont running in car lessons and a classroom course. Two instructors, about sixty learners a term, mostly sixteen to nineteen with a steady number of adult beginners. Our reputation is that we are calm and thorough, and every learner leaves certain about what they are looking at. Everything we make has to earn the trust of a nervous learner and the parent paying for the course.

## Audience
Learners sixteen to nineteen plus adult beginners, with the purchase decision usually made by a parent. People who want reassurance that the school is careful and legitimate.

## Positioning
Mid, accredited and dependable. Sixty dollars an hour. Not a budget app, not a premium driving experience brand.

## Palette
- Signal Green: #2E6B4F · mandatory. The scenario number, with its 3 px Caution Amber rule beneath (type system signature move), and the scenario title, as on milepost_guide_2019.pdf, always on Route White. Never a ground, a panel, step text or callout text.
- Route White: #F7F7F4 · mandatory. The only ground: every text card and text panel, as the page ground of milepost_guide_2019.pdf. All type sits on it, never directly on road footage.
- Slate Text: #2A2E33 · mandatory where steps, the callout or captions sit on Route White. Step lines, the callout and captions on Route White, as on milepost_guide_2019.pdf, and the stop glyph, drawn like the single dark ink of milepost_glyph_sheet.jpg. Never a ground or panel.
- Caution Amber: #E4A11B · mandatory as the 3 px rule beneath every scenario number. Thin rules only: the 3 px rule directly beneath the scenario number, and at most a short rule above the callout, as on milepost_guide_2019.pdf. Never type, a ground, a panel or a fill.
- Sky Grey: #9AA6AE · optional accent. A footer or accreditation line and hairline rules only, as the footer of milepost_guide_2019.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, step text or callout text.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Route White is the only ground: the scenario number, title, steps and callout sit on a solid Route White card or panel, never directly on road footage, as milepost_guide_2019.pdf keeps its type off the photograph. On Route White, the scenario number and scenario title are Signal Green, and step lines, the callout and captions are Slate Text, as on milepost_guide_2019.pdf. Caution Amber is for thin rules only and Sky Grey for a footer or accreditation line and hairlines only; together they take no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never a ground, a panel, step text or callout text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, once a card or panel is fully on screen. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the road footage: text sits on a solid Route White card or panel.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules or glyphs, so no red stop sign glyph, and no gradient, glow, outline, drop shadow or metallic effect on any type, panel, rule or glyph: nothing is stylised.
- Photography: Road footage and road stills keep their own filmed colour and are never tinted or graded toward the palette. The warm low-sun cast of milepost_road_still_01.jpg and milepost_road_still_02.jpg is not pushed toward Caution Amber, the cool fog of milepost_road_still_03.jpg and milepost_road_still_04.jpg is not pushed toward Sky Grey, and the painted road markings in every take keep the colours they were filmed in.

## Typography
Display face: Baskerville Display PT. Text face: Inter.

- 01 Display / H1: Baskerville Display PT, 72 px / 80 px, Regular, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Inter, 56 px / 64 px, Semibold, tracking -5 units, tabular lining figures.
- 03 Body: Inter, 44 px / 58 px, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters, tabular lining figures.
- 04 Fine print: Inter, 32 px / 42 px, Regular, tracking +20 units, tabular lining figures.

Signature move: Every scenario carries its number set in Baskerville Display PT Regular at three times the scenario title size, 216 px against a 72 px title, in Signal Green, with a 3 px Caution Amber rule directly beneath it. On the vertical cutdown the number scales with the 64 px title and the rule holds at 3 px.

Rules:
- Never set a step in italic or caps.
- Baskerville Display PT sets the school name in Bold and scenario titles in Regular, and nothing else.
- Every scenario number sets in Baskerville Display PT at three times the title size, in Signal Green, with a 3 px Caution Amber rule directly beneath.
- Inter carries every step, callout and caption, with tabular lining figures throughout.
- One idea at a time, numbered where it helps. No slang and no drama.
- Nothing stylised. A learner and a parent must always be sure what they are looking at.
- The printed guide sets body at 11 pt on 16 pt.

## Voice
Calm, instructive, reassuring. One idea at a time, numbered where it helps, no slang and no drama. Speaks plainly to a beginner and to the parent reading over their shoulder.

## Supplied Identity
Everything a learner currently sees from us dates to 2019 and looks it. The printer holds the press file for the guide, but the source files for our simplified sign shapes are gone: they only survive as a photograph of a sheet our first designer printed and taped to the wall, so those glyphs have to be recovered from that. No brand typeface was ever locked, so the type system is open for you to set to our legibility standard.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
