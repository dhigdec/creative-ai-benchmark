# SB3-074-LAY | Rivermeadow Trust - Year-end donor acknowledgement system

Replace our manual year-end donor letters with a dependable reusable system

Rivermeadow Trust's office manager spent three weeks preparing acknowledgements last year, and two donors were still missed. We need this December's letters and receipts produced correctly, plus templates the office can reuse next year by changing the data.

The commission

 Donor acknowledgement letter: 19 named exports. The salutation greets each donor household by name, matching the donor record. The acknowledged amount is accurate, and each letter carries the trust mark from the Fenn Hollow plaque and the household's mailing address and number of gifts as recorded.

 Donation receipt: 19 named exports. The receipt date is accurate: every receipt is dated December 31, 2026, as on our printed receipt. The donor identity is unambiguous, and each receipt carries the trust mark from the Fenn Hollow plaque and the household's number of gifts as recorded.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
