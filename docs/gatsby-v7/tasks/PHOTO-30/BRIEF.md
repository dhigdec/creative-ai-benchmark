# SB3-030-PHO | Palewell Health - Licensed care image campaign

A restrained healthcare image set with a reliable clinical-lead cutout

Palewell needs photography that feels credible together, even though most of the set will be licensed and only one frame is owned. Please prioritise compatible camera scale, neutral balance and depth over a collection of individually attractive stock photographs.

Scope
1. Care-service information poster: 1 named export. Its headline is set at six times the size of the Inter line directly beneath it; that ratio governs over the scale's point sizes. The service wording stays within approved claims: the poster carries our approved tall portrait headline and subhead from palewell_copy.txt, and any body text is that file's compliance-cleared one-pager paragraph, all word for word. The care contact is readable: it names Palewell Health and invents no telephone number, email or web address, since no supplied file gives Palewell Health's own.
2. Care website header: 1 named export. Its headline is one of our two approved header headline options in palewell_copy.txt, word for word, set at step 02; the four-word headline limit applies only to the poster's step 01 headline. Stock people are not identified as named clinicians. The service name, Palewell Health, is clear. The clinical lead has a clean hair edge on the deep brand ground.
3. Clinical lead transparent portrait: 1 named export. The portrait preserves the person in palewell_lead_frame.jpg. The portrait shows the head and both shoulders, and the shoulder edge has no visible halo.
4. Referral information panel: 1 named export. Its main heading is one whole headline, subhead or tagline from palewell_copy.txt, word for word, and the panel names Palewell Health in full. The referral steps follow approved copy: they are numbered, each is one whole headline, subhead, tagline or paragraph from palewell_copy.txt, word for word apart from its step number, and they add no contact, timescale or service that no supplied file gives. The clinical lead identity is accurate: the panel shows our clinical lead from palewell_lead_frame.jpg.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
