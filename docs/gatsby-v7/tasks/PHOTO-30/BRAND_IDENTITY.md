# Palewell Health

Palewell is a subscription digital health platform for people managing ordinary, everyday conditions from home. Members pay a modest monthly fee for clinician backed guidance, and about half our growth now comes through employer benefits teams who offer us to their staff. We exist so that steady, unglamorous care feels reachable rather than clinical.

## Audience
Members aged thirty to fifty five who want reassurance without a hospital, plus benefits and HR leads who evaluate us for risk and coverage before putting us in front of their people

## Positioning
Fifteen to forty dollars a month, plus employer plans. Mid market and accessible: above a free wellness app, well below concierge medicine. The read must never feel like a two hundred dollar boutique practice or a supplement funnel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: The header sits on Deep Slate. The poster and the referral panel sit on a Warm Chalk page ground, as palewell_employer_onepager.pdf does, and may carry solid Deep Slate panels, as that page's photograph panel, and small Paper White panels or cards. On Warm Chalk or Paper White, headlines, names and the contact block are Deep Slate, and body text and other fine print are Soft Ink, apart from a Sage Signal link or small label, as on palewell_employer_onepager.pdf. On Deep Slate, all type is Warm Chalk or Paper White. Sage Signal is the one accent, for a link, a short rule or a small label only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, and never a ground, a panel, a headline or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no translucent scrim, tint, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph; an opaque solid palette panel may cover part of one, and type sits on a solid ground or on such a panel. A cut-out sits straight on its ground with no added outline, glow or shadow.
- Forbidden: No bright or clinical blue, no gloss, sheen, glow or metallic effect, and no black or near-black on any type, ground, panel, rule, button or icon, since calm is never cold, sterile, blue, glossy or near-black (palewell_brand_notes.md). Never a second accent beside Sage Signal, and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and share one neutral balance; none is tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette. The clinical lead's pale sage-grey scrub top in palewell_lead_frame.jpg keeps its own colour and is never pushed toward Sage Signal.

## Typography
Display face: Archivo Black. Text face: Inter.

- 01 Display / H1: Archivo Black, 120 pt / 112 pt, Black, tracking -15 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Archivo Black, 72 px / 76 px, Black, tracking -10 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Inter Regular, 18 px / 28 px, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Inter Medium, 13 px / 20 px, Medium, tracking +15 thousandths.

Signature move: An extreme size jump: a headline of four words at most in Archivo Black sitting at six times the body size, with Inter beginning immediately beneath it, so one short heavy line reads against a wide field of quiet text. Step 01 over step 03 is that jump.

Rules:
- Never set a clinical claim in Archivo Black.
- The headline never exceeds four words.
- The headline sits at six times the body size, with Inter beginning immediately beneath it.
- The contact block is set in Inter Medium tabular figures.
- No hype, no superlatives, no clinical jargon; speak to a person, not a patient.
- Step 04 size and leading were invented because the prose states none; every family in this scale is the brand's own.

## Voice
Plain, warm and unhurried; we speak to a person, not a patient. We avoid hype, superlatives and clinical jargon. Example line the brand would write: "Manage the everyday stuff with someone in your corner, from home."

## Supplied Identity
There is no formal brand book. What exists is an internal notes file describing the palette in words (a deep slate, a warm chalk, a single muted signal colour), the tone of voice, and what the founders mean by calm. We have never run a photo shoot: every image on the current site came free with the template, and we own exactly one photograph outright, of our clinical lead, cleared for use.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
