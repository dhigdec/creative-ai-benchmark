# Sterling Row

Sterling Row is nine full floor residences in a converted warehouse block, starting just over four million dollars, sold to buyers who are not in a hurry and have seen everything. The channel is where most buyers first meet the broker, so every piece has to feel made by one hand. Restraint is the whole proposition: these buyers are reassured, not persuaded.

## Audience
Ultra high net worth buyers, typically on their third or fourth property, reassured by evidence and calm rather than by benefit claims, who still need the floor, the square footage and the exposure before they ask to view.

## Positioning
Full floor residences from just over four million dollars, the top of the market. Presentation sits near neutral and deliberately small: anything that looks like a promotion reads, at this tier, as a building that is struggling to sell.

## Palette
- Near Black: #14161A · mandatory. On screen, the solid ground of every text card, and of every panel that carries type, in both films: type_system steps 02 and 03 set every figure and word on Near Black. On sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf it is the unit name type.
- Cast Brass: #B08D57 · mandatory wherever a figure appears. Every figure, with the figure line it sits in (type_system step 02, sample Floor 9 · 4,300 square feet), set in Geist Mono on Near Black as the signature move sets, and hairline rules, as the two rules on sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf. Never other words, a card ground or a panel. The recovered wordmark is excepted and judged by the wordmark checks.
- Stone Grey: #6E7378 · optional word colour: every word on Near Black outside the figure line is Stone Grey or Warm White. Labels, secondary standing text and any disclosure line on Near Black (type_system step 03), as sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf uses it for spec labels and disclosure lines. Never figures, a card ground or a panel.
- Warm White: #F2F1ED · optional word colour: every word on Near Black outside the figure line is Warm White or Stone Grey. Words on Near Black, such as the tagline, the residence name and exposure, and the viewing action (type_system step 03: Stone Grey or Warm White on Near Black). It is the print ground of sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf, but never a card ground on screen, where every step sits on Near Black.
- Slate: #2C333A · optional, with no required on-screen use. On sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf it sets the availability note and fills the panel behind the photograph. On screen it may fill a solid panel that carries no type, and it never colours a word or figure, since type_system step 02 sets figures in Cast Brass and step 03 sets words in Stone Grey or Warm White.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: On screen, Near Black is the only ground for type: every text card, and every panel that carries type, is solid Near Black, and no type is set directly on footage (type_system steps 02 and 03 set every figure and word on Near Black). Every figure, with the step 02 figure line it sits in, is Cast Brass in Geist Mono; every other word is Warm White or Stone Grey, either one or both. Cast Brass is otherwise used only for hairline rules, as on sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf, and those rules cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-footage area of any card; Cast Brass figures are type and are not counted. Slate is optional with no required on-screen use: it never colours a word or figure, and it may fill a solid panel that carries no type, as it fills the panel behind the photograph on sterlingrow_spec_sheet.pdf. The recovered wordmark is judged by the wordmark checks, not by these type colour, effect or palette rules.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, glow, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour or type, and no scrim, vignette, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage. A panel is a solid, fully opaque area of a palette colour, Near Black wherever it carries type, laid over part of a shot that otherwise fills the frame, such as a lower third: it is not an overlay, whereas any translucent, gradient or feathered area over footage is. Footage shrunk inside Near Black bands, with or without type in them, is a letterbox or pillarbox, not a panel. A card or line may cut or fade in and out as a whole; colour and opacity are judged on a settled frame.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, cards, panels or rules. Apart from the recovered wordmark, no metallic, foil, glow, shadow or gradient effect on any type, Cast Brass figures included, which stay flat #B08D57. Nothing that looks like a promotion, which the type rules forbid: no price on screen and no large benefit caption in any colour.
- Photography: Footage keeps its own colour: the brick and oak keep their natural warmth under one grade that sits near neutral and slightly cool, as sterlingrow_brand_note.txt sets (clean whites, no crushed blacks, no warm saturated push). White walls, cabinets and window light read the same near-neutral white in every shot, so no shot drifts into a different colour world; the natural colour of brick, oak and sky is not a grade shift. No palette colour is laid over footage as a tint, duotone or colour wash, and no pixels are generated or extended to reframe a clip; cropping to the delivery ratio is allowed.

## Typography
Display face: Geist Regular. Text face: Geist Regular, with Geist Mono carrying every numeral.

- 01 Display / H1 · opening card: Geist Regular, 42 px / 48 px, Regular, tracking minus 0.010 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · the figure line, floor, area, price, date: Geist Mono, 26 px / 32 px, Regular, tabular figures, tracking 0.
- 03 Body · labels and standing text: Geist Regular, 20 px / 30 px, Regular, tracking plus 0.060 em.
- 04 Fine print · disclosure: Geist Regular, 16 px / 22 px, Regular, tracking plus 0.020 em.

Signature move: No numeral ever sets in the text face. Every floor, square footage, price and date sets in Geist Mono at 26 px, tabular, in Cast Brass on Near Black, ranged on one right edge, so the figures read as a narrower, engraved species of type beside the quiet Geist Regular words.

Rules:
- Never set a numeral in the text face; every floor, square footage, price and date sets in Geist Mono.
- Figures set tabular in Cast Brass on Near Black and range on one right edge, one alignment for the whole piece.
- A numeral inside a sentence still breaks out into Geist Mono; if that reads badly, the number is written as a word instead.
- The disclosure wording is fixed by the developer and cannot be changed.
- The developer's restraint rules are contractual: nothing may look like a promotion, since at this tier that reads as a building struggling to sell.
- In print the opening card sets 11 pt and the rest of the scale holds the same proportion.

## Voice
Spare, composed, confident without pressure. Example: "Nine residences. Full floor. Sold one at a time, to buyers in no hurry."

## Supplied Identity
The only surviving version of the wordmark is a handheld photograph of the cast brass plaque mounted in the lobby, lit from one side. There is no vector file and no clean scan. The developer's spec sheet exists only as a printed PDF, and the disclosure wording is fixed and cannot be changed.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
