# Girdermark Components

Girdermark Components is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Cleveland, Ohio, and this commission covers phase 2: data-sheet and bin-label library.

## Audience
Working buyers aged thirty five to sixty who have specified this class of part for twenty years and judge a supplier by whether the picture and the numbers match.

## Positioning
Parts from 400 to 9,000 dollars. A flat, even, technical register: one neutral photographic ground, the whole part in frame, generous spec setting, prices plain in the run of the page. Never bright consumer tool styling and never dark cinematic hero work. The bin label answers to a picker at arm's length, so it is bigger, blunter and carries less.

## Palette
- Girder Blue: #1C3A5E · mandatory beneath every part number. Only the 2 pt rule locked directly beneath each part number at the part number's own measure, as the type system sets it and label_2026_PRESS.pdf prints it. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Signal Amber: #D8A233 · optional accent. Neither press PDF uses it. At most a small marker or a short rule, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. Never type, a ground, a panel, or a flash or highlight on a price.
- Steel Grey: #6E747A · optional secondary. Spec field labels and small panel titles, hairline dividers, the footer rule, the keyline around the part photograph and the certification-strip keyline, as sheet_2026_PRESS.pdf prints them, and the picture keyline, as label_2026_PRESS.pdf prints it. Never a ground, panel, part number, spec value or price.
- Warehouse White: #F4F5F6 · mandatory. The only designed, non-photographic ground and panel colour of every data sheet and bin label, as sheet_2026_PRESS.pdf and label_2026_PRESS.pdf print it; the ground the house mark sits on. Never a type colour.
- Ink Black: #1A1C1E · mandatory. The house mark in one ink, the part number, description, spec values, ordering code, prices and footer line; on the sheet the two full-width header rules and the mark and ordering-panel keylines, as sheet_2026_PRESS.pdf prints them; on the label the mark keyline, as label_2026_PRESS.pdf prints it. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Warehouse White is the only designed, non-photographic ground and panel colour on every sheet and label, as on sheet_2026_PRESS.pdf and label_2026_PRESS.pdf. On it, the house mark, part number, description, spec values, ordering code, prices and footer line are Ink Black; spec field labels and small panel titles may be Steel Grey, as on sheet_2026_PRESS.pdf. On the sheet the two full-width header rules and the mark and ordering-panel keylines are Ink Black; hairline dividers, the footer rule, the photograph keyline and the certification-strip keyline are Steel Grey, as on sheet_2026_PRESS.pdf. On the label the mark keyline is Ink Black and the picture keyline Steel Grey, as on label_2026_PRESS.pdf. The 2 pt rule beneath every part number is Girder Blue. Signal Amber is an optional small accent, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, and never type, a ground or a panel.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, as both press PDFs print them. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, keylines, markers or icons. No dark ground or panel, since the brand never does dark cinematic hero work; no badge, burst, colour flash or highlight panel on a price, since it never does bright consumer tool styling; no gradient, glow, gloss or metallic effect. Girder Blue and Signal Amber never set type.
- Photography: Photographs keep each part's own true colour and finish and are never tinted or duotoned toward the palette. Every part sits whole in frame on one neutral photographic ground, the same on every sheet and label.

## Typography
Display face: Saira Condensed. Text face: IBM Plex Sans.

- 01 Display / H1: Saira Condensed Black, 30 pt sheet, 36 pt label / 33 pt sheet, 40 pt label, Black, tracking optical, tracked to fill the exact panel measure edge to edge.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Saira Condensed Black, 18 pt / 20 pt, Black, tracking +0.02 em.
- 03 Body: IBM Plex Sans Regular, 9 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: IBM Plex Sans Regular, 7 pt / 10 pt, Regular, tracking +0.03 em.

Signature move: The part number is optically tracked to fill the exact measure of its panel, edge to edge, with a 2 pt Girder Blue rule locked directly beneath it at that same measure, so a viewer sees first a condensed black code underscored corner to corner. That behaviour governs step 01 at every size: 30 pt on the sheet, 36 pt on the bin label, 96 px on screen.

Rules:
- Never italicise anything.
- The part number is optically tracked to the exact panel measure, edge to edge, with a 2 pt Girder Blue rule beneath it at the same measure.
- Saira Condensed Black sets every part number, sheet header and bin label line.
- The bin label sets the part number at 36 pt and screen sets it at 96 px.
- IBM Plex Sans Regular sets spec rows at 9 pt on 12 pt with tabular figures on, and prices at 14 pt.
- Prices sit plain in the run of the page, never bright consumer tool styling and never dark cinematic hero work.
- The picture and the spec must agree; values are never reworded to fit, and anything that will not fit is raised before it prints.
- The bin label answers to a picker at arm's length, so it is bigger, blunter and carries less than the sheet.

## Voice
Blunt, factual and useful. No marketing adjectives, no styling in the words. Example line the brand would write: "Sixteen active parts this season. Specs, codes and prices are exactly as our system holds them."

## Supplied Identity
The division owns none of its own artwork. The spec sheet and the bin label exist only as the flat press PDFs the printer and the label house return. The house mark was lost in an office move and survives only in the printed sheet header and cast into an aluminium nameplate riveted to an old traction machine in the back, photographed for reference. Part photography was shot in house on the bench over several months and does not match.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
