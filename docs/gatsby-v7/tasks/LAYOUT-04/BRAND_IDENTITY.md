# NORTHGROVE

NORTHGROVE is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Burlington, Vermont, and this commission covers phase 2: wholesale lookbook, hang tags and launch collateral.

## Audience
Buyers and wearers twenty eight to forty five who value provenance and construction over logos, and who read fabric and make before they read a brand mark.

## Positioning
Seventy four to one hundred twenty eight dollars per piece, mid premium wholesale and direct. Muted grounds, full frame photography that shows cloth and seam, and copy that leads on material and make. Deliberately clear of both the twenty four dollar fast fashion grid and the price free editorial luxury a buyer cannot work from.

## Palette
- Charcoal: #33352F · mandatory. The NORTHGROVE wordmark, style names, look titles, section heads, body copy, prices and fine print on Oat Cotton, as on northgrove_hangtag.pdf. On the lookbook's inner pages it may also be a solid panel that carries Oat Cotton type; never a panel on the cover or on a hang tag.
- Workshop Olive: #5E6844 · mandatory for the leaf glyph. The leaf glyph beside the wordmark, always, as on northgrove_hangtag.pdf; otherwise only the colourway line and small labels such as FABRIC and MAKE. Never a ground, a panel or body copy.
- Oat Cotton: #D8CDB6 · mandatory. The solid ground of every hang tag, edge to edge, as on northgrove_hangtag.pdf, and the main page ground of the lookbook; also any type reversed out of a Charcoal panel.
- Forge Rust: #9A5A38 · optional accent. The short rule above the price, as on northgrove_hangtag.pdf, and other hairline rules. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Cast Pewter: #8C8A85 · optional accent. Hairline rules, dividers and keylines only. Never type, a ground or a panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Every hang tag is a solid Oat Cotton ground, as on northgrove_hangtag.pdf, and Oat Cotton is the lookbook's page ground. The lookbook cover, like every hang tag, is a solid Oat Cotton ground; Charcoal panels sit on the lookbook's inner pages only. On Oat Cotton, the wordmark, style names, titles, heads, body copy, prices and fine print are Charcoal, and the colourway line and small labels may be Workshop Olive, as on northgrove_hangtag.pdf. On Charcoal, all type is Oat Cotton. The leaf glyph is always Workshop Olive. Forge Rust and Cast Pewter are for rules, dividers and keylines only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: a garment whose colourway shares a palette name (Charcoal, Workshop Olive, Oat Cotton, Forge Rust, Cast Pewter) keeps its own cloth colour. The ten flat-lays, northgrove_flatlay_01.jpg to northgrove_flatlay_10.jpg, read as one shoot on one neutral mid gray backdrop, as the brand assets note describes the shoot (flat, overhead, on a mid gray backdrop). The cast hardware in northgrove_hardware_01.jpg, northgrove_hardware_02.jpg, northgrove_hardware_03.jpg and northgrove_hardware_04.jpg keeps true metal tone with no cast, as northgrove_copy.txt asks. The Kestrel Lane crest from kestrel_pennant.jpg is the club's mark, not a NORTHGROVE design element, and may keep its own thread colours.

## Typography
Display face: Literata. Text face: Freight Sans Pro.

- 01 Display / H1, the wordmark and look title: Literata, caps, 30 pt / 33 pt, Bold 700, tracking spanned to the measure, about 420 units on a lookbook page and 180 units on a 38 mm hang tag.
- 02 Subheading / H2, section head: Literata, caps, 18 pt / 21 pt, Bold 700, tracking +60 units.
- 03 Body: Freight Sans Pro, 9.5 pt / 14 pt, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Freight Sans Pro, 7.5 pt / 11 pt, Book, tracking +15 units.

Signature move: The wordmark is always letterspaced to fill the exact measure of whatever carries it, roughly 180 units on a 38 mm hang tag and 420 units on a lookbook page, so the tracking changes from piece to piece but the name spans edge to edge every time.

Rules:
- Never set the wordmark at default tracking.
- The wordmark is always letterspaced to fill the exact measure of whatever carries it, roughly 180 units on a 38 mm hang tag and 420 on a lookbook page.
- Tracking changes from piece to piece. The width of the wordmark never does.
- Literata Bold 700 caps sets the wordmark, look titles and section heads.
- Freight Sans Pro Book sets fabric notes at 9.5 pt on 14 pt leading, and 7.5 pt on the tag.
- The linesheet grid sets in Freight Sans Pro Medium at 8.5 pt in tabular figures.
- Tag style names hold at 11 pt.

## Voice
Understated and maker focused, provenance before promotion. Example line the brand would write: 'Ten pieces, cut and sewn in Burlington, meant to be worn hard.'

## Supplied Identity
The NORTHGROVE wordmark and olive leaf glyph are set. The whole capsule was shot flat, overhead, on a mid gray backdrop across two mornings, with the cast hardware shot separately on the bench. The collaborating club has no artwork left: its crest survives only as a photograph of an old wool pennant on a wall. Last season's hang tag exists as the printer's press file, same size and stock this year.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
