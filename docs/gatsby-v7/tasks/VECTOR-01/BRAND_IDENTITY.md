# Cordwain Overland

Cordwain Overland makes graphic apparel for people who drive the back roads and walk the ridgelines. Our graphics are the reason people buy: customers zoom in on the print detail before they add to cart. Every drop is a small set of designs printed with care on garments meant to be worn hard.

## Audience
Buyers aged twenty four to forty who care how a print is made, can tell a discharge print from a thick plastisol slab, and screenshot the print detail before they buy

## Positioning
Tees at thirty four dollars, hoodies at seventy eight. Mid tier: a clear step above a bargain blank, priced on print quality rather than logo. Two or three tight inks with a bit of texture read as the craft they pay for.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: In the application sheet and lookbook, page grounds and text panels are Bone or Basalt and nothing else. On Bone, headings, running copy, spec lines and the wordmark with its 2 pt rule are Basalt; on Basalt, all type is Bone, so the wordmark sits on Bone where its Basalt rule reads. Trail Sand, Pine Shadow and Rust Ridge are for ink swatches and thin rules only, never a type colour, together no more than 5% of the designed area outside the marks, drawn garments and photographs. A drawn garment takes the palette colour of the garment colour it stands for (Bone, Basalt, Trail Sand or Pine Shadow) and is outside the 5% accent cap. In each recovered mark, every fill is one of the inks that graphic's source carries.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. In the marks every ink is a solid fill; any texture kept from the source is solid ink broken by gaps, never partial opacity, and there is no gradient or halftone, as the printer's sheet requires.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for any page ground, text panel, type, rule, icon, drawn garment or mark fill; no gradient or halftone in any mark; no graphic specified in more than three inks.
- Photography: Garment photographs keep their own colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: the dark hoodie in cordwain_sample_hoodie_badge.jpg stays dark and the light tee in cordwain_sample_tee_compass.jpg stays light.

## Typography
Display face: GT America Expanded Bold 700. Text face: Atlas Grotesk Regular 400 (Atlas Typewriter Regular 400 for ink codes).

- 01 Display / H1 · badge lettering: GT America Expanded Bold, capitals only, 34 pt / 37 pt, Bold 700, tracking plus 0.060 em, letterspaced to optical justification.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · drop and product heads: GT America Expanded Bold, capitals only, 18 pt / 20 pt, Bold 700, tracking plus 0.020 em.
- 03 Body: Atlas Grotesk Regular, 10 pt / 13.5 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · ink codes and print specs: Atlas Typewriter Regular, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Regular 400, tracking plus 0.030 em.

Signature move: The wordmark is always a two line stack, CORDWAIN directly over OVERLAND, both lines letterspaced until they optically justify to the identical measure and split by a 2 pt Basalt rule the full width of the block. The squared block registers before the mountain does.

Rules:
- Never break the stack onto one line, at any size, including the 44 px screen headline.
- Both lines of the wordmark are letterspaced until they optically justify to the identical measure; the measure wins over any fixed tracking value.
- The two lines are always divided by a 2 pt Basalt rule running the full width of the block.
- Display is GT America Expanded Bold 700 in capitals only.
- Atlas Grotesk Regular 400 carries running copy and nothing else carries it.
- Ink codes and print specs are always Atlas Typewriter Regular 400, never Atlas Grotesk.
- Optical justification is reserved for the badge lock up; ordinary heads are never letterspaced to a measure.
- Two or three tight inks with real texture, never a muddy multi colour job, and never price the line out of its own customers.

## Voice
Direct, plainspoken and a little dry; we talk about how a thing is made, not how it will change your life. Example line the brand would write: "Three tight inks, printed to last the season."

## Supplied Identity
The brand sheet is two pages of internal notes: the palette in words, the placement rules we have always used, our maximum print widths for chest and full back, and a paragraph on what we mean by a tight print. Our old designer worked off site and we never got the layered files; for three of our best selling graphics all that survives is a few flat web exports and two printed samples we photographed on the bench.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
