# SB3-039-VEC | Halcyon Grid - Production logo family

Turn rough raster brand concepts into a credible SaaS identity system

Halcyon Grid is a small logistics software team. We have three AI-generated raster marks, but we do not yet have a usable identity. We need a designer to resolve the geometry, not preserve every artefact in those images.

Deliverables
1. Halcyon Grid logo variant: 3 named exports. The geometry preserves the master identity. Each variant pairs its mark with the stacked HALCYON / GRID wordmark, the Dispatch and Relay variants add their product name, and the lettering is optically balanced.
2. Identity usage guide: 1 named export. The guide shows usable clear-space examples: one for each of the three variants, with the clear space drawn around it and its size stated. The variant naming is unambiguous: the guide calls the three variants Master, Dispatch and Relay, as the output register does, and gives each name to one mark only.
3. Team website identity application: 1 named export. The correct team names accompany their portraits: every person in team_roster.csv appears once, labelled with their name and role as written there. The parent and product identities have a clear hierarchy: the delivered master variant leads the page, and the delivered product variants sit below it at a smaller size.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
