# Halcyon Grid

Halcyon Grid builds scheduling infrastructure for logistics operations teams, sold by the seat to the engineers and operations leads who run the floor. The product is judged on reliability, and the brand is meant to say the same thing the software does: precise, plain, and built to be depended on.

## Audience
Engineering and operations leads at mid-sized logistics firms, decided by a technical evaluator rather than a brand buyer. An audience that reads fuss as sloppiness and crispness as competence.

## Positioning
Seats 20 to 60 dollars per user per month. Mid-market infrastructure pricing, sold on reliability and evaluation rather than on brand aspiration. The visual language reads as engineering credibility, never as marketing spend.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Paper White; Grid Indigo or Slate Ink may be the ground only of a reversed example. On Paper White all type is Slate Ink, as all type on exhibitor_card_2025_PRINT.pdf is, and so is the wordmark, the signature move's dense black brick. In the three transparent logo SVGs every mark is Grid Indigo, with Signal Violet as its only possible second colour, and the wordmark and any product name are Slate Ink. On any other light ground a mark takes the same colours or is a one-colour Slate Ink version. On Grid Indigo or Slate Ink, marks and type are Paper White. Outside the marks, Signal Violet and Cool Steel are for rules, dividers and clear-space guides only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel or type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, blur or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No glow, halo, gradient, drop shadow, bevel, gloss highlight or metallic effect on any mark, type, panel, rule or icon, and no colour outside the five palette hexes (#2B2E6B, #6C4BD8, #1C2230, #8A93A6 and #F6F7FA) for marks, type, panels, rules or icons.
- Photography: The eleven portraits keep their own photographic colour and are never tinted, duotoned or overlaid toward Grid Indigo or Signal Violet. Any colour correction moves a portrait toward neutral, never toward a palette colour, including the warm lamp cast of team_kraft.jpg and the slight green cast of team_whitfield.jpg.

## Typography
Display face: Gotham Ultra. Text face: Inter.

- 01 Display / H1: Gotham Ultra, 40 px / 23 px, Ultra, tracking 0 units, never letterspaced.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Inter Regular, 64 px / 68 px, Regular, tracking -10 units.
- 03 Body: Inter Regular, 10 pt / 14 pt, Regular, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Inter Regular, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular, tracking +8 units.

Signature move: The wordmark is Gotham Ultra caps stacked flush left on two lines with leading at 0.82 of cap height, 23 px at a 40 px setting, held deliberately small so it reads as one dense black brick rather than a large logo. Step 01 carries the brick, and tabular figures at step 03 keep the engineering half of the house behaviour visible.

Rules:
- Never letterspace the Ultra. Tracking on the wordmark stays at 0 units.
- The wordmark never goes above 40 px on screen or 12 pt in print. Weight rather than scale carries it.
- The wordmark is always caps stacked flush left on two lines with leading at 0.82 of cap height, so both words lock into one block.
- Gotham Ultra is the wordmark only. No heading, subheading or body line is ever set in it.
- Interface and documentation figures are always tabular so columns align.
- Grid references are always JetBrains Mono Regular, never Inter.
- No hype and no gloss in the copy that fills the scale. Direct, engineer to engineer.

## Voice
Direct, unadorned, engineer to engineer. No hype, no gloss. Example line: "Sharp, plain, restrained. If something in our own list turns out to be a bad idea, tell us before you build it."

## Supplied Identity
The three marks (the main mark and two sub-product marks) were generated by the founder in an AI tool two years ago, on separate nights with different settings. Only the soft-edged raster exports survive, with no source files. They go soft as soon as anyone scales them, and the printer and the app store listing have both said they cannot use them. No production vector artwork exists.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
