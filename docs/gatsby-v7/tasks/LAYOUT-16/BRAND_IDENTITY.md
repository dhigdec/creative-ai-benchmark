# Anvil and Oak

Anvil and Oak is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Providence, Rhode Island, and this commission covers phase 1: studio opening identity and member cards.

## Audience
People aged 25 to 45 choosing coached strength work over a commodity gym, here to get strong, who value programming detail and competence over amenities.

## Positioning
Memberships run 110 to 185 dollars a month with a 28 dollar drop in. Roughly double a chain gym, priced on coaching. Confident and specific, neither a soft boutique wellness studio nor a discount chain.

## Palette
- Anvil Black: #14161A · mandatory. The dark ground of every card and spotlight, and the only ground a display stack or athlete name sits on (type rule: Bone reversed out of Anvil Black). It stands where meetcard_press.pdf uses its own near black #171310.
- Iron: #2C2F35 · optional. A second dark for panels, such as behind the lift figures or around a portrait, where meetcard_press.pdf uses its steel-olive panel #3A3C33. Bone body text, figures and labels may sit on it; display stacks and athlete names do not. Never a type colour.
- Brass: #B8863B · mandatory. The 2 pt rule locked tight under the last line of every display stack and athlete name, as meetcard_press.pdf rules its name in its own brass. Optionally the field labels, fine print or the one colour of the mark. Always flat; never a ground, panel, quote, body text or lift figure.
- Ember: #B5462E · optional accent. Dividers and small marks only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. Never type, a ground, a panel, the mark or the rule under a stack.
- Bone: #E9E3D9 · mandatory. Every display stack and athlete name, reversed out of Anvil Black, and the body copy, quotes and lift figures, where meetcard_press.pdf uses its cream #EDE6D6. Optionally the field labels, fine print or the one colour of the mark. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Anvil Black or Iron and nothing else; display stacks and athlete names sit on Anvil Black. Display stacks and athlete names are Bone, each closed by its 2 pt Brass rule. Body copy, quotes and lift figures are Bone. Field labels and fine print are Bone or Brass. The mark is one solid colour, Bone or Brass, on the dark ground. Ember is an optional accent for dividers and small marks only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. No type sits on a light ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: the 20% multiply tint of #3A3C33 and the dark gradient footer strip that meetcard_press.pdf lays over its photograph are not carried over.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark, including the near matches meetcard_press.pdf prints (#171310, #EDE6D6, #A87B3A and #3A3C33). Brass is the flat #B8863B, never a metallic, foil, gradient or shine effect. No dark type on a light ground, and no brick texture or wall colour carried into the mark.
- Photography: Portraits keep their own corrected colour, graded to one shared white balance with no colour cast, and are never tinted, duotoned or washed toward Brass, Ember, Iron or the #3A3C33 photo-panel tint of meetcard_press.pdf to make them match.

## Typography
Display face: Right Grotesk Compact Black. Text face: Atlas Grotesk.

- 01 Display / H1: Right Grotesk Compact Black, 104 px / 112 px, Black, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Right Grotesk Compact Black, 34 pt / 37 pt, Black, tracking -8 units.
- 03 Body: Atlas Grotesk Regular, 9.5 pt / 13 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Atlas Grotesk Regular, 7.5 pt / 10 pt, Regular, tracking 120 units.

Signature move: Every name and headline is a justified stack of Right Grotesk Compact Black caps, all lines sized to the identical width so the type forms one solid rectangle, closed by a 2 pt Brass rule locked tight under the last line.

Rules:
- Never track the display face positive.
- Every display stack is justified: all lines sized to the identical width so the block reads as one solid rectangle.
- The 2 pt Brass rule is locked tight under the last line of the stack, never floated away from it.
- Display stacks set in Bone reversed out of Anvil Black, never black type on a light ground.
- Lift figures use Atlas Grotesk Medium tabular figures so columns align.
- No hype and no shouted percentages in any line of copy.

## Voice
Direct, plain and sure of itself, the way a good coach cues a lift. No hype, no percentages shouted. Example line the brand would write: Show up, do the work, add weight. We handle the programming so you can focus on the bar.

## Supplied Identity
There is no logo file. The mark, an anvil silhouette inside a ring of lettering with an oak sprig crossing the anvil's horn, exists only as the sign painter's hand painted work on the studio's brick wall, which the coaches have photographed. Athlete portraits were shot by the coaches over three evenings and are not consistent. The old printer's press file is all that survives of the athlete card.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
