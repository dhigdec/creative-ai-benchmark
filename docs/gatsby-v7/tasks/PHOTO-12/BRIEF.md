# SB3-012-PHO | Kilnmore - Ceramic ecommerce image system

A coherent ceramics range, with the glaze differences left intact

We sell the surface as much as the form at Kilnmore. Fourteen pieces and their glaze details need to become one usable product image library, but the work should not flatten the glazes into the same colour or texture.

Scope
1. Ceramic product-page module: 14 named exports. The vessel geometry matches the named product. The glaze retains its material texture, and each module gives the piece's name, glaze, height and diameter, plus its capacity wherever kilnmore_range.csv lists more than 0 ml.
2. Wholesale range one-sheet: 1 named export. The range names agree with the supplied register, and the sheet carries the Kilnmore mark. Trade information is easy to scan: for every piece, each field the brand's own shelf card carries, with capacity only wherever kilnmore_range.csv lists more than 0 ml.
3. Ceramic range launch carousel: 5 named exports. Kilnmore is identified on the opening page by its mark. The range sequence makes product differences clear: every glaze in kilnmore_range.csv is shown on at least one piece, and each piece shown is named with its glaze.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
