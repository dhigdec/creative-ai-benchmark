# Kilnmore

Kilnmore is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Portland, Maine, and this commission covers phase 1: ecommerce product image system.

## Audience
Buyers aged 30 to 55 who buy craft and buy a specific glaze, reading the piece and its finish before they order.

## Positioning
Considered handmade stoneware at 38 to 95 dollars a piece. Above mass import homeware, below gallery ceramics. The house look is neutral and airy with the glaze true to the piece in the hand, never punchy and saturated, never near black gallery styling.

## Palette
- Warm Grey House Ground: #CFC7BB · mandatory where kilnmore_notes.txt places it. The house ground kilnmore_notes.txt names; elsewhere an optional ground or panel. Never a type colour.
- Celadon Drift: #9FB3A2 · optional accent. Only the glaze swatch for pieces in the Celadon Drift glaze, as the colour band on kilnmore_shelf_card.pdf. Never a ground, panel, rule or type colour.
- Oatmeal: #E3DBCB · optional ground and glaze swatch. A light ground or solid panel, and the glaze swatch for pieces in the Oatmeal glaze. Never a type colour.
- Bone: #EFE9DD · optional ground and glaze swatch. A light ground or solid panel, as behind the picture block on kilnmore_shelf_card.pdf, and the glaze swatch for pieces in the Bone glaze. Never a type colour.
- Iron Slip: #3B342B · mandatory. All type, the Kilnmore mark, and the 0.75 pt rule under every piece name and every wordmark or section head set as type in Marcellus, as the glaze line and foot wordmark on kilnmore_shelf_card.pdf; also the glaze swatch for pieces in the Iron Slip glaze. Never a ground or panel.
- Kiln Rust: #9A5A3C · optional accent. Only the glaze swatch for pieces in the Kiln Rust glaze. Never a ground, panel, rule or type colour.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Warm Grey House Ground, Bone or Oatmeal. All type, the Kilnmore mark and the 0.75 pt rule are Iron Slip. A glaze swatch is a solid chip or band in the palette hex of the glaze it labels (Celadon Drift, Bone, Oatmeal, Iron Slip or Kiln Rust), as the Celadon Drift band on kilnmore_shelf_card.pdf; a glaze with no palette hex gets no swatch. Swatches together take no more than 20% of any output's area and never sit behind photography.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No near-black or dark ground and no gallery styling: Iron Slip is never a ground or panel. No punchy or saturated colour in the design, and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, the mark, grounds, panels, rules, swatches, buttons or icons; no hex is made up for a glaze the palette does not name.
- Photography: Photographs keep the true colour of each piece in the hand and are never recoloured toward the palette: the glaze hexes label glazes and are not grading targets, so no celadon is pushed greener or toward Celadon Drift #9FB3A2. The raw wooden bench around a glaze close-up, as in kilnmore_glaze_macros_01.jpg, keeps its own wood colour and is not taken green by a whole-frame correction.

## Typography
Display face: Marcellus. Text face: Untitled Sans.

- 01 Display / H1 · piece name: Marcellus Regular, 56 px / 62 px, Regular, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · wordmark and section head: Marcellus Regular, 34 px / 40 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 03 Body · glaze names and specs: Untitled Sans Regular, 20 px / 30 px, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · dimensions, price, care line: Untitled Sans Regular, 14 px / 21 px, Regular, tracking 0.03 em.

Signature move: A 0.75 pt Iron Slip rule, cut to exactly the width of the word above it and never wider, sits 4 pt under every wordmark and every piece name set in Marcellus.

Rules:
- Never let the rule overhang the word.
- The rule is 0.75 pt in Iron Slip, set exactly the width of the word above it and no wider, sitting 4 pt beneath.
- Piece names and the wordmark are Marcellus Regular only; glaze names and specs stay in Untitled Sans Regular.
- Untitled Sans carries tabular figures throughout, on every spec line and every trade column.
- Piece names run 56 px on the 1440 by 1800 px modules and 24 pt on the A4 sheet; do not resize them per piece.

## Voice
Plain, grounded, specific about material and process. We name the glaze, the form and the firing and stop there. Example line the brand would write: 'Wheel thrown stoneware, matte celadon, oxidised recesses left dark.'

## Supplied Identity
The only clean copy of our mark left is the printed wrapping tissue we wrap orders in. The wordmark and enclosed kiln arch device need recovering from that photograph as placeable artwork; there is no source file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
