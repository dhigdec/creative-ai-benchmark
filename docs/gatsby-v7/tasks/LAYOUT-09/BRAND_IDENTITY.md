# Nimbadesk

Nimbadesk is help desk software for the internal IT and operations teams that keep mid sized companies running. We replace the tangle of shared inboxes and half configured legacy tools with one queue, clear ownership and reporting a director can actually read. We sell to the person who has to defend the line item, so everything we show is real: real screens, real numbers, real periods.

## Audience
IT and operations leads aged thirty to fifty at fifty to five hundred person firms who sign annual contracts and route the invoice past a finance director. They evaluate carefully, compare tiers before reading copy, and distrust hype.

## Positioning
Per seat tiers at 29, 79 and 249 dollars, with enterprise annual near 18,000. Mid market: above the free consumer tools, well below the enterprise suites, priced to be defended rather than impulse bought.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds are Paper, with Mist as a secondary ground or panel and Nimbus Ink as an optional solid panel. On Paper or Mist, headlines, body text and the product name are Nimbus Ink, as the name on nimbadesk_badge_2026.pdf; published figures are Nimbus Ink or Signal Blue, as nimbadesk_ui_03.png sets its KPI figures in Signal Blue on white cards; roles, captions and the mono measurement line are Nimbus Ink or Slate, as the role line on the badge; footnotes are Slate. On Nimbus Ink or Signal Blue, all type is Paper. Signal Blue is only for bands, rules, buttons and figures, and Verify Green only for small status marks; together they cover no more than 10% of the designed, non-photographic area (about 5% on the badge), neither is ever a ground or the colour of a headline or body text, and Verify Green is never a figure.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, as every fill on nimbadesk_badge_2026.pdf is. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over an interface capture or a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons: not the lighter sky blue of the gradient inside nimbadesk_logo_v4.png, and not the amber or red of the status badges inside the captures. The Nimbadesk mark keeps its own colours, and none of them is borrowed for the design.
- Photography: Interface captures and booth headshots keep their own colour and content and are never recoloured toward the palette. The Verify Green, amber and red status colours inside the captures belong to the real screens: they are not counted against the palette and not changed. Faces keep the skin tones of their own frames, and the warm beige and pale grey office walls behind the headshots are not tinted toward Mist or Signal Blue.

## Typography
Display face: Zilla Slab Bold. Text face: Inter Regular.

- 01 Display / H1: Zilla Slab Bold, 64 pt / 68 pt, Bold 700, tracking -8 units, tabular figures.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Zilla Slab Bold, 28 pt / 32 pt, Bold 700, tracking -5 units.
- 03 Body: Inter Regular, 10 pt / 14.5 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 9 pt / 12 pt, Medium 500, tracking +5 units.

Signature move: Every published number is a Zilla Slab Bold figure at 64 pt with its measurement window in IBM Plex Mono Medium at 9 pt flush left directly beneath it, so a figure never stands on its own.

Rules:
- Never publish a figure without its mono measurement line beneath it.
- The measurement window is always flush left under the figure, never centred and never set beside it.
- Every claim states its period.
- Only real screens, real numbers and real periods; nothing illustrative or invented.
- Footnotes stay 7.5 pt Inter Regular in Slate.
- Nothing in the collateral reads as hype; the numbers carry the argument.

## Voice
Direct, specific, understated. Numbers carry the argument and every claim states its period. Example line the brand would write: "Thirty one teams moved their queue to Nimbadesk this year. Here is what changed, and over what window."

## Supplied Identity
No source files exist. The mark was generated by our first designer with an AI tool and delivered only as a flat picture, which is the single copy anywhere. There is no vector, no guideline document and no defined weight. Screenshots were captured ad hoc across the year at whatever scale the display was set to.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
