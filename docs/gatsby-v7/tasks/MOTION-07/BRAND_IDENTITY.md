# Norvant

Norvant is workflow automation built for finance and operations teams. Seats are one hundred and forty dollars a month, and nothing gets bought without three people signing off, so everything we put out has to look like it was made by people who know what they are doing. We are a small team shipping a serious tool, and our March release is the work the whole company has been building toward.

## Audience
A buying committee of three in finance and operations, with a procurement review mid-cycle. They are looking for evidence of seriousness and real numbers, and they lengthen the cycle the moment something feels like consumer marketing.

## Positioning
Considered B2B mid-market. One hundred and forty dollars per seat per month, committee-approved. Not self-serve impulse software, and not heavyweight enterprise. A tool an operations lead can adopt in an afternoon and defend to a controller.

## Palette
- Slate: #33404B · mandatory. A solid ground for title, claim and closing cards; the release name and headings on Paper White, as the city heading on norvant_launch_card_2025.pdf; and the mark on light grounds, cut in slate as on norvant_reception_sign.jpg.
- Amber: #E0A02C · mandatory accent, the only accent. Rules, underlines and small labels, as the seat-tier line on norvant_launch_card_2025.pdf, or a solid panel the mark is reversed out of. Never a claim, the release name, a heading or a ground for type.
- Ink Near-Black: #191F24 · mandatory where claim or body text sits on Paper White. Claim cards, body text and the registration address on Paper White, as the venue, date and host lines on norvant_launch_card_2025.pdf. Never a ground or panel.
- Paper White: #F6F4EF · mandatory. The main light ground, as on norvant_launch_card_2025.pdf; all type set on Slate; and the mark reversed out of Slate or Amber.
- Cool Grey: #8A929A · optional, secondary type only. Fine print and secondary lines on Paper White, such as the address, host title and standing line on norvant_launch_card_2025.pdf. Never a ground, panel, claim, heading or the release name.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds, cards and panels are Paper White, as the ground of norvant_launch_card_2025.pdf, or Slate; Amber is a ground only behind the reversed mark. Any fill beside or around footage that does not fill the frame, such as a pillarbox beside the portrait founder take, is a solid Paper White or Slate ground. On Paper White, the release name and headings are Slate, and claims and body text are Ink Near-Black; fine print and the registration address may be Ink Near-Black or Cool Grey. On Slate, all type and the mark are Paper White; nothing but the reversed Paper White mark sits on Amber. Amber is the one accent, for rules, underlines and small labels: outside a mark panel, no more than 5% of the designed area of any frame.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, in every held frame. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, darkening, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage: text sits on a solid palette card or panel, or directly on the footage. A fade in or out of a whole card or shot during a transition is allowed.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#33404B, #E0A02C, #191F24, #F6F4EF and #8A929A) for type, cards, panels, rules or the mark; no second accent colour beside Amber; no metallic, glow, bevel, gradient or drop-shadow effect on any type, rule or the mark.
- Photography: Footage keeps its own natural colour; a neutral correction or matching grade between clips is allowed, but footage is never tinted, duotoned or washed toward Slate or Amber, and Elise Marchetti's skin tones and the daylight window wall in norvant_founder_take.mp4 stay natural.

## Typography
Display face: PP Right Serif. Text face: Söhne (Buch for copy, Mono for every figure).

- 01 Display / H1: PP Right Serif, 110 px / 118 px, Medium, tracking -6 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: PP Right Serif, 72 px / 80 px, Medium, tracking -4 units.
- 03 Body: Söhne Buch, 54 px / 70 px, Buch, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Söhne Mono, 32 px / 44 px, Buch, tabular figures, tracking +6 units.

Signature move: No figure is ever a serif. Every number, date, seat count and price sets in Söhne Mono tabular, 32 px on screen and 9 pt in print, so an otherwise all serif frame is interrupted only by machine set digits.

Rules:
- Never set a claim card or a figure in PP Right Serif.
- No figure is ever a serif: every number, date, seat count and price sets in Söhne Mono tabular, 32 px on screen and 9 pt in print.
- Headings and the release name set in PP Right Serif Medium at 110 px, 84 px on the vertical cutdown, 30 pt on the printed event card.
- Claim cards set in Söhne Buch at 54 px on 70 px leading; the registration address sets in Söhne Buch at 40 px.
- Print body sets in Söhne Buch at 9.5 pt on 13.5 pt leading.
- No superlatives at any step; a claim card states what the product does and stops.

## Voice
Plain, specific, confident without selling. States what the product does and lets the facts carry it. No stock smiles, no superlatives. Tells people directly what is and is not true.
