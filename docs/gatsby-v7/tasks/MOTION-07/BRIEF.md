# SB3-087-MOT | Norvant - Product explainer motion system

Norvant's March release is called Throughline, and we need something short enough for a finance or operations buyer to watch in a feed without a sales call. Two things about the pack, so you are not surprised: there is no product capture in it, and the footage that does exist is five seconds a clip with no audio. So this is not a demonstration film and it cannot be a narrated one. It is a release film carried by the cleared claims set as on screen text, cut over the founder take and the office material, closing on the one next step we want people to take.

Deliverables

1. Release film: 1 named export, 12 to 15 seconds, 16:9, under the supplied instrumental bed (norvant_bed.wav). It names the release, carries the cleared audit trail claim from norvant_launch_copy.txt word for word, and closes on the registration address. The other two cleared claims are optional. Elise appears from the supplied take as a portrait beat, not as a talking head: her line sits on screen as text.

2. Release film vertical cutdown: 1 named export, 6 to 8 seconds, 9:16, silent, cut from the same material, with Elise's portrait beat from the supplied take among its clips. The release name reads before the registration address.

3. Explainer cover: not part of this round; deliver only the two films above.

House rules for this package

- No speech anywhere in the package. Nothing is narrated, nothing is dubbed, and no synthetic voice is added over the founder take.
- The release is named “Throughline”, never “the Throughline update” and never “Throughline 1.0”.
- The three cleared claims in norvant_launch_copy.txt cannot be reworded, shortened or paraphrased, and no fourth claim exists. If a claim does not fit the runtime, leave it out rather than soften it.
- The closing card of each film carries the registration address norvant.com/march and the Norvant mark, the hexring glyph beside the wordmark NORVANT, recovered from norvant_reception_sign.jpg.
- Video delivery means a playable MP4, not a storyboard, still montage PDF or editing log. Live action edits require adequate distinct footage: looping one short clip is not a substitute for the missing coverage.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
