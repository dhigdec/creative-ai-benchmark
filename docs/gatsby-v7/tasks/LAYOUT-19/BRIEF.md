# SB3-064-LAY | Northwind Advisory - Team page and recruitment collateral

Make eight advisers look like one firm across the site and leave-behind

Northwind Advisory's team photographs were taken on three separate mornings. At present, we look like three different firms. We need a coherent portrait treatment and the team page, capabilities leave-behind and announcement posts built around it. All eight portraits share one neutral colour balance and one crop, and each piece carries the firm's compass-rose mark.

Please prepare
- Advisory team-page composition: 1 named export. Each adviser's entry carries their portrait, their name and the designations they hold. 
- Capabilities leave-behind: 1 named export. The sector offer is clearly structured: our six sectors in their stated order, and a credentials table giving each adviser's name, designations and year admitted. No unsupported credential is added.
- Recruitment announcement: 1 named export. The approved role or recruitment offer is clear: the headline reads, word for word, “We are looking for another adviser to join the firm.” The application action is readable and gives a way to apply using only contact details from our adviser records.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
