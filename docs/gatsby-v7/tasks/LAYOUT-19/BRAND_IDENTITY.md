# Northwind Advisory Partners

Northwind Advisory Partners advises endowments, foundations and around forty families on the long term stewardship of institutional and family capital. We are eight people on one floor, and most mandates we win begin with a trustee reading our team page and deciding whether we look like a firm they can place a hundred million dollars with. The brand exists to signal exactly that: a fiduciary, uniform, unshowy, credentialled, judged by a committee rather than an audience.

## Audience
Endowment trustees, foundation investment committees and families with twenty million dollars and up. Readers aged 45 to 75, most of them reading printed pages and reviewing a credentials table before a mandate is placed.

## Positioning
Billed 350 to 600 dollars an hour. Boutique institutional tier: understated and fiduciary, deliberately not luxury editorial personal branding and not startup about us.

## Palette
- Federal Navy: #1E2A3A · mandatory. Names, headings, body and contact lines on Paper White, as on adviser_card_2024_press.pdf (name, dial and email); the mark on light grounds, where the navy #1D2A3A of the supplied northwind_mark_transparent.svg counts as Federal Navy; and the one dark ground or panel, which carries only Paper White type.
- Paper White: #F5F3EE · mandatory. The main page ground, as on adviser_card_2024_press.pdf, and all type set on Federal Navy; the reversed mark on Federal Navy may be Paper White or keep the white #FFFFFF of the supplied northwind_mark_reversed.svg.
- Slate Grey: #5B646E · optional accent. Secondary type on Paper White only, such as the sector or practice line under a name, as on adviser_card_2024_press.pdf, and fine-print labels. Never a ground or panel.
- Compass Brass: #9C8452 · mandatory under every name. The 0.5 pt under rule beneath every adviser and firm name (the signature move), the designations line, and the mark in its brass colourway, as on adviser_card_2024_press.pdf. Flat ink only; never a ground, panel or body text.
- Conference Grey: #C9CBC9 · optional accent. Hairline rules and dividers, such as the row rules of the credentials table, and the flat ground behind a portrait cut out of its backdrop. Never type, and never a page ground or text panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Page grounds and text panels are Paper White or Federal Navy only; Conference Grey may be a ground only behind a cut-out portrait. On Paper White, names, headings, body and contact lines are Federal Navy, designations may be Compass Brass and secondary lines Slate Grey, as on adviser_card_2024_press.pdf. On Federal Navy, all type is Paper White. The mark is Federal Navy, the navy #1D2A3A of the supplied northwind_mark_transparent.svg, or Compass Brass on Paper White; and Paper White, the white #FFFFFF of the supplied northwind_mark_reversed.svg, or Compass Brass on Federal Navy. Compass Brass is limited to the name under rules, the designations and the mark, and never exceeds 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No metallic, foil or shine effect on the mark, type, rules, panels, buttons or icons, in keeping with a brand that is understated and deliberately not luxury; and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. The mark alone may keep the navy #1D2A3A of the supplied northwind_mark_transparent.svg or the white #FFFFFF of the supplied northwind_mark_reversed.svg.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward Federal Navy, Compass Brass or any other palette colour. The eight portraits are corrected to one shared neutral balance rather than graded toward the palette.

## Typography
Display face: Cabinet Grotesk Medium. Text face: Miller Text Roman.

- 01 Display / H1: Cabinet Grotesk Medium, 56 px / 62 px, Medium, tracking -10 thousandths.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Cabinet Grotesk Medium, 28 px / 32 px, Medium, tracking 60 thousandths.
- 03 Body: Miller Text Roman, 10.5 pt / 15 pt, Roman, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Miller Text Roman, 7.5 pt / 10.5 pt, Roman, tracking 80 thousandths.

Signature move: Every name carries a brass under rule: Cabinet Grotesk Medium caps tracked 60 thousandths sitting above a 0.5 pt Compass Brass rule that runs exactly one third of the name's width, with designations following in Miller Text small caps at 62 percent of the name size.

Rules:
- Never italicise a designation such as CFA.
- Regulated credential designations are reproduced exactly as the practice system holds them.
- Every name sits above a 0.5 pt Compass Brass rule running exactly one third of that name's width, no more and no less.
- Designations follow the name in Miller Text small caps at 62 percent of the name size.
- Credentials tables use tabular figures so the columns align down a printed page.
- The pages must read as one firm rather than eight individuals: one name treatment, no personal variation.
- No superlatives, no performance promises, no exclamation points.

## Voice
Measured, plain, institutional. Speaks of stewardship and process, never of performance promises. Example line the firm would write: We advise a small number of institutions and families on capital they intend to hold for generations. No superlatives, no exclamation points.

## Supplied Identity
The firm mark is a compass rose and wordmark. Two versions survive and neither is a working file: a flat raster export pulled off the old website, and a photograph of the letterpressed card the mark was originally cut for. The mark has to be recovered as placeable artwork with its counters intact before the rebuilt pages can carry it. A third rendering survives on a foil-blocked presentation folder that carries the compass rose alone at large scale, and the only standalone emblem the old web developer ever produced is a fuzzy favicon that blurs at any size, so the mark cannot ship as one lockup: it has to be rebuilt as a working family across formats, from letterhead down to the browser icon, with its counters holding at every reproduction size. Regulated credential designations must be reproduced exactly as the practice system holds them.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
