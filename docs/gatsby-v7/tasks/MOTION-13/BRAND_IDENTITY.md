# Emberline

Emberline is a bright, friendly daily food and craft show built around one host, a sunny studio kitchen and an accessible dish or make each day. It is free to watch and mainstream in the best sense: warm, unfussy and appetising. Because the promo ships every single day, the brand lives or dies on a look that can be reached again tomorrow by whoever is at the desk.

## Audience
Mainstream daytime viewers skewing thirty five to sixty five, watching on a mid range television and on their phones. The show is free and the products featured run ten to thirty dollars.

## Positioning
Regional daytime, free to watch, everyday products. The look must be lively, clean and appetising, never moody, filmic or corporate, and never a two hundred dollar tasting menu with deep shadows and fine type. Hard broadcast punch is the other failure: it stops the food looking edible.

## Palette
- Ember Coral: #E8623D · mandatory. The house colour: the thumbnail's ground behind the dish and the fills behind on-screen straps, as emberline_rundown.md sets them; on the programme cards, the segment-number disc; and the Emberline bug's mark, as on emberline_card_template.pdf. Type on it is Fresh Cream. Never body text or a card note.
- Fresh Cream: #FBF4E7 · mandatory. The ground of every programme card and of any text panel, as on emberline_card_template.pdf; the panel or pill behind the Emberline bug, as in emberline_bug_grab.png; and all type reversed out of Ember Coral.
- Herb Green: #5FA05A · mandatory on the programme cards, for the time and card note. The time line and card note on Fresh Cream, as on emberline_card_template.pdf. Elsewhere optional, for small type on Fresh Cream only. Never a ground, panel or fill.
- Warm Charcoal: #33302B · mandatory where titles, segment names or the strap sit on Fresh Cream. Titles, segment names, the strap line and the show name set on Fresh Cream, as the segment name and strap on emberline_card_template.pdf. Never a ground, panel or fill.
- Sunlit Butter: #F2C14E · optional accent. The inner ember of the Emberline bug, as on emberline_card_template.pdf, and small accents such as an underline or divider. Never a ground, panel, fill or line of type.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds, panels and fills are Fresh Cream or Ember Coral, solid, and nothing else; every programme card sits on Fresh Cream. On Fresh Cream, titles, segment names and the strap are Warm Charcoal, the show name is Warm Charcoal or Ember Coral as in the bug's wordmark, and the time and card notes are Herb Green, as on emberline_card_template.pdf. On Ember Coral, all type is Fresh Cream, as the segment number reversed out of the disc. On-screen text over footage sits on an Ember Coral strap fill or a Fresh Cream panel, never straight on the picture. Sunlit Butter is a small accent, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground or a line of type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, with one exception taken from emberline_card_template.pdf: a Fresh Cream #FBF4E7 pill at 92% opacity may sit behind the Emberline bug where the bug is placed over a photograph or footage, as the bug and its pill sit inside the photo panel on emberline_card_template.pdf. No other tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is used, and no translucent scrim, vignette, gradient, tint or shadow of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage or photographs; solid Ember Coral strap fills and solid Fresh Cream panels carrying type, and the bug with its pill, may sit over them.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, fills, rules, buttons or icons. No Warm Charcoal or other dark ground, panel or fill, which reads moody or corporate, and no gradient, glow or drop shadow on type.
- Photography: Footage and stills keep their own colour. They may be corrected for white balance and exposure, but are never tinted or recoloured toward the palette, never graded moody, filmic or low-key, and never given hard broadcast punch (heavy sharpening, crushed blacks, oversaturation) that stops the food looking edible. The gratin keeps its own golden crust and white glazed dish.

## Typography
Display face: Optima nova. Text face: Omnes.

- 01 Display / H1: Optima nova, 150 px / 165 px, Demi, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Optima nova, 96 px / 106 px, Demi, tracking -0.005 em.
- 03 Body: Omnes, 36 px / 48 px, Regular 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Omnes, 24 px / 32 px, Regular 400, tracking +0.03 em.

Signature move: A single oversized initial. The first letter of every title at step 01 sets at 262 px, 1.75 times the cap height of the rest, sits on the shared baseline and hangs left outside the text block, so one big flared Optima letter always breaks the margin and is the first thing seen.

Rules:
- Never condense Optima to fit.
- Every title carries the oversized initial at 1.75 times cap height, on the shared baseline, hung left outside the text block.
- Titles stay title case so the flared initial reads as an initial.
- Lively, clean and appetising, never moody, filmic or corporate.
- Never hard broadcast punch: it stops the food looking edible.
- The scale must be reachable again tomorrow by whoever is at the desk.

## Voice
Upbeat, plain and welcoming, like a host talking to a regular viewer. Example line the brand would write: "Today on Emberline: a white bean and rosemary gratin you can have on the table in under an hour."

## Supplied Identity
There is no clean brand kit. The typefaces the on air package used survive only as a previous designer's note written from memory, in loose form. The house colour is recorded twice with two different values, one from the on air graphics package and one from the print kit, with nothing saying which governs, so it must be settled before use.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
