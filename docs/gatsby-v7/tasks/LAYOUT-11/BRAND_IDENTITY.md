# Lanternwood Folk Fest

Lanternwood Folk Fest is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is outside Burlington, Vermont, and this commission covers phase 1: performer passes and on-sale campaign.

## Audience
A discovery led crowd aged eighteen to forty, scene literate rather than corporate, who buy on atmosphere and word of mouth and expect the field weekend to feel real rather than packaged.

## Positioning
Weekend general admission 145 dollars, VIP 320, roughly four thousand weekend tickets. Mid tier independent: energetic and coherent, a credible VIP tier without tipping into either luxury restraint or bargain flyer chaos.

## Palette
- Night Field: #1B2A2E · mandatory. The solid ground of every credential, edge to edge, in the place of last year's dark ground on lanternwood_pass_2026.pdf. The only ground; never type.
- Lantern Amber: #E8A13A · optional accent. A full-width bar behind the role word, with the role word in Dusk Ink, in the place of last year's role bar on lanternwood_pass_2026.pdf; small labels such as Stages and Days on Night Field; and the lantern's three window panes in the festival mark, painted yellow on lanternwood_banner_mainstage.jpg. Never the ground or body text.
- Ember Red: #C24634 · mandatory. The 3 pt rule beneath the person's name and beneath any typeset festival name, as the type system requires; may also fill a full-width role bar with the role word in Bone. Never type, the ground or a panel.
- Meadow: #6E8B5B · optional accent. Hairline rules and dividers; may also fill a full-width role bar with the role word in Bone. Never type, the ground or a panel.
- Bone: #F2EAD9 · mandatory. All type set on Night Field (the name, act, role word, access areas, days on site and sponsor lines) except small labels, which may be Lantern Amber, in the place of last year's cream type on lanternwood_pass_2026.pdf; the role word on an Ember Red or Meadow role bar, reversed out as last year's was; and the festival mark reversed out of Night Field.
- Dusk Ink: #12181B · mandatory where type sits on Lantern Amber. The role word, or any other type, set on a Lantern Amber bar. Never type on Night Field, where it sits too close in value to read, and never the ground.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Night Field is the only ground: every credential is solid Night Field from edge to edge. On Night Field, all type and the festival mark are Bone; small labels may be Lantern Amber, and the lantern's window panes in the mark may be Lantern Amber or knocked out to the Night Field ground. A full-width bar behind the role word may be Lantern Amber (role word in Dusk Ink), Ember Red or Meadow (role word in Bone), one colour per role. Otherwise Ember Red is only the 3 pt rule beneath each name line and Meadow only hairline rules and dividers. Lantern Amber, Ember Red and Meadow together cover no more than 10% of the credential's area and are never the ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, bars, panels, rules, icons or the festival mark; no metallic, foil or gradient finish on any of them; and no type in Dusk Ink on Night Field, or in any colour that sits close in value to the ground beneath it.
- Photography: A credential carries no photograph unless that person's own headshot is supplied, and a supplied headshot keeps its own colour, never recoloured toward the palette. The festival mark is lifted from the painting on lanternwood_banner_mainstage.jpg as flat artwork in palette colours; the banner's canvas weave, stains, shading and charcoal-green paint are not carried onto the credential.

## Typography
Display face: Syne Extra. Text face: Public Sans Regular, with Roboto Mono Regular for credential numbers and set times.

- 01 Display / H1: Syne Extra, 30 pt / 32 pt, Extra, tracking force justified, tracked out to roughly +200 units at this size until the line touches both trim edges.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Public Sans, 13 pt / 16 pt, Regular, tracking +40 units.
- 03 Body: Public Sans, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Roboto Mono, 9 pt / 12 pt, Regular, tracking +30 units.

Signature move: Every performer name and the festival name is force justified: Syne Extra tracked out until the single line touches both trim edges of the card, sitting on a 3 pt Ember Red rule. At credential size that is 30 pt on 32 pt at roughly +200 units, on the poster 240 px on 250 px, and the tracking is re-cut for every line so the block always reads as type jammed against both edges.

Rules:
- Never let a performer name wrap to a second line.
- The festival name and every performer name are force justified, tracked out until the single line touches both trim edges, and always sit on a 3 pt Ember Red rule.
- Syne Extra is reserved for names. It never sets roles, access areas, body or numbers.
- Public Sans Regular sets roles, access areas and body. Roboto Mono Regular sets credential numbers and set times, and nothing else.
- Body is flush left and ragged right at 8.5 pt on 12 pt leading. Never force justify the body.
- Give the discovery acts the poster space, at the same weight and treatment as the headliners.
- Keep sponsor and billing lines exactly as agreed.

## Voice
Warm, direct, a bit of grit. It talks up the discovery stages as loudly as the headliners and keeps sponsor and billing lines exactly as agreed. Example line the brand would write: "Gates at five. Three stages till late. Come early for the names you will be bragging about next year."

## Supplied Identity
Our designer left in 2024 and took the logo files. The mark now survives only as a photograph of the painted canvas banner that has hung over the main stage since 2022. There is no vector, no source file and no written type or colour spec. Last year's photography was shot by two volunteers on two cameras with no brief.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
