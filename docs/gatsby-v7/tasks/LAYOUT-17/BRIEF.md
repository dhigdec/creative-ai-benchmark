# SB3-062-LAY | Apexguard - Product label and dealer collateral system

Dealer-ready roll labels and bay imagery for Apexguard

Our dealer meetings need a reliable label system, not another set of labels assembled one by one. Apexguard has three tiers that should read as one family, and the current stock export must control the product information.

The commission

 Product roll label: 13 named exports. The tier is identified accurately, and each label carries the Apexguard mark. The complete warranty_statement remains inside the label trim.

 Dealer range brochure: 1 named export. The product tiers are easy to compare: for each tier it gives film gauge, clarity retained, self-healing topcoat, hydrophobic contact angle, UV rejection, finish and warranty term, and each width offered with its sku_code and dealer price. The uses are supported by the approved range data, and the brochure carries the Apexguard mark and at least one of the supplied bay photographs.
