# Apexguard Surface Systems

Apexguard Surface Systems is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Charlotte, North Carolina, and this commission covers phase 2: dealer labels and channel collateral.

## Audience
Dealer group principals and fixed operations managers aged 35 to 55 deciding whether to put us on their reconditioning line, and independent installer owners aged 28 to 50 who buy film by the roll and post their own work. Behind both sits a vehicle owner aged 30 to 55 protecting a sixty thousand dollar car.

## Positioning
Full PPF installs 1,800 to 6,000 dollars, tint packages 400 to 1,200 dollars, film by the roll to installers 900 to 4,200 dollars. Premium performance tier, well above discount tint shops and deliberately not tuner.

## Palette
- Graphite Black: #191C21 · mandatory. All type on Titanium White (SKU, tier name, roll size, performance labels and figures, warranty, price), as on rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf, and the one dark ground or panel colour, which carries Titanium White type and the reversed mark.
- Titanium White: #F3F4F5 · mandatory. The label stock and main ground, as on rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf, and all type reversed out of Graphite Black.
- Steel Grey: #6C7178 · optional secondary neutral. Small section heads on Titanium White only: the SURFACE SYSTEMS line and the PERFORMANCE and WARRANTY heads, which rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf draws as 72% and 65% tints of Graphite Black. Never a ground, panel, figure or main type.
- Silver Line: #B9BEC4 · optional secondary neutral. Table row rules and dividers on Titanium White, where rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf draws its row rules as a 16% tint of Graphite Black. Never type, a ground, a panel or a figure.
- Guard Bronze: #B08A54 · mandatory: at least one hairline rule on every label and on the brochure, as the one bronze rule of rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf. Hairline rules only, at least one on each label and in the brochure: the 0.5 pt hairline under the APEXGUARD wordmark wherever the wordmark is set as type, and any other accent rule. Never type, a ground, a panel or a fill.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Titanium White or Graphite Black and nothing else. On Titanium White, type is Graphite Black, as on rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf, with Steel Grey only for small section heads such as PERFORMANCE, WARRANTY and the SURFACE SYSTEMS line. On Graphite Black, type is Titanium White only. Silver Line is for table rules and dividers only. Guard Bronze is the one accent and is mandatory: at least one hairline rule on every label and on the brochure, as the one bronze rule of rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf, including the 0.5 pt hairline under a typeset wordmark; hairline rules only, never type, a ground or a panel, and under 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. The Apexguard mark keeps its one supplied ink, dark on light grounds and white on Graphite Black, and is not counted as type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. Where rolllabel_2025_PRINT.pdf draws heads at 72% and 65% and row rules at 16% of Graphite Black, use solid Steel Grey for the heads and solid Silver Line for the rules.
- Forbidden: No chrome, bevel, foil, sheen or other metallic effect on any type, panel, rule or icon (Guard Bronze is a flat ink), no heavy gradient, starburst or percentage flash, and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. The Apexguard mark prints in one flat ink, as supplied, never two-tone, gradient or metallic: dark on light grounds, white on Graphite Black. That supplied mark ink is exempt from the hex list.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted, duotoned or desaturated toward Graphite Black, Steel Grey or Guard Bronze. The dark window tint in bay_frame_06.jpg still reads as tint, not plain grey glass, and the clear film edge in bay_frame_04.jpg stays clear and glossy, not a grey wash.

## Typography
Display face: Excon Bold. Text face: Whitney Book.

- 01 Display / H1: Excon Bold, 40 pt / 44 pt, Bold, tracking -10 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Excon Bold, 30 pt / 33 pt, Bold, tracking 20 units.
- 03 Body: Whitney Book, 9.5 pt / 13.5 pt, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Whitney Book, 7 pt / 10 pt, Book, tracking 15 units.

Signature move: Every performance figure is set in Excon Bold at three times its label size with the unit locked to the same baseline at exactly one third of the figure, so value and unit read as a single measured object. Against the 8 pt spec label that resolves to a 24 pt figure with an 8 pt unit.

Rules:
- Never italicise a figure.
- Every performance figure is Excon Bold at three times its label size, with the unit on the same baseline at exactly one third of the figure.
- The wordmark is Excon Bold caps tracked 120 units over a 0.5 pt Guard Bronze hairline.
- Spec tables and every figure in them use tabular lining figures.
- No exclamation points and no percentage flashes; state the number and let it carry.
- Nothing in the system reads tuner: restrained, technical, premium.

## Voice
Plain, exact, performance led. States numbers and lets them carry. Example line the brand would write: Ten year warranty, self healing topcoat, 92 percent clarity retained. No exclamation points, no percentage flashes, no hype.
