# Sable and Finch Realty

Sable and Finch Realty sells character homes along the North Shore to move up families who are choosing carefully between a small number of houses. We present a property the way a buyer wants to feel about it: calm, warm and reassuring. Our work is quiet on purpose, because at this price calm reads as confidence.

## Audience
Move up buyers roughly 35 to 48 with children, weighing this house against three or four comparable homes in the same school district, who need the facts on screen and the feeling in the frame.

## Positioning
Homes around and above 1.2 million dollars, the established coastal middle of the market. Presentation sits between a fast starter home listing and a near silent luxury film, and must read as neither.

## Palette
- Sable Ink: #2B2724 · mandatory. The address and body type on Shell, as on sablefinch_open_house_card.pdf; fine print (type system step 04); and the caption band behind caption type over footage, solid or at the one permitted 62% tint.
- Finch Gold: #C9A24B · mandatory, for the town rule alone. The 3 pt town rule, 120 px wide and flush left, directly beneath the address. Otherwise only the Sable and Finch mark's own colour, its wordmark included, as in sablefinch_mark_scan.png. Never a line of type outside the mark, a panel, a band, a button or any other rule.
- Shell: #EFE9DE · mandatory. The ground of every caption panel and of the end card, as on sablefinch_open_house_card.pdf, and all type set on the Sable Ink caption band. Never replaced by plain white.
- Harbor Slate: #3E5567 · optional secondary type colour. The town word, fact lines and fine print on Shell, as on sablefinch_open_house_card.pdf. Never a ground, panel or band.
- Sea Mist: #8FA79E · optional accent. Hairline dividers and the tagline “The North Shore, unhurried” on Shell, as on sablefinch_open_house_card.pdf. Never fine print, a ground, a panel or a band.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Shell, solid, or a Sable Ink caption band, solid or at the one permitted tint; nothing else is a ground, and no type is set straight onto footage. On Shell, the address is Sable Ink, the town word, fact lines and fine print are Sable Ink or Harbor Slate, and the tagline may be Sea Mist, as on sablefinch_open_house_card.pdf. On the Sable Ink band, all type is Shell. Finch Gold is the town rule alone, plus the mark's own gold as in sablefinch_mark_scan.png. Sea Mist and Finch Gold together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-footage area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, with one exception taken from sablefinch_open_house_card.pdf: a Sable Ink #2B2724 caption band at 62% opacity may sit directly behind caption type laid over footage, carrying Shell type. No other tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is used, and no other scrim, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage or stills.
- Forbidden: No Finch Gold on any line of type, panel, band, button or rule other than the 3 pt town rule (the Sable and Finch mark keeps its own gold); no gold, bronze, copper or other metallic effect; no plain white panel in place of Shell; and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, bands, rules, buttons or icons.
- Photography: Footage and stills keep their own colour: they are graded only so the takes match one another, never tinted or recoloured toward the palette, and no sky, window, wall or room is repainted, filled, extended or regenerated.

## Typography
Display face: Manrope Light. Text face: National 2 Regular.

- 01 Display / H1 · address: Manrope Light, 58 px / 64 px, Light, tracking minus 0.010 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · gold town line: Manrope Medium, 20 px / 26 px, Medium, capitals, tracking plus 0.300 em.
- 03 Body · property facts and price: National 2 Regular, 30 px / 42 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print · disclosure: National 2 Regular, 20 px / 28 px, Regular, tracking plus 0.020 em.

Signature move: The gold town line. Under every address the town sets in Manrope Medium capitals at 20 px, about a third the address size, tracked plus 0.300 em, on a 3 pt Finch Gold rule 120 px wide and flush left, so a viewer first registers a small gold bar carrying one very quiet, very spread word.

Rules:
- Never let a line other than the town set in capitals.
- The town always sits on a 3 pt Finch Gold rule 120 px wide and flush left, directly beneath the address, and the rule length never changes with the length of the town name.
- Finch Gold is reserved for that rule; no other line takes it.
- The price sets in National 2 tabular figures at 34 px so figures align across listings.
- Every figure a buyer relies on, the price, the bed and bath count, the school district and the disclosure line, must be exactly right on every piece.
- In print the address sets 30 pt and the rest of the scale holds the same proportion.
- Presentation stays between a fast starter home listing and a near silent luxury film, and must read as neither.

## Voice
Warm, measured, reassuring, never a hard sell. Example: "Two streets back from the water, shingled, and the porch light is already on."

## Supplied Identity
There is no packaged brand kit. Last season's open house card survives only as the printer's press PDF, and the on screen captions have always been rebuilt by hand each listing. Colour and type live in prior collateral rather than in a guideline anyone can hand over.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
