# Meridian Academy

Meridian Academy sells a single self-paced program, Operations Analytics for Working Managers, at two hundred and ninety dollars. Our learners are thirty to fifty, mid-career, watching lessons late at night after their own workday. We are not a bootcamp and we are not a university. We teach one useful thing well and we respect the viewer's time and intelligence.

## Audience
Working managers aged thirty to fifty, paying with their own money, fitting the course around a full-time job. They want a working skill, not a transformation story, and they distrust anything that feels like marketing.

## Positioning
Mid-market. A two hundred and ninety dollar considered purchase. The register sits between an impulse-priced tutorial and a formal institution: competent, quiet, self-funded professional development.

## Palette
- Deep Slate: #2E3A45 · mandatory. The lesson-card ground behind the Cream Stock numeral and topic (type system step 01), a ground around a framed screen panel, and type or the crest set on Cream Stock, as the deep ink on meridian_certificate_print.jpg.
- Cream Stock: #F4EFE4 · mandatory. The lesson numeral and all type or crest set on Deep Slate (type system step 01), and the light ground for teaching cards and the crest, as the cream stock of meridian_certificate_print.jpg.
- Ink Charcoal: #1C232A · optional type colour. Teaching-card, label and fine-print type on Cream Stock grounds, as a darker alternative to Deep Slate. Never a ground or panel.
- Muted Sage: #7C8A78 · optional accent. Hairline rules and the frame line around a screen panel only. Never a ground, panel or type colour.
- Warm Grey: #B7B0A4 · optional accent. Hairline rules and the frame line around a screen panel only. Never a ground, panel or type colour.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Deep Slate or Cream Stock and nothing else. The lesson card is Deep Slate with the numeral and all its type in Cream Stock, as the type system sets the numeral. On Deep Slate, all type and the crest are Cream Stock. On Cream Stock, type and the crest are Deep Slate or Ink Charcoal, as meridian_certificate_print.jpg prints the crest in one deep ink on cream stock. Type is never set directly over footage: it sits on a solid Deep Slate or Cream Stock ground beside, above or below the footage. Muted Sage and Warm Grey are for hairline rules and the frame line of a screen panel only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-footage area, never a ground, panel or type colour.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, on every held frame. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over the screen or lecture footage. A straight cut or a plain fade between two shots is a transition, not a tint.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, frames or icons, plain white and black included. No gradient, glow, drop shadow or other effect on any type, panel or the crest. The crest is never set in a sans, and no line carries an exclamation mark.
- Photography: The supplied footage (meridian_lesson4_lecture.mp4, meridian_screencast_01.mp4, meridian_screencast_02.mp4, meridian_screencast_03.mp4, meridian_screencast_04.mp4 and meridian_screencast_05.mp4) keeps its own colour and is never tinted, duotoned or graded toward the palette. The crest from meridian_certificate_print.jpg is shown as clean artwork in one solid palette colour, never as a cut-out of the photographed certificate.

## Typography
Display face: Larish Neue. Text face: Untitled Sans.

- 01 Display / H1: Larish Neue, 280 px / 294 px, Medium, tracking -12 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Larish Neue, 96 px / 104 px, Medium, tracking -8 units.
- 03 Body: Untitled Sans, 54 px / 68 px, Regular, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Untitled Sans, 38 px / 48 px, Regular, tracking +8 units.

Signature move: Every lesson card opens with an oversized Larish Neue Medium lesson numeral at 280 px, Cream Stock on Deep Slate, cropped by the left frame edge so a third of the figure runs off screen with the 96 px topic stacked beside it.

Rules:
- Never typeset the crest in a sans.
- Certificates set the crest in Larish Neue small caps at 22 pt, tracked 120 units, over a 1.5 pt rule.
- The oversized lesson numeral is never fully inside the frame; the left frame edge always crops about a third of it.
- The lesson topic drops to 72 px on the vertical cutdown; every other step holds.
- The lesson label sets in Untitled Sans Medium at 34 px; teaching cards and captions stay Untitled Sans Regular.
- No exclamation and no hype in any line set at any step.

## Voice
Direct, warm, matter of fact. Speaks to a peer, not a beginner. Short sentences, no hype, no exclamation. The tone of a good colleague explaining something over coffee.
