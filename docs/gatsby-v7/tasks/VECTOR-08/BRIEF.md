# SB3-038-VEC | Coble & Vane - One-color mark system

Distillery mark recovery and a matching set of packaging photographs

This is a focused identity-production commission for Coble & Vane. We need the existing mark recovered in colour, as a supporting preview, and in true one-ink form, alongside our three packaging photographs, of the neck seal, the crate end and the cap top, which currently disagree because of their lighting: the supplier sheet shows each one beside the version applied there, all three brought to one neutral light.

The commission

 One-colour packaging identity: 1 named export. The mark uses the one-ink treatment our suppliers ask for in supplier_emails.txt. The counter spaces remain open on the neck seal, at the seal size the supplier sheet states and shows at actual size.

 Cask-release label artwork: 8 named exports. The release details match this record: the label carries our recovered one-ink mark, the cask's photograph, and its expression name, cask number, strength, outturn, filling date, bottling date, volume and distilled-at line, each written as the record writes it, with the expression name, set in our display face, as its largest line, above the cask number and strength. Last season's tasting note does not carry over, and the label claims nothing beyond what this record and our brand identity support. The label respects the supplied packaging area.

 Packaging supplier application sheet: 1 named export. The ink treatment is unambiguous: for the neck seal, the crate end and the cap top, the sheet names the version that item's supplier receives and the one ink or finish it is applied in. The application scale is stated: the size of the mark on the neck seal, the crate end and the cap top, taking a supplier's own figure wherever one is given.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
