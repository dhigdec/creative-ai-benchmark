# Coble & Vane

Coble & Vane is a working distillery on a stretch of the North Sea coast, named for the flat-bottomed fishing boat that once beached below the still house. We release twelve casks a year and nothing we cannot stand behind. The bottle is meant to earn its place on a shelf and in a hand, not to shout across the shop.

## Audience
Buyers aged 30 to 55 choosing a gift or a considered bottle, and the independent merchant deciding whether the bottle earns its shelf facing. People who read a label closely and reward restraint.

## Positioning
Bottles 55 to 120 dollars: standard gin at 55, cask-strength single malt from 95 to 120. Upper-craft tier, sitting deliberately apart from the twelve-dollar shelf. Value is carried by finish, emboss and wax rather than by loud print.

## Palette
- Deep Sea Iron: #1F2A2E · mandatory on the labels and the supplier sheet. The one deep ink: expression names, the wordmark, cask numbers, strengths, dates, body text and headings, as on cask_labels_2025_PRINT.pdf, and the mark wherever it is printed on a label.
- Oxblood Wax: #5A1F1B · mandatory wherever the wordmark is set. The 0.75 pt hairline 1.5 mm beneath the wordmark, and rules, as on cask_labels_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, body text or the mark; on the supplier sheet it may also fill a small swatch naming an item's ink or finish.
- Cold Slate: #6E7B80 · optional accent. Small field labels such as CASK NO. and STRENGTH, and the legal line, as on cask_labels_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, body text or the mark; on the supplier sheet it may also fill a small swatch naming an item's ink or finish.
- Bleached Rope: #D9CFB8 · mandatory on the labels. The ground of the whole label sheet, as on cask_labels_2025_PRINT.pdf, and the only colour a ground or panel on the supplier sheet may take, apart from a small swatch naming an item's ink or finish. Never type.
- Coble Black: #141414 · mandatory in one-ink-mark.svg. The single solid fill of one-ink-mark.svg, the black-on-transparent art supplier_emails.txt asks for, and that mark and the versions drawn from it wherever the supplier sheet shows them as supplied, or a small swatch naming an item's ink. Never type, and never on a label, where Deep Sea Iron is the one deep ink.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: On every label the ground is Bleached Rope; expression names, the wordmark, values, body text and the mark are Deep Sea Iron, the one deep ink; Cold Slate is for small field labels and the legal line; Oxblood Wax is for the wordmark hairline and rules, as on cask_labels_2025_PRINT.pdf. The supplier sheet keeps the same pairings on Bleached Rope or the unprinted page, shows one-ink-mark.svg and the versions drawn from it in Coble Black as supplied, and may show the ink or finish an item is applied in as a small swatch. Cold Slate and Oxblood Wax together stay under 5% of the designed, non-photographic area of any label or page, and are never a ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, in the mark, on the labels and on the supplier sheet: no tint, screen, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: Nothing shiny: no gold, silver, bronze, copper, foil, metallic or gloss effect on any type, rule, mark, panel or icon. No second deep ink beside Deep Sea Iron on a label, and no colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, the mark or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. cv_packaging_wax_seal.jpg, cv_packaging_crate_end.jpg and cv_packaging_cap_top.jpg are brought to one neutral light, and the wax in cv_packaging_wax_seal.jpg keeps its photographed texture and is not replaced by a flat fill. Each cask photograph keeps the colour of its spirit as shot.

## Typography
Display face: Mercury Display Roman. Text face: National 2.

- 01 Display / H1: Mercury Display Roman, 22 pt / 24 pt, Roman, tracking 200 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: National 2 Medium, 14 pt / 17 pt, Medium, tracking +25 units.
- 03 Body: National 2 Regular, 8 pt / 11 pt, Regular, tracking 0 units · max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: National 2 Regular, 6 pt / 8.5 pt, Regular, tracking +12 units.

Signature move: The wordmark is Mercury Display Roman caps at 22 pt tracked to 200 units with a 0.75 pt Oxblood Wax hairline cut to the exact optical width of that letterspaced line sitting 1.5 mm below it. Step 01 carries the tracked bar and the hairline, and tabular lining figures at step 02 keep the second half of the house behaviour visible.

Rules:
- Never track the wordmark below 200 units.
- The 0.75 pt Oxblood Wax hairline is cut to the exact optical width of the letterspaced wordmark and sits 1.5 mm beneath it, never a standard rule width.
- Cask number and strength are always National 2 Medium tabular lining figures so columns align across a case.
- Supplier sheets set body at 9.5 pt rather than 8 pt. No other body size exists.
- One deep ink, generous clear space and nothing shiny. Value is carried by finish, emboss and wax, never by louder print.
- Say plainly what a version can and cannot do. The type sheet is written as instruction, not as advertisement.

## Voice
Plain, dry, unhurried. Written as instruction, not advertisement. Example line: "One ink, plenty of air, and nothing shiny. If our own list looks wrong to you, say so before you make it."

## Supplied Identity
The house mark was drawn six years ago by an illustrator who has since moved abroad and cannot be reached. It survives only as a full-colour illustration flattened to a single PNG. There are no vector files, no one-ink versions, and no supplier guidance sheet. Every single-colour version a supplier has ever received was improvised from that one file, and no two have matched.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
