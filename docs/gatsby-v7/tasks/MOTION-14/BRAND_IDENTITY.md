# The Halden Diaries

The Halden Diaries is a self shot, self edited vlog in which Nate Halden films an ordinary week in his own flat and puts out one episode every Sunday. The channel promises intimacy over production: the same rooms, the same face, the same unhurried attention week after week. Season two is its first with a sponsor, and the whole point is that the sponsor changed nothing about how the week feels.

## Audience
Loyal rather than large, aged 24 to 38, patrons who pay 6 dollars a month for closeness and receive a printed card set at each season end. They buy the feeling of a friend filming his week, not a studio production.

## Positioning
Patron subscriptions at 6 dollars a month with a printed card set mailed at season end, first sponsored season attached. Sits deliberately between a phone edit and a studio channel: domestic warmth held to a professional standard.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Grounds, cards, panels and plates are Warm Paper, Limewashed Pine or Board Ink and nothing else; Warm Paper is the main ground of every cover and of each film's closing card, as on halden_patron_card_2025.pdf. On Warm Paper or Limewashed Pine, the week numeral, episode title, date, standfirst and captions are Board Ink, and the channel address is Board Ink or Window Sage, as on the card. On Board Ink, all type is Warm Paper. Type set directly on footage or a photograph is Warm Paper or Board Ink. The HALDEN wordmark and its hairline rule are always Board Ink on Warm Paper or Limewashed Pine, as painted on halden_desk_board.jpg. Window Sage is used only for the channel address, and Kitchen Terracotta and Evening Dusk only for rules; together these three accents cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area of any cover or frame, and none is ever a ground.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, on every cover and in every held frame. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, darkening, gradient, vignette or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over footage or a photograph: text sits on a solid palette card, panel or plate, or directly on the picture. A fade in or out of a whole card or shot during a transition is allowed.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes (#2B2A33, #EDE6D6, #C06A47, #8B9B82, #6E7480 and #F6F2E9) for type, cards, panels, plates, rules or the wordmark; no metallic, glow, bevel, gradient or drop-shadow effect on any type, rule or the wordmark; and no pine grain, board edge, hanging string, wall or lamp light from halden_desk_board.jpg carried into the wordmark artwork.
- Photography: Footage keeps its own colour, or a neutral white-balance correction of it. Stills used on the covers are pulled to one neutral white point, so the three covers read as one season, without turning the flat into a studio. Nothing is tinted, duotoned or washed toward a palette colour, and halden_still_03.jpg and halden_still_05.jpg are never pushed toward Kitchen Terracotta.

## Typography
Display face: Avenir Next. Text face: Freight Text Pro.

- 01 Display / H1: Avenir Next, 260 px / 273 px, Demi Bold, tracking -0.02 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Freight Text Pro, 44 px / 58 px, Book 400, tracking +0.01 em.
- 03 Body: Freight Text Pro, 44 px / 58 px, Book 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Avenir Next, 28 px / 38 px, Regular 400, tracking +0.04 em.

Signature move: An extreme size jump. Step 01 sets the week number as one Avenir Next Demi Bold numeral at 260 px directly above step 02, the episode title at 44 px, roughly six to one, with the board's redrawn 2 px rule between them cut to the exact width of that numeral.

Rules:
- Never set HALDEN in Avenir Next.
- HALDEN stays the painted board lettering and is never retyped.
- The rule between the numeral and the title is redrawn at 2 px and cut to the numeral's exact width.
- Captions stay sentence case.
- Domestic warmth held to a professional standard: never a phone edit, never a studio channel.

## Voice
First person, plain, unhurried, a little self deprecating. He writes the way he talks to camera. Example line: 'An ordinary week, filmed. Thanks for staying with it.'

## Supplied Identity
No brand files have ever existed. The channel name lives only as a board Nate hand painted on limewashed pine before the first episode, one deep ink with a painted hairline rule beneath it, and it survives only as a handheld photograph. The wordmark HALDEN has to be recovered from that photograph as usable artwork. There is no logo file, no colour spec and no prior guideline.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
