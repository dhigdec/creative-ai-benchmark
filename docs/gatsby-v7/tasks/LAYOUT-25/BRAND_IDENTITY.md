# Aldervale Mutual Assurance

Aldervale Mutual Assurance is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is central Vermont, and this commission covers phase 2: personalised renewal mailer.

## Audience
Homeowners and farm owners aged forty to seventy in small Vermont towns, many insured here for decades, who value a known producer and a fair renewal over the lowest online quote.

## Positioning
Average home policy runs 1,100 to 1,900 dollars a year. Mid market and relationship led, not a discount carrier and not a private wealth boutique. Communications stay restrained: one photograph, generous margins, the offer stated once and quietly, the producer named larger than the offer.

## Palette
- House Green: #21473A · mandatory. The mark's shield ring and oak leaf, as on aldervale_producer_card.jpg and aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf; keylines and rules, such as the portrait frame on aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf; short labels such as that card's “Your renewal” line; any heading not set in Slate Ink; and any band or panel, which carries only Oat Paper type. Bands and panels together cover no more than 25% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Warm Grey: #8B8378 · mandatory in the mark's wordmark, otherwise optional. The ALDERVALE MUTUAL wordmark, and small supporting lines at 10 pt or smaller, such as the regulatory line, as on aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf, where on each side every Warm Grey line is set smaller than every Slate Ink line. Never the salutation, address, producer name, body, offer, a heading, a panel or a ground. A due date set as a step 02 heading is Slate Ink or House Green, not Warm Grey.
- Oat Paper: #F3EDE1 · mandatory. The ground of every mailer and the ground the mark sits on, as on aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf, and any type reversed out of House Green.
- Slate Ink: #2A2926 · mandatory. The salutation, address, producer name, body and offer on Oat Paper, as on aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf, and any heading not set in House Green. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Oat Paper is the ground and covers most of the designed, non-photographic area, as on aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf; House Green is the only other colour that may form a band or panel, and House Green bands and panels together cover no more than 25% of the designed, non-photographic area (aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf has none). On Oat Paper, the salutation, address, producer name, body and offer are Slate Ink, and headings are Slate Ink or House Green; House Green is also for the mark's shield ring and oak leaf, keylines, rules and short labels. Warm Grey is only for the mark's wordmark and small supporting lines at 10 pt or smaller, such as the regulatory line, as on the card, where on each side every Warm Grey line is smaller than every Slate Ink line; it is never the address, the producer name, a heading, the offer, a panel or a ground, and a due date set as a step 02 heading is Slate Ink or House Green. On House Green, all type is Oat Paper.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: the portrait sits in its frame as a solid photograph, with no fade into the ground.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the four palette hexes for type, panels, rules, keylines, buttons or icons. The mark is never recoloured: its shield ring and oak leaf stay House Green and its wordmark Warm Grey, as printed on aldervale_renewal_card_2025.pdf.
- Photography: Portraits keep their own colour and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward House Green or Oat Paper. In the four meeting-room frames, aldervale_producers/ada_fenwick.jpg, aldervale_producers/harlan_pease.jpg, aldervale_producers/merritt_dolan.jpg and aldervale_producers/roy_latimer.jpg, the pale collar is never repainted or recoloured to separate it from the cream wall behind it.

## Typography
Display face: Pitch. Text face: Chronicle Text G1.

- 01 Display / H1: Pitch Semibold, 26 pt / 29 pt, Semibold, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Pitch Medium, 16 pt / 19 pt, Medium, tracking 0.
- 03 Body: Chronicle Text G1 Roman, 11 pt / 16 pt, Roman, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Chronicle Text G1 Roman, 10 pt / 14 pt, Roman, tracking 0.015 em.

Signature move: The running sentence: the producer's name sets in Pitch Semibold at 26 pt as the opening clause of a sentence that carries on into the body, so step 01 always ends in a comma and the step 03 line beneath it begins in lower case.

Rules:
- Never set the offer in capitals or larger than the producer name; it appears once, at 12 pt.
- The producer's name always ends in a comma and the line beneath it begins in lower case.
- The producer is always named larger than the offer.
- Producer names and headings take Pitch; body, address and offer take Chronicle Text G1 Roman.
- One photograph, generous margins, the offer stated once and quietly.
- The mark never sits on a photograph.

## Voice
Warm, direct and adult. We write to the household, never at the market, and we never oversell. Example line the brand would write: "Your renewal is due in October, and your producer, will be glad to walk it through with you."

## Supplied Identity
