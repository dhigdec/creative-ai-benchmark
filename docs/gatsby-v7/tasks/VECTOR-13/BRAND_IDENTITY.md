# Maison Serault

Maison Serault is a small fine jewelry house built around its founder's own hand. Pieces are made and sold through the boutique and a few trusted stockists, and everything leaves in packaging people keep. The house is changing box suppliers, and the new supplier needs proper artwork before it will quote.

## Audience
Buyers roughly 30 to 55 purchasing as a gift or for themselves, plus a small number of stockists. They value discretion and craft over logos.

## Positioning
Fine jewelry from 600 to 4,000 dollars. Positioned as an independent house: hairline refinement, high contrast, a small mark on a large quiet field, never mass market and never loud.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: The packaging label and both stationery pages have a Card Cream ground and nothing else; wordmark.svg and monogram.svg have no ground and stay transparent. On Card Cream, every line of type is Serault Ink, as on serault_enclosure_card_press.pdf. Marks are not type: the foil monogram on the label is Warm Brass, the supplier's foil, and every other wordmark or monogram, in wordmark.svg, monogram.svg, on the label or on the stationery, is one flat colour, Serault Ink or Warm Brass. Soft Stone is for hairline rules and the piece-window border only, no more than 5% of the designed area, never a ground, panel, type or mark. Boutique Charcoal is the box board and is not used in these four deliverables.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. Foil is shown as a flat Warm Brass fill, as on serault_enclosure_card_press.pdf.
- Forbidden: In these four deliverables no colour other than Serault Ink #1B2A2E, Warm Brass #B08D4C, Card Cream #EFE9DC and Soft Stone #C9BEA8 is used for any ground, type, mark, rule or icon; Boutique Charcoal #2A2724 is kept for the box board. No gradient, bevel, glow or drop shadow on any mark or type, and no emboldened or hollow outline lettering: the brand is a small mark on a large quiet field, never loud.
- Photography: No photograph appears in any deliverable: serault_wordmark_export_2019.png, serault_wall_sign.jpg and serault_card_monogram.jpg are recovery sources only, and none is placed on the label or the stationery. Every mark is flat vector artwork in one palette colour.

## Typography
Display face: Marian 1800. Text face: Graphik.

- 01 Display / H1: Marian 1800 Regular, 96 pt / 104 pt, Regular (400), caps, tracking 0.30 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Marian 1800 Regular, 14 pt / 16 pt, Regular (400), caps, tracking 0.30 em.
- 03 Body: Graphik Regular, 9.5 pt / 14 pt, Regular (400), tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Graphik Regular, 6.5 pt / 9 pt, Regular (400), tracking +0.03 em.

Signature move: One unvarying 0.4 pt hairline at every size, from a 14 pt packaging label to a 96 pt window, caps tracked 0.30 em and holding exactly one third of the sheet width in otherwise empty field.

Rules:
- Never embolden or outline Marian 1800.
- Marian 1800 has no weight range at all, so hierarchy comes from size and empty field only.
- The hairline stays 0.4 pt at every size, from the 14 pt packaging label to the 96 pt boutique window.
- Marian caps are always tracked 0.30 em.
- The wordmark holds exactly one third of the sheet width, centred in an otherwise empty field.
- A small mark on a large quiet field, never loud and never mass market.

## Voice
Spare, assured, unhurried. A line the house would write: "Each piece is finished by hand and meant to outlive its first wearer."
