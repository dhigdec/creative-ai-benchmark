# SB3-081-MOT | Ember and Oak - Restaurant launch reel system

We are opening the second room at Ember and Oak and we need a launch package that makes people hungry before they read a word. Everything the shoot left us is short and silent, five second takes off the pass and around the room, so treat this as a short form set rather than a film with a story to tell. The food leads: each film opens on a shot of food, and the cut shows real char, visible texture and a room that feels alive. The message rides on type, not on a voice.

Please cut a 12 to 15 second launch reel in 16:9, built from at least five different takes, then an 8 to 10 second upright cutdown from at least four different takes and a 6 to 8 second square bumper from at least three, each standing on its own rather than reading as a crop of the wide one. The upright keeps all type and the mark clear of the top 250 and bottom 320 pixels. Every film stays silent by our own choice, so the words have to carry it on screen. Each film ends on its own closing card carrying the mark, the line “Second room now open” and the booking action “Book a table”, designed as a card. Please do not drop the mark over the last available frame and call that an ending. The square bumper calls out one dish from emberoak_menu_callouts.txt, with its price on the line beneath the name. In every film, any dish name, price or allergen line is taken word for word from emberoak_menu_callouts.txt.

One correction to the manifest: emberoak_pass_call.mov has no audio despite the name. Treat it as b roll like the rest.

The reel cover, the window poster and the three dish callout cards are being handled separately, so this delivery is the three films only.
