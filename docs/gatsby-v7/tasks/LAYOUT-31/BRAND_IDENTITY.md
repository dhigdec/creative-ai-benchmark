# Cellar & Cru

Cellar & Cru is a merchant, not a shop that happens to sell wine. The owner tastes and chooses every line, and the selection changes with the season. When there is one of us on the floor and eleven people in, the shelf card is how a wine sells itself, so the card carries the grower, the region, the vintage, our note, the score and the price.

## Audience
Confident domestic drinkers and restaurant buyers aged 40 to 65 who read a shelf card the way a reader reads a review, and who treat visual noise as a signal about the wine behind it.

## Positioning
Independent fine wine. Bottles from 14 pounds to 220. The card is restrained and led by grower, region and vintage: generous white space, the score present but small, the price plain in the run of the line. Never the supermarket aisle, never fashion brand minimalism that hides the vintage.

## Palette
- Cellar Ink: #23282B · mandatory. All type: the wine name, grower, region, vintage, style, buyer note and price, as talker_2025_PRINT.pdf sets them, and the wordmark. The key and vine device is this one solid ink, as it is printed in one ink on mark_card.jpg. Never a ground or panel.
- Claret: #6B2434 · optional accent. Only the short rule under the wine name, as on talker_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never type, the price, a score, a ground or a panel.
- Vine: #4A5A3C · optional accent. Optional. No supplied brand file gives it a role on the talker. If used, only as a small non-type accent, never type, the price, a score, a ground, a panel or any part of the key and vine device.
- Brass Key: #9A7B3F · mandatory. Only the hairline under the wordmark, 0.2 mm thick and exactly 32 mm long, as the signature move sets it. Never type, a panel or a ring, and never a metallic or foil effect.
- Chalk White: #F1ECE1 · mandatory. The card ground throughout, as on talker_2025_PRINT.pdf, and the only ground or panel colour. Never type on the card.
- Stone Grey: #9B958A · optional accent. Small labels, the score line, the picture keyline and the foot rule, as on talker_2025_PRINT.pdf. Never the wine name, the buyer note, the price, a ground or a panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Chalk White is the card ground and the only ground or panel colour, as on talker_2025_PRINT.pdf. On it, all type is Cellar Ink, the price included, as talker_2025_PRINT.pdf sets its wine name, record values, note and price; small labels and the score line may be Stone Grey. The key and vine device is Cellar Ink, printed in one ink as on mark_card.jpg. Brass Key is only the hairline under the wordmark. Claret is only the short rule under the wine name. Vine, if used, is only a small non-type accent. Stone Grey, Claret and Vine together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, the key and vine device is never a ghosted or transparent watermark, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a bottle photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, rules, keylines, the key and vine device or panels. No price or score is set in Claret, Vine or Brass Key or reversed out of a ring, badge or panel: the type rules never colour, embolden or enlarge a price or a score. No metallic, foil or gradient effect on any colour.
- Photography: Bottle photographs keep their own colour and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette: no bottle, label or wine is pushed toward Claret, Vine, Brass Key or Chalk White. Correcting a frame's white balance is allowed; restyling it is not.

## Typography
Display face: Eczar. Text face: Akzidenz-Grotesk Next.

- 01 Display / H1: Eczar, 17 pt / 20 pt, Regular 400, tracking -0.01 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Eczar, 7.5 pt / 10 pt, Medium 500, caps, tracking 0.30 em.
- 03 Body: Eczar, 8.5 pt / 11.5 pt, Regular 400, tracking 0.
- 04 Fine print: Akzidenz-Grotesk Next, 7 pt / 10 pt, Regular, letterspaced small caps, tracking 0.12 em.

Signature move: The wordmark is CELLAR & CRU in Eczar Medium 500 caps tracked 0.30 em, sitting above a Brass Key hairline that is always exactly 32 mm long and 0.2 mm thick. The lockup is fitted to that 32 mm measure at every size, so the point size follows the rule rather than the rule following the point size.

Rules:
- The Brass Key hairline is always exactly 32 mm long and 0.2 mm thick, and never scales with the type.
- The wordmark is always Eczar Medium 500 caps tracked 0.30 em, fitted to the 32 mm measure.
- On screen the wordmark sets at 22 px.
- The score is present but small, and the price stays plain in the run of the line: never colour, embolden or enlarge a price or a score.
- Region, vintage, score and price are the only items in Akzidenz-Grotesk Next: 7 pt letterspaced small caps for words, 9 pt tabular lining for figures.
- Never the supermarket aisle, and never fashion brand minimalism that hides the vintage: grower, region and vintage always lead the card.
- Generous white space is part of the card, not a leftover.

## Voice
Direct, measured and honest, the owner describing a wine as if standing beside you. It says what a wine is and is not, without hyperbole. Example: A co-op red that drinks well above its price, so long as you open it an hour before.
