# Lumora

Lumora is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Los Angeles, California, and this commission covers phase 1: pre-production lip-oil colour concepts.

## Audience
18 to 32 year olds discovering us on social and buying shade first on a phone, cross shopping a 6 dollar drugstore oil and a 38 dollar prestige one.

## Positioning
A 24 dollar lip oil. We want to look more expensive than we are while staying honest, so the treatment is bright, clean and daylight neutral, never moody prestige and never blown out drugstore.

## Palette
- Amber Hour: #C8792E · optional accent; mandatory only as the Amber Hour card's swatch dot. The swatch dot of the Amber Hour card, and small accents only. Never a ground, a panel, a rule or type.
- Warm Ink: #3A2A22 · mandatory. All type on Soft Cream or Clear Glass: the shade name, finish line, body, ingredient line, tagline and the concept-colour notice, as the template sets its name, finish, ingredient line and tagline. Never a ground or large panel.
- Soft Cream: #F7F0E6 · mandatory. The ground of every retailer shade card, as on lumora_shadecard_template.pdf, and of the product-page module, which lumora_placements.txt sets on Soft Cream; the main light ground of every other design. Also type reversed out of a dark photograph.
- Brushed Gold: #C6A268 · mandatory on the retailer shade cards; optional accent elsewhere. The hairline rule, the price and the small LUMORA brand line on the shade card, as on lumora_shadecard_template.pdf; elsewhere only rules, prices and small labels. Never a ground, a panel or body text.
- Rosewood: #9E5B57 · optional accent; mandatory only as the Rosewood card's swatch dot. The swatch dot of the Rosewood card, and small accents only. Never a ground, a panel, a rule or type.
- Clear Glass: #EDE7DE · optional. A secondary light panel or tile, for example behind a bottle, carrying Warm Ink type. Never type, and never the ground of the shade card.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Designed grounds and panels are Soft Cream #F7F0E6, the only ground on the shade card and the product page, or Clear Glass #EDE7DE as a secondary light panel; Warm Ink is never a ground, keeping the look bright and daylight neutral. Type is Warm Ink #3A2A22, as on lumora_shadecard_template.pdf; the price, the hairline rule and the small LUMORA brand line are Brushed Gold #C6A268, as on the template; type set directly on a dark part of a photograph may be Soft Cream. Amber Hour #C8792E and Rosewood #9E5B57 appear only as their own shades' swatch dots or as small accents. Brushed Gold, Amber Hour and Rosewood together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never a ground, a panel or body text. Each shade's own concept colour may fill that shade's swatch dot or chip; it is not a palette colour and is used nowhere else in the design.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type or a panel sits on or beside the photograph as a solid colour. The one exception is the shade card's swatch dot, which may keep the shaded fill and the faint Warm Ink edge ring at 14% opacity that it carries on lumora_shadecard_template.pdf, in that shade's own colour.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes (#C8792E, #3A2A22, #F7F0E6, #C6A268, #9E5B57 and #EDE7DE) for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons; the only other colour in the design is each shade's own concept colour in its swatch dot or chip. Brushed Gold #C6A268 is a flat solid colour, with no gradient, shadow or blend, per the opacity rule. No dark, moody ground.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own bright, daylight-neutral colour and are not recoloured toward the palette. The Amber Hour oil stays as photographed and is not pushed toward the Amber Hour swatch #C8792E. lumora_carton_flat.jpg is never a colour reference: nothing is matched to its fluorescent cast or gloss bloom.

## Typography
Display face: Abril Fatface. Text face: Inter, with Söhne Mono for all figures.

- 01 Display / H1 · shade name: Abril Fatface Regular, 96 pt / 101 pt, Regular, tracking -0.02 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · finish line: Abril Fatface Regular, 18 pt / 20 pt, Regular, tracking -0.01 em.
- 03 Body: Inter Regular, 7.5 pt / 10 pt, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · price, shade code, concept notice: Söhne Mono Regular, 6 pt / 9 pt, Regular, tracking 0.03 em.

Signature move: The shade name is always one oversized lowercase word in Abril Fatface, run at roughly five times the body size so the fat strokes nearly touch the card edge, while every numeral drops out of the display face into Söhne Mono.

Rules:
- Never set a shade name in capitals.
- Prices and shade codes stay in Söhne Mono Regular, so no numeral ever shares the display face.
- The shade name is one lowercase word at roughly five times the body size, sized so the fat strokes nearly touch the card edge.
- Body stays Inter Regular at 7.5 pt on 10 pt in print and 18 px on 28 px on screen; never promote it to the display face.
- The concept colour notice sets at fine print size and must still read without clipped characters at the delivered 55 by 85 mm card size.

## Voice
Warm, direct, a bit fun, never overclaiming. We describe the finish and let the shade speak. Example line the brand would write: 'A sheer wash of colour, a proper shine, no stickiness.'

## Supplied Identity
The wordmark and shade lockups exist as working digital files. There is no printed guideline; the carton photograph we have was shot under shop light and should not be trusted as a colour reference.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
