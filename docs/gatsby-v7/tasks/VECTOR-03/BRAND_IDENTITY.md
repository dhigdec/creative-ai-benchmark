# Apex Rally Club

The Apex Rally Club is a volunteer amateur motorsport club in Vermont. Six events a year, and a merch table that pays for the timing gear. The club crest goes back to 1987 and each event badge, the hillclimb, the night stage, the ice trial, the gravel loop and the finale, was drawn over the years by members. This autumn is our first proper collectible run with a mint.

## Audience
Club members aged roughly twenty to forty five who already own the previous years and buy on continuity with the club's history, plus impulse buyers at the event tent

## Positioning
A two tier line: twelve dollar impulse pins across the tent table and ninety five dollar numbered coins a member inspects in the hand. The register is club rather than corporate: confident and worn in, never novelty and never distressed for effect.

## Palette
- Rally Green: #2F5D3A · mandatory: on the livery, and on every face whose reference sheet prints it (all but Gravel Loop). A ground of livery pages (Snow White is the other) and the main colour of the livery on the vehicle, as the ground of apex_backing_card_2025.pdf; the green ink of the Club Crest, Hillclimb, Night Stage, Ice Trial and Finale faces.
- Ash Black: #212322 · mandatory on every face whose reference sheet prints it (Hillclimb, Ice Trial and Gravel Loop); optional elsewhere. The black ink inside those faces, and headings, body, values or line work on a Snow White livery ground. Never a page ground or a layout panel.
- Vintage Ochre: #C98A2E · mandatory on every face whose reference sheet prints it (Club Crest, Night Stage, Gravel Loop and Finale); optional accent elsewhere. The ochre ink inside those faces. On livery pages, rules and small label capitals only: on Rally Green as the field labels, rule and mount caption on apex_backing_card_2025.pdf, and on Snow White as the ochre ring lettering on apex_reference_sheet_nightstage.jpg. Never a page ground, a layout panel or body text.
- Struck Gold: #B7912F · optional, and only on the polished gold pins (Club Crest, Ice Trial and Finale) and the livery. Stands only for gold metal: the metal line of a polished gold pin face (Club Crest, Ice Trial and Finale), including any raised-metal lettering, and metal trim on the livery. Never on a black nickel pin face (Hillclimb, Night Stage and Gravel Loop), and never an enamel fill, a ground, a layout panel or body text.
- Snow White: #F1EFE8 · mandatory where livery type sits on a Rally Green ground; optional inside a face. Headings, body and values reversed out of a Rally Green livery ground, as on apex_backing_card_2025.pdf; a livery page ground or layout panel, as the pin-mount panel on that card; and white areas inside a face where its reference sheet shows white inside the design.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Faces have no ground: the background stays transparent, and each face keeps the two inks its own reference sheet prints, rebuilt at the palette hexes (Club Crest, Night Stage and Finale: Rally Green and Vintage Ochre; Hillclimb and Ice Trial: Rally Green and Ash Black; Gravel Loop: Vintage Ochre and Ash Black), with Snow White for white areas inside a design. Struck Gold is used only for the metal line, and any raised-metal lettering, of the polished gold pins (Club Crest, Ice Trial and Finale); the black nickel pins (Hillclimb, Night Stage and Gravel Loop) carry no Struck Gold. Tones the sheets print outside the palette, the light grey ice on apex_reference_sheet_icetrial.jpg and the olive where the beams cross the trees on apex_reference_sheet_nightstage.jpg, are rebuilt as Snow White and as Vintage Ochre or Rally Green respectively, never as a sixth colour. On the livery, page grounds and layout panels are Rally Green or Snow White. On a Rally Green livery ground, headings, body and values are Snow White, and only label capitals and rules may be Vintage Ochre, as on apex_backing_card_2025.pdf. On Snow White, headings, body and values are Rally Green or Ash Black; Vintage Ochre may carry only label capitals and rules there, as the ochre ring lettering on apex_reference_sheet_nightstage.jpg. Outside the faces, Vintage Ochre and Struck Gold together cover no more than 10% of any livery page's designed area and are never a ground, a layout panel or body text.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, in every face and on every livery page. No tint, transparency, gradient, SVG pattern fill, shadow, filter or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over an image. The footer strip of apex_backing_card_2025.pdf, drawn below full opacity, is not carried forward.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes (#2F5D3A, #212322, #C98A2E, #B7912F and #F1EFE8) for any fill, stroke, type, panel, rule or icon; no colour sampled from a reference photograph; no gradient, bevel, emboss, foil or chrome effect to imitate metal; and no distress, grunge or wear texture added for effect.
- Photography: The reference sheets and the jacket photograph are sources only and are never placed in a deliverable: each ink is rebuilt as the palette hex it was printed in, never sampled from the photograph's light, cast, exposure, paper tone or thread.

## Typography
Display face: GT Walsheim Bold 700. Text face: Gotham Book 400 (Gotham Medium 500 for label capitals).

- 01 Display / H1 · the year: GT Walsheim Bold, 22 pt / 24 pt, Bold 700, tracking minus 0.010 em.
- 02 Subheading / H2 · ring lettering and card titles: GT Walsheim Bold, capitals only, 16 pt / 18 pt, Bold 700, tracking plus 0.140 em, filled lozenge between words.
- 03 Body: Gotham Book, 9 pt / 12.5 pt, Book 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print · label capitals: Gotham Medium, 7 pt / 10 pt, Medium 500, tracking plus 0.060 em.

Signature move: Every face carries 1987 alone at six o'clock in GT Walsheim Bold at twice the cap height of the ring lettering above it, so the year is read before the club name. The ring runs in capitals at 0.140 em with a filled lozenge between words.

Rules:
- Never set the crest ring in Gotham. The ring is GT Walsheim Bold 700 only.
- 1987 always sits alone at six o'clock, with no other words on its line.
- The year is always exactly twice the cap height of the ring lettering above it.
- Ring capitals always run at 0.140 em with a filled lozenge between words.
- Label capitals are Gotham Medium 500; Gotham Book 400 carries spec sheets and cards.
- The coin's raised metal version must be line only.
- The register stays club rather than corporate: worn in, never novelty and never distressed for effect.
- All six faces read as one collection, never as six separate files.

## Voice
Warm, understated and proud of the years behind us; we talk about continuity, not hype. Example line the brand would write: "Same crest we ran in 1987, struck to hold up to the light."

## Supplied Identity
None of the art exists in a form a mint will accept. What we have is six reference sheets, the crest and five event badges, printed at different times on different paper and photographed on a workbench under whatever light was in the garage. A member also sent a photo of the crest as it is embroidered on the club jackets. The mint requirements file gives diameters, the hard enamel colour ceiling, plating options and the rule that the coin's raised metal version must be line only.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
