# SB3-033-VEC | Apex Rally Club - Motorsport face and livery system

Six club badges rebuilt for pin and coin supplier review

Apex Rally Club needs six badge faces recovered from photographs for a pin and coin project. The references contain the colours and details we want to retain, but they are not reliable production drawings.

Deliverables
1. Motorsport face artwork: 6 named exports. These are the six pin faces: the coin's line-only raised-metal face and the crest colourways on struck metal are not part of this delivery. The face design matches the named design record, drawn as that design's hard enamel pin face with its pin diameter from apex_designs.csv stated in the file. Each face remains recognisable at that design's pin diameter in apex_designs.csv.
2. Applied livery presentation: 1 named export. The graphics follow the vehicle panel boundaries. No vehicle photograph is supplied, so apply the livery to your own plain line drawing of a generic rally car with no manufacturer's badge or name, declared as a concept drawing in the delivery manifest, with the whole vehicle shown on page 1. The livery hierarchy is readable at viewing distance, led by the delivered Club Crest face, larger than any other badge or lettering on the vehicle.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
