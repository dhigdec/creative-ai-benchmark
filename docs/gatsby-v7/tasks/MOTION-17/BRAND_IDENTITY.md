# Pulsevault

Pulsevault is an independent monthly club night in a Cleveland warehouse, run by a small crew and promoted almost entirely on social in the days after each event. Door is 20 to 30 dollars, the room is late teens to twenties, and the night lives or dies on what goes up before the next tickets drop. Its audience follows the night, not the venue, and can smell something made by an agency from across the room.

## Audience
Late teens to twenties, following the night rather than the venue. They read agency polish as proof the night has been bought and is no longer theirs.

## Positioning
A 20 to 30 dollar door at an independent monthly night. Positioned deliberately rough: loud and immediate, kept clear of both a 100 dollar festival brand and a homemade flyer nobody made.

## Palette
- Blackout: #0C0C0F · mandatory. The only ground: the event recap cover, the next-event announcement and any title or closing card in the films sit on Blackout, as pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf does, and it is the only colour a photograph or footage may be screened with behind type. Never a type colour.
- Stage Magenta: #E0247C · mandatory in the Didot event line, where it floods the counters. The counters of the event line, and small chips with light type reversed out, as the billing pill on pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf. Never a line of type, a ground or a panel larger than a chip.
- Laser Cyan: #17C6D6 · optional accent. Numerals, secondary lines and thin rules, as the slot number on pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf. Never a ground or panel.
- Flood Amber: #E8933A · optional accent. Thin rules and dividers only; no supplied brand file sets it as type. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Concrete: #4A4A50 · optional secondary type colour. Set times, secondary lines and fine print on Blackout, as the set-time line on pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf. Never a ground or panel.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Blackout is the only ground: the cover, the next-event announcement and every title or closing card in the films sit on Blackout, as pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf does, and a photograph or footage may sit on it, screened darker with Blackout behind type. On Blackout, the event line and act names are the warm off-white #F2ECE0 of the card's masthead and act name; the date, ticket, doors, door and age lines are warm off-white, Laser Cyan or Concrete; slot numbers may be Laser Cyan and set times Concrete, as on the card. Stage Magenta fills the event line's counters and small chips carrying light type (#F7EEF4 on the card's billing pill). Laser Cyan, Flood Amber and Concrete together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never a ground or panel; Flood Amber is never type.
- Opacity: Every palette colour, and the card's warm off-white, is used solid at 100% opacity on type, chips, panels and rules, with no tint, transparency, gradient, glow or blend mode. The one overlay allowed is the one pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf uses, which screens its full-bleed floor still dark behind the type: a photograph or footage may be screened darker with Blackout #0C0C0F alone, which darkens it without changing its hues. No overlay, wash or gradient of any other colour, white included, is laid over a photograph or footage. A card or line may cut or fade in or out as a whole; colour and opacity are judged on a settled frame.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, chips, panels, rules or icons, except the warm off-white of pulsevault_lineup_card_press.pdf (#F2ECE0, and #F7EEF4 in its billing pill) and the painted wordmark's own paint as recovered from pulsevault_wall_mark.jpg. No light or white ground. No glow, gloss, bevel, chrome, metallic or 3D effect on any type, chip, rule or the wordmark, since nothing may read as agency polish.
- Photography: Footage and stills keep their own recorded light and colour and are never tinted or recoloured toward the palette. A picture may be screened darker with Blackout behind type; nothing else is laid over it, and no crowd, room or light is generated, extended or replaced.

## Typography
Display face: Linotype Didot Headline. Text face: Aktiv Grotesk.

- 01 Display / H1: Linotype Didot Headline, 150 px / 156 px, Bold, tracking -25 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Aktiv Grotesk, 96 px / 102 px, Medium, tracking -10 units.
- 03 Body: Aktiv Grotesk, 54 px / 76 px, Regular, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Aktiv Grotesk, 30 px / 38 px, Regular, tracking +40 units.

Signature move: The event line is Linotype Didot Headline Bold at 150 px on 156 px, blown up until the hairlines almost vanish and running off both edges so the trim crops the letters rather than the type fitting the frame, Stage Magenta flooding the counters.

Rules:
- Never re-typeset the painted wall mark. It is artwork recovered from the floodlit photograph, not type.
- Didot sets the event line and nothing else.
- The event line must run off both edges so the trim crops the letters. Never shrink it to fit the frame.
- Stage Magenta floods the counters of the event line.
- Aktiv Grotesk Medium names the acts, Regular sets times and the ticket line.
- The lineup card layout is fixed and reprints unchanged. Only the running order and the per act details change each month.
- Nothing may read as agency polish. Rough on purpose, made by the crew that runs the night.

## Voice
Direct and hyped but never fake, written the way the crew actually talks. Example line: 'Saturday. Warehouse. Tickets Tuesday. Do not sleep on it.'

## Supplied Identity
Pulsevault has never had a logo file. The mark exists only hand painted on the brick beside the entrance and survives as one photograph taken at the end of the night under a single floodlight. The wordmark has to be recovered from that photograph as artwork the night can reuse, and it has to hold on a cover that is close to black. There is no colour spec and no prior guideline. The one thing the night reprints unchanged is a plain lineup card its print shop runs a small batch of each month, supplied as last month's flat press artwork: the running order and the per act details change every date, the layout does not.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
