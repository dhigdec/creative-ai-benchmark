# Cedar Commons Farmers Market

Cedar Commons is a neighbourhood farmers market that runs on weekends in a Burlington park, free to walk into and funded entirely by the vendors who pay a daily stall fee. It has outgrown its chalkboards and its bedsheet banner. The signage exists to get families to the right lot, past the entrance and to the stall they are looking for, printed cheaply and well at a local sign shop.

## Audience
Neighbourhood families and weekend walkers who arrive on foot or park in the wrong lot, plus the vendors and volunteers who run the morning. Read from a moving car and from across a field.

## Positioning
A free public market funded by 25 to 60 dollar daily stall fees, often part-donated. Everything prints on coroplast and vinyl at a local sign shop. Flat inks and heavy type, never a refined tint palette that would be unreadable outdoors and would make a free market look like it is charging.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Sign White #FBFAF5 is the ground of every stall sign, as on cedar_commons_plate_card.pdf, and the ground behind the stacked wordmark wherever it is set. Meadow Green #3F7D44 or Cedar Ink #23301F may be a solid panel carrying Sign White type; Barn Red #B23A2E and Harvest Gold #E0A526 are never a ground or panel. The stacked CEDAR COMMONS wordmark is Meadow Green on a Sign White ground, on its 10 mm Barn Red rule. Other type on Sign White is Cedar Ink or Meadow Green. Every stall sign keeps the colours of cedar_commons_plate_card.pdf: Sign White ground, Cedar Ink stall number, Meadow Green rule and trader name. The recovered mark is one flat ink, Cedar Ink or Meadow Green, in market-mark.svg and wherever it is placed, as the cedar_commons_apron.jpg print is a single dark green ink. Beyond the wordmark rule, Barn Red and Harvest Gold are small accents, such as arrows, together no more than 5% of the designed area.
- Opacity: Flat inks throughout, no tints. Every palette colour prints solid, at 100%, with no tint, screen, halftone, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode, because it has to print cheaply on coroplast and vinyl. No scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No refined tint palette and no tint or screen of any ink; no metallic, foil or gloss effect, Harvest Gold included, which prints as a flat ink; no Harvest Gold type on Sign White. No colour outside the five palette hexes, #3F7D44, #B23A2E, #E0A526, #FBFAF5 and #23301F, for type, grounds, panels, rules, arrows, icons or the recovered mark.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. No colour sampled from a photograph, such as the canvas of cedar_commons_apron.jpg, is used for type, grounds, panels, rules or the recovered mark. cedar_commons_site_info_tent.jpg, shot in warmer light than the other site frames, is brought to the same morning look as cedar_commons_site_entrance.jpg and cedar_commons_site_vendor_row.jpg, not warmed or tinted toward Harvest Gold.

## Typography
Display face: Bodoni Egyptian Bold. Text face: Interstate.

- 01 Display / H1: Bodoni Egyptian Bold, 180 pt / 190 pt, Bold (700), tracking set per line, roughly +0.02 to +0.12 em, until CEDAR and COMMONS measure the same width.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Interstate Bold, 60 pt / 66 pt, Bold (700), tracking +0.01 em.
- 03 Body: Interstate Regular, 18 pt / 24 pt, Regular (400), tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Interstate Regular, 12 pt / 16 pt, Regular (400), tracking +0.03 em.

Signature move: The wordmark is always stacked, CEDAR above COMMONS, each line tracked out until both measure exactly the same width, the block sitting on a 10 mm Barn Red rule, so it reads as a green double square underlined in red before it reads as words.

Rules:
- Never set the name on one line. CEDAR always sits above COMMONS.
- Track each line of the wordmark individually until CEDAR and COMMONS measure exactly the same width.
- The stacked block always sits on a 10 mm Barn Red rule.
- Bodoni Egyptian Bold carries the name only. Wayfinding and listings are Interstate.
- Flat inks only, never a refined tint palette, because it has to print cheaply on coroplast and vinyl and stay readable outdoors in the rain.
- Signage is read from a moving car and from across a field, so nothing sets smaller than the 12 pt fine print step.

## Voice
Warm, plain and welcoming, like a volunteer at the gate. Example line: "Cheerful, legible from a distance, few colours, and it lives outdoors in the rain. Not precious. Tell us what our mark can and cannot do."

## Supplied Identity
The market mark was drawn nine years ago by a volunteer's cousin, who is not reachable, and screen-printed onto aprons and tote bags. No file of any kind was ever kept. The best surviving copy is a photograph of one apron folded on a picnic table. There is no vector art, so the mark has to be recovered from that photograph rather than redrawn.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
