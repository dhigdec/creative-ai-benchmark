# Cedarline and Vale

Cedarline and Vale is a six-agent brokerage in Marin that takes on architect-designed and materially serious homes. We present listings the way the architecture asks to be seen: quietly, accurately, and next to whatever three other agents are showing. Our credibility rests on buyers and vendors trusting that the pictures are the house.

## Audience
Buyers aged 40 to 60 with household incomes above 400 thousand who read light and material as proxies for build quality, and vendors of design-led homes. They view our listings alongside three competing agents and notice anything that looks retouched.

## Positioning
High-value residential, flagship listings from the low millions up. Positioned above volume agencies and apart from developer marketing, on restraint and accuracy rather than gloss.

## Palette

- Marin Slate: #3C444B · mandatory where body text appears. Body text on Plaster White, as the body on windowcard_2025_print.pdf; may also carry the spec table, the price and fine print. Never a ground or panel.
- Deep Charcoal: #1E2124 · mandatory. The stacked address, on Plaster White, as the address on windowcard_2025_print.pdf; may also carry headings, the price and the enquiry line. Never a ground or panel.
- Bay Grey: #7E8A8C · optional accent. Hairline rules and dividers only, as on windowcard_2025_print.pdf. Never a ground, panel or type colour.
- Quiet Sage: #9AA391 · optional accent. A small accent only, such as a key line or a small mark. Never a ground, panel or type colour.
- Warm Stone: #C7B9A4 · optional accent. A small accent only, such as a key line or a small mark. Never a ground, panel or type colour, so nothing reads warm and cosy.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Plaster White is the only ground behind type, on every page, panel and margin; type never sits directly on a photograph. Type is Deep Charcoal or Marin Slate on Plaster White and nothing else: the address in Deep Charcoal and body text in Marin Slate, as on windowcard_2025_print.pdf; headings, prices, the enquiry line and fine print in either. Bay Grey is for hairline rules and dividers only; Quiet Sage and Warm Stone are small accents only. Bay Grey, Quiet Sage and Warm Stone together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area and are never a ground, a panel or a type colour.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, no type carries a drop shadow or glow, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: type sits on a solid Plaster White block beside or over the photograph.
- Forbidden: No gold, brass, bronze or other metallic colour or effect on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, and no black-and-gold treatment: the office rules out developer gold-and-black. No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected, neutral colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. Cool does not mean blue: plaster whites stay neutral, blacks keep their detail, grass keeps its own green, and every sky and window view is the one captured in its frame, never dropped in. The colour prohibitions govern the design, not the photographs: the teak cladding and door of room_12.jpg and the steel range and tap of room_02.jpg stay as shot.

## Typography
Display face: Tobias Light. Text face: Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman.

- 01 Display / H1: Tobias Light, 28 pt / 30 pt, Light, tracking -8 units.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Tobias Light, 13 pt / 17 pt, Light, tracking -2 units.
- 03 Body: Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman, 9.5 pt / 14 pt, Roman 55, tracking 0 units.
- 04 Fine print: Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman, 8 pt / 11 pt, Roman 55, tracking +8 units.

Signature move: Every address is stacked in Tobias Light, the street number alone above the street name at the same size, flush left, unpunctuated and never italic.

Rules:
- Never set a price in Tobias.
- Prices and spec tables stay in Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55 Roman with tabular lining figures.
- The address is always stacked, the number alone above the street name, both at the same size, flush left.
- No punctuation in the address.
- The address is never set in italic.
- No superlatives, no cosiness and nothing that sounds sold.

## Voice
Cool, restrained and true. We describe what is there and let the room speak. No superlatives, no cosiness, nothing that sounds sold. Example line: 'Glass on three sides, the bay through the living room, the oaks off the kitchen.'
