# Verranza Coastal Retreats

Verranza Coastal Retreats is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Liguria, Italy, and this commission covers phase 2: booking collateral and in-villa season cards.

## Audience
Guests aged 40 to 65 booking a whole villa for a birthday, an anniversary or a multi-generation gathering, plus the travel agencies and private clients who book on their behalf. They have stayed in the real houses and will recognise anything that is not true.

## Positioning
Villas run 1,800 to 4,200 dollars a night depending on season, positioned against boutique hotels rather than other rentals. The grammar is generous space and quiet architecture, with rates carried in a discreet table rather than announced, so the collection never reads as an aggregator listing.

## Palette
- Verranza Stone: #D8C9B4 · optional accent. One panel behind the season note, carrying Warm Charcoal type, as on verranza_season_card.pdf. Never the card ground, a type colour, a rule or the bar.
- Deep Olive: #4E5A43 · optional accent. Hairline rules only, such as the hairline along the top edge of the note panel on verranza_season_card.pdf. Never a ground, panel, type colour or the bar.
- Coast Blue: #6E8CA0 · mandatory. The one bar, 24 mm long and 3 pt deep, centred directly beneath the villa name on every card, and nothing else: never type, a panel, or any other rule or bar.
- Warm Charcoal: #33302B · mandatory. All type: villa name, season line, nightly rate, minimum stay and season note, on Linen or on the Verranza Stone panel, as on verranza_season_card.pdf. Never a ground or panel.
- Linen: #F3EEE4 · mandatory. The card ground and the only ground colour, as on verranza_season_card.pdf.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Linen is the card ground and the only ground colour. Verranza Stone may be used for one panel behind the season note and for nothing else. All type is Warm Charcoal, set on Linen or on the Verranza Stone panel, as on verranza_season_card.pdf. Coast Blue is only the one 24 mm by 3 pt bar centred under the villa name. Deep Olive is for hairline rules only. Deep Olive and Coast Blue together stay under 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No Coast Blue anywhere but the bar under the villa name, and no type in Verranza Stone, Deep Olive, Coast Blue or Linen.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never tinted, washed or duotoned toward the palette, and no pool water in any photograph is recoloured to Coast Blue or any other palette colour.

## Typography
Display face: Aleo Light. Text face: Freight Sans Pro Book.

- 01 Display / H1: Aleo Light, 28 pt / 32 pt, Light 300, tracking +120 units, all caps.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Aleo Light, 16 pt / 19 pt, Light 300, tracking +40 units.
- 03 Body: Freight Sans Pro Book, 9.5 pt / 14 pt, Book 400, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Freight Sans Pro Book, 7.5 pt / 11 pt, Book 400, tracking +10 units.

Signature move: A Coast Blue bar exactly 24 mm long and 3 pt deep sits centred under the villa name and under nothing else, the one fixed measure on an otherwise airy page.

Rules:
- Never set a rate larger than the villa name.
- The 24 mm by 3 pt Coast Blue bar is always centred, always under the villa name, and never under anything else.
- Villa names are always caps tracked plus 120 units.
- Rates are carried in a discreet table rather than announced, so the page never reads as an aggregator listing.
- Rate conditions stay 7.5 pt in Warm Charcoal.
- Copy describes only what the house actually is, and never oversells it.

## Voice
Calm, warm, understated. Describes what the house actually is at that hour, never oversells. Example line: "Warm stone, a long terrace, and the pool in real afternoon light."

## Supplied Identity
The collection has a printed in-villa season card whose artwork is held by its printer as a press PDF, and a written owner note recording what the previous editor got wrong. There is no lost wordmark here; the constraint is photographic consistency across three villas shot on two trips by two people, with the second batch already worked on by an editor no longer used. The collection's one graphic mark is a small hand inked cove motif an artist drew for the founders, which survives only as a flatbed scan of the original inking and as the mark etched on Maison Aubine's door glass; it has never been redrawn or digitised, and the foil printer and the glass etcher need it as vector artwork. The contractual welcome provision left in every house exists only as a short still life set the manager shot on a villa's limestone counter.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
