# Cedarline Institute

Cedarline teaches working adults across two very different registers: an accessible community evening course and a high cost executive programme bought by employers. Each autumn cohort finishes with a ceremony where every candidate leaves with a certificate in hand and a file in their inbox the same evening. The Institute trades on being trusted rather than flashy, and on the two credentials reading honestly for what they cost.

## Audience
Community students in their twenties and thirties whose certificate is read by family at home, and executive candidates whose employer funded credential ends up framed in an office where their own clients see it.

## Positioning
Two credentials an order of magnitude apart: a 180 dollar community certificate and a 9,000 dollar employer funded executive credential. Neither can come off the press looking like the other. Cohort run posts around 600 to 1,500 dollars fixed.

## Palette
- Cedar Green: #33503C · mandatory. The institute line CEDARLINE INSTITUTE, as both press files set it; and a seal reproduced as flat artwork, alone or with Engraved Gold. Never a ground, panel or body text.
- Warm Certificate Cream: #F3EBD8 · mandatory. The ground of every credential, solid from edge to edge, as on adulted_2024_press.pdf and exec_diploma_2019_press.pdf. The only ground; never type.
- Deep Ink: #1E2620 · mandatory. The candidate name, credential title, award statement, any honours line, signatory names and signature lines, all on Warm Certificate Cream, as both press files set them. Never a ground or panel.
- Engraved Gold: #9A7B33 · mandatory. The 0.4 pt hairline beneath the institute line on every credential, as the type system requires; border rules, such as the double rule on exec_diploma_2019_press.pdf; and a seal reproduced as flat artwork. Never type, a ground or a panel.
- Slate Grey: #5A6066 · optional accent. Fine print only, such as the credential code or programme code, as the credential number is set on adulted_2024_press.pdf. Never the candidate name, the title, a rule or a ground.

### Palette rules
- Distribution: Warm Certificate Cream is the only ground, solid from edge to edge, as on both press files; no other colour is a ground, panel or block. On it, every line of type is Deep Ink, except the institute line CEDARLINE INSTITUTE in Cedar Green and fine print such as the credential code, which may be Slate Grey as on adulted_2024_press.pdf. Engraved Gold is for rules and borders: the 0.4 pt hairline under the institute line on every credential, and any border rule. A seal reproduced as flat artwork is solid Cedar Green, Engraved Gold or both. Cedar Green, Engraved Gold and Slate Grey together cover no more than 5% of the sheet.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no bevel, emboss effect or drop shadow is added to the seal. The one exception is the seal photograph: placed as adulted_2024_press.pdf places it, it may keep that file's Multiply blend so its cream stock sits into the cream ground.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for any type, rule, border, ornament or flat seal. Engraved Gold is a flat ink colour: no gradient, foil or metallic effect imitates gold, and no type is set in it.
- Photography: Neither press file carries a photograph other than the seal. Where the seal is placed as a photograph, as on adulted_2024_press.pdf, it keeps its own colourless cream relief and is never tinted or recoloured toward Cedar Green, Engraved Gold or any other palette colour. Any other photograph keeps its own corrected colour and is never recoloured toward the palette.

## Typography
Display face: Terza. Text face: Freight Text Pro.

- 01 Display / H1: Terza Light, 42 pt / 46 pt, Light, tracking 0.
- 02 Subheading / H2: Terza Medium small caps, 13 pt / 15 pt, Medium, tracking 0.22 em.
- 03 Body: Freight Text Pro Book, 11 pt / 16 pt, Book, tracking 0, max 65 characters.
- 04 Fine print: Freight Text Pro Book, 8.5 pt / 12 pt, Book, tracking 0.02 em.

Signature move: The institute line: CEDARLINE INSTITUTE set in Terza Medium small caps tracked 0.22 em at step 02, riding a 0.4 pt Engraved Gold hairline that runs the full measure, while the candidate name at step 01 stays the only element on the sheet above 24 pt.

Rules:
- Never letterspace a candidate name.
- The candidate name is the only element on the sheet above 24 pt.
- CEDARLINE INSTITUTE is always Terza Medium small caps tracked 0.22 em on a 0.4 pt Engraved Gold hairline running the full measure.
- The credential code is always set in oldstyle figures.
- Candidate names take Terza; the award statement and credential code take Freight Text Pro Book.
- The 180 dollar community certificate and the 9,000 dollar executive credential must never come off the press looking like each other.
- Every candidate name spelled and accented correctly, right up to the ceremony morning.

## Voice
Measured and earned, never boastful. We state what was completed and who signed it. Example line the brand would write: 'Completed the autumn programme in professional practice. Signed at Cedarline Institute, Vermont.'

## Supplied Identity
The seal die went to the printer that closed and cannot be recovered. The only surviving seal is the one blind embossed into an old certificate, plus a raking light photograph of that impression. Two dead press files remain, last year's community certificate and the 2019 executive diploma; there is no digital seal artwork.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
