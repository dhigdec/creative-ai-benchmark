Meridian Motors - Showroom vehicle image system

Client brief

An honest, consistent showroom look for Meridian Motors Our stock is photographed in three different bays. The changing light makes one dealership look like three, and the paint colours are not consistent from photograph to photograph. We want a clean, evenly lit set that still shows the cars a buyer will see in person. Please make six 1080 x 1350 px vehicle sales cards, one for each stock record in meridian_intake_week.csv. Use a photograph of the correct vehicle and include its stock number, year, make, model, trim, mileage, asking price and certification tier. Include a readable enquiry line asking buyers to enquire about that stock number at Meridian Motors in Portland, Maine. Keep visible condition details honest. We also need one 1920 x 1080 px forecourt campaign image. Show all six vehicles from the intake register and no others, using one of each vehicle's supplied photographs and a year, make and model caption. Make the dealership name clear and include the compass roundel. These are branded sales materials, not marketplace first-image photographs. The no-text, no-logo and 4:3 primary-image requirements in meridian_marketplace_rules.txt apply to marketplace primary images, which are not part of this delivery. Use the attached identity and the supplied roundel artwork. Deliver the seven files named below.

Brand identity

Meridian Motors

Meridian Motors sells certified pre-owned vehicles between 22,000 and 38,000 dollars to families replacing an ageing car. The listings are the whole business, and the visual argument is simple: this is an orderly operation with nothing to hide. Every car should look as if it were photographed in one clean, evenly lit showroom, with honest paint and legible plain information.

Audience

Buyers aged 30 to 55 replacing a ten year old family car, deciding on trust before they decide on the vehicle.

Positioning

22,000 to 38,000 dollars certified pre-owned. The treatment is an even, neutral, well lit showroom white with the full vehicle in frame and honest colour. Never a dark dramatic studio look, which reads as evasive at this price, and never hot saturated pictures with starbursts and a price slash, which read as a buy here pay here lot.

Colour rules

Distribution
Showroom White #F6F7F8 is the ground behind all type on every card and on the campaign. Meridian Navy #1F2A3A is the only other ground, for a band, panel or seal carrying reversed type, and never sits behind a vehicle. Prices, mileage and listing detail always sit on Showroom White, never on a Meridian Navy band. On Showroom White, prices, headings, labels and the dealership name are Meridian Navy, mileage and listing detail are Graphite, and fine print is Graphite or, as the one small muted line, Steel Grey. On Meridian Navy, type is Showroom White, except small seal lettering, which may be Steel Grey as on the certification seal of meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf. Compass Teal and Steel Grey are for rules, keylines, seal detail (that seal's Compass Teal compass needles and Steel Grey lettering) and one small muted line only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a band, panel or seal sits beside or over the photograph as a solid block.
Not allowed
No starburst, no price slash and no hot saturated sale colour on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, which read as a buy here pay here lot; no dark dramatic studio ground behind a vehicle; and no colour outside the 5 palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; the supplied roundel artwork (meridian_roundel_transparent.svg, meridian_roundel_reversed.svg, meridian_roundel_transparent.png and meridian_roundel_reversed.png) keeps its own colours.
Photography
Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The three bays' casts (warm apron, yellow-orange wash bay, dim cool-green back row) are corrected to one even, neutral showroom white, but each car keeps its true paint: the Ardmoor Ridge stays deep red and does not swing orange, the black Brookmere Sable keeps its shadow-side detail, and the pale rooflines of the white Norsted Alta and the silver Calder Kestrel are not clipped into the background.

Voice

Straightforward and unadorned, the voice of a desk that states the facts. Example line the brand would write: "2017 Calder Kestrel, 68,400 miles. Certified. 24,900 dollars."
