Client brief

Three villas, one collection: imagery, crops and the gate monogram For Verranza Coastal Retreats, the challenge is consistency without making three different villas interchangeable. We need the stone, limewash and coastal light to sit comfortably together across direct bookings and agent materials. The supplied do-not-want references are important. Please prepare - Villa booking hero: 3 named exports. The named villa matches the photograph. The offer price is that house's lowest 2027 nightly rate from rates_2027.csv, shown as a from price with its season named. The booking action is readable. - Villa comparison brochure: 1 named export. Villa identities are unambiguous, and the brochure carries the gate monogram at least once. The comparison makes differences between the properties easy to scan: for each house it gives sleeps, minimum stay and the nightly rate for each of the three seasons, from rates_2027.csv. Rates preserve their stated season. Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

Brand identity

Verranza Coastal Retreats

The cove, honestly

Sector
Luxury vacation rental collection of three villas above a cove, letting direct and to a small travel-agent trade channel, hospitality and travel.
Origin and scale
Liguria, Italy; canonical context for the repeat-client series.
About
Verranza Coastal Retreats is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Liguria, Italy, and this commission covers phase 1: villa image system and booking imagery.
Personality
  • restrained
  • warm
  • authentic
  • unhurried
  • coastal
  • trustworthy
Audience
Guests aged 35 to 60, American and Northern European families and groups of friends booking a whole house for a week, plus a small trade channel of travel agents working from printed rate cards. They pay for a real place and refuse anything that looks staged or cooked.
Price positioning
Upper-mid coastal rental, 520 to 780 euros a night in season. A restraint band, not a gloss band: honest light and true water rather than saturated marketplace glow, and firmly above budget literalism.
Values
Honest representation of a real place, true water and true stone, and a family mark used quietly.
Voice
Warm, plain and place-first. We name the stone, the water and the afternoon light, and we never oversell. Example line: 'Three houses above the cove, let by the family who has kept them.'
Brand assets
The collection has never had a logo file. The monogram exists only as the wrought-iron plate the village smith cut when the first house was bought, and all we hold is a photograph of it on the gate. The travel-agent rate cards survive only as the file the printer sent to press last year, with no working source.

Palette rules

Distribution
Grounds and panels are Lime Wash or Verranza Blue and nothing else. On Lime Wash, the house name, display lines and the booking-button label are Verranza Blue, and body text, rates and captions are Ink Charcoal, as on agentcard_2025_print.pdf. On Verranza Blue, all type is Lime Wash. The three accents are for hairline rules, dividers and small labels only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the photograph as a solid block.
Forbidden
No gold, bronze, copper, silver or other metallic effect on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons.
Photography
Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The Casa Scogliera pool in scogliera_pool.jpg stays pale turquoise and is not pushed toward the deep blue of verranza_pool.jpg or oliveto_pool.jpg.

Typography

Verranza Coastal Retreats runs the booking heroes in four steps. Step 01 is the house name in Canela Deck Light at 72 px on 78 px with tracking of minus 10 units, and its first letter is drawn to twice the cap height of the rest of the word, roughly 144 px, so Casa Oliveto opens on one tall C. Step 02 is the standfirst, also Canela Deck Light, at 26 px on 30 px with tracking of minus 4 units, with Light the heaviest weight the serif may take. Step 03 is body and the booking line in Untitled Sans Regular at 20 px on 30 px, tracking 0, held to a maximum of 65 characters. Step 04 is rates and captions in Untitled Sans Regular at 15 px on 22 px with tracking of plus 8 units and tabular lining figures. In the brochure the name is 34 pt, body 10 pt on 15 pt and rates 9 pt. No numeral is ever set in the serif, and there is no gold.

Signature move: Each house name opens with an oversized Canela Deck Light initial at twice the cap height of the rest of the word, and no numeral is ever set in the serif.

Rules
  • No numeral is ever set in the serif. Every figure lives in Untitled Sans, tabular lining.
  • No weight above Light in Canela Deck.
  • No gold.
  • The oversized initial is always the first letter of the house name, at twice the cap height of the rest of that word.
  • Rates and captions are Untitled Sans Regular only.
  • Name the stone, the water and the light, and never oversell.
