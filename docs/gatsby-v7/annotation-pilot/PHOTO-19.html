Client brief

Conference speaker portraits from eighteen very different submissions Our Cascadia Founders Summit speakers have sent their own photographs. They need to sit together in the programme, in tight squares and on wide stage screens. Please make them look like one event without making the people look alike. What we need back - Summit speaker card: 18 named exports. The portrait belongs to the named speaker. Each card sets the speaker's name as its largest text and carries their role line, affiliation and session from speakers.csv; the role is readable at mobile size. The summit identity is visible: each card carries the CFS mark as it appears on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. - Summit announcement: 1 named export. The event name is clear: “Cascadia Founders Summit” is the headline and the largest text, and the announcement is for the summit itself, not one speaker's confirmation. The approved date is visible. The registration action is readable: a short line inviting delegates to register, with no web address or deadline, and no price beyond the 600 to 1,200 dollar pass range in the brand identity. Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

Source of truth

Approved client notes and permissions govern factual corrections. V4 output register governs commissioned output classes and sizes. Original rows are not automatically approved copy.

Brand identity

Cascadia Founders Summit

Founders and the people who fund them

Sector
A paid two-day founders summit in Portland, Oregon, for founders, operators and institutional investors.
Origin and scale
Founded 2024, Portland, Oregon, now in its second edition, run by a core team of four staff for a room of about four hundred.
About
Cascadia Founders Summit is a two-day gathering for founders and the people who back them. Passes run six hundred to twelve hundred dollars and the room is roughly four hundred people who read the calibre of the event off its details. The summit sells seriousness and access rather than spectacle, and everything it puts in front of that audience is meant to look considered.
Personality
  • understated
  • credible
  • professional
  • quiet
  • serious
  • considered
Audience
Founders, operators and institutional investors aged 28 to 55 who expense the ticket and judge the event by how professional it feels.
Price positioning
Delegate passes 600 to 1,200 dollars, a credible professional summit priced above a free meetup and below a flagship conference. Restraint reads as calibre; a glossy or busy treatment would misprice it.
Values
A serious room worth the price of admission, where credibility is read off the smallest details.
Voice
Plain, direct and professional, confident without selling hard. Example line: Passes are limited and the room is small on purpose.
Brand assets
The summit builds its own website and needs no pages. Its badge artwork exists only as the press PDF the venue printer ran last year, with no working file behind it. The house ground, mark and tone are described in the brand notes rather than handed over as a kit.

Colour palette

Cascadia Slate #23303Amandatory

The dark ground or band that carries the CFS mark, as the upper band of summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Type on it is Warm Paper or Fog Grey. Never the ground behind a sitter.

Evergreen #2C4A3Boptional accent

Not used on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. If used at all, only for thin rules and dividers. Never a ground, a panel, a type colour or the ground behind a sitter.

Fog Grey #D9DDDEmandatory where the CFS mark sits on Cascadia Slate

The CFS mark and any small type on Cascadia Slate, as on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf, and the cool end of the Fog Grey to Warm Paper range for the ground behind each sitter (summit_brand_notes.txt). Never type on Warm Paper.

Warm Paper #F2EEE7mandatory

The main light ground or panel that carries the speaker name, role line, affiliation and session, as the lower panel of summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf; type reversed out of Cascadia Slate; the warm end of the house-ground range behind each sitter (summit_brand_notes.txt).

Signal Copper #B06B3Eoptional accent

The affiliation line on Warm Paper and a thin rule along the bottom trim, as on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never a ground, a panel, the speaker name, the role line or a headline.

Near Black #1A1F24mandatory where type sits on Warm Paper

The speaker name, role line, session, headline and running text on Warm Paper, as the name and role line on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. Never a ground or panel.

Palette rules

Distribution
Grounds and panels are Warm Paper or Cascadia Slate and nothing else; the ground behind each sitter is the portrait's house ground (see photography). On Warm Paper all type is Near Black, apart from an affiliation line in Signal Copper, as on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. On Cascadia Slate all type is Warm Paper or Fog Grey, and the CFS mark is Fog Grey on Cascadia Slate, as on the badge. Signal Copper (the affiliation line, thin rules) and Evergreen (thin rules and dividers only) are accents, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel, a speaker name, a role line or a headline.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the portrait as a solid block.
Forbidden
No gloss, glow, drop shadow, outline, gradient, metallic or busy texture on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, since restraint reads as calibre and a glossy or busy treatment would misprice the room; no coloured or busy ground behind any sitter; and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons, apart from the ground behind a sitter, which may be any soft, even tone in the Fog Grey #D9DDDE to Warm Paper #F2EEE7 range (summit_brand_notes.txt).
Photography
Portraits keep natural skin and their subjects' own colouring and are never recoloured toward the palette. The one palette-range tone in a portrait is its ground: a soft, even tone within the Fog Grey #D9DDDE to Warm Paper #F2EEE7 range, the same behind every sitter (summit_brand_notes.txt). Mixed-light casts, such as the green-tinged fluorescent light of speaker_portrait_04.jpg and staff_portrait_03.jpg, the warm lamplight of speaker_portrait_07.jpg and the cool open shade of speaker_portrait_12.jpg, are brought to natural skin, with no glossy warm filter and no flat, hard direct-flash look, and are not pushed toward Signal Copper or Evergreen.

Typography

Cascadia Founders Summit runs four steps. Step 01 is the summit title and announcement headline in Piazzolla Medium at 96 px on 104 px leading, tracking -10 units, with the optical size axis pinned to 8 so letterforms drawn for 8 pt appear at 96 px and the headline reads as printed rather than styled. Step 02 is the speaker name in Suisse Int'l Medium at 56 px on 62 px, tracking -5 units, and a speaker name is never set in Piazzolla. Step 03 is the role line and running text in Suisse Int'l Regular at 30 px on 42 px, tracking 0 with the measure capped at 65 characters. Step 04 is the session time in Suisse Int'l Mono Regular at 26 px on 34 px, tracking +15 units, the same size affiliations take in Suisse Int'l Regular. The printed programme holds the title at 28 pt and the body at 9.5 pt on 13 pt.

Signature move: Every summit title and announcement headline is set in Piazzolla Medium with the optical size axis pinned to 8, so letterforms drawn for 8 pt appear at 96 px and the headline reads as printed rather than styled.
