Aldervale Mutual - District insurance counter-card run

SB3-060-LAY · Layout & Data

Client brief

Rebuild our insurance counter-card template and produce the district run The printer who made Aldervale Mutual's counter cards has closed. We still have the press file and need a reusable production route, not a one-time approximation of the design. Scope 1. District insurance counter card: 14 named exports. The compliance wording, meaning the record's Disclosure Text, is reproduced without alteration. The contact information, meaning the record's Office, belongs to this producer. The complete Disclosure Text remains inside the card trim. The card carries no district line; none of the supplied records names a district. Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. Set the rebuilt card in the attached brand identity's type system and colours; the old press file shows which fields the card carries, not its typefaces, type sizes or rule positions. The license line and office are set as body text. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

Production constraints

  • Do not invent names, prices, claims, product features, locations, credentials, records, or documentary events that are absent from the approved inputs.
  • Do not silently replace a weak supplied asset. Use it honestly, reject it with a reason, or take only the brief's declared Stock or concept exception.
  • Record every omission, substitution, reconciliation, and material quality compromise in the delivery manifest.
  • Reconcile records before merge and prove page, row, name, and amount completeness from the delivered artifacts.
  • Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Do not invent claims, prices, dates, credentials or product features.
  • A clean preview is not a final file. Supply every named export.
  • Retouching, masks, crop matrices and contact sheets support production; they do not replace the commissioned finished collateral.

Brand identity

Aldervale Mutual

Owned by the people we cover.

Sector
A policyholder owned mutual life insurer selling whole life and final expense through appointed producers.
Origin and scale
central Vermont; canonical context for the repeat-client series.
About
Aldervale Mutual is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is central Vermont, and this commission covers phase 1: producer sales and compliance kit.
Personality
  • Sober
  • Trustworthy
  • Plain spoken
  • Institutional
  • Steady
  • Respectful
Audience
Households aged 35 to 60 with modest savings, buying because they do not want to leave a burden. They trust plainness and distrust anything that reads like a sales pitch.
Price positioning
Premiums run 45 to 180 dollars a month, drop in comparison irrelevant. A regulated mutual at a modest, accessible band, deliberately not a private wealth manager and not a discount direct response mailer.
Values
Nothing may read as an advertisement, and everything must survive the kitchen table. Compliance and honesty come before flourish, and the material should feel as dependable as the carrier behind it.
Voice
Sober, plain and reassuring, written the way a trusted advisor speaks across a table. No exclamation, no urgency. Example line the brand would write: A whole life policy is a promise that holds its value, and the premium you start with is the premium you keep.
Brand assets
The printer who built the counter cards has closed, so the only counter card artwork that survives is the flat press PDF that went to press. Producer headshots are self submitted from personal phones and vary widely. Compliance owns the disclosure language and updates it when a state changes its rules.

Palette rules

Distribution
Document Paper is the card ground and the only ground or panel colour, as on countercard_2026_print.pdf. All type is Ink Navy or Slate: the producer name and the Disclosure Text in Ink Navy and the license line and office in Slate, as on countercard_2026_print.pdf; any premium in either. Heritage Green is only the two 1 pt rules that bracket the card. Mist Grey is only for thin dividers or a keyline. Heritage Green and Mist Grey together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
Forbidden
No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No type in Heritage Green, Mist Grey or Document Paper, no premium picked out in any colour, bold or italic, and no burst or starburst callout in any colour, as compliance_note.txt rules out.
Photography
Photographs keep their own natural colour and skin tones and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette. Neutralising a phone's warm or cool cast is allowed; pushing a suit, a face or a wall toward Ink Navy, Slate, Heritage Green or Mist Grey is not.
