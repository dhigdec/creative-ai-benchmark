Northgrove - Apparel colorway image system

SB3-010-PHO · Photo & Image

Client brief

Colour-consistent apparel images and clean retailer cutouts Our Northgrove range is affordable premium basics. Customers choose between colours and fabrics, so a polished set that changes either of those is not useful to us. Please prepare - Apparel style product-page module: 6 named exports. Each module is a 1440 x 1800 px PNG, as the output register sets; it is a designed module, not the 1600 x 2000 px JPEG hero listed in northgrove_placements.txt. The module names its style as northgrove_copy.txt does, and the garment geometry matches its source style. The colourway is identified accurately: each garment keeps the colour it was photographed in, and any colourway name it carries is one northgrove_colourways.csv approves for that style and whose line in northgrove_copy.txt describes that colour. Fabric texture survives the treatment. - Capsule launch carousel: 6 named exports. The capsule has a coherent visual direction. The opening page identifies Northgrove with our mark, recovered as real artwork and set to our type system. The closing page contains the approved shopping action: our tall post line from northgrove_copy.txt, word for word. Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

Source of truth

Approved client notes and permissions govern factual corrections. V4 output register governs commissioned output classes and sizes. Original rows are not automatically approved copy.

Production constraints

  • Do not invent names, prices, claims, product features, locations, credentials, records, or documentary events that are absent from the approved inputs.
  • Do not silently replace a weak supplied asset. Use it honestly, reject it with a reason, or take only the brief's declared Stock or concept exception.
  • Record every omission, substitution, reconciliation, and material quality compromise in the delivery manifest.
  • Preserve identity, product geometry, material behavior, and documentary truth; retouching may improve presentation but may not change what the subject is.
  • Color variants are approval concepts until physical samples are photographed and signed off.
  • Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Do not invent claims, prices, dates, credentials or product features.
  • A clean preview is not a final file. Supply every named export.
  • Retouching, masks, crop matrices and contact sheets support production; they do not replace the commissioned finished collateral.

Brand identity

Northgrove

Better than it looks in the price.

Sector
Elevated basics apparel: a six style seasonal capsule sold direct online and through one marketplace feed, including a supporters' crew made with a local amateur football club.
Origin and scale
Burlington, Vermont; canonical context for the repeat-client series.
About
Northgrove is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Burlington, Vermont, and this commission covers phase 1: apparel colour and ecommerce proofing.
Personality
  • honest
  • quiet
  • considered
  • warm
  • unfussy
  • durable
Audience
Women and men aged 22 to 38 choosing on a phone, cross shopping against a 9 dollar fast fashion tee and a 140 dollar heritage one, buying a 32 to 60 dollar piece they expect to keep.
Price positioning
Elevated basics at 32 to 60 dollars. We sit above fast fashion and below heritage. The whole proposition is that the cloth is better than the price suggests, so nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.
Values
Honesty between the picture and the product, and small batch cloth that earns its price.
Voice
Plain, specific, a little dry. We name the cloth and the cut and leave the adjectives out. Example line the brand would write: 'Loopback cotton, dyed in small lots, made to soften with wear.'
Brand assets
Our olive leaf glyph and charcoal wordmark only survive as a picture lifted off an old lookbook page. The studio that drew them has closed and no one has the source file, so the mark needs recovering as real artwork before it can go on cards or launch pieces.

Colour palette

Charcoal #2E2E2Cmandatory

All type, rules and the NORTHGROVE wordmark on Off White, Oat or Warm Grey Paper, as the Charcoal text and rules on northgrove_colourcard.pdf.

Warm Grey Paper #C9C4BAoptional

The warm grey seamless the six garments were photographed on: it may stay inside a photograph, fill a frame behind one (the swatch window on northgrove_colourcard.pdf) or be a secondary panel under Charcoal type on the Off White ground. Not the ground of a product-page module or carousel page.

Olive #6B7248mandatory in the mark's leaf on light grounds; otherwise optional accent

The olive leaf of the Northgrove mark, and small labels such as the season line on northgrove_colourcard.pdf. Never a ground, panel or body text.

Oat #E4DCCBoptional

A secondary panel or band on the Off White ground that carries Charcoal type. Not the ground of a product-page module or carousel page.

Club Navy #1B2A44optional, only for supporters' crew content

A ground only behind the supporters' crew or its line “Made with Rowanfield Athletic.”, since northgrove_placements.txt ties navy to the club. Everything on it, type and the whole reversed mark, is Off White.

Off White #F5F2ECmandatory

The ground of every product-page module (northgrove_placements.txt) and of every carousel page, as the stock of northgrove_colourcard.pdf, apart from Club Navy behind supporters' crew content; also all type and the reversed mark on Club Navy.

Palette rules

Distribution
Grounds: Off White #F5F2EC is the ground of every product-page module and of every carousel page, as the stock of northgrove_colourcard.pdf; Oat #E4DCCB and Warm Grey Paper #C9C4BA may be secondary panels or bands on it; Club Navy #1B2A44 is a ground only behind supporters' crew content. Type: Charcoal #2E2E2C on Off White, Oat and Warm Grey Paper, as on northgrove_colourcard.pdf; Off White on Club Navy. Olive #6B7248 is the accent for the mark's leaf and small labels only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, panel or body text.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: any text panel is a solid block beside or over the photograph.
Forbidden
No colour outside the six palette hexes (#2E2E2C, #C9C4BA, #6B7248, #E4DCCB, #1B2A44 and #F5F2EC) for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000 included; no gold, foil, metallic or gloss effect, since nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.
Photography
Photographs keep the colour each garment was photographed in, apart from neutralising the shoot's light, and are never recoloured toward a palette hex or another colourway; no recoloured colourway variant is shown as a product. The tee in northgrove_style_02_heavy_tee.jpg is not pushed to the palette's Olive #6B7248, the crew in northgrove_style_01_loopback_crew.jpg and the henley in northgrove_style_04_henley.jpg are not pushed to the palette's Oat #E4DCCB, and the raglan in northgrove_style_03_raglan.jpg is not pushed to Charcoal #2E2E2C. Palette rules govern the design, not the pixels of a photograph.
