Verda Reformer Studio - Fitness campaign image system

SB3-028-PHO · Photo & Image

Client brief

A calm launch image system for a reformer studio with a limited shoot Verda is a reformer studio, and the launch should feel calm, approachable and grounded in the room we actually have. Our two room photographs are dusky. We need them prepared, not replaced by a fictional studio. The launch page shows our own room from at least one of those two photographs. Please prepare - Studio launch-page composition: 1 named export. The studio location is accurate. The class offer is clear: every class we teach, each with its price from roster_2027.csv. The booking action is visible. - Class announcement: 1 named export. The class details match the approved roster: the pictured instructor's own class, its price and its validity. The pictured instructor is identified accurately, by the full name the roster gives for that portrait, and is cut out onto a solid Warm Off White or Sage ground. - Studio booking story: 1 named export. The booking action is inside the safe area. The studio identity is readable. Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. Every export carries our wordmark as drawn in verda_logo_web.png, its letterforms kept, not retyped in another face. Where the brand identity describes the wordmark as Monument Extended caps tracked +320 thousandths, the drawing takes the place of that setting; the wordmark still sits on one line, never larger than 34 px on screen, and on the launch page it sits above the headline as its own lock. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

Brand identity

Verda Reformer Studio

Room to breathe.

Sector
Boutique reformer pilates studio
Origin and scale
Founded 2022, first studio run for four years; opening a second location on Calloway Row in a North American city. Owner led by Marit and Dana, with six instructors. Small, independent, growing to two rooms.
About
Verda is a boutique reformer pilates studio built on calm. One body, one room, generous air, and unhurried attention. Marit and Dana have run a single studio for four years and are now opening a second on Calloway Row. The studio is deliberately the opposite of a chain gym: no crowding, no shouting, no gloss of the everyday. What clients pay for is space, focus and a considered environment they return to.
Personality
  • Calm
  • Considered
  • Warm
  • Quietly confident
  • Unhurried
Audience
Considered buyers, mostly women in professional work, aged 28 to 48. They typically audition three studios before committing to one, and they are paying for calm, attention and space rather than volume or intensity.
Price positioning
Premium neighborhood, not luxury. Intro pack 180 dollars, membership 220 a month. Positioned above a high volume budget gym and below wellness luxury: the tone is soft daylight and quiet confidence, never pretension and never a discount.
Values
Calm, attention, generous space, and honesty about the room a client will actually walk into.
Voice
Plain, warm and unhurried. Speaks to one person rather than a crowd. Confident without hype, and honest about what the room actually is.
Brand assets
No brand guidelines were ever written. The wordmark was drawn by a designer who has since left, and the only surviving file is a small low resolution web export she handed over. There is no master artwork, no color specification and no font list on record, so the freelancer inherits the studio look from this brief and from the room itself.

Colour palette

Sage #9CA98Foptional

The one alternative ground: panels, buttons or the solid ground behind a cut-out portrait; and small labels on Warm Off White, as the field labels on passcard_2025_print.pdf. Never body text.

Oak #C7A97Aoptional accent

Hairline rules and dividers only, as the rule under the name on passcard_2025_print.pdf. Never a ground, panel, button or text.

Warm Off White #F4F0E8mandatory

The main ground, as on passcard_2025_print.pdf, and the ground behind a cut-out portrait; also type or the wordmark reversed out of Sage.

Soft Ink #33352Emandatory

Headlines, body text, prices, fine print and the wordmark on Warm Off White, as the name, values and wordmark on passcard_2025_print.pdf. Never a ground, panel or button fill.

Palette rules

Distribution
Grounds, panels and buttons are Warm Off White or Sage and nothing else, and a cut-out portrait sits on one of the two. On Warm Off White, headlines, body text, prices, fine print and the wordmark are Soft Ink, and only small labels may be Sage, as on passcard_2025_print.pdf. On Sage, all type and the wordmark are Soft Ink or Warm Off White. Oak is for hairline rules and dividers only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode; no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph. Type, the wordmark included, set beside or over a photograph sits on a solid Warm Off White or Sage panel, never directly on the photograph. Last year's pass, passcard_2025_print.pdf, sets its wordmark at 92%; on these exports the wordmark is solid Soft Ink or Warm Off White.
Forbidden
No colour outside the four palette hexes (#9CA98F, #C7A97A, #F4F0E8, #33352E) for type, the wordmark, panels, rules, buttons or icons. The orange skin and black rubber floor that owner_note.txt rejects are photographic prohibitions, set out under photography.
Photography
Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. In verda_studio_frame_01.jpg and verda_studio_frame_02.jpg the reformer upholstery is brought to its own sage green and the floor to its own natural oak in one soft daylight, neither pushed to the Sage or Oak hexes. No skin reads orange and no photograph shows a black rubber floor, as owner_note.txt asks.

Typography

Verda Reformer Studio runs four steps. Step 01, Display and H1, is Monument Extended Regular at 88 px on 104 px leading, tracked -10 thousandths, as the launch headline; the wordmark sits above it as a separate lock, caps on one line, tracked +320 thousandths, never larger than 34 px on screen or 11 pt on the pass card. Step 02, Subheading and H2, is Monument Extended Regular at 56 px on 68 px, tracked -5 thousandths, for feed posts and stories. Step 03, Body, is Basis Grotesque Regular at 22 px on 34 px, tracking 0, measure held to a maximum of 65 characters. Step 04, Fine print, is Basis Grotesque Medium at 16 px on 24 px, tracked +15 thousandths, and it carries class times and prices; the leading was invented because the prose states none. The wordmark is never stacked onto two lines.

Signature move: The wordmark is always Monument Extended caps on one line, tracked 320 thousandths open and never larger than 34 px, so it reads as a long calm horizontal band rather than a word.
