    02 · Context

    Client brief and brand system

    Client brief

    Colour-consistent apparel images and clean retailer cutouts

Our Northgrove range is affordable premium basics. Customers choose between colours and fabrics, so a polished set that changes either of those is not useful to us.

Please prepare
- Apparel style product-page module: 6 named exports. Each module is a 1440 x 1800 px PNG, as the output register sets; it is a designed module, not the 1600 x 2000 px JPEG hero listed in northgrove_placements.txt. The module names its style as northgrove_copy.txt does, and the garment geometry matches its source style. The colourway is identified accurately: each garment keeps the colour it was photographed in, and any colourway name it carries is one northgrove_colourways.csv approves for that style and whose line in northgrove_copy.txt describes that colour. Fabric texture survives the treatment.
- Capsule launch carousel: 6 named exports. The capsule has a coherent visual direction. The opening page identifies Northgrove with our mark, recovered as real artwork and set to our type system. The closing page contains the approved shopping action: our tall post line from northgrove_copy.txt, word for word.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

    Northgrove

    Audience
    Women and men aged 22 to 38 choosing on a phone, cross shopping against a 9 dollar fast fashion tee and a 140 dollar heritage one, buying a 32 to 60 dollar piece they expect to keep.
    Positioning
    Elevated basics at 32 to 60 dollars. We sit above fast fashion and below heritage. The whole proposition is that the cloth is better than the price suggests, so nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.
    Personality
    honest · quiet · considered · warm · unfussy · durable
    Voice
    Plain, specific, a little dry. We name the cloth and the cut and leave the adjectives out. Example line the brand would write: 'Loopback cotton, dyed in small lots, made to soften with wear.'
    Typography
    Northgrove sets four steps. Step 01, display and H1, is GT Super Display Bold at 96 px on 102 px leading, tracked minus 20 thousandths, for the carousel headline, as in The loopback crew, back in oat; the wordmark sets here in the same face at that same closed tracking, NORTHGROVE in caps with the letters almost touching as one solid charcoal block and the olive leaf locked at cap height one leaf width to its left. Step 02, the subheading, is GT Super Display Bold at 56 px on 62 px, tracked minus 10 thousandths, for the style name, as in Loopback Crew, Oat. Step 03, body, is Freight Sans Pro Book at 20 px on 32 px leading, tracking 0, held to a maximum of 65 characters a line for cloth notes and colourway names, as in: Loopback cotton, dyed in small lots, made to soften with wear; the price takes Freight Sans Pro Medium at 24 px. Step 04, fine print, is Freight Sans Pro Book at 14 px on 20 px, tracked 40 thousandths, as in Burlington, Vermont. Free returns within thirty days; that size is the one figure the existing notes never stated, set proportionate under the 20 px body. The headline to body jump stays near five to one.
