    02 · Context

    Client brief and brand system

    Client brief

    A calm launch image system for a reformer studio with a limited shoot

Verda is a reformer studio, and the launch should feel calm, approachable and grounded in the room we actually have. Our two room photographs are dusky. We need them prepared, not replaced by a fictional studio. The launch page shows our own room from at least one of those two photographs.

Please prepare
- Studio launch-page composition: 1 named export. The studio location is accurate. The class offer is clear: every class we teach, each with its price from roster_2027.csv. The booking action is visible.
- Class announcement: 1 named export. The class details match the approved roster: the pictured instructor's own class, its price and its validity. The pictured instructor is identified accurately, by the full name the roster gives for that portrait, and is cut out onto a solid Warm Off White or Sage ground.
- Studio booking story: 1 named export. The booking action is inside the safe area. The studio identity is readable.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. Every export carries our wordmark as drawn in verda_logo_web.png, its letterforms kept, not retyped in another face. Where the brand identity describes the wordmark as Monument Extended caps tracked +320 thousandths, the drawing takes the place of that setting; the wordmark still sits on one line, never larger than 34 px on screen, and on the launch page it sits above the headline as its own lock. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

    Verda Reformer Studio

    Audience
    Considered buyers, mostly women in professional work, aged 28 to 48. They typically audition three studios before committing to one, and they are paying for calm, attention and space rather than volume or intensity.
    Positioning
    Premium neighborhood, not luxury. Intro pack 180 dollars, membership 220 a month. Positioned above a high volume budget gym and below wellness luxury: the tone is soft daylight and quiet confidence, never pretension and never a discount.
    Personality
    Calm · Considered · Warm · Quietly confident · Unhurried
    Voice
    Plain, warm and unhurried. Speaks to one person rather than a crowd. Confident without hype, and honest about what the room actually is.
    Typography
    Verda Reformer Studio runs four steps. Step 01, Display and H1, is Monument Extended Regular at 88 px on 104 px leading, tracked -10 thousandths, as the launch headline; the wordmark sits above it as a separate lock, caps on one line, tracked +320 thousandths, never larger than 34 px on screen or 11 pt on the pass card. Step 02, Subheading and H2, is Monument Extended Regular at 56 px on 68 px, tracked -5 thousandths, for feed posts and stories. Step 03, Body, is Basis Grotesque Regular at 22 px on 34 px, tracking 0, measure held to a maximum of 65 characters. Step 04, Fine print, is Basis Grotesque Medium at 16 px on 24 px, tracked +15 thousandths, and it carries class times and prices; the leading was invented because the prose states none. The wordmark is never stacked onto two lines.
