    02 · Context

    Client brief and brand system

    Client brief

    Watch campaign treatment plus colour-accurate dealer product files

Two kinds of files are needed for Lunara Chronometry, and they must not be confused. The campaign can carry our tonal treatment; the dealer needs clean product files, including a dial-finish reference that stays true to colour.

Scope
1. Autumn watch key visual: 1 named export. The key visual names the one reference it shows, and that watch's dial matches the approved description of that reference. The watch remains the dominant visual.
2. Watch product-page feature: 5 named exports. The case geometry matches the named watch. The specification wording is accurate: each feature gives its watch's reference, caliber, case metal and dial finish exactly as its record in lunara_register.csv gives them.
3. Watch dealer launch sheet: 1 named export. Model identities are unambiguous: the sheet names each of the five references with its photograph, dial finish and caliber. The sheet also carries the fixed timing line word for word. The layout reflects the stated price position.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

    Lunara Chronometry

    Audience
    Collector-literate buyers, 35 to 60, who judge a watch on form, finish and the hand behind it.
    Positioning
    Four to twelve thousand dollars a piece, positioned as independent horology: above fashion watchmaking and below the grand houses.
    Personality
    Precise · Warm · Restrained · Credible · Considered · Quiet
    Voice
    Exact, understated, collector to collector. Example line: 'Five references. Each one regulated by hand and signed.'
    Typography
    Lunara Chronometry runs Boska for display and Suisse Int'l for text and for every figure. Step 01, display and H1, is Boska Medium small caps at 72 px on 76 px, tracked 180 units, the wordmark always stacked as Lunara over Chronometry; a dealer sheet heading takes the same setting at 28 pt on 31 pt. Step 02, the reference name, is Boska Medium small caps at 40 px on 44 px, tracked 180 units, and 48 px on 53 px on a product page; any numeral inside it switches to Suisse Int'l tabular lining at the same size. Step 03, body, is Suisse Int'l Regular at 20 px on 30 px, tracking 0, measure held to 65 characters, with specification at 22 px on 32 px and dealer sheet body at 10 pt on 14 pt. Step 04, fine print, is Suisse Int'l Regular tabular lining at 16 px on 22 px, tracked plus 20 units, and carries prices, serials and the timing result. The timing line keeps its fixed wording and is never set in italic or in the serif.

    Palette

    Warm near-black#191512
    Bone cream#EDE6D6
    Tempered steel#9A9EA3
    Aged brass#9C7A3C
    Oxblood#5A2A28
