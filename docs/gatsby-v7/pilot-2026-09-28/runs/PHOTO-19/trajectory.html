    02 · Context

    Client brief and brand system

    Client brief

    Conference speaker portraits from eighteen very different submissions

Our Cascadia Founders Summit speakers have sent their own photographs. They need to sit together in the programme, in tight squares and on wide stage screens. Please make them look like one event without making the people look alike.

What we need back
- Summit speaker card: 18 named exports. The portrait belongs to the named speaker. Each card sets the speaker's name as its largest text and carries their role line, affiliation and session from speakers.csv; the role is readable at mobile size. The summit identity is visible: each card carries the CFS mark as it appears on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf.
- Summit announcement: 1 named export. The event name is clear: “Cascadia Founders Summit” is the headline and the largest text, and the announcement is for the summit itself, not one speaker's confirmation. The approved date is visible. The registration action is readable: a short line inviting delegates to register, with no web address or deadline, and no price beyond the 600 to 1,200 dollar pass range in the brand identity.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.

    Cascadia Founders Summit

    Audience
    Founders, operators and institutional investors aged 28 to 55 who expense the ticket and judge the event by how professional it feels.
    Positioning
    Delegate passes 600 to 1,200 dollars, a credible professional summit priced above a free meetup and below a flagship conference. Restraint reads as calibre; a glossy or busy treatment would misprice it.
    Personality
    understated · credible · professional · quiet · serious · considered
    Voice
    Plain, direct and professional, confident without selling hard. Example line: Passes are limited and the room is small on purpose.
    Typography
    Cascadia Founders Summit runs four steps. Step 01 is the summit title and announcement headline in Piazzolla Medium at 96 px on 104 px leading, tracking -10 units, with the optical size axis pinned to 8 so letterforms drawn for 8 pt appear at 96 px and the headline reads as printed rather than styled. Step 02 is the speaker name in Suisse Int'l Medium at 56 px on 62 px, tracking -5 units, and a speaker name is never set in Piazzolla. Step 03 is the role line and running text in Suisse Int'l Regular at 30 px on 42 px, tracking 0 with the measure capped at 65 characters. Step 04 is the session time in Suisse Int'l Mono Regular at 26 px on 34 px, tracking +15 units, the same size affiliations take in Suisse Int'l Regular. The printed programme holds the title at 28 pt and the body at 9.5 pt on 13 pt.
