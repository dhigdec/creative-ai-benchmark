# SB3-098-MOT | Velvet Hour - Social film and story cutdowns

Velvet Hour needs a short film for the next show, a square cut for the feed and the next show poster. The camera sits in the same corner every month and it gives us short silent takes, a few seconds each, with no sound at all, so we are sending five more takes from that corner under the same lamp. Cut the film upright. It is filmed upright and that is where people will watch it.

No narration and no voice over. The words go on screen, set small and quietly, the way we write a listing. The music is the cleared instrumental bed we supply, not a recording of anyone's set, so nothing on these films is anyone's performance audio.

The commission
1. Performance film: 1 named export, 1080 by 1920, 12 to 15 seconds, music from the supplied bed. At least three different takes. The singer stays in frame. The end card bills the next show.
2. Square performance cutdown: 1 named export, 1080 by 1080, 6 to 10 seconds, music from the supplied bed. The singer stays in frame in the square crop.
3. Next show poster: 1 named export, unchanged at 420 by 594 mm, one page. The billing order matches the lineup and the show date is accurate.

Two things matter to us as much as the atmosphere. The act on the end card is next month's act, so bill her as next and do not attribute the footage in the film to her. The billing order, the dates and the prices come from the lineup file and are not ours to reorder or round.

One warning about the framing: the camera clears a pillar, so the singer sits in the left third of the picture and the centre of the frame is a stool and a pair of plain black road cases. A centred crop can be technically correct and still contain no performer, so check the crop rather than trusting it.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.
Video delivery means a playable MP4, not a storyboard, a still montage PDF or an editing log. Audio expectations are stated on each film. No film in this commission carries speech: the message is carried by on screen text you set, and the music is the cleared bed we supply.
Authored animation can exceed the duration of still source images. Live action edits require adequate distinct footage; looping a short clip is not a substitute for missing coverage.
Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
