# SB3-001-PHO | Aurelian Skin - Ecommerce skincare image system

Make eight skincare products look like one launch, not eight shoots

Our launch range needs to look as considered online as it does on the shelf. At the moment the eight bottles look as though they belong to different brands.

What we need back
- Skincare product-page module: 8 named exports. The photograph shows the product named on this module. The bottle label remains legible at the delivered size. The product benefit is visually subordinate to the product name.
- Skincare range launch key visual: 1 named export. The composition identifies Aurelian Skin. The cleared launch claim is reproduced without a stronger efficacy claim. The product range is the primary visual focus.
- Retailer launch carousel: 5 named exports. The opening page identifies the range. The closing page presents the approved shopping action. The product sequence has a clear narrative.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
