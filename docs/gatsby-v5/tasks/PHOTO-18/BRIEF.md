# SB3-018-PHO | Fernwell and Rowe - Hartley wedding gallery finishing

Wedding retouching pilot: one warm house look across changing light

We are Fernwell & Rowe, and this is a pilot using the Hartley wedding shortlist. There are sixteen photographs spanning window light, golden hour and the reception. We want a natural warm treatment that holds together through those changes without giving every person the same skin colour.

Scope
1. Wedding review image: 16 named exports. The people retain their source identities. Skin retains natural texture. The scene remains faithful to the event.
2. Full-resolution wedding keeper: 6 named exports. The delivered keeper is derived from its named source photograph. Highlight detail is retained where present in the source.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
