# Emberline

Emberline is a bright, friendly daily food and craft show built around one host, a sunny studio kitchen and an accessible dish or make each day. It is free to watch and mainstream in the best sense: warm, unfussy and appetising. Because the promo ships every single day, the brand lives or dies on a look that can be reached again tomorrow by whoever is at the desk.

## Audience
Mainstream daytime viewers skewing thirty five to sixty five, watching on a mid range television and on their phones. The show is free and the products featured run ten to thirty dollars.

## Positioning
Regional daytime, free to watch, everyday products. The look must be lively, clean and appetising, never moody, filmic or corporate, and never a two hundred dollar tasting menu with deep shadows and fine type. Hard broadcast punch is the other failure: it stops the food looking edible.

## Palette
- Ember Coral: #E8623D
- Fresh Cream: #FBF4E7
- Herb Green: #5FA05A
- Warm Charcoal: #33302B
- Sunlit Butter: #F2C14E

## Typography
A friendly rounded sans for straps and captions with a slightly warmer sans or soft slab for the show name and endboard. Generous, plain and legible on a television and a phone alike. Nothing precious, condensed or editorial.

## Voice
Upbeat, plain and welcoming, like a host talking to a regular viewer. Example line the brand would write: "Today on Emberline: a white bean and rosemary gratin you can have on the table in under an hour."

## Supplied Identity
There is no clean brand kit. The typefaces the on air package used survive only as a previous designer's note written from memory, in loose form. The house colour is recorded twice with two different values, one from the on air graphics package and one from the print kit, with nothing saying which governs, so it must be settled before use.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
