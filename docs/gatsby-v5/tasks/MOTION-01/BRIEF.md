# SB3-081-MOT | Ember and Oak - Restaurant launch reel system

We are opening the second room at Ember and Oak and we need a launch package that makes people hungry before they read a word. Everything the shoot left us is short and silent, five second takes off the pass and around the room, so treat this as a short form set rather than a film with a story to tell. The food leads: real char, visible texture, a room that feels alive. The message rides on type, not on a voice.

Please cut a 12 to 15 second launch reel in 16:9, built from at least five different takes, then an 8 to 10 second upright cutdown and a 6 to 8 second square bumper that each stand on their own rather than reading as a crop of the wide one. The upright keeps all type clear of the top 250 and bottom 320 pixels. Every film stays silent by our own choice, so the words have to carry it on screen. The closing card carries the mark, the line “Second room now open” and the booking action “Book a table”, designed as a card. Please do not drop the mark over the last available frame and call that an ending.

One correction to the manifest: emberoak_pass_call.mov has no audio despite the name. Treat it as b roll like the rest.

The rest of the package is unchanged. We still need the reel cover, the window poster and the three dish callout cards, with dish names, prices and allergen lines taken word for word from emberoak_menu_callouts.txt.
