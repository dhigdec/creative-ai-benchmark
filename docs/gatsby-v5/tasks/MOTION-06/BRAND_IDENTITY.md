# Meridian Academy

Meridian Academy sells a single self-paced program, Operations Analytics for Working Managers, at two hundred and ninety dollars. Our learners are thirty to fifty, mid-career, watching lessons late at night after their own workday. We are not a bootcamp and we are not a university. We teach one useful thing well and we respect the viewer's time and intelligence.

## Audience
Working managers aged thirty to fifty, paying with their own money, fitting the course around a full-time job. They want a working skill, not a transformation story, and they distrust anything that feels like marketing.

## Positioning
Mid-market. A two hundred and ninety dollar considered purchase. The register sits between an impulse-priced tutorial and a formal institution: competent, quiet, self-funded professional development.

## Typography
One restrained type system across every card and still: a plain, legible workhorse for body and captions, paired with a slightly more characterful face for titles. No display gimmicks, generous size for late-night laptop reading.

## Voice
Direct, warm, matter of fact. Speaks to a peer, not a beginner. Short sentences, no hype, no exclamation. The tone of a good colleague explaining something over coffee.
