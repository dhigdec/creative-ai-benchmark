# SB3-099-MOT | Milepost Driving Academy - Scenario clip training library

Milepost is starting with two scenarios rather than the whole library. If these two land with learners and with the parents watching over their shoulder, we will commission the rest on the same pattern.

Our learners watch on a phone with the sound off, so nothing is spoken in these clips. No narration, no voice over, no synthetic instructor. The instruction goes on screen, word for word from the approved copy, one step at a time, in the order the copy gives, and the clips run silent.

Deliverables
1. Right turns explainer: 1 named export, 1920 by 1080, 12 to 15 seconds, silent. All five steps from the Right turns row on screen in order, then the callout for that row.
2. Four-way stops explainer: 1 named export, 1920 by 1080, 10 to 12 seconds, silent. All four steps from the Four-way stops row on screen in order, then the callout for that row.
3. Vertical lane changes example: 1 named export, 1080 by 1920, 6 to 9 seconds, silent. The scenario title and the callout only, so we can see how the road reads upright on a phone before we commit the rest of the library to that format.

Show the manoeuvre, do not imply it. A learner should be able to see the car do the thing the step describes. If a step is not visible in the footage we have given you, say so in the delivery manifest rather than cutting unrelated road shots together until the manoeuvre looks finished.

Two notes on the source. The two four way stop takes are the same junction filmed on two passes in different lanes, so please do not present them as two different junctions. Road context has to stay readable in the upright example: if the lane line leaves the frame, the example teaches nothing.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.
Video delivery means a playable MP4, not a storyboard, a still montage PDF or an editing log. Audio expectations are stated on each film. No film in this commission carries speech or music: every clip is delivered silent, with the instruction set on screen.
Authored animation can exceed the duration of still source images. Live action edits require adequate distinct footage; looping a short clip is not a substitute for missing coverage.
Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
