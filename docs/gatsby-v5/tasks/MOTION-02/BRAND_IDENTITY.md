# Ironwood Kitchen

Ironwood Kitchen builds honest, high protein bowls for the office lunch run, in the fridge case by mid morning and gone by early afternoon. We are a working kitchen, not a tasting room. The promise is simple: food you would actually eat at your desk, twelve dollars, nothing invented and nothing dressed up.

## Audience
Office workers 24 to 45 on a weekday lunch run with roughly eleven minutes to spend, buying on macros, speed and trust rather than on spectacle.

## Positioning
Twelve dollars a bowl, the everyday middle of grab and go. Above a supermarket meal deal, well below a chef counter. We are not a tasting menu and never pretend to be one.

## Palette
- Bench White: #F6F5F1
- Ink Charcoal: #23262A
- Field Green: #3F7D4E
- Salsa Clay: #E2593B
- Steel Grey: #9AA0A6

## Typography
One plain, high legibility grotesque sans carries everything, set large and tight. Names and prices sit big and quiet, one claim per card, no scripts and no display flourish. The type should read from arm's length in a fridge case.

## Voice
Direct, warm, counter honest, one thought per line. Example: "Twelve bucks, in the case by half ten, gone by two. That is the whole promise."

## Supplied Identity
No brand kit survives. The wordmark exists only as a flat picture on a white ground, exported by a designer who has since left, with no vector file and no source anywhere. Everything else is a photograph or a spreadsheet.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
