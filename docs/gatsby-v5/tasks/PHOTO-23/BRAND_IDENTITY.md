# Ironline Forge

Ironline Forge casts bespoke iron and bronze for architects and developers who have mostly never stood on a foundry floor. We sell permanence and provenance: the heat, the hands, and the fact that the piece will still be here in eighty years. The brand has to feel like the shop floor, hot, dirty and precise.

## Audience
Specifiers, architects and developers, 40 to 65, buying craft and confidence rather than a catalogue product.

## Positioning
Bespoke castings from five to sixty thousand dollars a piece, priced on provenance and never presented as a promotion.

## Palette
- Iron black: #1A1A1C
- Furnace orange: #C6591F
- Cast bronze: #7A5A2E
- Ash grey: #6E6E6B
- Foundry sand: #C9BCA3

## Typography
A generous editorial serif for headlines with plenty of black around it, and a strong grotesque for labels and spec lines. Numbers are set plainly.

## Voice
Plain, declarative, unshowy. Example line: 'Iron and bronze, cast in this building since 1911.'

## Supplied Identity
Our maker's mark was cut into a die generations ago and survives only struck into the metal. There is no artwork for it anywhere, and the trade-fair stand and the brand story page are being rebuilt at the same time.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
