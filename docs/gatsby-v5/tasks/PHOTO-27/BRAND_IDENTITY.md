# Fennhollow Botanical Soda

Fennhollow Botanical Soda makes three botanical sodas in the Hudson Valley: elderflower and lime, rhubarb and bitters, and spruce tip. Three dollars fifty a can, sold across upstate natural grocers and by subscription, produced through a co-packer whose cleared nutrition claim cannot be reworded. Two founders run everything and neither is a photographer. The whole brand lives on summer: sun lit, warm, natural, a little restrained. The spruce tip flavour steps to the front in August, so the summer campaign has to be built to be revisited as the season shifts.

## Audience
Twenty-five to forty, shopping the natural grocer aisle and reading the ingredient panel. Paying a premium for the promise that this is closer to a real thing than a mass soda.

## Positioning
Craft better-for-you at three fifty a can. Warmer and more human than a twelve-dollar apothecary tonic, and far more restrained and natural than a hyper-saturated ninety-nine-cent soda.

## Palette
- Label Green: #3F6B3A
- Warm Cream Ground: #F4ECD8
- Afternoon Amber: #E5AF54
- Rhubarb Rose: #C56E68
- Deep Ink: #2C2A24

## Typography
A warm humanist sans across the family, legible over photography, paired with a friendly display cut for the flavour names. Natural and restrained, not wellness-minimal and not soda-aisle shout.

## Voice
Plain, warm, a little rural. Names the botanicals and the valley, not a lifestyle. The co-packer's cleared claim line stays exactly as written.

## Supplied Identity
No written brand guidelines have ever existed and the founders asked for the first one to be created. Their brand note points to a printed swatch card that was never sent with the package, so the house green survives only as the printed label on the two studio can shots the brand owns. There is no lifestyle photography of any kind.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
