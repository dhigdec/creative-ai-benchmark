# Norvant

Norvant is workflow automation built for finance and operations teams. Seats are one hundred and forty dollars a month, and nothing gets bought without three people signing off, so everything we put out has to look like it was made by people who know what they are doing. We are a small team shipping a serious tool, and our March release is the work the whole company has been building toward.

## Audience
A buying committee of three in finance and operations, with a procurement review mid-cycle. They are looking for evidence of seriousness and real numbers, and they lengthen the cycle the moment something feels like consumer marketing.

## Positioning
Considered B2B mid-market. One hundred and forty dollars per seat per month, committee-approved. Not self-serve impulse software, and not heavyweight enterprise. A tool an operations lead can adopt in an afternoon and defend to a controller.

## Palette
- Slate: #33404B
- Amber: #E0A02C
- Ink Near-Black: #191F24
- Paper White: #F6F4EF
- Cool Grey: #8A929A

## Typography
One quiet, credible type system across the cards, the announcement set and the handover: a plain neutral face for detail and body, a measured companion for headings. Nothing expressive, nothing decorative. Legibility at small print sizes matters more than personality.

## Voice
Plain, specific, confident without selling. States what the product does and lets the facts carry it. No stock smiles, no superlatives. Tells people directly what is and is not true.

## Supplied Identity
Brand files went with the agency that made them. The mark now survives only as the sign on our reception wall: a hexring glyph beside the wordmark, cut in slate on a brushed metal panel. Last year's regional event card exists only as the print-ready file we sent to the printer. We need the mark recovered as scalable artwork and this year's card run rebuilt from last year's file so it matches. No editable logo source, colour build or master template remains on our side.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
