# SB3-087-MOT | Norvant - Product explainer motion system

Norvant's March release is called Throughline, and we need something short enough for a finance or operations buyer to watch in a feed without a sales call. Two things about the pack, so you are not surprised: there is no product capture in it, and the footage that does exist is five seconds a clip with no audio. So this is not a demonstration film and it cannot be a narrated one. It is a release film carried by the three cleared claims set as on screen text, cut over the founder take and the office material, closing on the one next step we want people to take.

Deliverables

1. Release film: 1 named export, 12 to 15 seconds, 16:9, under the supplied instrumental bed (norvant_bed.wav). It names the release, carries at least one of the three cleared claims word for word, and closes on the registration address. Elise appears from the supplied take as a portrait beat, not as a talking head: her line sits on screen as text.

2. Release film vertical cutdown: 1 named export, 6 to 8 seconds, 9:16, silent, cut from the same material. The release name reads before the registration address.

3. Explainer cover: 1 named export, unchanged from the register.

House rules for this package

- No speech anywhere in the package. Nothing is narrated, nothing is dubbed, and no synthetic voice is added over the founder take.
- The release is named “Throughline”, never “the Throughline update” and never “Throughline 1.0”.
- The three cleared claims in norvant_launch_copy.txt cannot be reworded, shortened or paraphrased, and no fourth claim exists. If a claim does not fit the runtime, leave it out rather than soften it.
- The closing card carries the registration address norvant.com/march.
- Video delivery means a playable MP4, not a storyboard, still montage PDF or editing log. Live action edits require adequate distinct footage: looping one short clip is not a substitute for the missing coverage.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table. Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
