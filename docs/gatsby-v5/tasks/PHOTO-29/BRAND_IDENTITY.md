# Cape Marren Coast Alliance

Cape Marren Coast Alliance markets a stretch of independent coast to shoulder season visitors. Summer sells itself; the work is October to April, when a harbour seafood room, a cider barn, a point light, a bookshop, a bakery and an oyster landing either make a living or do not. The campaign has to feel like that season honestly: warm low sun, weather, wool, wet stone, a lit window at four in the afternoon, quiet compositions with one or two people. Members have to see themselves in it, because members who cannot see themselves do not renew.

## Audience
Travellers aged 30 to 60, driving from within four hours, mostly couples and pairs of friends rather than families. They spend 200 to 600 dollars per party across a two or three night shoulder season weekend on rooms, meals, cider and a walk.

## Positioning
Mid range and real. Positioned above a peak summer holiday treatment (which would oversell blue water and hard sun) and below a 1,500 dollar luxury coastal weekend (which would go near monochrome and strand the bakery and the cider barn). Editorial rather than promotional, and never promising a season the coast cannot deliver in November.

## Palette
- Warm October Sun: #C8894B
- Wet Stone: #5B5F5C
- Wool Cream: #E7DDC9
- Deep Harbour: #2E3A3C

## Typography
Editorial, not promotional. A quiet serif for headlines with a plain sans for captions, trail numbers and opening months. Restrained weights, comfortable spacing, nothing that reads as a sale.

## Voice
Grounded and truthful. Warm about the place without overselling it, and specific about what a visitor will actually find when they arrive out of season.

## Supplied Identity
No written brand guidelines exist. The only brand file on hand is the alliance mark left by the previous agency, supplied as a single vector file with nothing else: no color system, no type list and no usage notes. Members submit their own photographs and object shots, and the campaign look is set by this brief rather than by any existing kit.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
