# Pulsevault

Pulsevault is an independent monthly club night in a Cleveland warehouse, run by a small crew and promoted almost entirely on social in the days after each event. Door is 20 to 30 dollars, the room is late teens to twenties, and the night lives or dies on what goes up before the next tickets drop. Its audience follows the night, not the venue, and can smell something made by an agency from across the room.

## Audience
Late teens to twenties, following the night rather than the venue. They read agency polish as proof the night has been bought and is no longer theirs.

## Positioning
A 20 to 30 dollar door at an independent monthly night. Positioned deliberately rough: loud and immediate, kept clear of both a 100 dollar festival brand and a homemade flyer nobody made.

## Palette
- Blackout: #0C0C0F
- Stage Magenta: #E0247C
- Laser Cyan: #17C6D6
- Flood Amber: #E8933A
- Concrete: #4A4A50

## Typography
Display: a heavy, loud display cut doing real work, painted or condensed, with attitude. Text: a plain grotesque for the lineup and details, legible at story size. Loud, never corporate, never clip art. The type should feel like the room, not an advert.

## Voice
Direct and hyped but never fake, written the way the crew actually talks. Example line: 'Saturday. Warehouse. Tickets Tuesday. Do not sleep on it.'

## Supplied Identity
Pulsevault has never had a logo file. The mark exists only hand painted on the brick beside the entrance and survives as one photograph taken at the end of the night under a single floodlight. The wordmark has to be recovered from that photograph as artwork the night can reuse, and it has to hold on a cover that is close to black. There is no colour spec and no prior guideline. The one thing the night reprints unchanged is a plain lineup card its print shop runs a small batch of each month, supplied as last month's flat press artwork: the running order and the per act details change every date, the layout does not.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
