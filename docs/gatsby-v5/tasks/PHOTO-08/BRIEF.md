# SB3-008-PHO | Meridian Motors - Showroom vehicle image system

Standardise a used-car photo set without changing the cars

We photograph stock in three different bays at Meridian Motors. That is showing up in our listings: the same forecourt can look like three dealerships, and paint colours move around with the light.

The commission

 Vehicle sales card: 6 named exports. The vehicle photograph matches this stock record. The vehicle condition is not misrepresented by retouching. The enquiry action is readable.

 Forecourt inventory campaign: 1 named export. The dealership name is clear. The campaign uses vehicles present in the intake register.
