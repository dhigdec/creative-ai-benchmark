# SB3-075-LAY | Emberwell Brew House - Live insert and taproom collateral

Two reusable menu templates for fourteen locations, not fourteen manual edits

Our managers have been retyping the same Emberwell insert in a word processor. That led to a customer finding a different flat-white price twenty minutes down the road. We need two controlled templates that take their local details and prices from the data.

Deliverables
1. Current-menu insert: 15 named exports. The availability wording matches the current record. The price is legible.
2. Taproom counter card: 15 named exports. The store details match this record. The offer is understandable at counter distance.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
