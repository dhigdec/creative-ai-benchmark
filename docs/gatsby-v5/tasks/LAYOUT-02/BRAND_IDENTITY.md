# Corner & Cure

Corner & Cure is the repeat client for this benchmark series. The canonical client context is Brooklyn, New York, and this commission covers phase 2: counter refit, cold-case labels and signage.

## Audience
Walk up trade, mostly twenty to forty five, three visits a week, deciding in about eight seconds at the counter or six on a phone. They want appetite and honesty, not polish.

## Positioning
Twelve to sixteen dollars for a sandwich or a bowl. Impulse priced but not cheap: the price is the offer and sits large and proud, while the whole shop works to look like a place that is worth it rather than a bargain or a franchise.

## Palette
- Deli Red: #B4382C
- Counter Mustard: #D9A536
- Butcher Cream: #F2E7CE
- Slate Char: #2A2622
- Pickle Green: #4E6B3A

## Typography
The hand painted board sign carries the name and sets the tone. Around it, a bold condensed display face for the wall board, prices and specials, prices at tile size so they read across the room, with a plain workhorse sans for hours, loyalty rules and small print. Warm and loud, never a novelty pile.

## Voice
Plainspoken, neighbourhood, a little brash and generous. Example line the brand would write: 'Pulled together, piled high, see you at the counter.'

## Supplied Identity
There is no digital version of the sign: the name survives only as a board hand painted by a sign painter twenty years ago and screwed to the wall over the slicer. Everything else was shot on the owner's phone over the counter. A two page written note holds the voice, hours, loyalty rules and section names, and the label printer holds a press file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
