# Ferncroft Nursery

Ferncroft Nursery grows heirloom and specialty stock for gardeners who read the botanical name first. Everything is sold off the bench with a tag that reads like a plant person wrote it. Spring opening is the last Saturday of April, and every plant on the rack needs its tag by then.

## Audience
Gardeners aged thirty five to sixty five who buy for provenance, read the Latin name, and ask where the stock was grown. They distrust a rack where one tag looks like a different shop.

## Positioning
An heirloom specialty grower at eighteen to ninety five dollars a plant, above the six dollar big-box rack and below a fine-press botanical print. The tag is provenance-led and botanical-forward: the Latin carries, the price sits small, the photograph is a supporting note.

## Palette
- Slate Grey: #4A4E48
- Fern Green: #3E5A34
- Bench Cream: #EFE9DC
- Terracotta: #B0623D
- Bark Brown: #6B4E36
- Sage: #9AA988

## Typography
A humanist serif with real presence for the common and botanical names, the Latin set in italic beneath, paired with a quiet, small sans for the care line, pot size and price. Restraint is the point: the price should never shout.

## Voice
Warm, knowledgeable, unhurried, the voice of a grower. Example line the brand would write: 'Slow to establish, then generous for decades. Give it morning sun and patience.'

## Supplied Identity
