# SB3-024-PHO | Lunara Chronometry - Autumn watch campaign

Watch campaign treatment plus colour-accurate dealer product files

Two kinds of files are needed for Lunara Chronometry, and they must not be confused. The campaign can carry our tonal treatment; the dealer needs clean product files, including a dial-finish reference that stays true to colour.

Scope
1. Autumn watch key visual: 1 named export. The watch dial matches the approved reference. The watch remains the dominant visual.
2. Watch product-page feature: 5 named exports. The case geometry matches the named watch. The specification wording is accurate.
3. Watch dealer launch sheet: 1 named export. Model identities are unambiguous. The layout reflects the stated price position.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
