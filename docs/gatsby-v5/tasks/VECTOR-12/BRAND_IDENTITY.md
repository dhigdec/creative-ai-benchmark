# Static Union

Static Union is a small Portland label that puts out two considered drops a year and sells them direct and through roughly forty specialist stockists. The marks came out of the founder's own hand and the early tee runs, and they have been carried on garments for two seasons. Autumn is the first move into small goods, so the same marks now have to survive being made rather than printed.

## Audience
Buyers roughly 17 to 30 who follow small labels, shop drops rather than seasons, and buy direct or from specialist streetwear stockists. They notice when a pack does not match.

## Positioning
Accessible independent streetwear. Stickers at 4 dollars, pins at 14, patches at 18, wholesale to about forty stockists. Priced to be cheap enough to collect and good enough to keep, never luxury.

## Palette
- Union Black: #141414
- Bone: #EDE7DA
- Bolt Red: #C0392B
- Rail Gold: #C79A3B
- Slate: #4A5560

## Typography
Display is a heavy condensed grotesque with a workwear feel, used for the label name and drop titles. Text is a plain, legible sans for stockist copy and specs. The type stays quiet so the marks lead.

## Voice
Direct, plain, a little dry, talking to its own buyers rather than advertising. A line the brand would write: "Autumn small goods. Twelve pieces, made to last, gone when they are gone."

## Supplied Identity
There is no proper brand kit. The four marks that matter survive only as whatever files people happened to send back over three years: two came from a printer, one exists only as a photograph of a woven cap label because the file is gone, and one came out of an old tee artwork folder. They look fine on screen and have been used for two years, but there are no editable masters.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
