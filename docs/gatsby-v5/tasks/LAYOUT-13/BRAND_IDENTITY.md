# Marisol Cove

Marisol Cove is a small clifftop hotel built around a path down to a private beach and a dining room people plan a trip for. There is no pool and no spa, and the point of the place is the light, the water and being left alone. The autumn and winter shoulder season is the first the owners are pushing properly.

## Audience
Couples and small groups aged 30 to 55, experience led rather than amenity led, who book for the setting and the food and return in the quiet months.

## Positioning
Rooms run 340 to 620 dollars a night. Boutique premium, above a coastal inn and well below a resort or private estate. The rate is carried by the setting, not by facilities.

## Palette
- Bluff Sand: #E7DBC8
- Cove Grey Blue: #6E8894
- Cypress: #465448
- Warm Ink: #2C2621
- Dusk Clay: #C39A82

## Typography
A quiet high contrast serif for display, set large and given air, paired with a plain humanist sans for rates, captions and body. Coastal premium, never resort brochure and never luxury estate. Generous leading, restrained sizes.

## Voice
Calm, plain and specific. It describes what is actually there and lets the reader picture it. Example line the brand would write: An hour before sunset the light comes off the water and the whole cove goes gold. That is when to walk down.

## Supplied Identity
The logo file was lost with the designer who drew it in 2019. The mark, a small caps wordmark over a hairline wave rule, survives only as the painted sign at the entrance, which the owners have photographed. Room photography is the owners' own phone frames plus licensed stock. No usable brand files remain beyond the sign photo.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
