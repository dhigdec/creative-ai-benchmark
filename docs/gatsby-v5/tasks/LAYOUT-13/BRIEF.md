# SB3-058-LAY | Marisol Cove - Shoulder-season hospitality campaign

Shoulder-season hospitality campaign built from carefully matched photography

Marisol Cove needs a shoulder-season campaign that reflects the light we actually have. Most of the coastal pictures will be licensed; the usable room photographs are ours. The work is to make them belong together, not to promise a different coast or a different property.

Please prepare
- Shoulder-season key visual: 1 named export. The pictured property is correctly identified. The tagline is exactly “The quiet side of the coast.” No date, discount or unlisted amenity is added.
- Hospitality booking story: 1 named export. The booking action stays inside the safe area.
- Seasonal offer card: 10 named exports. The words “Shoulder Rate” appear beside the supplied Shoulder Rate value. The property identity is visible.
- Seasonal hospitality brochure: 1 named export. The offer is understandable without the original brief. The property imagery is truthful.

Use the supplied approved copy and factual records. Permission restrictions in the owner's notes take precedence over inclusion in a source table.

Use the attached brand identity and the source files listed in the asset manifest. The output register defines exact filenames, pages, sizes and record bindings. Supporting previews are welcome but cannot replace those files.
