# STATIC FOX

STATIC FOX is a loud, fast comedy gaming channel built on co op chaos and the creator's on camera reactions. Around four hundred thousand subscribers watch for the disasters, the bad calls and the running jokes. The videos land once people click, so the whole brand fight happens on the thumbnail, in the feed, at a glance.

## Audience
Mostly fifteen to twenty five, watching in a crowded feed on phones. They want energy and payoff, not taste. Income comes from advertising and channel memberships.

## Positioning
Mid tier creator funded by ad revenue and memberships. The work has to be loud, saturated and heavy on the face and the moment, because that is what wins the click at this level. Restrained and tasteful reads as the wrong channel; pure noise stops reading as one channel at all. Loud and coherent is the line.

## Palette
- Fox Orange: #FF5A1F
- Static Purple: #7A2FF2
- Acid Signal: #B6FF2E
- Jet Black: #101014
- Signal White: #FFFFFF

## Typography
A heavy condensed display face for titles that has to punch at thumbnail scale and survive both very short and very long titles, paired with a bold grotesque for supporting lines. Big, tight, unembarrassed. No delicate or editorial type anywhere.

## Voice
Fast, funny, self deprecating and a bit chaotic, always in on the joke. Example line the brand would write: "We had one job. We did not do the job. New one up now."

## Supplied Identity
The mascot, Scrap the fox in a headset, was drawn two years ago and survives only as a flat shaded render on a dark panel, with no clean cutout. The merch fox head emblem's original file is long gone and exists only as a photo of the embroidered patch on the creator's hoodie. There is no brand kit; titles and catchphrases live in a text file.

Production identity and cross-image continuity remain subject to the release asset checks.
